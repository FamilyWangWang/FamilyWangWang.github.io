20-9 through 短语动词（Phrasal Verbs with Through）

本组第 9 篇 · 共 10 篇 | 本篇 60 个主词条 + 10 个固定表达 | 精讲 15 项

场景导入

through 常表示从一端到另一端、从开始到完成，因而可表达经历、审查、完成、克服或成功联系。go through 是经历或检查，work through 是逐步解决，get through 是完成或联系上。

可分结构的代词宾语放在中间：talk me through it。多数带介词色彩的结构不可分：wade through the report、follow through on a promise。

精讲

get through

可表示完成任务、渡过难关或打通电话。宾语和语境决定意义。

I finally got through to technical support.

go through

可指经历事件、检查材料或履行程序；go through with 强调最终照做。

We went through every item on the checklist.

work through

强调按步骤解决复杂问题或处理情绪，不追求快速完成。

The team worked through the remaining defects.

talk through

可表示向某人逐步讲解，也可共同讨论方案。人作宾语时可说 talk me through it。

Can you talk me through the recovery process?

walk through

与 talk through 相近，但更常包含实际演示、界面操作或逐项检查。

She walked us through the new dashboard.

fall through

主语通常是计划、交易或安排，表示未能实现，不能把人作主语。

The sale fell through at the last minute.

pull through

表示从重病或危险中恢复。医疗语境中常单独使用，不接宾语。

The doctors expect him to pull through.

follow through on

表示不只承诺，还完成必要的后续行动。

Managers must follow through on agreed changes.

see through

see through a lie 是识破；see a task through 是坚持完成，宾语位置不同。

She saw through the false explanation.

run through

可表示快速讲解、排练或查看清单。它通常比 go through 更快、更概括。

Let us run through the agenda.

sift through

强调从大量材料中仔细筛选有价值的部分；sort through 更偏整理分类。

Investigators sifted through hundreds of messages.

skim through

只快速获取大意；需要全面理解时应使用 read through。

I skimmed through the report before the meeting.

make it through

强调成功熬过困难时段或达到结束点，it 是固定组成部分。

We made it through the busiest week of the year.

cut through

抽象义表示去除复杂性、噪声或官样文章，直接触及重点。

The summary cuts through the technical jargon.

slip through

常表示错误或人员未被检查发现。固定表达 slip through the cracks 指被系统遗漏。

One duplicate entry slipped through the review.

易混辨析

  • skim through：浏览大意；read through：完整通读；pore over：深入研究。
  • run through：快速讲解或排练；walk through：逐步演示。
  • get through：完成或联系上；make it through：成功熬过困难。
  • fall through：计划失败；go through with：仍按计划做完。
  • sort through：整理分类；sift through：筛选有价值的信息。

语境回收

We went through the incident log, sifted through the evidence, and worked through each possible cause. One warning had slipped through the first review. Maya walked us through the fix, and the team followed through on every corrective action. We eventually made it through the final audit.

  5. 翻译：我们逐步解决了剩余问题。
  6. 选择：快速浏览报告用 skim through / sift through。
  7. 填空：She went through ______ the difficult decision.
  8. 翻译：一个错误漏过了检查。
  9. 改错：We made through it the night.
  10. 选择：从重病中恢复用 pull through / see through。
