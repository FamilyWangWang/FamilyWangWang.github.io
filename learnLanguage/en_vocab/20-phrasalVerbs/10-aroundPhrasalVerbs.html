20-10 around 短语动词（Phrasal Verbs with Around）

本组第 10 篇 · 共 10 篇 | 本篇 60 个主词条 + 10 个固定表达 | 精讲 15 项

场景导入

around 常表示环绕、在附近活动、改变方向或避开障碍，并延伸出传播、随意尝试、拖延和想办法解决等意义。look around 是四处看看，come around 是恢复或改变看法，work around 是绕过限制。

可分结构的代词宾语放在中间：show them around、boss me around。带后续介词的结构通常不可分：get around to a task、play around with an idea。

精讲

get around

可表示四处出行、规避规则或消息传播。get around to 则表示终于处理拖延事项。

I finally got around to answering the email.

come around

可表示恢复意识、到访或改变看法。接受某观点通常说 come around to。

She eventually came around to our way of thinking.

go around

可表示传播、四处活动或数量足够分配。There is enough to go around 是常见句型。

A stomach bug is going around the office.

hang around

表示无明确目的地留在附近；接同伴时用 hang around with someone。

I will hang around until the bus arrives.

look around

表示环顾、参观或寻找。找具体物品时常用 look around for。

We looked around the neighborhood before renting.

show around

表示带访客参观场所。可分，必须说 show them around。

I will show the new employees around.

work around

表示不直接消除限制，而是找到替代路径继续完成目标。

We found a way to work around the software bug.

shop around

表示购买前比较不同商家的价格、条款或质量，常接 for。

Shop around for a better interest rate.

mess around with

可表示随意摆弄，也可警告不要干涉危险设备。语气比 experiment with 更不严肃。

Do not mess around with the electrical panel.

play around with

表示低风险、非正式地尝试不同方案，常用于设计、措辞和设置。

We played around with several color combinations.

kick around

表示非正式地讨论尚未成熟的想法，可分：kick it around。

Let us kick the idea around before deciding.

dance around

表示刻意回避直接谈核心问题，通常带批评意味。

The statement danced around the question of cost.

spread around

可表示把工作、资源或信息分散给多人。代词放中间：spread it around。

We spread the tasks around the whole team.

stick around

表示在预期离开时间之后仍留在附近，常用于友好邀请或等候。

Stick around for the questions after the talk.

rally around

表示群体在困难时共同支持某人、组织或目标。

Neighbors rallied around the affected families.

易混辨析

  • look around：查看环境；shop around：比较购买条件。
  • fool around / mess around：都可指胡闹，后者更常接设备或事物。
  • dance around / talk around / skirt around：都指回避核心，语气依次从形象到正式。
  • hang around / stick around：前者常无明确目的，后者强调留下不走。
  • come around to：改变看法；get around to：终于有时间做。

语境回收

We asked around for advice and shopped around for a better service. One supplier tried to dance around the support issue, so we looked around elsewhere. In the end, we worked around the technical limitation and spread the workload around. Even the skeptical managers came around to the new approach.

自测

  1. 填空：I finally got around ______ booking the appointment.
  2. 改错：Let me show around them.
  3. 选择：比较不同价格用 shop around / sit around。
  4. 填空：She came around ______ our point of view.
  5. 翻译：我们找到了绕过这个限制的办法。
  6. 选择：回避核心问题用 dance around / rally around。
  7. 填空：A rumor is going ______ the office.
  8. 翻译：请留下来参加问答环节。
  9. 改错：They spread around it fairly.
  10. 选择：集体支持当地家庭用 rally around / boss around。
答案
  1. to
  2. Let me show them around.
  3. shop around
  4. to
  5. We found a way to work around the limitation.
  6. dance around
  7. around
  8. Please stick around for the question-and-answer session.
  9. They spread it around fairly.
  10. rally around
