20-6 over 短语动词（Phrasal Verbs with Over）

本组第 6 篇 · 共 10 篇 | 本篇 60 个主词条 + 10 个固定表达 | 精讲 15 项

场景导入

over 常表示越过、覆盖、翻转或从头到尾，也可延伸为复查、克服、接管和延续。look over 是查看，get over 是克服，turn over 是翻转，talk over 是商量。

可分结构的代词宾语放在中间：think it over、win them over。介词结构通常不可分：pore over the data、take over from a colleague。

精讲

get over

表示从疾病、打击或失望中恢复，也可指克服障碍。它不可分：get over it。

It took her weeks to get over the flu.

go over

强调复查、复习或逐项讲解；look over 往往只是快速浏览。

Let us go over the final figures once more.

think over

表示决定前认真考虑，可分，代词放中间：think it over。

Think the offer over before you reply.

talk over

强调与别人讨论后作决定，常说 talk something over with someone。

I need to talk the plan over with my team.

turn over

可表示翻面、移交控制权或产生营业额。具体含义由宾语决定。

Please turn the document over and sign the back.

run over

可指车辆撞倒，也可指超时或快速复述。教学和会议中常用 run over the main points。

The presentation ran over by ten minutes.

win over

表示通过理由或行动使原本怀疑的人转而支持，可分：win them over。

The clear evidence won the committee over.

gloss over

表示轻描淡写地略过问题，往往暗示有意回避，不等于中性的简要概括。

The report glossed over several safety failures.

pore over

表示长时间专注研究。注意拼写是 pore，不是倒液体的 pour。

Analysts pored over the data all night.

start over

表示现有尝试不能保留，需要从头开始。美式英语特别常用，其他地区也通行。

The file was corrupted, so we had to start over.

spill over

表示影响超出原边界，常接 into 指明扩展到哪个领域。

The conflict spilled over into neighboring regions.

blow over

表示风暴、争议或麻烦自行减弱并过去，主语通常是事件而不是人。

The criticism may blow over in a few days.

tide over

表示提供短期帮助，让某人撑到困难结束，常见结构是 tide someone over。

This payment should tide us over until Friday.

get something over with

表示尽快完成令人不快但必须做的事。宾语固定放在 get 与 over 之间。

I want to get the appointment over with.

take over from

明确表达从谁手中接替工作；只说接管某项工作时用 take over something。

Lina will take over from me next month.

易混辨析

  • look over：快速查看；go over：仔细复核；pore over：长时间深入研究。
  • think over：独自考虑；talk over：与别人商量。
  • fall over：失去平衡倒下；tip over：倾倒；knock over：被碰倒。
  • come over：到访；stay over / sleep over：在别人处过夜。
  • gloss over / paper over：分别侧重淡化细节和掩盖未解决的矛盾。

语境回收

Before I took over from Maya, we went over every open task. I pored over the risk log and asked her to talk over two options with me. After I had thought them over, I chose the simpler plan. It helped tide us over until the new system was ready.

自测

  1. 填空：She took over ______ the previous manager.
  2. 改错：Please think over it.
  3. 选择：长时间仔细研究数据用 pore over / gloss over。
  4. 填空：The tension spilled over ______ the next meeting.
  5. 翻译：我们需要从头开始。
  6. 选择：快速查看合同用 look over / tide over。
  7. 填空：This loan should tide us ______ until June.
  8. 翻译：这场争议很快就会过去。
  9. 改错：The evidence won over them.
  10. 选择：尽快做完不愉快的事用 get it over with / turn it over。
答案
  1. from
  2. Please think it over.
  3. pore over
  4. into
  5. We need to start over.
  6. look over
  7. over
  8. The controversy will soon blow over.
  9. The evidence won them over.
  10. get it over with
