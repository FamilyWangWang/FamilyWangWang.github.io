20-8 back 短语动词（Phrasal Verbs with Back）

本组第 8 篇 · 共 10 篇 | 本篇 60 个主词条 + 10 个固定表达 | 精讲 15 项

场景导入

back 常表达返回原处、恢复原状、向后移动或作出回应，并延伸出削减、阻止和回顾等含义。give back 是归还，bounce back 是恢复，hold back 是抑制，think back 是回想。

可分结构的代词宾语放在中间：pay it back、read it back。带后续介词的结构通常不可分：fall back on savings、go back on a promise。

精讲

get back

不及物时表示返回；及物时表示取回。get back to someone 表示稍后回复。

I will get back to you after checking the figures.

go back

表示回到地点、时间或旧状态。go back on 则是违背承诺，意义不同。

We cannot go back to the old system.

hold back

可表示阻止前进、隐瞒信息或强忍情绪。可分，代词放中间。

She held back the final detail until the review.

cut back

表示有计划地缩减规模；说明减少某种消费或习惯时用 cut back on。

We need to cut back on unnecessary travel.

bounce back

强调从疾病、失败或经济下滑中较快恢复，常接 from。

Sales bounced back after a weak quarter.

fall back on

表示主要方法失效时使用备用资源、技能或计划。

We can fall back on the manual process.

push back

可表示推迟时间，也可表示反对要求或观点。语境决定是时间调整还是抵制。

Several teams pushed back against the proposal.

set back

表示使进度延误；set someone back + 金额 还可表示花费多少钱。

The fault set the project back two weeks.

roll back

表示撤销已实施的政策、价格或软件版本，常暗示回到先前状态。

Engineers rolled back the faulty update.

walk back

除步行返回外，新闻和职场语境常指收回或弱化先前的说法。

The spokesperson later walked back the claim.

trace back to

表示通过调查把当前结果追溯到最初来源。

The outage was traced back to a configuration error.

circle back

表示暂时搁置后重新谈某个话题，常用于职场沟通，但不宜过度使用。

Let us circle back to staffing after lunch.

fight back

可表示实际反击，也常与 tears 搭配，表示强忍眼泪。

He fought back tears during the farewell.

think back

表示主动回想，常用 think back to + 时间/经历；look back 更偏整体回顾。

Think back to how the problem first appeared.

bite back

表示把愤怒的话或反应强行忍住，宾语常为 reply、comment、tears。

She bit back a sharp reply.

易混辨析

  • get back：返回或取回；give back / hand back：归还。
  • cut back / scale back：都指削减，后者更强调总体规模。
  • fight back / strike back：都指反击，后者更强调对攻击采取回击行动。
  • think back：主动回想；flash back：记忆突然重现。
  • push back：推迟或反对；put back：放回原位或把时间后移。

语境回收

The failed update set us back by two days, so we rolled it back and fell back on the previous version. Sales soon bounced back. I reported back to the team, promised to get back to the client, and then circled back to the longer-term repair plan.

自测

  1. 填空：I will get back ______ you tomorrow.
  2. 改错：Please pay back it by Friday.
  3. 选择：从失败中恢复用 bounce back / hold back。
  4. 填空：We had to fall back ______ the old system.
  5. 翻译：这次故障使项目延误了两周。
  6. 选择：违背承诺用 go back on / come back to。
  7. 填空：The fault was traced back ______ a loose cable.
  8. 翻译：发言人后来收回了这一说法。
  9. 改错：They rolled back it immediately.
  10. 选择：强忍住愤怒的回答用 bite back / answer back。
答案
  1. to
  2. Please pay it back by Friday.
  3. bounce back
  4. on
  5. The fault set the project back two weeks.
  6. go back on
  7. to
  8. The spokesperson later walked back the claim.
  9. They rolled it back immediately.
  10. bite back
