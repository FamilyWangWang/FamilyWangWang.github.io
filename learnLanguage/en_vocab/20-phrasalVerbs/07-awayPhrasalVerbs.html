20-7 away 短语动词（Phrasal Verbs with Away）

本组第 7 篇 · 共 10 篇 | 本篇 60 个主词条 + 10 个固定表达 | 精讲 15 项

场景导入

away 的核心意象是“离开原处”，并延伸出消失、清除、持续不停和储存备用等意义。fade away 是逐渐消失，wash away 是冲走，work away 是不停工作，squirrel away 是收藏备用。

可分结构的代词宾语放在中间：give it away、lock them away。三词结构通常不可分：get away with cheating、shy away from conflict。

精讲

get away

表示逃脱或短暂离开日常生活休息；说明远离什么用 get away from。

We hope to get away for a few days.

get away with

表示做错事后未受惩罚，也可表示某种选择勉强可行。它不可分。

He cannot get away with ignoring the rules.

give away

可表示赠送，也可表示无意泄露秘密、答案或身份。代词放中间：give it away。

Her accent gave her identity away.

take away

可表示拿走、数学中的减法，或从经历中获得主要认识。

What did you take away from the workshop?

carry away

be/get carried away 表示兴奋得失去分寸；不要按字面理解为“被搬走”。

We got carried away and spent too much.

do away with

表示废除制度或去除不需要的东西。三个部分不能分开。

The update does away with manual entry.

fade away

强调声音、影像、记忆等缓慢变弱并消失；go away 只说明不再存在。

The footsteps gradually faded away.

chip away at

强调通过许多小动作逐步减少任务、阻力或信心。

We chipped away at the backlog each morning.

eat away at

可指物理腐蚀，也可指忧虑长期损害一个人的内心。

Guilt had been eating away at him.

shy away from

表示因害怕、不愿或缺乏信心而回避困难事项。

Good leaders do not shy away from criticism.

walk away from

表示主动离开交易、关系或局面，通常因为条件不可接受。

Be prepared to walk away from a bad deal.

wear away

强调长期摩擦造成逐步损耗；wash away 则强调水流造成清除或侵蚀。

Years of use wore the lettering away.

squirrel away

比 save 更形象，指把钱或物品分散藏好以备后用。

She squirreled away a little money each month.

fire away

用于允许对方立即开始提问或发言，语气友好而口语化。

If you have questions, fire away.

while away

表示用轻松活动愉快地消磨时间，宾语通常是时间段。

We whiled away the evening playing cards.

易混辨析

  • fade away / die away：都指逐渐消失，后者常用于声音、风或光。
  • go away：离开或消失；get away：成功逃脱或外出休息。
  • brush away：轻轻扫除；wipe away：擦除；wash away：用水冲走。
  • store：中性储存；squirrel away：秘密或零散地储备。
  • shy away from：因不愿而回避；back away from：从承诺或威胁前退缩。

语境回收

We did not shy away from the backlog. Instead, we chipped away at it every morning and worked away until the pressure began to melt away. One discrepancy could not simply be explained away, so we fixed its cause. I came away with a much clearer view of the process.

自测

  1. 填空：She got away ______ breaking the rule.
  2. 改错：Do not give away it.
  3. 选择：逐步削减积压用 chip away at / fade away。
  4. 填空：He shied away ______ difficult questions.
  5. 翻译：疼痛终于消失了。
  6. 选择：废除旧制度用 do away with / walk away from。
  7. 填空：Rust was eating away ______ the pipe.
  8. 翻译：这次经历让我获得了新的认识。
  9. 改错：Please wipe away them.
  10. 选择：兴奋得失去克制用 get carried away / be tucked away。
答案
  1. with
  2. Do not give it away.
  3. chip away at
  4. from
  5. The pain finally went away.
  6. do away with
  7. at
  8. I came away from the experience with new insights.
  9. Please wipe them away.
  10. get carried away
