03-6 社交行为动词（Social Behavior）

场景导入

得体的英语交流不仅靠 please 和 thank you，还包括选择合适的称呼、轮流发言、倾听和礼貌收尾。直接并不等于无礼，但请求、追问和不同意见通常需要用缓和表达。

初次见面可以 greet、introduce 和 make small talk；谈话中可以 clarify、rephrase 或请对方 elaborate；离开时则应 excuse yourself 并说合适的告别语。

精讲

politeness / etiquette / manners

politeness 是礼貌品质，etiquette 是特定场合的规则，manners 是一个人表现出的礼貌习惯。

small talk / conversation

small talk 是轻松、不深入私事的寒暄；conversation 可以讨论任何深浅的内容。

compliment / complement

compliment 是赞美；complement 是补充并使事物更完整，读音相近但含义不同。

request / ask

ask someone to do something 最自然。正式动词 request 常接事物或 that 从句；request someone to do 也正确，但语气正式。

explain

说 explain something to someone，不能说 explain someone something。

discuss

discuss a topic 直接接宾语，不加 about；名词结构才说 a discussion about a topic。

mention / elaborate

mention 是简短提到；elaborate on 是进一步展开说明。

reply / answer / respond

answer 可直接接问题或人；reply、respond 通常接 to，后两者稍正式。

apologize

结构为 apologize to someone for something / doing something，人和事的介词不同。

compliment / flatter

compliment 通常是真诚具体的赞美；flatter 可能带讨好目的或夸大成分。

offer / provide

offer 强调主动提出；provide 强调实际供应所需事物。

interrupt / interject

interrupt 强调打断他人；interject 是短暂插入一句话，仍可能被视为不礼貌。

direct / rude

直接表达本身不等于无礼。语气、场合、权力关系和缓和措辞共同决定是否合适。

formal / informal

正式程度影响称呼、缩写和句型，但 informal 不等于随便或不尊重。

身体问候

握手、拥抱、鞠躬和眼神接触的规范因文化、关系和个人边界而异；不确定时先观察或询问。

易混辨析

  • thanks / apology：前者承认善意，后者承认自己造成的不便或伤害。
  • question / request：前者索取信息，后者希望对方采取行动。
  • say / tell：say something，但 tell someone something。
  • courteous / friendly：礼貌周到不一定表示想建立私交。
  • reserved / impolite：话少不等于无礼，应根据行为而非性格判断。

语境回收

At a formal dinner, Priya greeted the host and said, “Thank you for having me.” The host introduced her to another guest, and Priya struck up a conversation with him. She began with small talk, then asked an open question and listened without trying to interrupt. When she did not understand one point, she politely asked him to clarify and then summarized what she had heard. Later, she complimented the host on the meal. Before leaving, she excused herself, offered a warm farewell, and thanked everyone for a pleasant evening.
