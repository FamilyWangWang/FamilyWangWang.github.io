21-3 程度词与强化弱化表达（Degree Words and Intensifiers）

本组第 3 篇 · 共 5 篇 | 本篇 60 个主词条 + 10 个固定表达 | 精讲 15 项

场景导入

程度词调节描述的强弱、完整程度和比较差距。slightly 表示轻微，fairly 表示中等程度，extremely 表示极强，completely 表示完全。它们并非都能自由搭配：自然英语说 absolutely impossible，通常不说 very impossible。

学习时要分清可分级形容词与极限形容词，还要注意词序：very useful、old enough、far more useful、too difficult to finish。

精讲

very / really

very 主要修饰分级形容词和副词；really 还可修饰动词：really like。

The instructions are very clear.

quite

与分级形容词搭配常是“相当”，与极限形容词搭配可表示“完全”。英美语感有细微差异。

The task is quite difficult, but the answer is quite certain.

fairly / rather

fairly 常给出温和、可接受的评价；rather 较强，常带意外或不满意。

The room is fairly large but rather dark.

slightly / somewhat

两者都弱化程度。slightly 更精确地表示小差异，somewhat 更正式且范围稍宽。

The revised estimate is slightly lower.

barely / hardly

两者都带否定意义。barely 强调刚达到最低限度；hardly 强调几乎没有发生。

We barely had enough time.

almost / nearly

多数场景可互换；almost 搭配范围更广，可修饰 everyone、never 等。

Almost everyone agreed.

completely / absolutely

常与极限或不可分级形容词搭配：completely wrong、absolutely impossible。

The two designs are completely different.

fully / thoroughly

fully 强调达到完整状态；thoroughly 强调过程全面、没有遗漏。

Read the instructions thoroughly before you begin.

highly / deeply / strongly

三者有搭配偏好：highly effective、deeply concerned、strongly recommend。

We strongly recommend a full backup.

significantly / substantially

都表示明显变化；significantly 强调差异值得注意，substantially 强调幅度很大。

The update significantly reduced loading time.

too / enough

too 放在形容词前，表示超过合适程度；enough 放在形容词后、名词前。

It is too heavy to lift but small enough to store.

such / so

such 修饰名词短语：such a problem；so 修饰形容词或副词：so difficult。

It was such a useful session.

much / even + 比较级

两者都能加强比较级。much 强调差距大，even 强调在已有程度上进一步增加。

The second version is much easier to use.

not nearly / nowhere near

都表示远未达到，nowhere near 语气更强、更口语化。

The work is nowhere near complete.

just / only

just 可表示恰好、刚刚或仅仅；only 主要限定范围。位置不同可能改变焦点。

We have just enough time for one more question.

易混辨析

① very / absolutely

very 配分级形容词；absolutely 常配极限形容词，如 absolutely essential。

② too / very

very 只加强程度；too 表示超过需要或可接受的限度。

③ enough / sufficiently

enough 更日常，且形容词后置；sufficiently 较正式，放在形容词前。

④ almost / most

almost 是“几乎”；most 是“大多数”或最高级，二者不可混用。

语境回收

English

The first draft was fairly clear but slightly too long. The revised version is much shorter and significantly easier to scan. It is not completely finished, but it is nearly ready. The examples are highly relevant, and the instructions are clear enough for a new user. One section is far too technical and needs simpler wording.

中文

第一稿还算清楚，但稍微太长。修订版短得多，也明显更容易浏览。它还没有完全完成，但已经接近就绪。例子非常贴切，说明也足够清楚，新用户能够理解。只有一节实在太技术化，需要更简单的措辞。

自测

  1. 选择：修饰动词 like 使用 really / very。
  2. 填空：The box is small ________ to fit under the desk.
  3. 改错：The task is enough easy.
  4. 翻译：第二个方案便宜得多。
  5. 选择：搭配 impossible 更自然的是 very / absolutely。
  6. 填空：We ________ had enough time to finish.（勉强）
  7. 选择：“远未完成”用 nowhere near / almost。
  8. 翻译：我强烈建议先备份。
  9. 改错：It was so a useful meeting.
  10. 填空：The update ________ reduced loading time.（显著地）
答案
  1. really
  2. enough
  3. The task is easy enough.
  4. The second option is much cheaper.
  5. absolutely
  6. barely
  7. nowhere near
  8. I strongly recommend making a backup first.
  9. It was such a useful meeting.
  10. significantly
