19-3 意愿与决定（Willing and Deciding）

本组第 3 篇 · 共 9 篇 | 本篇 60 个主词条 + 12 个固定表达 | 精讲 15 项

场景导入

wish 和 desire 表示想要某种结果，intend 表示准备采取行动，decide 则是在选项之间形成确定结果。日常表达可以说 I want to ...，正式计划常说 We have in mind ...、We aim for ... 或 We decided on ...。

一项决定还受到 choice opportunity、decision authority 和 decision latitude 的限制：有多个理论选项，不一定代表当事人拥有真实可行的选择；有选择自由，也不一定有代表组织作出正式决定的权限。

精讲

wish / desire / intended action

Wish 是普通愿望，desire 通常强度更高；intended action 已经从愿望推进到准备实施的具体行动。

plan / undertaking

Plan 是步骤、时间和资源安排；undertaking 是准备承担的重要任务或项目，未必已经列出完整步骤。

firm decision / selection outcome

Firm decision 强调个人或集体已明确决定；selection outcome 是挑选程序产生的结果，不特别强调意志坚定。

option / alternative / possibility

Option 是可选择的具体方案；alternative 是替代方案；possibility 只说明可能发生或做到，不一定已成为可执行选项。

determination / willpower

Determination 是面对困难仍朝目标行动的坚定态度；willpower 特别指抵抗短期诱惑、维持意图的自我控制能力。

decision-making capacity / decision authority

Decision-making capacity 是理解和权衡后作决定的能力；decision authority 是依据角色或授权代表组织决定的权限。

freedom of choice / choice opportunity

Freedom of choice 是不受不当强迫的选择空间；choice opportunity 是现实中确有两个或更多可行选项。

decision latitude / external control

Decision latitude 是规则内自主选择的范围；external control 是行动主要由外部力量规定。两者常呈程度关系而非绝对二分。

free will / self-determination

Free will 是关于意志自主性的哲学或法律观念；self-determination 更常指依个人价值安排生活与行动的能力或权利。

motive / decision basis

Motive 是促使某人行动的理由；decision basis 是支持选择的事实、标准和资料。动机不能替代证据基础。

goal definition / goal conflict

Goal definition 明确希望实现什么；goal conflict 表示两个目标相互妨碍，通常需要排序、折中或重构目标。

prioritization / personal priority

Prioritization 是确定先后的过程；personal priority 是某个人实际优先重视的目标或事项。

decision readiness / formal decision-making

Decision readiness 表示信息已足够、可以负责任地决定；formal decision-making 是有权主体依程序通过决定。

no-alternative claim / wishful vision

No-alternative claim 声称没有可行替代；wishful vision 则由愿望主导而缺乏现实条件。两者都应通过证据和选项分析检验。

decide on / make up one's mind / change one's mind

Decide on 强调确定对象或方案；make up one's mind 强调结束犹豫；change one's mind 表示后来改变原决定或看法。

易混辨析

  • want / wish：直接想要或打算 / 希望某情况出现，常更委婉或较难实现。
  • intend / plan：有行动意图 / 已安排步骤、时间或资源。
  • choose / decide：从选项中挑选 / 形成决定，可能涉及是否行动。
  • prefer / choose：表达相对偏好 / 实际作出选择。
  • reconsider / revoke：重新考虑 / 正式撤销已经存在的许可或授权。
  • voluntary / willing：不受强迫的性质 / 某人愿意行动的状态。

语境回收

After some hesitation, Mara reviewed every realistic course of action. Her goal definition was clear, but a goal conflict remained between cost and flexibility. Once the team had a reliable decision basis, she made up her mind, decided on an online program, and committed herself to the start date. The decision process still allowed her to reconsider if material facts changed.

自测

  1. 选择：准备实施的重要任务或项目是 undertaking / possibility。
  2. 填空：The ability to resist short-term temptation is called ______.
  3. 改错：Decision authority and decision-making capacity mean exactly the same thing.
  4. 翻译：我们需要在成本和灵活性之间解决目标冲突。
  5. 选择：结束犹豫并形成决定是 make up one's mind / refrain from。
  6. 填空：The committee decided ______ the second option.
  7. 改错：A theoretical possibility is always a genuine choice opportunity.
  8. 选择：根据新信息重新考虑是 reconsider / revoke。
  9. 翻译：她后来改变了对这个提议的看法。
  10. 填空：Please establish a reliable decision ______ before choosing.
参考答案
  1. undertaking
  2. willpower
  3. Capacity is the ability to understand and decide; authority is the permission or power to make a binding decision.
  4. We need to resolve the goal conflict between cost and flexibility.
  5. make up one's mind
  6. on
  7. A genuine choice opportunity requires realistic, available options.
  8. reconsider
  9. She later changed her mind about the proposal.
  10. basis
