19-5 争论与说服（Arguing and Persuading）

本组第 5 篇 · 共 9 篇 | 本篇 60 个主词条 + 12 个固定表达 | 精讲 15 项

场景导入

一段完整论证通常包含 thesis、supporting reasons、evidence 和 conclusion。convince 是通过理由使对方接受某个观点，persuade 常强调促使对方采取行动；两者在日常英语中有重叠，但结构不同：convince someone that ...，persuade someone to do ...。

面对反方意见时，可以先准确表述 objection，再判断它需要 rebuttal、refutation，还是只需补充限定。削弱某条理由并不等于证明整个结论为假。

精讲

reason / supporting reason / evidence

Reason 解释为什么应接受结论；supporting reason 直接连接到特定主张；evidence 是可检查的资料、观察或数据。

thesis / assertion

Thesis 是整篇论证准备支持的核心主张；assertion 只是以确定语气说出的陈述，可能没有论证。

example / proof

Example 帮助说明或提供个案支持；proof 要达到相应领域确认命题成立的标准。单个例子通常不能证明普遍命题。

objection / contradiction

Objection 是反对某观点的一条理由；contradiction 是两个命题不能同时成立的逻辑关系。

debate / controversy

Debate 是组织化的意见交锋活动；controversy 是可能长期存在的社会、政治或学术争议。

argumentation / persuasive force

Argumentation 是主张、理由和证据的组织；persuasive force 是它对特定听众产生说服效果的强度。

line / structure / chain of reasoning

Line of argument 是贯穿全文的主线；argument structure 是各部分关系；chain of reasoning 是相互依赖、逐步推进的环节。

argument gap / counterclaim

Argument gap 是缺少必要前提或步骤；counterclaim 是与原主张相反的陈述，本身也需要理由和证据。

credibility / evidential force

Credibility 评价来源是否值得相信；evidential force 评价具体材料对特定结论支持多强。可信来源也可能提供不相关材料。

rebuttal / refutation

Rebuttal 回应并削弱反方理由；refutation 旨在证明主张或论证不成立，所需证明强度更高。

rhetoric / polemic

Rhetoric 是有目的地组织语言的宽泛方法；polemic 强调尖锐对抗和攻击，常弱化平衡分析。

convince / persuade

Convince 常接观点：convince someone that ...；persuade 常接行动：persuade someone to do ...。实际使用有重叠。

support / prove

Support with evidence 表示增加可信度；prove 表示达到确认成立的标准。证据“支持”结论不必然“证明”结论。

weaken / refute

Weaken an objection 只降低其力量；refute 需要证明它错误或无效。局部削弱不等于彻底反驳。

convincing / well-founded

Convincing 描述对听众有说服效果；well-founded 描述理由和证据基础充分。修辞有力的说法也可能缺乏依据。

易混辨析

  • argument / quarrel：由理由构成的论证，也可指争吵 / 人际争吵。
  • evidence / proof：支持结论的材料 / 足以确认命题的证明。
  • objection / counterclaim：反对理由 / 相反主张。
  • convince / persuade：使人接受观点 / 促使人采取行动。
  • rebut / refute：回应并削弱 / 证明错误或无效。
  • consensus / unanimity：广泛共同认可 / 每一参与者都同意。

语境回收

In the debate, Lea formulated a central thesis and added two supporting reasons. Another participant raised a well-founded objection and put forward a counterclaim. Lea did not simply repeat her assertion. She cited new data as evidence, acknowledged an argument gap, and narrowed the claim. The revised argument was more convincing, and the group began to build a consensus.

自测

  1. 选择：等待论证的核心主张是 thesis / example。
  2. 填空：A missing premise creates an argument ______.
  3. 改错：One illustrative example necessarily proves a universal claim.
  4. 翻译：请用可核查的证据支持这个说法。
  5. 选择：证明原主张错误是 refutation / persuasion。
  6. 填空：The source is credible, but the material has little evidential ______.
  7. 改错：Convince someone to participate is always the preferred standard structure.
  8. 选择：反方提出、同样需要证据的主张是 counterclaim / objection。
  9. 翻译：双方最终开始逐步建立共识。
  10. 填空：A statement made confidently but without support is an unsupported ______.
参考答案
  1. thesis
  2. gap
  3. An example may illustrate or support a claim, but it does not necessarily prove a universal claim.
  4. Please support the claim with verifiable evidence.
  5. refutation
  6. force
  7. The usual structure is persuade someone to participate; convince commonly introduces a belief or proposition.
  8. counterclaim
  9. The two sides eventually began to build a consensus.
  10. assertion
