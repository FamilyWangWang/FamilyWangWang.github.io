人称与归属

谁做 动作对象／介词后 名词前的归属 单独说“……的”
I me my mine
you you your yours
he him his his
she her her hers
it it its 通常重复名词或改写
we us our ours
they them their theirs

This is my bag. That bag is hers.（这是我的包，那个包是她的。）its 是归属；it's 是 it is / it has 的缩写。性别未知的单个人也可以用 they，对应 them/their/theirs。

be、have、do 的现在形式

主语 be have do
I am have do
you / we / they are have do
he / she / it is has does

普通动词现在时 he/she/it 常加 -s：works；以适当词尾结尾时加 -es：watches；辅音 + y 常变 -ies：tries。does / doesn't 后的普通动作保持原形：She doesn't work.。这里不要把助动词本身和后面的动作混淆。

时间与进度句型

目标 肯定 提问／否定
平常做 I work here. Do you work here? / I don't work here.
现在正在做 I'm working. Are you working? / I'm not working.
明确过去发生 I sent it yesterday. Did you send it? / I didn't send it.
到现在已完成 I've sent it. Have you sent it? / I haven't sent it yet.
到现在持续活动 I've been waiting. How long have you been waiting?
过去某动作前已完成 I had sent it before she called. I hadn't sent it when she called.
打算 I'm going to call. Are you going to call?
已约好的安排 I'm meeting Anna tomorrow. Are you meeting Anna tomorrow?
承诺或预测 I'll call tomorrow. Will you call? / I won't call.

这些是常用选择，不表示每种时间只能对应一个结构。具体含义见 时间模块。

请求、需要与否定

结构 后接形式 例子
can / could / should / must / will / would 原形，不加 -s Could you send it?
want / need / plan / decide 常用 to + 原形 I plan to send it.
enjoy / finish -ing I've finished checking it.
would like to + 原形 I'd like to meet.
would rather 原形 I'd rather meet online.
would mind -ing Would you mind checking it?
介词后动作 -ing Thank you for helping.

禁止：You mustn't share it.；不必：You don't have to share it.；没能力或不能来：I can't come.。三个意思要分开。

比较与数量：日常够用的几组

目标 句型
更便宜／更方便 This is cheaper / more convenient than that. 这个比那个便宜／方便。
一样大 This room is as big as that one. 这个房间和那个一样大。
不够大／太小 It isn't big enough. / It's too small. 不够大／太小。
最高级 This is the cheapest option. 这是最便宜的选项。
不规则比较 good → better → best；bad → worse → worst
可数／不可数 a few copies / a little time；many copies / much time

比较级常用 -er 或 more，最高级常用 -est 或 most，不把两种同时加成 more cheaper。常用搭配需逐个熟悉。

句子检查

独立问句：Where is the office?；间接问句：Could you tell me where the office is?。关系从句：the file that you sent，不要重复 it。书信可先用短句准确说明，再连接原因和条件。
