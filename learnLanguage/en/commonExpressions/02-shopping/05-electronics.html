第5课：电子产品店 Electronics Store

场景背景

在美国买电子产品，最常见的是 Best Buy（百思买），还有 Apple Store（苹果直营店）、Micro Center 等。英国有 Currys、PC World（已并入 Currys）、Argos（目录式购物）。电子产品店的店员通常比较有技术背景，会推销延保（extended warranty）和配件——这是他们赚佣金的地方。在美国买电子产品也要加消费税，而且大件商品可以考虑分期付款。

子场景一：买笔记本电脑

背景：老李想买一台笔记本电脑，进了 Best Buy。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 Staff: Hey, welcome in! Looking for anything in particular, or just browsing? Hello, welcome. Are you looking for anything specific, or just browsing? 嘿欢迎光临！在找什么特定的东西，还是随便看看？ welcome in = 欢迎进来，比 welcome 更口语。"just browsing" = 随便看看，逛街常用词。✅推荐 🇺🇸
2 You: Yeah, I'm actually in the market for a laptop. Nothing too fancy — just for everyday use. I am looking to purchase a laptop. Nothing too expensive — just for everyday use. 对，我在找笔记本电脑。不用太高级——日常用就行。 in the market for... = 正在考虑买…，地道表达。"Nothing too fancy" = 不需要太高端的。✅推荐 🇺🇸
3 Staff: Gotcha. What's your budget looking like? I understand. What is your budget? 明白。你的预算大概是多少？ Gotcha = Got you = 明白了/了解了。"budget looking like" = 预算大概多少，用 looking like 更随意。✅推荐 🇺🇸
4 You: Somewhere around five, six hundred bucks. Approximately $500 to $600. 大概五到六百块。 "five, six hundred bucks" = $500-$600，省略了 "to" 和 "dollars"，口语中非常常见。✅推荐 🇺🇸
5 Staff: Cool. So at that price point, you're probably looking at something like this HP or maybe an Acer. Let me show you. Great. At that price range, you might consider this HP or perhaps an Acer. Let me show you. 好。这个价位的话，你大概可以看这款惠普或者宏碁。我给你看看。 price point = 价格档次。"you're looking at" = 你可以考虑/你适合。✅推荐
6 You: What's the difference between these two? I'm not super tech-savvy, so... What is the difference between these two? I am not very knowledgeable about technology. 这两款有什么区别？我不太懂技术，所以… tech-savvy = 懂技术的/对科技在行的。"so..." 句末省略，暗示"所以请简单解释"。非常实用的自我介绍句。✅推荐

子场景二：讨论配置 & 推销延保

背景：店员展示了两款笔记本后，老李开始问问题。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
7 Staff: This one's got 16 gigs of RAM, 512 SSD — pretty solid for the price. This model has 16GB of RAM and a 512GB SSD. It is quite good for the price. 这款16G内存，512G固态硬盘——这个价位很实在了。 gigs = gigabytes（GB）的口语简称。SSD = 固态硬盘。"pretty solid" = 相当不错/挺靠谱的。✅推荐 🇺🇸
8 You: Is that enough? I don't do gaming or anything, just email, YouTube, that kind of stuff. Is that sufficient? I do not play games. I only use email, YouTube, and similar applications. 够用吗？我不打游戏什么的，就收邮件、看YouTube之类的。 that kind of stuff = 之类的，口语万能结尾。"or anything" = 什么的，加强否定。✅推荐
9 Staff: Oh, for sure. You'd be more than fine with either of these. Yes, certainly. Either of these would be more than sufficient. 哦当然。这两款你用绰绰有余。 more than fine = 绰绰有余/完全没问题，比 "fine" 更加强。✅推荐 🇺🇸
10 Staff: Now, I gotta mention — we do offer an extended warranty. It covers pretty much everything for three years. I should mention that we offer an extended warranty. It covers almost everything for three years. 对了我得提一下——我们确实有延保。基本上什么都保，三年。 gotta = have got to = 必须/得。"pretty much everything" = 几乎所有东西。延保推销是电子产品店的标配环节。✅推荐 🇺🇸
11 You: How much extra are we talking? How much additional cost would that be? 额外多少钱？ How much extra are we talking? = 额外要多少钱？口语化询价。✅推荐 🇺🇸
12 Staff: It's about another hundred and thirty on top. It is approximately an additional $130. 大概再加一百三。 on top = 在此基础上额外加。不说 "on top of the price"，直接说 "on top"。✅推荐 🇺🇸
13 You: Hmm, I think I'll pass on that for now. I'll just take the laptop. I think I will decline the warranty for now. I will just purchase the laptop. 嗯，我暂时不要延保了。就要这台电脑。 pass on that = 不要了/跳过，非常委婉的拒绝方式，比直接说 "No" 礼貌得多。✅推荐 🇺🇸
14 Staff: No problem at all. Let me ring you up. You want to do financing or pay outright? No problem. Let me process your purchase. Would you like to finance it or pay in full? 没问题。我帮你结账。你想分期还是全款？ ring you up = 帮你结账。financing = 分期付款。pay outright = 一次性全款付清。✅推荐 🇺🇸
15 You: I'll just pay outright. Do you take cash? I will pay in full. Do you accept cash? 直接全款。你们收现金吗？ straightforward 表达。✅推荐
16 Staff: We sure do! Head on over to register three and Jose will take care of you. Yes, we do. Please proceed to register 3 where Jose will assist you. 当然收！去3号收银台找Jose，他会帮你办。 Head on over to... = 去那边…，指路用。"take care of you" = 帮你办/服务你。✅推荐 🇺🇸
17 You: Thanks for your help, man. Thank you for your assistance. 谢谢你的帮忙。 man 在这里不是"男人"，是口语中男性之间的友好称呼，类似"哥们"。50岁的人也可以用，不违和。✅推荐 🇺🇸

子场景三：Apple Store 简短对话

背景：老李后来去了 Apple Store，想给手机买个壳。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
18 You: Hey, I need a case for this. (shows phone) Hello, I need a phone case for this phone. 嘿，我需要一个手机壳。（展示手机） 直接展示实物比描述型号更高效。✅推荐
19 Genius: Sure, what model is this? iPhone 14? Certainly. What model is this? iPhone 14? 好的，这是什么型号？iPhone 14？ Apple Store 的员工不叫 staff，叫 Specialist（销售）或 Genius（技术支持，在 Genius Bar）。✅推荐
20 You: Uh, honestly I'm not sure. It's the one my kid gave me. I am honestly not sure. It is the one my child gave me. 呃，说实话我不确定。是我孩子给我的。 honestly I'm not sure = 说实话我不知道，很自然的应对方式。✅推荐
21 Genius: (checks) It's a 14 Pro. Alright, cases are right over here — pick whichever you like! It is a 14 Pro. The cases are over here. Choose whichever you prefer. 这是14 Pro。好的，手机壳在这边——随便挑！ pick whichever you like = 随便挑。whichever = 无论哪个。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国电子产品购买须知

  • Best Buy Price Match：Best Buy 有价格匹配政策，如果你在别处看到更低的价格，他们可能匹配
  • 延保（Extended Warranty）：推销延保是电子产品店的利润来源。是否购买见仁见智——一般来说，中低价产品不建议买延保
  • Open-box：展示机/退回品，打折出售，性价比高但数量有限
  • Black Friday / Cyber Monday：每年11月底的黑色星期五和网络星期一，电子产品折扣最大
  • credit card 延保：很多信用卡自带延长保修期功能，买前可以了解

🇬🇧 英国差异

  • Currys / PC World 是最大的电子产品零售商
  • Argos：独特模式——在店里看目录/平板下单，然后去柜台取货
  • 英国退换货法律保障更完善：Consumer Rights Act 2015 规定商品必须"as described"（如描述）、"fit for purpose"（适合用途）、"satisfactory quality"（质量合格）
  • 价格含 VAT（20%），所见即所得
