第12课：眼镜店 Optician / Glasses Store

场景背景

在美国买眼镜，常见连锁有 LensCrafters、Visionworks、America's Best、Warby Parker（线上线下结合）。英国有 Specsavers、Vision Express、Boots Opticians。在美国，验光（eye exam）和配镜（getting glasses）通常是分开的——你可以找 optometrist（验光师）验光拿到 prescription（处方），然后拿着处方去任何一家店配镜。美国的眼镜价格差异很大：连锁店便宜（$60-$200），设计师品牌贵的可以到 $500+。近年来在线配镜（如 Zenni、EyeBuyDirect）越来越流行，价格只要 $15-$50。英国 NHS 为部分人群提供免费验光。

子场景一：验光

背景：老李觉得看东西有点模糊，预约了验光。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Hi, I made an appointment online for an eye exam — the name's Li. Hello, I made an appointment online for an eye exam. The name is Li. 你好，我在网上预约了验光——姓李。 eye exam = 眼睛检查/验光，是最常见的说法。✅推荐
2 Receptionist: Great, let me pull you up. Have a seat — the doctor will be right with you. Great, let me find your record. Please have a seat. The doctor will see you shortly. 好的，我查一下你的信息。请坐——医生马上就来。 pull you up = 调出你的信息/查到你的预约。be right with you = 马上就来（服务用语）。✅推荐 🇺🇸
3 (After the exam)
4 Optometrist: So, your prescription hasn't changed much — you're still slightly nearsighted, and now there's a touch of presbyopia kicking in. Your prescription has not changed much. You are still slightly nearsighted, and now there is mild presbyopia. 你的度数变化不大——还是轻度近视，现在有点老花眼了。 nearsighted = 近视（美式），英式说 short-sighted。presbyopia = 老花眼。a touch of = 一点/轻微的。kicking in = 开始出现/开始发挥作用。✅推荐 🇺🇸
5 You: Presbyopia? That's... aging eyes, right? Presbyopia? That means aging eyes, correct? 老花眼？就是…老化的眼睛，对吧？ 50岁左右出现老花眼非常正常，不用不好意思问。✅推荐
6 Optometrist: (smiles) Pretty much! It happens to everybody around your age. You might wanna consider progressive lenses — they're like bifocals but without the visible line. Essentially, yes. It happens to everyone around your age. You might consider progressive lenses. They are like bifocals but without the visible line. （微笑）基本是！你这个年龄的人都会遇到。你可以考虑渐进多焦点镜片——像双光镜片但没有明显分界线。 progressive lenses = 渐进多焦点镜片。bifocals = 双光镜片（上下两片不同度数）。✅推荐
7 You: How much more are those compared to regular lenses? How much more expensive are those compared to regular lenses? 跟普通镜片比贵多少？ 直问价格，合理的问题。✅推荐
8 Optometrist: They run about a hundred to two hundred more, depending on the brand. I'll give you your prescription — you can take it to any optician. They cost approximately $100 to $200 more depending on the brand. I will give you your prescription to take to any optician. 大概多一百到两百，看品牌。我给你处方——你可以去任何眼镜店配。 处方是你的，你可以拿着去任何地方配镜，不用非在验光处配。✅推荐

子场景二：挑选镜架

背景：老李拿着处方来到 LensCrafters 挑选镜架。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
9 You: Hi, I need to get a pair of glasses made up. I've got my prescription right here. I need to have a pair of glasses made. I have my prescription here. 你好，我要配一副眼镜。处方在这儿。 get... made up = 让…做好/配好，这里指配眼镜。✅推荐
10 Staff: Perfect. Are you looking for frames, contacts, or both? Are you looking for frames, contact lenses, or both? 好的。你要镜架、隐形眼镜，还是都要？ frames = 镜架。contacts = 隐形眼镜（contact lenses 的口语简称）。✅推荐
11 You: Just frames for now. I'd like something lightweight — my old ones dig into my nose. Just frames for now. I would like something lightweight. My old ones press into my nose. 先配镜架。我想要轻一点的——我那副旧的夹鼻子。 dig into = 勒住/夹住/嵌进去（形容不舒服）。✅推荐
12 Staff: I hear you. Let me show you some titanium frames — they're super light. And we've got these rimless ones that are basically weightless. I understand. Let me show you some titanium frames. They are very light. We also have rimless ones that are almost weightless. 我理解。我给你看几款钛架——特别轻。还有这种无框的，基本没什么重量。 I hear you = 我理解你/我懂你，共情式回应。rimless = 无框的。titanium = 钛（金属材质）。✅推荐 🇺🇸
13 You: Can I try these on? May I try these on? 我能试试这副吗？ 试戴是必须的。✅推荐
14 Staff: Go for it! There's a mirror right there. (you try them on) Those look great on you, actually — nice and subtle. Please try them on. The mirror is right there. Those look great on you — nice and subtle. 试吧！那边有镜子。（你试戴）这副你戴着其实很好看——简约大方。 Go for it = 去吧/试试看。look great on you = 你戴着很好看。"on you" 是关键介词。✅推荐 🇺🇸
15 You: They feel pretty good. What about the lenses — do you guys do anti-glare coating? They feel quite good. What about the lenses? Do you offer anti-glare coating? 感觉挺好的。镜片呢——你们有防眩光镀膜吗？ anti-glare coating = 防眩光镀膜。配镜时通常会有多个附加选项（加钱）。✅推荐
16 Staff: We do. It's an extra forty bucks, but honestly it makes a huge difference, especially for night driving and screen time. Yes, for an additional $40. It makes a significant difference, especially for night driving and screen time. 有。加四十块，但说实话效果差别很大，尤其是夜间开车和看屏幕的时候。 makes a huge difference = 效果差别很大/很值得。screen time = 看屏幕的时间。✅推荐 🇺🇸
17 You: Throw it in. And how long till they're ready? Please include it. How long until they are ready? 加上吧。多久能做好？ Throw it in = 加上/一起算。✅推荐
18 Staff: About five to seven business days. We'll give you a call when they're in. Approximately five to seven business days. We will call you when they are ready. 大概五到七个工作日。做好了我们给你打电话。 when they're in = 到货了/做好了。✅推荐

子场景三：取眼镜 & 调整

背景：一周后老李来取眼镜。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
19 You: Hi, I'm here to pick up my glasses. The name's Li. I am here to collect my glasses. The name is Li. 你好，我来取眼镜。姓李。 pick up = 来取。✅推荐
20 Staff: One sec... Here they are! Go ahead and put them on — how do they feel? One moment. Here they are. Please put them on. How do they feel? 稍等…给你！戴上试试——感觉怎么样？ One sec = 等一下（second 的缩写）。✅推荐 🇺🇸
21 You: They feel a little loose on the sides. Keeps sliding down my nose. They feel slightly loose on the sides. They keep sliding down my nose. 两边有点松。老是往下滑。 sliding down = 往下滑。✅推荐
22 Staff: No worries, I can adjust that for you — just a quick tweak. (adjusts the temples) How's that? I can adjust that for you with a quick tweak. How is that? 没事，我帮你调一下——很快就好。（调整镜腿）怎么样？ tweak = 微调/小调整。temples = 镜腿（眼镜两侧的长臂）。调整镜架是免费服务。✅推荐
23 You: Oh yeah, that's way better. Thanks! That is much better. Thank you. 哦对，好多了。谢谢！ way better = 好多了。✅推荐 🇺🇸
24 Staff: You bet. If they loosen up again, just bring them back in — adjustments are always free. You are welcome. If they loosen again, bring them back. Adjustments are always free. 不客气。如果又松了就拿回来——调整永远免费。 You bet = 不客气/当然。loosen up = 变松。✅推荐 🇺🇸

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国配镜文化

  • 验光和配镜分开：你可以拿处方去任何店配镜，也可以在线配镜（Zenni、EyeBuyDirect 等价格极低）
  • vision insurance（视力保险）：很多美国人通过雇主购买视力保险，可以覆盖验光和部分镜架费用
  • FSA/HSA：Flexible Spending Account / Health Savings Account 可以用税前钱配眼镜
  • 验光费大约 $75-$150（无保险），配镜 $100-$500+（含镜架和镜片）
  • adjustments（调整镜架）在所有眼镜店都是免费的，即使不是在那里买的也可以去调
  • contact lens 需要单独的处方，眼镜处方不能用于买隐形眼镜

🇬🇧 英国差异

  • Specsavers 是英国最大的眼镜连锁，广告语 "Should've gone to Specsavers" 已成为流行文化梗
  • NHS 为 60 岁以上人群、低收入者提供免费验光
  • 英国眼镜价格含 VAT（增值税）
  • NHS optical voucher：符合条件的人可以拿代金券补贴配镜费用
  • 英式说 short-sighted（近视），不说 nearsighted
