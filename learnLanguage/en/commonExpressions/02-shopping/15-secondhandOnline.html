第15课：在线二手市场 Online Marketplace (eBay, Facebook Marketplace)

场景背景

在线二手交易在欧美非常普遍。eBay 是全球最大的在线拍卖和二手交易平台，既有个人卖家也有商家。Facebook Marketplace 近年来飞速发展，成为本地二手交易的首选——因为可以看到卖家 Facebook 个人资料，相对更安全。Craigslist（美国老牌分类广告网站）虽然界面老旧但仍在使用，适合租房、买二手大件。英国常用的有 Gumtree（类似 Craigslist）和 Shpock。Mercari 和 Poshmark 专注二手服装和配饰。在线二手交易的核心是 listing（商品页面）、bidding / making an offer（出价）、pickup / shipping（自提或邮寄），以及最重要的——trust（信任）。

子场景一：Facebook Marketplace 本地自提

背景：老李想在 Facebook Marketplace 上买一辆二手自行车，联系了卖家。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You (message): Hey, is this still available? Is this still available? 嘿，这个还在吗？ "Is this still available?" 是 Facebook Marketplace 上的标准开场白，几乎所有对话都从这里开始。✅推荐
2 Seller: Yep, still got it! Yes, I still have it. 对，还有！ still got it = 还有/还没卖掉。简洁回应。✅推荐 🇺🇸
3 You: Nice! What's the lowest you'd go? I can pick up today. What is your lowest price? I can pick it up today. 不错！最低多少？我今天就能来取。 What's the lowest you'd go? = 最低多少钱？在线二手交易的砍价标准句。"can pick up today" = 今天就能来自提，表示你是个认真的买家，卖家更愿意让步。✅推荐 🇺🇸
4 Seller: I listed it at a hundred and twenty, but I'd take a hundred if you're coming today. I listed it at $120, but I would accept $100 if you pick it up today. 我挂的一百二，但如果今天来一百就行。 listed it at = 挂牌价/标价。"I'd take..." = 我接受…价。能快速成交卖家通常愿意降价。✅推荐
5 You: Deal! Where do you want to meet? Agreed. Where would you like to meet? 成交！你想在哪碰面？ 公共场所交易是安全原则。✅推荐
6 Seller: How about the Starbucks on Main Street? Say, 5 PM? How about the Starbucks on Main Street? Say, 5 PM? Main Street 那个星巴克怎么样？比如下午五点？ Say, 5 PM? = 比如五点？"Say" 在这里表示提议/比如说。公共场所交易是标准做法。✅推荐 🇺🇸
7 You: Works for me. I'll be in a blue Honda. See you at five. That works for me. I will be in a blue Honda. See you at five. 可以。我开一辆蓝色本田。五点见。 告诉对方你的车颜色/型号，方便辨认。✅推荐

子场景二：当面验货交易

背景：老李到了 Starbucks 停车场，见到了卖家和自行车。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
8 Seller: Hey, you must be Li! Here she is — take a look. You must be Li. Here is the bicycle. Please take a look. 嘿，你就是李吧！这就是——你看看。 Here she is = 在这里（she 指代自行车，口语中车辆常用人称代词 she/he）。✅推荐 🇺🇸
9 You: Looks good! Mind if I take it for a quick spin? It looks good. Do you mind if I ride it briefly? 看起来不错！介意我骑一圈试试吗？ take it for a spin = 试骑/试开一圈，买二手交通工具时的标准请求。"Mind if I...?" = 介意我…吗？✅推荐 🇺🇸
10 Seller: Go for it! Brakes are solid, gears shift smooth — you'll see. Go ahead. The brakes are solid and the gears shift smoothly. You will see. 去吧！刹车没问题，换挡很顺——你试试就知道。 Go for it = 去吧/试试看。solid = 靠谱的/没问题的。✅推荐 🇺🇸
11 (after test ride)
12 You: Rides nice. There's a little squeak from the back brake though — is that an easy fix? It rides well. There is a slight squeak from the back brake. Is that easy to fix? 骑着不错。不过后刹车有点吱吱响——好修吗？ easy fix = 容易修的问题。发现小问题时提出但不过分计较，二手交易中很正常。✅推荐
13 Seller: Yeah, that's just the pads — a five-minute job at a bike shop. Tell you what, how about I knock five bucks off since you pointed that out? That is just the brake pads. It is a quick fix. How about I reduce the price by $5 since you mentioned it? 对，就是刹车皮的事——车店五分钟搞定。这样吧，既然你发现了，我减五块怎么样？ knock five bucks off = 减五块钱。Tell you what = 这样吧。诚实的卖家听到问题会主动降价。✅推荐 🇺🇸
14 You: Fair enough! (hands over cash) Here you go — ninety-five. Fair enough. Here is the $95. 可以！（递现金）给你——九十五。 Fair enough = 可以/合理。现金交易是本地二手交易的标准方式。✅推荐 🇺🇸
15 Seller: Thanks, man. Enjoy the ride! Thank you. Enjoy the bicycle. 谢了哥们。好好骑！ Enjoy the ride = 好好享受骑行，双关语（也指"享受这段旅程"）。✅推荐 🇺🇸

子场景三：eBay 网上竞拍

背景：老李在 eBay 上看中了一套二手高尔夫球杆，准备参与竞拍。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
16 You (talking to friend): Check this out — a set of Callaway irons on eBay, and the bidding's only at a hundred and fifty right now. Look at this. A set of Callaway irons on eBay, and the current bid is only $150. 你看这个——eBay上一套 Callaway 铁杆，现在竞价才一百五。 bidding = 竞价/出价。eBay 的拍卖模式（auction）允许用户出价竞拍，价高者得。✅推荐
17 Friend: That's a solid deal if you can snag it. What's the shipping look like? That is a good deal if you can get it. What is the shipping cost? 能抢到的话很划算。运费多少？ snag = 抢到/弄到手。"What's the shipping look like?" = 运费大概多少？✅推荐 🇺🇸
18 You: Says free shipping. The auction ends in like two hours though, so I gotta decide quick. It says free shipping. The auction ends in approximately two hours, so I must decide quickly. 上面写免运费。不过拍卖大概两小时后结束，得赶紧决定。 like 作为填充词 = 大概。gotta = have got to = 必须。eBay 拍卖有截止时间。✅推荐 🇺🇸
19 Friend: Check the seller's feedback first — you don't wanna get burned. Check the seller's feedback first. You do not want to be scammed. 先看看卖家评价——别被骗了。 feedback = 评价/信誉。eBay 的评价系统是判断卖家可信度的关键。get burned = 被坑/被骗。✅推荐 🇺🇸
20 You: Good call. (checks) OK, 98% positive, been on eBay since 2012... Looks legit. I'm gonna place a bid. Good idea. 98% positive feedback, member since 2012. It appears legitimate. I will place a bid. 好建议。（查看）好，98%好评，2012年就用eBay了…看起来靠谱。我出价了。 Good call = 好建议/说得好。Looks legit = 看起来靠谱/正规（legit = legitimate 的口语缩略）。place a bid = 出价/竞价。✅推荐 🇺🇸
21 Friend: Don't forget — eBay does that auto-bid thing, so someone might outbid you at the last second. Remember that eBay has automatic bidding, so someone may outbid you at the last second. 别忘了——eBay有自动出价功能，所以有人可能在最后一秒超过你。 outbid = 出价超过你。auto-bid = 自动出价（eBay 的代理竞价系统，你设最高价，系统自动帮你逐步加价）。at the last second = 在最后一秒（也叫 sniping）。✅推荐
22 You: I know, it's nerve-wracking! Let me put in my max — two hundred — and see what happens. It is nerve-wracking. I will set my maximum at $200 and see what happens. 我知道，紧张死了！我把最高价设到两百，看结果吧。 nerve-wracking = 令人紧张的/紧张的。设最高价后系统自动竞拍，不需要一直盯着。✅推荐 🇺🇸

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国 Facebook Marketplace / Craigslist 安全须知

  • 公共场所交易：选择有监控的公共场所（Starbucks 停车场、警局停车场等），永远不要去陌生人家中
  • 带朋友一起去：尤其买卖大件或贵重物品时
  • 现金或移动支付：Venmo、Zelle、Cash App 是常用支付方式，但注意防骗
  • "If it's too good to be true, it probably is"：如果价格好得不像真的，那大概有猫腻
  • 不提前付款：在看到实物并满意之前不要转账
  • Craigslist 没有评价系统，风险比 Facebook Marketplace 更高

eBay 交易技巧

  • Buy It Now（一口价）vs Auction（拍卖）：一口价直接买，拍卖竞价买
  • feedback score：低于 95% 好评的卖家要谨慎
  • shipping cost：有些商品标价低但运费高，看总价
  • return policy：eBay 有买家保护政策（eBay Money Back Guarantee）
  • sniping（最后一秒出价）：有人专门在拍卖结束前几秒出高价抢拍

🇬🇧 英国差异

  • Gumtree 是英国最常用的本地二手交易平台（类似 Craigslist）
  • Shpock 在英国也比较流行（名字来自 "SHop in your POCKet"）
  • 英国也用 eBay，是全球通用平台
  • 英国银行转账常用 Bank Transfer 或 PayPal，不用 Venmo/Zelle
  • car boot sale 仍然是英国线下二手交易的重要方式（参见第8课）
