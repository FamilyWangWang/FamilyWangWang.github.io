第16课：网购 Online Shopping

场景背景

Amazon 是美国网购的绝对霸主，几乎什么都能买到，Prime 会员（$139/年）可享受免费两日送达。英国人网购也用 Amazon UK，但 Argos（目录式购物）、John Lewis、ASOS（服装）等本土平台也很流行。网购中常见的问题包括：shipping/delivery issues（物流问题）、wrong item（发错货）、damaged in transit（运输中损坏）、late delivery（延迟送达）、missing parts（缺少零件）。退货方面，Amazon 的退货政策非常宽松——大多数商品 30 天内免费退货，很多情况下甚至不需要把商品寄回去（小额商品直接退款不退货）。

子场景一：在 Amazon 上下单

背景：老李想在 Amazon 上买一个咖啡机，正在跟朋友讨论选择。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: I'm looking at this coffee maker on Amazon — the reviews are pretty solid. Four and a half stars, like, two thousand reviews. I am looking at this coffee maker on Amazon. The reviews are good — 4.5 stars with approximately 2,000 reviews. 我在 Amazon 上看这款咖啡机——评价挺好的。四星半，大概两千条评价。 pretty solid = 挺不错的/靠谱的。like 作为填充词 = 大概。Amazon 上看评价数量和星级是选购的基本功。✅推荐 🇺🇸
2 Friend: What brand is it? And is it sold by Amazon or a third-party seller? What brand is it? Is it sold by Amazon or a third-party seller? 什么牌子？是 Amazon 自营还是第三方卖家？ third-party seller = 第三方卖家（在 Amazon 平台上卖东西的其他商家）。第三方卖家的售后可能不如 Amazon 自营。✅推荐
3 You: Says "Ships from and sold by Amazon." That's the good one, right? It says "Ships from and sold by Amazon." That is the better option, correct? 上面写"Amazon 发货并销售"。这个比较好对吧？ Ships from and sold by Amazon = Amazon 自营自营发货，质量和售后最有保障。✅推荐 🇺🇸
4 Friend: Yep, you're good. Is it Prime eligible? Yes. Is it eligible for Prime shipping? 对，没问题。Prime 能用吗？ Prime eligible = 符合 Prime 会员条件（免费快速配送）。✅推荐 🇺🇸
5 You: Yeah, I could get it by Thursday if I order now. Yes, I could receive it by Thursday if I order now. 对，现在下单周四就能到。 get it by Thursday = 周四之前收到。"by" 表示截止日期。✅推荐
6 Friend: Pull the trigger then! You've been talking about getting one for months. Make the purchase then. You have been talking about getting one for months. 那就下单吧！你念叨要买一个都念叨好几个月了。 Pull the trigger = 下定决心/果断行动（原意是扣扳机，引申为"做决定"），在购物语境中指"别犹豫了下单吧"。✅推荐 🇺🇸

子场景二：快递出了问题——联系客服

背景：老李在 Amazon 上买的包裹显示"已送达"，但他根本没收到。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
7 You: Hi, I've got an issue with my order. The tracking says it was delivered yesterday, but I never got it. I have an issue with my order. The tracking says it was delivered yesterday, but I never received it. 你好，我的订单有问题。物流显示昨天已送达，但我根本没收到。 tracking = 物流追踪。"never got it" = 没收到，比 "did not receive it" 更口语化。✅推荐
8 CS Rep: I'm sorry to hear that. Let me pull up your order. Can you give me the order number? I am sorry to hear that. Let me look up your order. Can you provide the order number? 很抱歉听到这个。让我查一下你的订单。能给我订单号吗？ pull up = 调出/查找。Amazon 客服通常先要订单号。✅推荐
9 You: Sure, it's 112-4567890-3456789. It is 112-4567890-3456789. 好的，是 112-4567890-3456789。 ✅推荐
10 CS Rep: OK, I see it here. Sometimes carriers mark things as delivered before they actually drop them off. Did you check around your porch or with your neighbors? I see the order. Sometimes carriers mark items as delivered before actually delivering them. Did you check your porch or ask neighbors? 好，我看到了。有时候快递员会提前标记已送达。你检查过门口走廊或者问过邻居吗？ porch = 门廊/门口。美国的包裹通常放在门口，不像中国送上门。carriers = 快递公司/快递员。✅推荐 🇺🇸
11 You: Yeah, I looked everywhere — porch, mailbox, even behind the bushes. Nothing. I looked everywhere — porch, mailbox, even behind the bushes. Nothing. 看了，到处找了——门口、邮箱、甚至灌木丛后面都找了。没有。 详细描述你做了什么，帮助客服更快推进解决。✅推荐
12 CS Rep: I completely understand. Here's what I can do — I can either send you a replacement right away at no extra cost, or issue a full refund. Which would you prefer? I can send a replacement at no extra cost, or issue a full refund. Which do you prefer? 我完全理解。这样吧——我可以马上免费给你补发一个，或者全额退款。你更倾向哪个？ at no extra cost = 不额外收费/免费。Amazon 对未收到的包裹处理很爽快。✅推荐
13 You: I'll take the replacement, please. Same item, same delivery address. I would like a replacement, please. Same item, same address. 我要补发。同样的商品，同样的地址。 明确告知选择和细节。✅推荐
14 CS Rep: Done! You should get it within two business days. I'll also send you a confirmation email. Is there anything else I can help with? Done. You should receive it within two business days. I will send a confirmation email. Is there anything else? 搞定！两个工作日内应该到。我也会发确认邮件。还有什么能帮你的吗？ Done! = 搞定了/办好了。Amazon 处理问题效率通常很高。✅推荐 🇺🇸
15 You: Nope, that's it. Thanks a lot — I really appreciate the quick help! No, that is all. Thank you very much for the quick assistance. 没了。非常感谢——很感谢你的快速处理！ Nope, that's it = 没有了/就这些。"appreciate the quick help" = 感谢快速处理。✅推荐 🇺🇸

子场景三：退换网购商品

背景：老李在网上买的衣服尺码不对，需要退货。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
16 You: Hey, I need to return something I bought online. It's the wrong size — I should've ordered a large instead of a medium. I need to return an online purchase. It is the wrong size. I should have ordered a large instead of a medium. 嘿，我需要退一件网上买的衣服。尺码不对——我应该买大号不是中号。 should've = should have = 本应该。"instead of" = 而不是。承认是自己的错（选错尺码），退货仍然没问题。✅推荐
17 CS Rep: No problem at all. I'll get a return label emailed to you — just print it out, slap it on the box, and drop it off at any UPS or Kohl's location. I will email a return label. Print it, attach it to the box, and drop it off at UPS or Kohl's. 完全没问题。我把退货标签发你邮件——打印出来贴在盒子上，放到任何 UPS 或 Kohl's 就行。 return label = 退货标签（预付邮资的快递单）。slap it on = 贴上（随意快速的动作）。drop it off = 放下/投递。Amazon 退货可以送到 Kohl's 百货商店或 UPS Store。✅推荐 🇺🇸
18 You: Do I have to pay for return shipping? Do I have to pay for return shipping? 退货运费我要自己出吗？ 问清楚很重要。✅推荐
19 CS Rep: Nope, it's totally free. You should see the refund in your account in about three to five business days after we receive it. No, it is free. The refund should appear in your account in three to five business days after we receive it. 不用，完全免费。我们收到后大概三到五个工作日退款到账。 Amazon 大多数退货免运费。✅推荐
20 You: Great. Oh, one more thing — can I exchange it for the right size instead of getting a refund? Can I exchange it for the correct size instead of a refund? 好的。哦还有一件事——我可以换正确尺码而不是退款吗？ exchange = 换货（不同于 refund 退款）。✅推荐
21 CS Rep: Actually, the easiest way is to just return this one and place a new order for the right size. That way you don't have to wait for us to process the exchange. The easiest way is to return this one and place a new order for the correct size. 其实最简单的方式是退掉这个，然后重新下单买对尺码。这样你不用等我们处理换货。 Amazon 通常建议"退货+重新下单"而不是换货，因为更快。✅推荐
22 You: Makes sense. I'll do that. Thanks! That makes sense. I will do that. Thank you. 有道理。就这么办。谢谢！ Makes sense = 有道理/说得通。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 Amazon 购物实用知识

  • Amazon Prime：$139/年，含免费两日送达、Prime Video（视频流媒体）、Prime Music 等
  • Subscribe & Save：定期自动送货（如洗衣液、纸巾等日用品），可享受 5%-15% 折扣
  • Amazon Warehouse：二手/退货商品打折出售
  • 退货最方便的方式：Kohl's 百货商店（无需打印标签，扫码即可）、UPS Store、Whole Foods、Amazon Hub Locker
  • package theft（包裹被盗）是美国常见问题，叫 "porch piracy"。重要包裹建议选 Amazon Hub Locker（自提柜）或要求签收
  • "Sold by" vs "Fulfilled by Amazon"：前者是第三方卖家自己发货，后者是第三方卖家但由 Amazon 仓库发货——后者退货更方便

🇬🇧 英国网购差异

  • 英国 Amazon 也用，但 Argos（当日到店自提）、John Lewis（高品质百货）也很大
  • ASOS 是英国最大的在线服装零售商，免费退货
  • 英国快递通常由 Royal Mail、DPD、Hermes/Evri、Yodel 配送
  • 英国法律规定网上购物有 14天取消权（cooling-off period），从收到商品之日算起
  • 英国网购价格含 VAT
