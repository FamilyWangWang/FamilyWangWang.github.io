第17课：珠宝店 Jewelry Store

场景背景

在美国买珠宝，常见连锁有 Kay Jewelers、Zales、Jared、Tiffany & Co.（高端）。英国有 H. Samuel、Ernest Jones、Goldsmiths 等。买珠宝最常见的场合是 engagement ring（订婚戒指）、wedding band（婚戒）、anniversary gift（纪念礼物）、birthday present（生日礼物）。珠宝店的关键术语包括：carat（克拉，钻石重量）、cut（切割/切工）、clarity（净度）、color（颜色），即所谓的"4C"。美国珠宝价格不含税。珠宝店通常提供 appraisal（估价证书）和 warranty（质保）。西方有各种 anniversary traditions（纪念传统），如银婚（25年）、金婚（50年）。

子场景一：买纪念日礼物

背景：老李想给老婆买一条项链当结婚纪念日礼物，走进了当地一家珠宝店。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Hi, I'm looking for something for my wife — it's our 25th wedding anniversary. I am looking for something for my wife. It is our 25th wedding anniversary. 你好，我想给老婆买点东西——是我们结婚25周年。 25th wedding anniversary = 银婚（silver anniversary），在西方传统中银婚送银制或珠宝礼物。✅推荐
2 Salesperson: Congratulations! That's a big one — silver anniversary! She must be a lucky lady. What kind of piece are you thinking — necklace, bracelet, earrings? Congratulations. That is the silver anniversary. What kind of jewelry are you considering — necklace, bracelet, or earrings? 恭喜！这可是大日子——银婚！她一定很幸运。你想买什么样的——项链、手链还是耳环？ a big one = 大日子/重要里程碑。"piece" 指首饰单品（a piece of jewelry）。✅推荐 🇺🇸
3 You: I was thinking a necklace, maybe. Something elegant but not over the top — she's not really into flashy stuff. I was thinking a necklace. Something elegant but not excessive. She does not like flashy items. 我在想项链吧。优雅但不要太夸张的——她不太喜欢花哨的东西。 not over the top = 不要太夸张/过分的。not into flashy stuff = 不喜欢花哨的。"be into" = 喜欢。✅推荐 🇺🇸
4 Salesperson: I hear you. How about this sterling silver pendant? It's simple, classic — and for a silver anniversary, it's right on theme. How about this sterling silver pendant? It is simple and classic. For a silver anniversary, it is appropriate. 我理解。这条纯银吊坠怎么样？简约经典——而且银婚送银的主题也正好对。 sterling silver = 925纯银（含银量92.5%）。pendant = 吊坠。right on theme = 正好切题/正合主题。✅推荐
5 You: That's beautiful. What's the price on this one? What is the price of this one? 真漂亮。这个多少钱？ ✅推荐
6 Salesperson: This one runs three eighty-five. And we're actually running a promotion — 20% off for the month. This one is $385. We are running a promotion — 20% off this month. 这个三百八十五。而且我们正好在做活动——本月八折。 runs = 标价/价格是。running a promotion = 在做促销活动。✅推荐 🇺🇸
7 You: So that brings it down to...? So that reduces the price to...? 那折下来是…？ 让对方帮你算，完全正常。✅推荐
8 Salesperson: Three hundred and eight. And that includes a complimentary gift box and a polishing cloth. $308. That includes a free gift box and polishing cloth. 三百零八。含免费礼盒和擦银布。 complimentary = 免费赠送的（比 free 更正式但同样常用）。✅推荐
9 You: I'll take it. Can you engrave the back? Like, with our initials and the date? I will take it. Can you engrave the back with our initials and the date? 我要了。背面能刻字吗？比如我们的名字首字母和日期？ engrave = 刻字。initials = 名字首字母（如 L.L.）。刻字是珠宝店常见的个性化服务。✅推荐
10 Salesperson: Absolutely — engraving's on the house. It'll be ready to pick up in about three days. Engraving is free. It will be ready in approximately three days. 当然——刻字免费。大概三天后来取。 on the house = 免费/店家请客，餐饮和零售行业都用。✅推荐 🇺🇸

子场景二：帮朋友了解订婚戒指

背景：老李的朋友（小王）要向女朋友求婚，想买订婚戒指，拉着老李一起去珠宝店参谋。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
11 Friend: I have no idea what I'm doing. How do I even pick a ring? I do not know what I am doing. How do I choose a ring? 我完全不知道自己在干什么。怎么挑戒指啊？ 第一次买订婚戒指的人通常都很紧张，这完全正常。✅推荐
12 Salesperson: Don't worry — that's what I'm here for! First things first: do you know her ring size? Do not worry. Do you know her ring size? 别担心——我就是干这个的！首先：你知道她的戒指尺寸吗？ First things first = 首先最重要的是。ring size = 戒指尺寸（数字，美国常用 4-9）。✅推荐 🇺🇸
13 Friend: Uh... no. Is that bad? No, I do not. Is that a problem? 呃…不知道。这不好吧？ 不知道尺寸很常见，店员有办法解决。✅推荐
14 Salesperson: Not at all! A lot of guys don't. You can borrow one of her rings and bring it in — we can measure it. Or just estimate for now and we offer one free resizing. Not at all. You can bring in one of her rings for measurement. Or estimate now and we offer one free resizing. 完全不会！很多男生都不知道。你可以借她一个戒指拿来——我们能量。或者先估一个，我们提供一次免费改号。 resizing = 改戒指大小/改号。买订婚戒指不知道尺寸不怕，改号是免费服务。✅推荐 🇺🇸
15 You (to friend): What's your budget, by the way? What is your budget? 对了，你预算多少？ 帮朋友参谋时提醒对方想清楚预算，很实用。✅推荐
16 Friend: I was thinking somewhere around two grand? I was thinking approximately $2,000. 我想着两千左右吧？ two grand = two thousand dollars / $2,000。grand = 一千（口语中指 $1,000）。✅推荐 🇺🇸
17 Salesperson: That'll get you a nice solitaire in the half-carat range. Here, let me show you a few options. That will get you a nice solitaire around half a carat. Let me show you some options. 这个预算可以买到一颗不错的半克拉单钻。来，我给你看几个选择。 solitaire = 单钻戒指（只有一颗主钻石）。half-carat = 半克拉。订婚戒指价格差异巨大，从几百到几万都有。✅推荐
18 Friend: What's the difference between these two? They look the same to me. What is the difference between these two? They look the same to me. 这两个有什么区别？我看着一样的。 外行看不出区别很正常，直接问。✅推荐
19 Salesperson: So this one's a VS1 clarity, and this one's an SI2. The VS1 is a bit clearer, but honestly, you can't really tell with the naked eye. The SI2 is a better bang for your buck. This one is VS1 clarity, and this one is SI2. The VS1 is clearer, but you cannot tell with the naked eye. The SI2 is better value. 这个是VS1净度，这个是SI2。VS1更净，但说实话肉眼根本看不出区别。SI2性价比更高。 clarity = 净度（VS1、SI2 是钻石净度等级）。naked eye = 肉眼（不借助放大镜）。bang for your buck = 性价比/花的钱值不值。✅推荐 🇺🇸
20 You (to friend): I'd go with the SI2 — save the extra money for the proposal dinner! I would choose the SI2 and save the money for the proposal dinner. （对朋友）我选SI2——省下的钱花在求婚晚餐上！ 帮朋友做决策时给出实际建议。✅推荐

子场景三：珠宝清洁和维修

背景：老李的结婚戒指变暗了，拿去珠宝店清洗。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
21 You: Hi, my wedding ring's gotten kinda dull over the years. Can you guys clean it up? My wedding ring has become dull. Can you clean it? 你好，我的婚戒这些年有点暗了。你们能帮忙清洗吗？ kinda dull = 有点暗淡/失去光泽。很多珠宝店提供免费清洗服务。✅推荐 🇺🇸
22 Salesperson: Of course — we do complimentary cleanings on any purchase, even if it wasn't bought here. Just leave it with us for about 20 minutes. We offer free cleanings on any jewelry, even if not purchased here. Please leave it for about 20 minutes. 当然——我们任何首饰都免费清洗，不是在我们这儿买的也行。大概留20分钟就好。 complimentary = 免费赠送的。珠宝店免费清洗是吸引顾客的常见服务。✅推荐
23 You: Nice! By the way, one of the small stones fell out — can you fix that too? One of the small stones fell out. Can you fix that? 好的！对了，有一颗小石头掉了——能修吗？ fell out = 掉出来了。镶嵌的小钻石/宝石掉落是常见问题。✅推荐
24 Salesperson: We sure can. Let me take a look... Yeah, that's a pretty easy fix — we'd just need to order a replacement stone and reset it. Probably run you about sixty, seventy bucks. Yes, we can fix it. We would order a replacement stone and reset it. It will cost approximately $60-$70. 当然可以。让我看看…嗯，挺好修的——我们需要订一颗替代的宝石重新镶上。大概六七十块。 reset = 重新镶嵌。run you about... = 大概花你…钱。✅推荐 🇺🇸
25 You: That's reasonable. How long would that take? That is reasonable. How long would it take? 合理。需要多久？ ✅推荐
26 Salesperson: About a week to ten days. I'll write up a repair ticket for you. About a week to ten days. I will write a repair ticket for you. 大概一周到十天。我给你开一张维修单。 repair ticket = 维修工单/取货凭证。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国珠宝购买文化

  • 订婚戒指传统：男方通常花 1-3 个月工资买订婚戒指（这是珠宝行业营销出来的"传统"，不必照搬）
  • 4C 标准：Carat（克拉重量）、Cut（切工）、Color（颜色，D最好）、Clarity（净度，FL最好）
  • 白金 vs 铂金：white gold（白金，含金合金，需要定期镀铑）vs platinum（铂金，更贵更稀有）
  • appraisal（估价证书）：贵重珠宝应该有估价证书，用于保险
  • jewelry insurance：贵重首饰建议买保险（可通过 renters/homeowners insurance 添加）
  • 大减季节：Black Friday、Valentine's Day 后、Mother's Day 前都有促销

🇬🇧 英国差异

  • H. Samuel 和 Ernest Jones 是最常见的珠宝连锁
  • 英国价格含 VAT（20%）
  • hallmark（印记）：英国法律规定贵金属必须有官方纯度印记
  • 英国订婚戒指传统类似美国，但预算可以更灵活
  • "Something old, something new, something borrowed, something blue"：英国婚礼传统，新娘要带四样东西
