第9课：家具店 Furniture Store

场景背景

在美国买家具，常见连锁有 IKEA（宜家，瑞典品牌但在美英都很流行）、Ashley HomeStore、Bob's Discount Furniture、Crate & Barrel、West Elm 等。英国有 DFS、Sofa.com、John Lewis 家具部等。家具店的特点是：店员会主动推销 delivery（送货）、assembly（组装）和 protection plan（保养计划）。IKEA 的特色是 flat-pack（平板包装），需要自己搬回家自己组装。大件家具通常需要安排 delivery window（送货时间段），而且美国送货经常只送到门口（curbside delivery），不包搬上楼——想搬进屋要加钱选 white-glove delivery。

子场景一：IKEA 买家具

背景：老李搬了新家，去 IKEA 买一张餐桌和几把椅子。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Excuse me, where's the dining section? I'm looking for a table for four. Excuse me, where is the dining section? I am looking for a table that seats four. 打扰一下，餐具区在哪儿？我在找一张四人桌。 table for four = 四人桌，简洁明了。IKEA 店面大，问路很正常。✅推荐
2 Staff: Head straight through the living room display, then hang a left — you'll see it right past the kitchens. Go straight through the living room display, then turn left. You will see it past the kitchens. 一直穿过客厅展示区，然后左转——过了厨房区就能看到。 hang a left = 左转，比 "turn left" 更口语化。✅推荐 🇺🇸
3 You: Got it, thanks. Do you guys have anything that extends? I sometimes have friends over. Understood. Do you have any extendable tables? I sometimes have guests. 明白了谢谢。你们有那种可伸展的吗？我有时候有朋友来。 extends = 可伸展/可折叠变大的。have friends over = 请朋友来家里做客。✅推荐
4 Staff: Oh for sure. The NORDEN series is super popular — seats four but pulls out to seat six. Yes, the NORDEN series is very popular. It seats four but extends to seat six. 哦当然。NORDEN系列特别受欢迎——坐四个人但拉出来能坐六个。 pulls out = 拉出来/展开。IKEA 的产品都有瑞典名字。✅推荐
5 You: Nice! How much is it? How much does it cost? 不错！多少钱？ 简洁直接。✅推荐
6 Staff: Two ninety-nine for the table, and the matching chairs run about sixty apiece. $299 for the table, and the matching chairs are approximately $60 each. 桌子299，配套的椅子大约每把60。 two ninety-nine = $299。"run about sixty" = 大约60块，run 在这里指"价格是"。apiece = 每个/每件。✅推荐 🇺🇸

子场景二：讨论送货和组装

背景：老李选好了家具，来到 IKEA 的家居配送服务台。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
7 You: Hi, I'd like to get this delivered. I don't think it's gonna fit in my car. I would like this delivered. I do not think it will fit in my car. 你好，我想安排送货。我车里放不下。 gonna fit in = 放得下。自己搬不了就安排送货，很正常。✅推荐
8 Staff: No problem. So we've got two options — standard delivery starts at forty-nine bucks, or you can do white-glove for a hundred and twenty-nine. No problem. We have two options. Standard delivery starts at $49, or white-glove service for $129. 没问题。我们有两个选择——标准送货49块起，白手套服务129。 white-glove delivery = 白手套服务，包括搬进屋、拆包、组装、清理垃圾。名字听起来高级但其实就是"全包服务"。✅推荐 🇺🇸
9 You: What's the difference exactly? What exactly is the difference? 具体有什么区别？ 花钱之前问清楚，完全合理。✅推荐
10 Staff: Standard gets it to your front door — or curb, actually. White-glove, they bring it inside, assemble everything, and haul away the packaging. Standard delivery is to your curb. White-glove service includes bringing items inside, assembly, and packaging removal. 标准的送到家门口——其实是路边。白手套的会搬进屋、全部组装好、把包装带走。 curb = 路边。haul away = 带走/清走（大件垃圾/包装）。这两个服务差距很大，要搞清楚。✅推荐 🇺🇸
11 You: I'll go with white-glove then. I'm not exactly handy, if you know what I mean. I will choose white-glove service. I am not very handy with tools. 那我要白手套吧。我手不太巧，你懂的。 handy = 手巧的/会动手修理的。"if you know what I mean" = 你懂的，自嘲式幽默。✅推荐 🇺🇸
12 Staff: (chuckles) Hey, no shame in that! When do you need it by? There is no shame in that. When do you need it delivered by? （笑）嘿这没什么丢人的！你什么时候要？ no shame in that = 这没什么好丢人的。"need it by" = 最迟什么时候需要，by 表示截止日期。✅推荐 🇺🇸
13 You: No huge rush, but within the next two weeks would be ideal. There is no rush, but within the next two weeks would be ideal. 不急，但最好两周内。 no huge rush = 不太急。说理想时间比说"必须哪天"更灵活。✅推荐
14 Staff: Shouldn't be a problem. Let me check available windows... How about next Saturday between 10 and 2? That should not be a problem. How about next Saturday between 10 AM and 2 PM? 应该没问题。我查一下可选时间…下周六十点到两点怎么样？ delivery window = 送货时间段。美国送货通常给一个4小时窗口，不会精确到几点。✅推荐 🇺🇸
15 You: That works perfectly. Do I pay for delivery now or when they show up? That works perfectly. Do I pay for delivery now or upon delivery? 完美。送货费现在付还是送到再付？ ✅推荐
16 Staff: Everything's paid upfront. I'll print you out a confirmation — just hold on to that. Everything is paid in advance. I will print a confirmation for you. Please keep it. 全部预付。我给你打印确认单——留好那个。 upfront = 预付/提前付。hold on to that = 留好/保管好。✅推荐 🇺🇸

子场景三：传统家具店砍价

背景：老李后来去了一家传统家具店（非连锁IKEA），想买一个沙发。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
17 You: I like this sofa, but eight hundred's a bit steep for me. Is there any wiggle room on the price? I like this sofa, but $800 is a bit expensive. Is there any flexibility on the price? 我喜欢这个沙发，但八百有点贵。价格上还有商量余地吗？ steep = 贵的/价格高的（口语）。wiggle room = 商量余地/弹性空间。传统家具店砍价是完全正常的。✅推荐 🇺🇸
18 Salesperson: Well, what number were you thinking? What price were you considering? 嗯，你心里什么价位？ 店员问你的心理价位，说明有谈判空间。✅推荐
19 You: Could you do six-fifty? I'd be willing to pick it up myself. Could you do $650? I would pick it up myself. 六百五行不行？我自己来拉。 six-fifty = 650。主动提出自己搬运来换取折扣，聪明的谈判策略。✅推荐 🇺🇸
20 Salesperson: Hmm... Let me talk to my manager and see what I can do. (walks away, comes back) OK, here's what I can do — seven hundred flat, and I'll throw in free delivery. Let me consult my manager. OK, I can do $700 exactly, with free delivery. 嗯…让我跟经理商量一下。（走开，回来）好，这样吧——七百整，我送免费送货。 talk to my manager = 跟经理请示，销售常用策略（也可能是真心需要授权）。flat = 整整（如700 flat = 700整，不零不碎）。throw in = 顺带加上。✅推荐 🇺🇸
21 You: You've got a deal. Thanks for working with me on this! We have a deal. Thank you for negotiating with me. 成交。谢谢你帮我想办法！ working with me on this = 帮我通融/跟我协商，比说 "thanks for the discount" 更体面。✅推荐 🇺🇸

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国家具购买文化

  • IKEA：自选自提模式，货架上有编号和位置码，去仓库区自己搬上推车
  • 组装：IKEA 家具需要自己组装，如果不会可以雇人（TaskRabbit 是 IKEA 官方合作的组装服务平台）
  • delivery tip：送货通常给4小时窗口，要在家里等。有些公司会提前打电话或发短信
  • mattress（床垫）是可以砍价的！床垫行业利润率极高，标价的 40%-60% 都有可能砍下来
  • traditional furniture stores 可以砍价，连锁店（IKEA、West Elm）一般不行
  • Box spring（床架底座）和 frame（床架）在美国通常和床垫分开卖

🇬🇧 英国家具差异

  • 英国 IKEA 跟美国差不多，但英国还有 Argos（目录式购物——在店里用平板选好，去仓库取货）
  • 英国叫 flat-pack furniture（平板包装家具），也叫 self-assembly furniture
  • 英国送货费通常比美国便宜一些，有些店免费送货
  • tip（小费）不需要给送货人员，但在美国可以给 $5-$10 per person

送货服务对比

服务类型 含义 价格范围
Curbside / Threshold 送到路边/门口 最低（$30-80）
White-glove 搬进屋+组装+清垃圾 最高（$100-300+）
Self-pickup 自己去店里搬 免费
