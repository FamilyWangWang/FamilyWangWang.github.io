第27课：裁缝/改衣 Tailor & Alterations

场景背景

在美国，tailor（裁缝/改衣师）和 alterations shop（改衣店）主要提供衣服修改服务——改裤脚、收腰、改袖长等。美国男性平均身高较高，亚洲人买美国品牌的衣服经常需要修改。很多干洗店（dry cleaner）也提供基本的改衣服务。高级百货公司（如 Nordstrom）买的衣服有时可以免费修改裤脚。定制衣服（bespoke / custom-made）在美国很贵，一套定制西装 $1000-5000+。英国裁缝传统更悠久，Savile Row（萨维尔街）是世界著名的定制西装街。英国改衣服务也常见，价格可能比美国稍便宜。改衣时最重要的是用英语准确描述你要怎么改。

子场景一：新买的裤子需要改裤脚

背景：你在美国买了一条裤子，但裤腿太长，需要改短。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Hi, I just bought these pants and they're way too long. Can you hem them for me? Hello, I just purchased these pants and they are too long. Can you hem them for me? 你好，我刚买了这条裤子太长了。能帮我改裤脚吗？ way too long = 太长了。hem = 缝边/改裤脚（作动词或名词）。✅推荐
2 Tailor: Sure, no problem. Put them on and put on the shoes you'll wear with them — I need to pin them to the right length. Sure, no problem. Put them on with the shoes you plan to wear so I can pin them to the correct length. 当然没问题。穿上裤子，再穿上你要搭配的鞋子——我要别针标记到合适的长度。 改裤脚时必须穿对鞋子，因为不同鞋子高度不同，裤脚长度也不同。✅推荐
3 You: (puts on pants and shoes) How's that? How does that look? （穿好裤子和鞋子）这样可以吗？ ✅推荐
4 Tailor: (pins the hem) How does that feel? Too short, too long, or just right? How does that feel? Too short, too long, or just right? （别好针）感觉怎么样？太短、太长还是正好？ 裁缝的标准检查方式。✅推荐
5 You: Maybe just a tiny bit shorter — about half an inch above where it is now. I don't like them bunching up at the bottom. Perhaps slightly shorter — about half an inch above the current length. I prefer them not bunching at the bottom. 再短一点点——比现在再短半英寸左右。我不喜欢裤脚堆在一起。 a tiny bit = 一点点。bunching up = 堆在一起/皱巴巴的。✅推荐
6 Tailor: Got it — right about here? (adjusts pin) Understood. Right about here? 明白——大概这里？（调整别针） ✅推荐
7 You: That's perfect. How much and how long? That is perfect. How much does it cost and how long will it take? 完美。多少钱，多久能好？ 简洁地问价格和时间。✅推荐
8 Tailor: $14, and you can pick them up on Thursday. $14, and they will be ready on Thursday. 14美元，周四来取。 ✅推荐

子场景二：西装外套需要修改

背景：你买了一件西装外套，肩膀合适但腰太宽了，想收一下。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
9 You: I've got a suit jacket that needs some work. It fits in the shoulders but it's too boxy through the body — can you take it in? I have a suit jacket that needs alterations. It fits in the shoulders but is too loose in the body. Can you take it in? 我有一件西装外套需要改。肩膀合适但腰身太方了——能帮我收一下吗？ boxy = 方方正正的/太宽的。take it in = 收紧/改小（改衣术语）。through the body = 腰身部分。✅推荐
10 Tailor: Yeah, I can take in the sides for you. Let me pin it while you're wearing it. (pins) How's that — too tight or just right? I can take in the sides. Let me pin it. How is that? 可以，我帮你收两侧。穿上我来别针。（别针）怎么样——太紧还是正好？ take in the sides = 收两侧/收腰。✅推荐
11 You: Feels good. Can you still let it out a little if it's too tight after? Feels good. Can you still let it out if it is too tight? 感觉不错。如果之后觉得太紧还能放出来一点吗？ let it out = 放出来/放宽（take in 的反义词）。✅推荐
12 Tailor: Absolutely — I always leave a little extra fabric inside for adjustments. Now, the sleeves are a touch long too — want me to shorten those as well? Absolutely. I always leave extra fabric for adjustments. The sleeves are a touch long. Should I shorten them? 当然——我总会在里面留一点多余的面料以便调整。对了，袖子也有点长——要不要也短一些？ a touch = 一点点（= a little bit）。extra fabric = 多余的面料/缝份。✅推荐
13 You: Yeah, let's do the sleeves too. Can you keep the working buttons? I've heard that can be tricky. Yes, do the sleeves too. Can you keep the working buttons? I have heard that can be difficult. 好，袖子也改。能保留功能扣吗？我听说那个比较麻烦。 working buttons = 功能扣（真扣眼，可以解开的那种）。高档西装外套有 working buttonholes，改袖长时不能简单地从袖口改，需要从肩膀处改。✅推荐
14 Tailor: You've got surgeon's cuffs on this one — so I'll need to shorten from the shoulder instead of the cuff. It's a bit more work but it'll look right. That'll be $65 for everything — taking in the body and shortening the sleeves. You have surgeon's cuffs, so I need to shorten from the shoulder. It is more work. The total is $65 for taking in the body and shortening the sleeves. 你这件有外科医生袖扣——所以我需要从肩膀处改短而不是袖口。多一点工作量但效果更好。总共65美元——收腰和改袖子。 surgeon's cuffs = 外科医生袖扣（= working buttonholes 的另一种叫法）。从 shoulder（肩膀）改比从 cuff（袖口）改贵但效果好。✅推荐
15 You: Sounds fair. When can I pick it up? Sounds fair. When can I pick it up? 听起来合理。什么时候能取？ ✅推荐
16 Tailor: Give me a week — next Wednesday should work. I'll give you a call when it's ready. One week — next Wednesday. I will call you when it is ready. 一周——下周三应该好了。好了我给你打电话。 ✅推荐

子场景三：试穿修改后的衣服

背景：一周后你来取修改好的西装。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
17 You: Hi, I'm here to pick up my suit jacket. Hello, I am here to pick up my suit jacket. 你好，我来取我的西装外套。 ✅推荐
18 Tailor: Great, let me grab it. Try it on before you go — I want to make sure the fit is right. Great. Try it on before you leave to ensure the fit is correct. 好的，我来拿。走之前试穿一下——我想确认合身。 ✅推荐
19 You: (puts it on) Oh wow, this is way better. The waist fits perfectly now and the sleeves look right. This is much better. The waist fits perfectly and the sleeves look right. （穿上）哇，好多了。腰身现在很合适，袖子也对了。 ✅推荐
20 Tailor: Turn around, let me check the back... Yeah, the drape looks good. No pulling, no bunching. Turn around so I can check the back. The drape looks good. No pulling or bunching. 转过来，我看看后面……嗯，垂感很好。没有拉扯、没有皱褶。 drape = 垂感/悬垂效果。pulling = 拉扯（太紧的标志）。bunching = 皱在一起。这些都是评价衣服合身度的专业术语。✅推荐
21 You: You did a great job. I'll definitely be coming back — I've got a couple more things that need work. You did an excellent job. I will return with more items that need alterations. 你做得很好。我肯定会再来的——我还有几件需要改的。 ✅推荐
22 Tailor: Happy to help. Next time just drop by anytime — no appointment needed. Happy to help. Drop by anytime without an appointment. 很高兴帮你。下次随时来——不需要预约。 drop by = 顺便过来/直接来。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国改衣须知

  • 价格参考：改裤脚 $10-20，收腰 $30-60，改袖长 $20-40，换拉链 $15-30
  • Nordstrom 免费改裤脚：在 Nordstrom 买的裤子可以免费改裤脚（仅限裤脚）
  • appointment vs walk-in：有些裁缝接受 walk-in（不用预约），有些需要预约
  • turnaround time：简单的改裤脚通常 2-3 天，复杂的修改可能需要 1-2 周
  • rush service：加急通常多收 50% 费用
  • 先试穿再走：取衣服时一定要当场试穿确认满意，如果还有问题可以当场调

🇬🇧 英国改衣差异

  • 英国裁缝传统更悠久，Savile Row（伦敦萨维尔街）是世界定制西装的中心
  • bespoke = 全定制（从零开始量体裁衣，英式英语）。made-to-measure = 半定制（基于现有版型修改）
  • 英国改衣价格可能比美国稍便宜
  • turn-ups = 裤脚翻边（英式），美式说 cuffs
  • 英国很多市场（market）里有改衣摊位，价格比店铺更便宜
  • Timpson 是英国最大的修鞋/改衣连锁店，在大多数高街（high street）都有
