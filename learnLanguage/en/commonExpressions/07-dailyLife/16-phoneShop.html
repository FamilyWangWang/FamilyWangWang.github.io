第16课：手机店 Phone Shop & Mobile Plans

场景背景

在美国，买手机和办手机套餐主要去 carrier store（运营商门店）：AT&T、Verizon、T-Mobile 是三大运营商。手机套餐（cell phone plan / mobile plan）分为 prepaid（预付费）和 postpaid（后付费/合约机）两种。预付费更灵活，不需要信用审查（credit check）；后付费通常可以分期买手机（installment plan），但需要信用分。美国手机店的文化是"疯狂推销"——店员会想方设法让你加服务、换套餐、买配件。英国三大运营商是 EE、Vodafone、Three，还有 O2 和 giffgaff（虚拟运营商）。英国也有类似的合约机和预付费（叫 pay-as-you-go / PAYG）。

子场景一：去手机店办新套餐

背景：你刚到美国，需要办一个手机套餐。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Hi, I'm looking to get a cell phone plan. I already have a phone — just need the service. Hello, I am looking to get a cell phone plan. I already have a phone and just need the service. 你好，我想办个手机套餐。我已有手机了——只需要服务。 cell phone plan = 手机套餐（美式），英式说 mobile plan。just need the service = 只需要服务/不需要买手机。✅推荐 🇺🇸
2 Employee: Great! Are you looking for prepaid or postpaid? And how much data do you usually go through? Great. Are you looking for prepaid or postpaid? How much data do you typically use? 好的！你想要预付费还是后付费？你一般用多少流量？ go through = 消耗/用掉（数据流量）。✅推荐
3 You: Prepaid, I think — I don't wanna deal with contracts. And I probably need about 10 gigs a month? Prepaid, I think. I do not want contracts. I probably need about 10 GB per month. 预付费吧——我不想签合同。大概每月10G够吗？ don't wanna deal with = 不想处理/不想麻烦。gigs = GB 的口语说法（gigabytes）。✅推荐
4 Employee: Our 15-gig prepaid plan is $40 a month — unlimited talk and text, plus 15 gigs of high-speed data. After that, it slows down but you're not cut off. Our 15 GB prepaid plan is $40 per month with unlimited talk and text plus 15 GB of high-speed data. After that, speeds are reduced but service continues. 我们15G预付费套餐每月40美元——无限通话和短信，加15G高速流量。用完之后会变慢但不会断网。 unlimited talk and text = 无限通话和短信。slows down = 变慢（限速）。cut off = 断网/中断。✅推荐
5 You: Is there an activation fee? Is there an activation fee? 有激活费吗？ activation fee = 激活费/开卡费。✅推荐
6 Employee: Normally $25, but I can waive it if you set up auto-pay. Normally $25, but I can waive it if you set up auto-pay. 平时25美元，但如果你设自动扣款我可以免掉。 waive = 免除。auto-pay = 自动扣款/自动付款。ISP 和运营商都经常用这种方式吸引客户开通 auto-pay。✅推荐
7 You: Deal. Let's do it. Deal. Let us proceed. 成交。办吧。 Deal = 成交/一言为定。✅推荐

子场景二：买新手机（分期付款）

背景：你的手机坏了，想在店里买一个新的。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
8 You: My phone just died on me. I'm thinking about upgrading. What do you have that's decent but won't break the bank? My phone just stopped working. I am considering upgrading. What do you have that is good but affordable? 我的手机刚坏了。我想换一个新的。有什么不错的但不会太贵的？ died on me = 突然坏了/罢工了。upgrade = 升级/换新。won't break the bank = 不会太贵/不会倾家荡产。✅推荐
9 Employee: The iPhone 15 starts at $799, or we've got the Samsung Galaxy S24 at $749. But if you want something more budget-friendly, the Google Pixel 7a is only $499. The iPhone 15 starts at $799, the Samsung Galaxy S24 at $749, or the Google Pixel 7a at $499 for a budget option. iPhone 15起价799美元，三星Galaxy S24是749。但如果你想更实惠的，Google Pixel 7a只要499。 budget-friendly = 对钱包友好的/实惠的。✅推荐
10 You: I'll go with the iPhone. Can I pay it off over time? I will choose the iPhone. Can I pay in installments? 我要iPhone。可以分期付款吗？ pay it off over time = 分期还清。go with = 选择。✅推荐
11 Employee: For sure. We can set you up on a 24-month installment plan — that breaks down to about $33 a month, no interest. But we'll need to run a credit check first. Certainly. We can set up a 24-month installment plan at approximately $33 per month with no interest. We need to run a credit check first. 当然。我们可以办24个月分期——每月大约33美元，无息。但需要先做信用审查。 breaks down to = 折合/分解为。no interest = 无利息。credit check = 信用审查。✅推荐
12 You: Will it be a hard pull on my credit? Will it be a hard inquiry on my credit report? 会影响我的信用分吗？ hard pull / hard inquiry = 硬查询（会影响信用分）。了解这个区别很重要。✅推荐
13 Employee: Yeah, it'll be a hard pull. Should only drop your score by a few points though. Yes, it will be a hard pull. It should only decrease your score by a few points. 对，是硬查询。不过只会让你的分数降几分。 ✅推荐
14 You: OK, go ahead. What do you need from me? OK, proceed. What information do you need? 好的，继续吧。你需要我提供什么？ go ahead = 继续/进行。✅推荐
15 Employee: Just your driver's license or passport and your Social Security number. Your driver's license or passport and your Social Security number. 驾照或护照，还有社会安全号。 SSN = Social Security number（社会安全号），美国办很多事都需要。✅推荐 🇺🇸

子场景三：应对推销和保护性对话

背景：店员开始疯狂推销配件和额外服务。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
16 Employee: So let me also show you our phone insurance plan — it covers cracks, water damage, even loss and theft. Only $15 a month. Let me show you our phone insurance plan. It covers cracks, water damage, loss, and theft for $15 per month. 我再给你介绍一下手机保险——覆盖碎屏、进水，甚至丢失和被盗。每月只要15美元。 手机保险通常利润率很高，店员推销很卖力。✅推荐
17 You: I think I'll pass on the insurance. I'm pretty careful with my phones. I will decline the insurance. I am careful with my phones. 保险就算了吧。我对手机比较小心。 pass on = 不要/跳过（婉拒推销）。I think I'll pass = 我觉得不需要/跳过吧。✅推荐
18 Employee: How about a screen protector and a case? We've got a bundle deal — $50 for both. How about a screen protector and a case? We have a bundle for $50. 贴膜和手机壳呢？我们有套装——两个一起50美元。 screen protector = 屏幕保护膜。case = 手机壳。bundle deal = 套装优惠。但店里配件通常比网上贵很多。✅推荐
19 You: Actually, I'll just order those on Amazon — they're way cheaper there. Just the phone and the plan today. I will order those on Amazon. They are cheaper there. Just the phone and the plan today. 其实我会在亚马逊上买——那里便宜多了。今天只要手机和套餐就行。 way cheaper = 便宜很多。way 在口语中作副词=很/非常。礼貌但坚定地拒绝推销。✅推荐
20 Employee: Fair enough! Let me ring you up. Fair enough. Let me process your purchase. 有道理！我来给你结账。 ring you up = 给你结账/收银（原意是收银机响铃）。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国手机文化

  • 三大运营商：Verizon（信号最好但最贵）、AT&T（居中）、T-Mobile（性价比高，信号在城市好）
  • MVNO（虚拟运营商）：Mint Mobile、Visible、Cricket 等，租用大运营商网络，价格更便宜（月费 $15-30）
  • 信用分与手机：后付费和分期买手机需要信用分。刚到美国没有信用记录的话，选 prepaid 最简单
  • SSN：办后付费需要社会安全号。没有SSN的外国人可能需要交押金
  • 配件贵：店里卖的手机壳和贴膜比 Amazon 贵 3-5 倍，建议网购
  • trade-in：旧手机可以折价换新，店员会帮你估价

🇬🇧 英国手机差异

  • 三大运营商：EE（最快）、Vodafone、Three（最便宜）。O2 也很流行
  • PAYG = Pay-As-You-Go = 预付费（英式说法）
  • SIM-only deal = 只要SIM卡的套餐（不买手机），英国很流行，月费 £5-20
  • ** handset = 手机（英式），美式说 phone / device**
  • 英国手机店推销不如美国那么激进
  • giffgaff、VOXI 等虚拟运营商很受欢迎，价格便宜
