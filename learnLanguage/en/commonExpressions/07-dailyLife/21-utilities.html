第21课：水电燃气费 Utility Bills（Electric, Gas, Water）

场景背景

在美国，utility bills（水电燃气费）通常每月或每两个月结算一次。电费由电力公司（electric company / utility company）收取，燃气费由燃气公司（gas company）收取，水费由水务局（water department）收取。有些地区是捆绑收费（bundled），一家公司管所有。账单通常包含 base charge（基础费用）+ usage charge（使用量费用）。美国的电费单位是 kilowatt-hour / kWh（千瓦时），燃气用 therms 或 CCF，水用 gallons。英国类似但单位用公制。utility bills 可以设置 auto-pay（自动扣款）或 budget billing（预算均摊——把一年的费用平均到12个月）。如果缴费困难，可以联系公司安排 payment plan。

子场景一：搬家后开通水电燃气

背景：你刚搬进新家，需要开通水电燃气账户。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Hi, I just moved into a new place and I need to set up utility service. Hello, I recently moved and need to set up utility service. 你好，我刚搬进新家需要开通水电。 set up = 开通/设立。utility service = 公共事业服务。✅推荐
2 Agent: Welcome! I can help you with that. What's the service address? Welcome. I can help. What is the service address? 欢迎！我可以帮你。服务地址是什么？ service address = 服务地址（要开通水电的那个地址）。✅推荐
3 You: 425 Elm Street, Springfield. 425 Elm Street, Springfield. Springfield，Elm街425号。 ✅推荐
4 Agent: OK, I see that address. I can set up electric and gas for you. For water, you'll need to contact the city water department separately. I can set up electric and gas. For water, you need to contact the city water department separately. 好的，我查到了这个地址。我可以帮你开通电和燃气。水的话你需要单独联系市政水务局。 很多地区电力和燃气是一家公司，水是另外的市政部门。✅推荐
5 You: Got it. Is there a deposit or connection fee? Understood. Is there a deposit or connection fee? 明白了。有押金或开通费吗？ deposit = 押金。connection fee = 开通费/接通费。✅推荐
6 Agent: There's a $25 connection fee. No deposit required if you pass the credit check. Would you like me to run that? There is a $25 connection fee. No deposit is required if you pass the credit check. Would you like me to proceed? 有25美元开通费。通过信用审查就不需要押金。要帮你查吗？ run a credit check = 做信用审查。开通水电也需要信用检查。✅推荐
7 You: Sure. And can you set me up on budget billing? I'd rather have a predictable amount every month. Sure. Can you set up budget billing? I prefer a predictable amount each month. 好的。能帮我设成预算均摊吗？我宁愿每月有个可预期的金额。 budget billing = 预算均摊/平均计费（把年度总费用平均到12个月，避免夏季/冬季账单暴增）。predictable = 可预测的。✅推荐
8 Agent: Absolutely. I'll estimate your monthly payment at about $120 based on the usage history at that address. It gets adjusted every six months. Absolutely. I will estimate your monthly payment at approximately $120 based on the usage history. It is adjusted every six months. 当然。根据那个地址的使用历史，我估算你每月大约120美元。每六个月调整一次。 usage history = 使用历史。adjusted = 调整。✅推荐

子场景二：账单异常高，打电话投诉

背景：你收到这个月的电费账单，比平时高了将近一倍，觉得不合理。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
9 You: Hi, I just got my bill and it's way higher than usual — $280 for last month. I normally pay around $150. Can someone explain what's going on? Hello, I received my bill and it is significantly higher than usual at $280 for last month. I normally pay $150. Can someone explain this? 你好，我刚收到账单比平时高很多——上个月280美元。我通常付150左右。能解释一下吗？ way higher = 高很多。what's going on = 怎么回事。✅推荐
10 Agent: Let me pull up your account... I see the spike. It looks like your usage went up quite a bit — from about 800 kWh to 1,300 kWh. Were you running anything extra last month? Let me access your account. I see the increase. Your usage went from 800 kWh to 1,300 kWh. Were you using anything extra? 我调一下你的账户……我看到了异常。你的用量涨了很多——从大约800千瓦时涨到1300。你上个月多用了什么吗？ spike = 突然上升/暴增。pull up your account = 调出你的账户信息。kWh = 千瓦时（电的计量单位）。✅推荐
11 You: Not that I can think of. We were out of town for a week, if anything it should've been lower. Could there be a meter issue? Not that I can think of. We were out of town for a week, so it should have been lower. Could there be a meter issue? 我想不出来。我们还外出旅行了一周，按说应该更低才对。会不会是电表有问题？ Not that I can think of = 我想不到有。if anything = 如果有的话/按说。meter issue = 电表问题。✅推荐
12 Agent: It's possible. Let me schedule a meter check — a technician will come out within the next five business days. In the meantime, I can set up a payment arrangement so you don't get hit with a late fee. It is possible. Let me schedule a meter check within five business days. I can set up a payment arrangement to avoid late fees. 有可能。我安排一次电表检查——五个工作日内技师会上门。同时我可以设一个付款安排，这样你不会被收滞纳金。 hit with = 被收取/被罚款（口语）。late fee = 滞纳金/逾期费。payment arrangement = 付款安排（分期或延期）。✅推荐
13 You: That'd be great. And if it turns out to be a meter error, will I get a credit on my next bill? That would be great. If it is a meter error, will I receive a credit on my next bill? 太好了。如果查出来是电表错误，下个月会给我抵扣吗？ turns out = 结果是/原来是。credit = 抵扣/退款（账单上的正数余额）。✅推荐
14 Agent: Yes, you'll get a full credit for any overcharge. We'll get it sorted out for you. Yes, you will receive a full credit for any overcharge. We will resolve it. 会的，多收的钱会全额抵扣。我们会帮你处理好的。 overcharge = 多收的费用。get it sorted out = 帮你处理好。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国水电燃气须知

  • 多家公司：电力、燃气、水可能分别是不同公司。搬家时要分别通知
  • average monthly bill：美国家庭平均月电费约 $115，燃气 $60-100（冬季更高），水 $40-60
  • auto-pay：强烈建议设置自动扣款，避免忘记交费被断电或收滞纳金
  • 断电规定：电力公司通常在欠费后30-60天才会断电，且需要提前通知。冬季很多州禁止断暖（heating shutoff moratorium）
  • 节能项目：很多电力公司提供免费节能评估（energy audit）和节能补贴（rebates）
  • smart meter：智能电表可以实时监控用电量，很多公司提供手机APP查看

🇬🇧 英国水电差异

  • 英国水电通常更贵：电费月均 £60-80，燃气 £50-70，水 £30-40
  • council tax：市政税（根据房屋价值等级），约 £100-200/月，必须交
  • standing charge：固定日费（无论用不用都要交），英国账单的特点
  • energy price cap：英国政府设有能源价格上限，保护消费者
  • prepayment meter：预付费电表（充多少用多少），低收入家庭常见
  • TV Licence：电视执照费 £169/年（看直播电视必须交），不交会被罚款
