第5课：理发店 Hairdresser / Barber

场景背景

在美国，男士理发去 barber shop（理发店），女士去 hair salon（美发沙龙），但也有不少男女都去的 salon。男士简单理发大概 25-40 美元，加上 15-20% 小费。英国男士理发约 15-25 英镑，小费不是必须但常见。美国理发师叫 barber（男）或 hairstylist（男女通用），英国口语中常叫 hairdresser。理发时最大的挑战是用英语准确描述你想要的发型。walk-in（不用预约直接去）很多店都接受，但周末人多时可能要等。

子场景一：男士理发（Barber Shop）

背景：你走进一家理发店，没有预约，想简单剪个头。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Hey, you guys take walk-ins? Hello, do you accept walk-in customers? 嘿，你们接受不用预约的吗？ walk-in(s) = 不用预约直接来的客人。这是美国最常见的理发店开场白。✅推荐
2 Barber: Yeah, for sure. Just sign in on the clipboard and it'll be about a 20-minute wait. Yes, certainly. Just sign in on the clipboard and it will be approximately a 20-minute wait. 当然。在签到板上写上名字，大概等20分钟。 clipboard = 签到板/签到表。sign in = 登记/签到。✅推荐
3 You: No problem, I'll wait. No problem, I will wait. 没问题，我等着。 简洁的回应，表示愿意等。✅推荐
4 (20 min later) Barber: Alright, you're up! Have a seat. What are we doing for you today? You are next. Please have a seat. What would you like done today? 好了，轮到你了！请坐。今天想怎么剪？ you're up = 轮到你了。What are we doing for you? = 你想怎么剪？理发师的标准问法。✅推荐
5 You: Just a trim, please. Keep it short on the sides but leave some length on top. Just a trim, please. Keep it short on the sides but leave some length on top. 修剪一下就好。两边剪短，但上面留点长度。 trim = 修剪（少量剪）。描述发型时 sides = 两边，top = 顶部。✅推荐
6 Barber: You want a fade or more of a regular taper? Would you like a fade or a regular taper? 你要渐变还是普通的渐收？ fade = 渐变剪法（从很短到稍长的渐变），非常流行的男式发型。taper = 渐收（自然的由长到短）。✅推荐
7 You: A fade would be great. Maybe a number 2 on the sides? A fade would be great. Maybe a number 2 guard on the sides? 渐变挺好的。两边用2号推子吧？ number 2 = 推子限位梳（guard）的号码，2号=6毫米。推子号码1-8对应不同长度。✅推荐
8 Barber: Gotcha. And how about the top — you want me to thin it out a bit or keep it full? Understood. And the top — would you like me to thin it out a bit or keep it full? 明白了。上面呢——要打薄一点还是保持厚度？ Gotcha = 明白了（= I've got you）。thin it out = 打薄。keep it full = 保持厚度。✅推荐
9 You: Thin it out just a little. And don't take too much off the front — I'm trying to keep it styled. Thin it out just a little. Do not take too much off the front. I am trying to keep it styled. 稍微打薄一点就行。前面别剪太多——我想保持造型。 take off = 剪掉。styled = 有造型的。✅推荐
10 Barber: You got it. I know exactly what you want. Understood. I know exactly what you want. 没问题。我完全知道你想要什么。 You got it = 没问题/包在我身上。✅推荐
11 Barber: (cutting) So how's it looking? Good so far? How does it look so far? Is it good? （剪到一半）看起来怎么样？目前为止还行吧？ 理发师通常会在剪的过程中让你确认。✅推荐
12 You: Yeah, looks good. Maybe just a touch shorter on the right side. Yes, it looks good. Maybe just slightly shorter on the right side. 挺好的。右边再稍微短一点点。 a touch = 一点点（= a little bit）。✅推荐
13 Barber: How about the back — you want it squared off or rounded? How about the back — would you like it squared off or rounded? 后面呢——要方形的还是圆弧的？ squared off = 齐剪成方形（美式流行）。rounded = 自然弧形。✅推荐
14 You: Squared off, please. Squared off, please. 方形的吧。 男士后面发型选择。✅推荐
15 Barber: Alright, we're all done. Check it out in the mirror — how's that? Alright, we are finished. Please check it in the mirror. How does it look? 好了，剪完了。照照镜子看看——怎么样？ Check it out = 看看/检查一下。✅推荐
16 You: That's perfect, man. Nice work! That is perfect. Excellent work. 完美。手艺不错！ Nice work = 干得好/手艺不错，称赞别人的工作。✅推荐
17 Barber: Glad you like it. That'll be 28 bucks. Glad you like it. That will be $28. 很高兴你满意。28美元。 bucks = 美元。✅推荐 🇺🇸
18 You: (hands over $35) Keep the change. Here is $35. Keep the change. （递过35美元）不用找了。 Keep the change = 不用找零了，差价当小费。35-28=7美元，约25%小费，很慷慨。✅推荐

子场景二：女士美发（Hair Salon）

背景：你的妻子想去做头发，剪发加染发，打电话预约。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
19 Wife: Hi, I'd like to book an appointment for a cut and color. Hello, I would like to book an appointment for a haircut and coloring. 你好，我想预约剪发和染发。 cut and color = 剪发加染发，salon 中的常见组合服务。✅推荐
20 Receptionist: Great! Do you have a stylist preference, or is anyone available fine? Great. Do you have a preference for a stylist, or is anyone available acceptable? 好的！你有指定的发型师吗，还是谁都可以？ stylist preference = 指定发型师。anyone available fine = 有空的就行。✅推荐
21 Wife: Anyone's fine, as long as they're good with Asian hair. Anyone is fine, as long as they are experienced with Asian hair. 谁都行，只要擅长处理亚洲人的头发就好。 good with = 擅长处理……的。亚洲人的头发质地和西方人不同，有些发型师不熟悉。✅推荐
22 Receptionist: Maria does a lot of Asian clients — she's great. How about next Wednesday at 2? Maria has many Asian clients and she is excellent. How about next Wednesday at 2 PM? Maria有很多亚洲客户——她很棒。下周三下午2点怎么样？ does a lot of = 服务很多/接待很多。✅推荐
23 Wife: That works. About how long does a cut and color usually take? That works. Approximately how long does a cut and color usually take? 可以。剪发加染发一般需要多长时间？ 问清楚时长以便安排日程，通常 2-3 小时。✅推荐
24 Receptionist: Plan on about two and a half hours. Maybe bring something to read! Plan on approximately two and a half hours. You might want to bring something to read. 大概两个半小时吧。可以带点东西看看！ Plan on = 预计/做好……的准备。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国理发文化

  • 小费是必须的：理发通常给 15-20% 小费。帮你洗头的人有时单独给 3-5 美元
  • Barber shop vs Salon：Barber shop 通常只剪男发，价格较便宜（$25-40）；Salon 男女都做，价格更高（$50-100+）
  • 推子号码：#0=几乎没有(1.5mm), #1=3mm, #2=6mm, #3=10mm, #4=13mm。男士剪短两侧时常用
  • 产品推销：理发后理发师可能会推荐护发产品，不想买就礼貌拒绝 "No thanks, I'm good."

🇬🇧 英国理发差异

  • 英国男士理发叫 having a haircut 或 getting your hair done
  • Barber 在英国也常见，价格比美国便宜，约 £15-25
  • 英国传统 barber shop 有红白蓝三色旋转柱标志
  • 英国理发小费不是必须，但凑整很常见（如 £18 的理发给 £20）
  • 英式用语：fringe = 刘海（美式：bangs），plait = 编辫子（美式：braid）
