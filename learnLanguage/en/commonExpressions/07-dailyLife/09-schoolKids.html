第9课：孩子学校 School-Related（家长会、与其他家长交流）

场景背景

在美国，家长与学校的互动主要通过 PTA（Parent-Teacher Association，家长教师协会）和 parent-teacher conference（家长会）进行。美国的家长会通常是一对一（你和老师单独聊10-15分钟），不像中国那种全班家长坐一起开会的模式。学校活动非常丰富：back-to-school night（返校夜）、science fair（科学展）、field trip（校外教学）等都需要家长志愿者。英国类似的组织叫 PTA 或 Friends of the School，家长会叫 parents' evening。如果你有孩子在英美上学，这些对话场景你一定会遇到。

子场景一：家长会上和老师交流

背景：你参加孩子的家长会，和班主任（homeroom teacher）聊孩子的学习情况。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 Teacher: Hi, Mr. Li! Great to see you. Have a seat. So, Kevin's been doing really well overall — he's a pleasure to have in class. Hello, Mr. Li. It is great to see you. Please have a seat. Kevin has been doing very well overall. He is a pleasure to have in class. 你好，Li先生！很高兴见到你。请坐。Kevin整体表现很好——他在班上很讨人喜欢。 美国老师通常会先说好话，然后才指出需要改进的地方。a pleasure to have in class = 在班上很让人愉快。✅推荐
2 You: That's good to hear! How's he doing with reading? That's been a bit of a struggle at home. That is good to hear. How is he doing with reading? That has been somewhat difficult at home. 听到这个很高兴！他阅读怎么样？在家里这方面有点吃力。 a bit of a struggle = 有点吃力/有点困难。委婉表达孩子的问题。✅推荐
3 Teacher: He's actually made a lot of progress since the start of the year. His phonics are much stronger now. The main thing is just getting him to read more at home — even 15 minutes a day would make a huge difference. He has made significant progress since the start of the year. His phonics are much stronger. The key is getting him to read more at home. Even 15 minutes a day would help significantly. 其实他从年初开始进步很大。他的自然拼读能力强了很多。主要就是让他在家多读——哪怕每天15分钟都会有很大改善。 phonics = 自然拼读法。make a huge difference = 产生巨大差别/效果显著。✅推荐
4 You: We'll definitely work on that. Is there anything else we should be concerned about? We will definitely work on that. Is there anything else we should be concerned about? 我们一定会加强的。还有什么我们需要注意的吗？ work on that = 加强/改进那方面。concerned about = 担心/关注。✅推荐
5 Teacher: Not really — he gets along great with the other kids, which is huge at this age. He does tend to rush through his math sometimes, so just remind him to slow down and double-check his work. Not really. He gets along well with the other children, which is very important at this age. He tends to rush through math, so remind him to slow down and check his work. 没什么——他和其他孩子相处得很好，这个年龄段这点非常重要。他数学有时候会赶进度，提醒他慢下来再检查一遍就好。 gets along great with = 和……相处得很好。which is huge = 这非常重要。rush through = 赶着做完/匆匆完成。double-check = 再检查一遍。✅推荐
6 You: That sounds like Kevin! We'll keep an eye on that. Thanks so much for everything — we really appreciate it. That sounds like Kevin. We will monitor that. Thank you for everything. We truly appreciate it. 这确实是Kevin的风格！我们会注意的。非常感谢你做的一切——我们真的很感激。 That sounds like Kevin = 这就是Kevin的样子（表示认同老师对孩子的观察）。keep an eye on = 留意/关注。✅推荐

子场景二：和其他家长聊天（学校活动）

背景：在学校运动会（field day）上，你和其他家长站在旁边看孩子们比赛，自然地聊起来。

7 Other parent: Hey, which one's yours? Hello, which child is yours? 嘿，哪个是你家的？ 家长之间最经典的破冰问题。✅推荐
8 You: The one in the blue shirt, number 12 — Kevin. Yours? The one in the blue shirt, number 12 — Kevin. And yours? 穿蓝色衣服那个，12号——Kevin。你家的呢？ 简洁描述自家孩子。✅推荐
9 Other parent: Oh nice! Emma — she's in the red, over by the finish line. This is her first field day, she's so excited. Oh nice. Emma — she is in the red, near the finish line. This is her first field day and she is very excited. 哦不错！Emma——穿红色的那个，在终点线那边。这是她第一次参加运动会，她特别兴奋。 field day = 运动会/户外活动日（美国学校常见）。in the red = 穿红色的。✅推荐
10 You: Same here! Kevin's been talking about it all week. Do you guys live nearby? Same here. Kevin has been talking about it all week. Do you live nearby? 我们也是！Kevin整个礼拜都在说这个。你们住附近吗？ Same here = 我们也是。从孩子话题自然过渡到家庭话题。✅推荐
11 Other parent: Yeah, we're over on Elm Street — about a five-minute walk from here. You? Yes, we live on Elm Street, about a five-minute walk from here. How about you? 是的，我们在Elm街上——走过来大概五分钟。你呢？ 美国人描述距离常用时间而非距离，如 "5-minute walk" 而非 "500 meters"。✅推荐

子场景三：收到老师邮件，关于孩子行为问题

背景：你收到老师邮件说Kevin上课讲话太多，你想和老师沟通一下。

15 You (phone): Hi Ms. Johnson, thanks for reaching out about Kevin. I wanted to touch base and see how we can work together on this. Hi Ms. Johnson, thank you for contacting me about Kevin. 17 You: Yeah, he can be a talker! We'll definitely have a chat with him at home. Is there anything specific you'd like us to reinforce? Yes, he can be talkative. We will talk to him at home. 注意事项

🇺🇸 美国学校家长须知

  • parent-teacher conference：每学期1-2次，一对一，每个家长约10-15分钟，需提前在网上预约时段
  • PTA：家长教师协会，组织各种活动和筹款（fundraiser），参与度高的家长在社区中更有影响力
  • volunteer（志愿者）：学校经常需要家长志愿者，如 field trip 的 chaperone（陪同人员）
  • back-to-school night：每学年开学初的家长开放日，家长去教室听老师介绍课程安排
  • report card：成绩单，通常每季度（quarter）一次
  • 沟通方式：美国老师更倾向于用邮件或 ClassDojo（一款家校沟通APP）沟通，不太打电话

🇬🇧 英国学校差异

  • parents' evening：英国家长会，也是一对一，但通常在下午/傍晚举行
  • school governors：学校理事会，类似美国 PTA 但更正式
  • year group：年级（英国叫法），如 Year 3 = 小学三年级
  • school uniform：英国大多数公立学校要求穿校服，美国公立学校通常没有校服要求
  • Ofsted：英国教育标准局，学校评级（Outstanding, Good, Requires Improvement, Inadequate）
