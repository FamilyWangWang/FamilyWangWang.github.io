第4课：打电话 Phone Calls（预约、联系商家）

场景背景

在美国和英国，电话沟通仍然是日常生活中非常重要的技能。预约医生、联系维修、确认订单、咨询信息……都需要打电话。很多中国学习者怕打电话，因为没有肢体语言和面部表情辅助理解。美式电话用语有一些固定模式：接电话先自报家门，来电者说明来意，然后进入正题。英国电话礼仪也类似，但可能更正式一些。现在很多预约可以网上完成，但有些事情还是需要打电话——尤其是一些小商家和老牌诊所。

子场景一：打电话预约牙医

背景：你需要约个牙医做检查和洗牙，拨打牙医诊所的电话。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 Receptionist: Dr. Miller's office, this is Jessica speaking. How can I help you? Dr. Miller's office, this is Jessica speaking. How may I help you? Miller医生的诊所，我是Jessica。请问有什么可以帮您？ 美国诊所/商家接电话的标配：先报公司/医生名，再自报名字。✅推荐
2 You: Hi Jessica, I'm calling to schedule a cleaning and a checkup. Do you guys take new patients? Hello Jessica, I am calling to schedule a cleaning and a checkup. Do you accept new patients? 你好Jessica，我打电话是想预约洗牙和检查。你们接收新病人吗？ cleaning = 洗牙。checkup = 检查。take new patients = 接收新病人（也可以用 accept）。✅推荐
3 Receptionist: We sure do! Are you already in our system, or is this your first time? We certainly do. Are you already in our system, or is this your first visit? 当然接收！您的信息已经在我们的系统里了吗，还是第一次来？ in our system = 在我们的数据库/系统里有记录。✅推荐
4 You: First time. I just moved to the area. This is my first time. I recently moved to the area. 第一次。我刚搬到这个地区。 moved to the area = 搬到这个地区/附近。✅推荐
5 Receptionist: Welcome! I'll need some basic info — your full name, date of birth, and insurance provider. Welcome! I will need some basic information — your full name, date of birth, and insurance provider. 欢迎！我需要一些基本信息——您的全名、出生日期和保险公司。 insurance provider = 保险公司。美国看牙一定要有牙科保险（dental insurance）。✅推荐
6 You: Sure, it's Alex Chen — A-L-E-X, C-H-E-N. DOB is April 23rd, 1988. And I have Delta Dental. Sure, it is Alex Chen. Date of birth is April 23rd, 1988. And I have Delta Dental insurance. 好的，Alex Chen——A-L-E-X，L-I。出生日期是1988年4月23日。保险是Delta Dental。 DOB = Date of Birth 的缩写，口语中常用。拼写姓名时逐字母念。Delta Dental = 美国最大的牙科保险公司之一。✅推荐
7 Receptionist: Perfect. We have an opening on Thursday the 23rd at 2 PM, or Friday the 24th at 10:30 AM. Which works better for you? Perfect. We have an available appointment on Thursday the 23rd at 2 PM, or Friday the 24th at 10:30 AM. Which works better for you? 好的。23号周四下午2点有一个空位，或者24号周五上午10点半。哪个对您更方便？ opening = 空位/空档（预约用）。Which works better for you? = 哪个对你更方便？✅推荐
8 You: Let's go with Thursday at 2. Let us go with Thursday at 2 PM. 那就周四下午2点吧。 Let's go with = 就选……，做选择时的地道说法。✅推荐
9 Receptionist: All set! You'll need to fill out some paperwork when you get here, so maybe come about 15 minutes early. All set. You will need to complete some paperwork when you arrive, so please come about 15 minutes early. 都安排好了！到的时候需要填一些表格，所以最好提前15分钟到。 All set = 都办好了/一切就绪。paperwork = 表格/文件。首次就诊通常要填新病人表格。✅推荐
10 You: Will do. Thanks, Jessica! I will do that. Thank you, Jessica. 好的。谢谢你Jessica！ Will do = 我会的/好的，答应做某事的简洁回应。✅推荐

子场景二：打电话给餐厅订位

背景：周末想带家人去一家不错的餐厅吃饭，打电话预订座位。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
11 You: Hi, I'd like to make a reservation for Saturday night. Hello, I would like to make a reservation for Saturday night. 你好，我想预订周六晚上的位置。 make a reservation = 预订，标准说法。也可以说 book a table（英式更常用）。✅推荐
12 Host: Sure, for how many? Sure, for how many people? 好的，几位？ for how many = 几位（人），省略了 people。✅推荐
13 You: Four adults and one kid — so five total. Around 7-ish if possible? Four adults and one child, so five in total. Around 7 PM if possible. 四个大人一个小孩——一共五位。尽量7点左右？ -ish = 左右/大约，加在时间后面表示大概。kid = 小孩（口语），正式场合用 child。✅推荐
14 Host: Let me check... We're pretty booked up, but I've got a 6:30 or an 8:15. Does either of those work? Let me check. We are quite fully booked, but I have 6:30 or 8:15. Does either of those work? 我查一下……我们订得差不多了，但有6:30和8:15的。哪个可以吗？ pretty booked up = 基本订满了。Does either work? = 两个里有一个可以吗？✅推荐
15 You: Hmm, 6:30 might be a little tight. Let's do 8:15. 6:30 might be too early. Let us do 8:15. 嗯，6:30可能有点赶。那就8:15吧。 a little tight = 有点紧/有点赶（时间上）。Let's do = 就定……，做决定时的常用说法。✅推荐
16 Host: Great. Can I get a name for the reservation? Great. Can I have a name for the reservation? 好的。请问预订人姓名？ 餐厅预订的标准问法。✅推荐
17 You: Li — L-I. The name is Li, spelled L-I. Li，L-I。 电话里提供姓名时拼出来，避免听错。✅推荐
18 Host: OK, you're all set — party of five, Saturday at 8:15 under Li. See you then! OK, your reservation is confirmed — a party of five, Saturday at 8:15 under the name Li. See you then! 好的，都订好了——五位，周六8:15，预订人Li。到时候见！ party of five = 五位（用餐人数的标准说法）。under = 以……的名字预订。✅推荐

子场景三：打电话咨询/投诉

背景：你网购的一件商品迟迟没到，打电话给客服询问。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
19 You: Hi, I placed an order about two weeks ago and it still hasn't shown up. I was hoping you could look into it for me. Hello, I placed an order about two weeks ago and it still has not arrived. I was hoping you could investigate it for me. 你好，我大约两周前下了一个订单，到现在还没收到。希望你能帮我查一下。 shown up = 到达/出现（= arrived）。look into it = 调查/查一下。✅推荐
20 Agent: Of course. Can I get your order number? Of course. Can I have your order number? 当然。请问您的订单号？ Can I get = 请给我（比 Can I have 更口语）。✅推荐
21 You: Sure, it's... hold on, let me pull it up... OK, it's 4-7-2-8-alpha-bravo-9. Sure, it is... wait a moment, let me retrieve it... OK, it is 4-7-2-8-A-B-9. 好的，是……等一下我找一下……好的，是4-7-2-8-A-B-9。 pull it up = 调出来/找出来（在电脑或手机上查找信息）。字母在电话中常用 NATO 音标（Alpha, Bravo 等）以避免混淆。✅推荐
22 Agent: OK, I'm seeing it right here. Looks like it got stuck at the sorting facility in Memphis. I'll go ahead and put in a trace on it. OK, I can see it here. It appears to be stuck at the sorting facility in Memphis. I will put in a trace on it. 好的，我看到了。看起来邮件在孟菲斯的分拣中心卡住了。我来帮你查一下物流追踪。 got stuck = 卡住了/滞留了。put in a trace = 提交物流追踪请求。go ahead and = 就（做某事），口语中常见的前缀。✅推荐
23 You: Is there a chance it's just lost? Because honestly, I kinda need it by this weekend. Is it possible that it is lost? Because honestly, I need it by this weekend. 有没有可能已经丢了？说真的，我这周末之前需要它。 Is there a chance = 有没有可能……kinda need = 有点需要（用 kinda 缓和语气）。✅推荐
24 Agent: I hear you. Tell you what — let me go ahead and send out a replacement. If the original one shows up, you can just refuse the delivery. I understand. I will send out a replacement. If the original arrives, you can refuse the delivery. 我理解。这样吧——我给你重新发一个。如果原来的那个到了，你拒收就行。 I hear you = 我理解你。send out a replacement = 重新发一个替换品。refuse the delivery = 拒收快递。✅推荐
25 You: That sounds great. Thanks for sorting this out. That sounds great. Thank you for resolving this. 听起来很好。谢谢帮我解决了这个问题。 sorting this out = 解决这个问题/处理好这件事。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国电话礼仪

  • 自报家门：接电话通常先说公司名或自己的名字，不像中国直接说"喂"
  • 语音信箱（voicemail）：美国非常依赖语音信箱，打电话对方没接就留一段语音。很多人不爱回陌生号码的未接来电
  • 被转接很常见：打电话给大公司经常被转来转去（transfer），听到 "Let me transfer you" 或 "I'll put you through" 时要有耐心
  • 电话菜单（phone tree）：先按1、再按3……有时需要等很久。可以说 "speak to a representative" 或反复按0转人工
  • 电话号码读法：通常三位一组或四位一组读，如 555-1234 读作 "five-five-five, twelve thirty-four"

🇬🇧 英国电话差异

  • 英国人电话中可能更正式，常说 "May I speak to..." 而非 "Is ... there?"
  • 英国电话号码读法通常逐位念，如 020 7946 0958 读作 "zero two zero, seven nine four six, zero nine five eight"
  • 英国客服可能称呼你 mate 或 love（视地区和性别而定），这很正常不要惊讶
  • mobile = 手机（英式），cell phone / cell = 手机（美式）

打电话实用技巧

  • 没听清时说："Sorry, could you repeat that?" 或 "I didn't quite catch that."
  • 需要时间思考时说："Just a moment, let me think..." 或 "Hang on a sec..."
  • 对方语速太快时说："Would you mind slowing down a bit?"
  • 拼写姓名时用音标字母：A-Alpha, B-Bravo, C-Charlie, D-Delta, E-Echo...
