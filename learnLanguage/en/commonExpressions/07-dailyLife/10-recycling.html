第10课：垃圾分类与回收 Recycling, Garbage & Waste Management

场景背景

美国的垃圾处理系统因城市而异，但基本分为三类：trash（不可回收垃圾）、recycling（可回收物）和 yard waste / green waste（园林废弃物）。大多数家庭有三种不同颜色的垃圾桶（bin / cart / toter）。回收桶通常是蓝色，垃圾桶是绿色或黑色，园林废物桶是灰色或棕色。垃圾每周收一次（pickup day），不同城市规定不同。英国垃圾分类更严格，通常分为 general waste、recycling、garden waste 和 food waste。英国很多地区还有 household recycling centre（大型垃圾回收中心）。大件垃圾（furniture, appliances）不能直接扔垃圾桶，需要特殊处理。

子场景一：了解当地的垃圾分类规则

背景：你刚搬到一个新的社区，想搞清楚垃圾分类的规则，问了邻居。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Hey, quick question — what's the deal with trash pickup around here? Which day do they come? Hello, quick question. What is the trash pickup schedule here? Which day do they come? 嘿，快问一下——这里垃圾回收是怎么回事？他们哪天来收？ what's the deal with = ……是怎么回事/……怎么安排的，询问某事的运作方式。✅推荐
2 Neighbor: Trash and recycling get picked up on Wednesdays. You just wheel the bins out to the curb Tuesday night. Trash and recycling are picked up on Wednesdays. You just wheel the bins out to the curb on Tuesday night. 垃圾和回收物周三收。周二晚上把桶推到路边就行。 wheel out = 推出去（垃圾桶下面有轮子）。curb = 路边/路缘（美式），英式常用 kerb。✅推荐
3 You: And what goes in the recycling bin? Is it the same as everywhere — paper, plastic, glass? What goes in the recycling bin? Is it the standard — paper, plastic, and glass? 回收桶里放什么？跟其他地方一样吗——纸、塑料、玻璃？ what goes in = 里面放什么。✅推荐
4 Neighbor: Pretty much, yeah. But you gotta make sure the containers are clean — like, rinse out your cans and bottles. They won't take 'em if they've got food residue. For the most part, yes. But you must ensure the containers are clean. Rinse out cans and bottles. They will not accept them if they have food residue. 基本是的。但容器要干净——就是罐头和瓶子要冲洗一下。有食物残留的话他们不收。 gotta make sure = 必须确保。rinse out = 冲洗。food residue = 食物残留。✅推荐
5 You: Got it. What about pizza boxes? I've heard mixed things about those. I understand. What about pizza boxes? I have heard conflicting information about those. 明白了。披萨盒呢？关于那个我听过不同的说法。 mixed things = 不同的说法/矛盾的信息。披萨盒上的油脂让它不能被回收，这是常见的困惑点。✅推荐
6 Neighbor: Yeah, that's a tricky one. If it's got grease stains, it goes in the regular trash. If it's clean, you can recycle it. That is a tricky one. If it has grease stains, it goes in the regular trash. If it is clean, you can recycle it. 那个比较麻烦。如果有油渍就扔普通垃圾桶。如果是干净的就可以回收。 tricky = 麻烦的/棘手的。grease stains = 油渍。✅推荐

子场景二：处理大件垃圾和旧家具

背景：你换了一张新沙发，旧的需要处理掉，但不知道怎么弄。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
7 You: I've got an old couch I need to get rid of. Can I just put it out by the curb? I have an old couch I need to dispose of. Can I place it by the curb? 我有一张旧沙发需要处理。我可以直接放路边吗？ get rid of = 处理掉/扔掉。couch = 沙发（美式），英式常用 sofa 或 settee。✅推荐
8 Neighbor: Not really — the regular trash guys won't take bulky items. You've got a few options though. Not really. The regular trash collectors will not take bulky items. However, you have several options. 不太行——普通收垃圾的不收大件。不过你有几个选择。 bulky items = 大件物品。trash guys = 收垃圾的人（口语）。✅推荐
9 You: What do you suggest? What do you suggest? 你有什么建议？ 简洁地问建议。✅推荐
10 Neighbor: You could call the city for a bulk pickup — they do it once a month for free. Or you could post it on Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist. Someone might pick it up for free. You could call the city for a bulk pickup. They do it once a month at no charge. Or you could post it on Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist. Someone might take it for free. 你可以打电话给市政要求大件回收——他们每月免费来收一次。或者发到Facebook Marketplace或Craigslist上，可能有人免费来拿。 bulk pickup = 大件垃圾回收。pick it up = 来取。美国人很喜欢在二手平台上免费送家具，"free" 的东西很快就会被人拿走。✅推荐
11 You: That's a great idea — I hadn't thought of that. How does the bulk pickup work? That is a great idea. I had not thought of that. How does the bulk pickup work? 好主意——我没想到这个。大件回收怎么操作？ ✅推荐
12 Neighbor: Just call 311 or go on the city website and schedule it. They'll give you a date, and you just put it out the night before. Call 311 or visit the city website to schedule it. They will give you a date, and you put it out the night before. 打311或者上市政网站预约就行。他们会给你一个日期，前一晚放出去就好。 311 = 美国很多城市的非紧急市政服务电话（类似110但不是紧急电话）。✅推荐 🇺🇸

子场景三：处理有害废弃物（Hazardous Waste）

背景：你清理车库时发现一些旧油漆、电池和化学品，不知道该怎么安全丢弃。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
13 You: I've got some old paint cans and batteries I need to throw out. Can those go in the regular trash? I have some old paint cans and batteries to dispose of. Can those go in the regular trash? 我有一些旧油漆罐和电池需要扔。这些能放普通垃圾桶吗？ ✅推荐
14 Neighbor: Nah, you definitely don't wanna do that — that's hazardous waste. You gotta take it to the drop-off site. No, you should not do that. That is hazardous waste. You need to take it to the designated drop-off site. 不行，千万别那么做——那是有害垃圾。你得送到指定回收点去。 Nah = 不/不行（口语化的 No）。hazardous waste = 有害废弃物。drop-off site = 指定投放点。✅推荐
15 You: Where's the nearest one? Where is the nearest drop-off site? 最近的一个在哪里？ ✅推荐
16 Neighbor: There's one over on Industrial Road — they're open on Saturdays. You just drive up, pop your trunk, and they'll unload it for you. It's free. There is one on Industrial Road. They are open on Saturdays. You drive up, open your trunk, and they unload it for you at no charge. Industrial Road上有一个——周六开放。你开过去，打开后备箱，他们帮你卸。免费的。 pop your trunk = 打开后备箱。pop 在口语中=快速打开/弹出。✅推荐
17 You: Easy enough. What about electronics — like old phones and laptops? That is easy. What about electronics, such as old phones and laptops? 挺简单的。电子产品呢——比如旧手机和笔记本电脑？ Easy enough = 挺简单的/不难。✅推荐
18 Neighbor: Same deal — Best Buy actually takes old electronics for free. You just drop 'em off at the customer service desk. The same applies. Best Buy takes old electronics for free. You drop them off at the customer service desk. 一样——Best Buy其实免费回收旧电子产品。送到客服台就行。 Same deal = 一样的/同样的情况。Best Buy 和 Staples 等商店提供免费的电子产品回收。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国垃圾处理须知

  • 垃圾分类因城市而异：每个城市规定不同，搬新地方时先查当地规则（搜 "your city + waste management"）
  • 311 电话：美国很多城市的市政服务热线，可以预约大件回收、报告路灯坏了、投诉噪音等
  • recycling symbol：塑料瓶底部的三角形回收标志里面有数字（1-7），不同城市接受不同的数字
  • compost：有些城市有堆肥回收（厨余垃圾），有些没有
  • dumpster：大型垃圾箱（公寓楼常见），住宅区用 individual bins
  • HOA 规定：有些 HOA 对垃圾桶什么时候可以放出来、什么时候必须收回有严格规定

🇬🇧 英国垃圾分类差异

  • 英国的垃圾分类通常比美国更严格，很多地区要求把回收物分得更细
  • bin day = 垃圾回收日（英式说法）
  • tip / recycling centre = 大型垃圾回收中心（英式），美式说 dump / transfer station
  • 英国有些地区使用 wheelie bin（带轮垃圾桶）
  • fly-tipping = 非法倾倒垃圾（英式），是违法行为可被罚款
  • 英国大多数地区有 food waste collection（厨余垃圾单独收集）
