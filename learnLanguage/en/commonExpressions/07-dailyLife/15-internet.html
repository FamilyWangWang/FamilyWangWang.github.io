第15课：安装网络 Internet Setup & Dealing with ISP

场景背景

在美国，家庭网络（internet / Wi-Fi）主要由几家大型 ISP（Internet Service Provider，互联网服务提供商）提供：Comcast Xfinity、AT&T、Verizon Fios、Spectrum 等。月费约 $40-80，但促销价通常只有 12 个月，之后大幅涨价。安装网络可以选择专业安装（professional installation，约 $50-100）或自助安装（self-installation，免费或约 $10）。跟 ISP 打交道是出了名的头疼——隐藏费用、账单错误、涨价、客服等待时间都是常见问题。英国主要 ISP 是 BT、Sky、Virgin Media、Plusnet 等。英国宽带通常更便宜，很多还包含固定电话和电视。

子场景一：打电话开通网络服务

背景：你刚搬进新公寓，需要开通网络。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Hi, I just moved into a new place and I need to set up internet service. What plans do you guys have? Hello, I recently moved and need to set up internet service. What plans are available? 你好，我刚搬了新家需要开通网络。你们有什么套餐？ set up = 开通/安装。plans = 套餐/方案。✅推荐
2 Agent: Great, welcome! Let me check what's available at your address. What's the zip code? Great, welcome. Let me check availability at your address. What is the zip code? 好的，欢迎！我查一下你那个地址能装什么。邮政编码是什么？ zip code = 邮政编码（美式），英式叫 postcode。ISP 需要地址来确认可用服务。✅推荐 🇺🇸
3 You: It's 90210. It is 90210. 90210。 ✅推荐
4 Agent: OK, looks like we've got fiber available in your area. We've got a 300-meg plan for $45 a month, or a gig plan for $65. Both are promotional rates for the first 12 months. We have fiber available. We offer a 300 Mbps plan for $45 per month or a 1 Gbps plan for $65. Both are promotional rates for the first 12 months. 好的，你们区域有光纤。300兆的每月45美元，或千兆的65美元。都是前12个月的促销价。 300-meg = 300Mbps。gig = 1Gbps（千兆）。promotional rates = 促销价（注意：促销到期后会涨价！）。✅推荐
5 You: The 300-meg should be plenty. What's the regular price after the promo ends? The 300 Mbps plan should be sufficient. What is the regular price after the promotion ends? 300兆应该够了。促销结束之后正常价多少？ should be plenty = 应该足够了。promo = promotion 的缩写。提前问好涨价后的价格很重要。✅推荐
6 Agent: After 12 months, it goes up to $75 a month. After 12 months, the price increases to $75 per month. 12个月后涨到每月75美元。 goes up to = 涨到。从45涨到75，几乎翻倍，这就是 ISP 的套路。✅推荐
7 You: Is there a data cap on that plan? Is there a data cap on that plan? 这个套餐有流量限制吗？ data cap = 数据流量上限。Xfinity 通常有 1.2TB 的月流量限制，超出另收费。✅推荐
8 Agent: It's 1.2 terabytes per month. Most households don't come close to that, but if you stream a lot of 4K, you might want to keep an eye on it. It is 1.2 terabytes per month. Most households do not approach that limit, but heavy 4K streaming users should monitor it. 每月1.2TB。大多数家庭用不到那么多，但如果你大量看4K流媒体的话要注意一下。 come close to = 接近。stream = 流媒体播放。keep an eye on = 留意。✅推荐
9 You: OK. And is there an installation fee, or can I do it myself? Is there an installation fee, or can I self-install? 好的。有安装费吗，还是可以自己装？ ✅推荐
10 Agent: Self-installation is free — we'll mail you a kit with the modem and instructions. Should take about 10 minutes to set up. Self-installation is free. We will mail you a kit with the modem and instructions. Setup takes about 10 minutes. 自装免费——我们寄一套设备给你，有调制解调器和说明。大概10分钟装好。 kit = 套装/工具包。modem = 调制解调器（"猫"）。✅推荐

子场景二：自己安装网络设备

背景：你收到了ISP寄来的self-installation kit，打开按照说明操作。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
11 You (to wife): OK, let me crack this open. Looks like there's a modem, a router, a power cable, and an Ethernet cable. Let me open this. It appears to contain a modem, a router, a power cable, and an Ethernet cable. 好，我来拆开。看起来有一个猫、一个路由器、一根电源线和一根网线。 crack this open = 打开（包装）。modem = 调制解调器。router = 路由器。Ethernet cable = 网线。✅推荐
12 Wife: That doesn't look too bad. What does the instruction say? That does not look too difficult. What do the instructions say? 看起来不太复杂。说明书怎么说？ ✅推荐
13 You: So first, plug the coax cable into the wall and the modem... then wait for the online light to turn solid green. Could take up to 15 minutes, it says. First, connect the coaxial cable to the wall outlet and the modem. Then wait for the online light to turn solid green. It may take up to 15 minutes. 先把同轴电缆插到墙上和猫上……然后等在线灯变绿色常亮。说可能需要15分钟。 coax cable = 同轴电缆（有线电视用的那种圆形线）。solid green = 绿色常亮（不闪烁）。✅推荐
14 Wife: It's blinking... still blinking... oh, there it goes — solid green! It is blinking. Still blinking. Oh, it is solid green now. 在闪……还在闪……哦，绿色常亮了！ blinking = 闪烁的。✅推荐
15 You: Now connect the router... and let's see if we can get online. (connects laptop) All right, we've got Wi-Fi! Now connect the router. Let us see if we can get online. All right, we have Wi-Fi. 现在连路由器……看看能不能上网。（连接笔记本）好了，有Wi-Fi了！ get online = 上网。✅推荐

子场景三：网络出问题，联系客服

背景：网络用了几个月突然变很慢，打电话给ISP投诉。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
16 Agent: Thank you for calling Xfinity. My name is Alex. How can I help you today? Thank you for calling Xfinity. My name is Alex. How may I assist you? 感谢致电Xfinity。我是Alex。请问有什么可以帮您？ ISP客服的标准问候。✅推荐
17 You: Hi Alex. My internet's been crawling for the past few days — like, I'm paying for 300 megs but I'm barely getting 50. Hello Alex. My internet has been very slow for the past few days. I am paying for 300 Mbps but barely receiving 50. 你好Alex。我的网络这几天慢得要死——我付的是300兆的但只有50兆。 crawling = 慢得像蜗牛/非常慢（比喻）。megs = Mbps 的口语简称。barely getting = 只收到/勉强达到。✅推荐
18 Agent: I'm sorry to hear that. Let me run a quick check on your line. Can you tell me if you've restarted the modem recently? I apologize for the inconvenience. Let me check your line. Have you restarted the modem recently? 抱歉听到这个。让我查一下你的线路。你最近重启过猫吗？ run a check on your line = 检查你的线路。ISP 客服永远先让你重启设备（"Have you tried turning it off and on again?"）。✅推荐
19 You: Yeah, I've already tried that — unplugged it, waited a minute, plugged it back in. Same thing. Yes, I have already tried that. I unplugged it, waited, and plugged it back in. The problem persists. 试了——拔了等了一分钟再插上。还是一样。 unplugged = 拔掉电源。Same thing = 还是老样子/没变化。✅推荐
20 Agent: OK, I'm seeing some signal issues on our end. I'd like to send a technician out to take a look. Would tomorrow between 8 and 10 AM work? I am detecting signal issues. I would like to send a technician. Would tomorrow between 8 and 10 AM work? 我看到你的线路确实有信号问题。我想派一个技师去看看。明天早上8到10点之间可以吗？ on our end = 我们这边/我们的系统里。technician = 技师/技术员。✅推荐
21 You: That works. And just to confirm — there's no charge for this visit, right? Since it's an issue on your end. That works. Just to confirm, there is no charge for this visit since the issue is on your side. 可以。确认一下——这次上门不收费对吧？因为是你们那边的问题。 on your end = 你们那边的问题。ISP 派技师上门通常收费，但如果是他们的问题应该免费。提前确认很重要。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国网络服务须知

  • 促销价陷阱：ISP 最常见的套路——前12个月便宜，之后涨价50-100%。到期前打电话威胁取消，通常能拿到新的促销价（retention deal）
  • equipment rental fee：租用 ISP 的 modem/router 每月约 $10-15。买自己的设备更划算（约 $50-100）
  • data cap：Xfinity 有 1.2TB 月流量限制，超出每50GB收 $10。AT&T Fiber 和 Verizon Fios 通常没有流量限制
  • speed test：用 speedtest.net 测速，如果实际速度远低于承诺速度，可以要求ISP解决
  • 合同 vs 无合同：有些套餐要求签1-2年合同（有提前终止费 ETF），有些是 month-to-month

🇬🇧 英国网络差异

  • 英国主要 ISP：BT、Sky、Virgin Media（最快）、Plusnet、TalkTalk
  • 英国宽带通常更便宜，月费约 £20-40
  • Openreach：英国宽带基础设施由 Openreach（BT子公司）运营，大多数 ISP 使用其网络
  • 英国大多数宽带没有 data cap（流量限制）
  • 英国宽带安装通常由 engineer（工程师）上门，费用约 £0-50
  • UK broadband speed guarantee：一些ISP承诺最低网速，达不到可以退钱
