第12课：看房 Apartment Hunting & Viewing

场景背景

在美国找公寓（apartment hunting）通常通过 Zillow、Apartments.com、Craigslist 等网站搜索。找到感兴趣的就可以预约看房（viewing / showing）。看房时中介（leasing agent / realtor）会带你参观，介绍各种设施和条款。美国租房一般需要提供收入证明（proof of income）、信用分（credit score）、前房东推荐信（reference）等。英国找房用 Rightmove、Zoopla、SpareRoom（合租），看房时见到的是 letting agent（租房中介）。租房市场在夏季最紧张，好房子很快就被租走，看到满意的要尽快决定。

子场景一：联系中介预约看房

背景：你在网上看到一套不错的公寓，打电话约看房。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 You: Hi, I saw your listing on Zillow for the two-bedroom on Oak Street. Is it still available? Hello, I saw your listing on Zillow for the two-bedroom apartment on Oak Street. Is it still available? 你好，我在Zillow上看到你们在Oak街的两居室房源。还在吗？ listing = 房源/挂牌信息。still available = 还有没有/是否已租出。这是租房咨询的标准开场。✅推荐
2 Agent: Let me check... Yes, it is! Would you like to come take a look? Let me check. Yes, it is available. Would you like to schedule a viewing? 我看看……还在的！你想来看看吗？ come take a look = 来看看/来看看房。比 schedule a viewing 更口语。✅推荐
3 You: I'd love to. When's a good time? I would like to. When is a convenient time? 我想看。什么时候方便？ I'd love to = 我很想。✅推荐
4 Agent: I've got an opening tomorrow at 11 AM, or Thursday at 4 PM. Which works better? I have an opening tomorrow at 11 AM or Thursday at 4 PM. Which works better for you? 明天上午11点或周四下午4点有空。哪个更方便？ opening = 空档/可用时段。✅推荐
5 You: Tomorrow at 11 works great. Should I meet you there? Tomorrow at 11 works well. Should I meet you at the property? 明天11点很好。是在那儿碰面吗？ meet you there = 在那儿见。✅推荐
6 Agent: Perfect. I'll meet you at the front entrance. Just bring a valid ID. See you tomorrow! Perfect. I will meet you at the front entrance. Please bring a valid ID. See you tomorrow. 好的。我们在正门入口碰面。带个身份证就行。明天见！ valid ID = 有效身份证件。看房时通常需要出示身份证。✅推荐

子场景二：实地看房

背景：你来到公寓，中介带你参观。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
7 Agent: Come on in! So this is the living room — as you can see, it gets a ton of natural light. Please come in. This is the living room. As you can see, it receives a lot of natural light. 请进！这是客厅——你可以看到，采光特别好。 a ton of = 大量的/很多（夸张口语）。natural light = 自然光。中介的卖点话术。✅推荐
8 You: Nice. Is that a balcony off the living room? Nice. Is that a balcony accessible from the living room? 不错。客厅外面是阳台吗？ off = 连着/从……出去（描述空间关系）。✅推荐
9 Agent: Yeah, it's a decent size — you can easily fit a small table and a couple of chairs out there. Let me show you the kitchen. Yes, it is a good size. You can easily fit a small table and chairs. Let me show you the kitchen. 对，大小还可以——放个小桌子和两把椅子完全没问题。我带你看厨房。 decent size = 大小还不错/还可以。✅推荐
10 You: Oh, the appliances are included? That's great. The appliances are included? That is great. 哦，家电都包含在内吗？太好了。 appliances = 家电（冰箱、烤箱、洗碗机等）。美国出租房通常包含主要家电。✅推荐
11 Agent: Yeah — fridge, stove, dishwasher, and microwave all stay. Washer and dryer are in-unit too, which is a big plus. Yes, the refrigerator, stove, dishwasher, and microwave are all included. The washer and dryer are in-unit, which is a significant advantage. 对——冰箱、灶台、洗碗机、微波炉都有。洗衣机和烘干机也是屋内的，这算是个很大的加分项。 stay = 留下来的/包含在出租中的。in-unit = 屋内的（不是公共的）。big plus = 大大的加分项。✅推荐
12 You: What about parking? Is there a dedicated spot? What about parking? Is there a designated parking spot? 停车呢？有固定车位吗？ dedicated spot = 专属车位/固定车位。✅推荐
13 Agent: You get one covered spot, and there's plenty of guest parking too. Now, the bedrooms are this way... You get one covered parking space, and there is ample guest parking as well. The bedrooms are this way. 有一个带遮盖的车位，而且客人停车位也很多。这边是卧室…… covered spot = 有遮盖的车位（车库或棚子下）。plenty of = 大量的/充足的。✅推荐

子场景三：讨论租金和申请

背景：看房结束，你对这套房子感兴趣，想了解租金和申请流程。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
14 You: I really like the place. What's the asking rent, and what's included? I really like the apartment. What is the listed rent, and what is included? 我很喜欢这个地方。租金是多少，包含什么？ asking rent = 标价租金。what's included = 包含什么（水电暖网等）。✅推荐
15 Agent: It's $1,650 a month. Water and trash are included. Electric, gas, and internet are on you. The rent is $1,650 per month. Water and trash are included. Electricity, gas, and internet are your responsibility. 每月1650美元。水和垃圾处理费包含在内。电、燃气和网络自己付。 on you = 由你承担/你自己负责（= your responsibility）。✅推荐 🇺🇸
16 You: What's the move-in cost look like? What are the total move-in costs? 入住费用大概是多少？ move-in cost = 入住时要付的全部费用（押金+首月+末月租金等）。✅推荐
17 Agent: First month's rent, plus a security deposit of one month's rent. So $3,300 to get you in the door. And there's a $50 application fee. The first month's rent plus a security deposit equal to one month's rent. So $3,300 to move in. There is also a $50 application fee. 第一个月租金加一个月押金。所以3300美元入住。另外还有50美元申请费。 get you in the door = 让你入住。application fee = 申请费。✅推荐
18 You: And the lease term — is it a 12-month lease? What is the lease term? Is it a 12-month lease? 租期呢——是一年的租约吗？ lease term = 租期。✅推荐
19 Agent: Standard 12-month lease. After that, it goes month-to-month. It is a standard 12-month lease. After that, it converts to month-to-month. 标准一年租约。之后按月续。 goes month-to-month = 转为按月续租。✅推荐
20 You: OK, I'd like to go ahead and apply. What do you need from me? OK, I would like to apply. What do you need from me? 好的，我想申请。需要我提供什么？ go ahead and apply = 提交申请。✅推荐
21 Agent: Great! I'll need your last two pay stubs, a copy of your ID, and we'll run a credit and background check. Should have an answer for you within 48 hours. Great. I need your last two pay stubs, a copy of your ID, and we will run a credit and background check. You should have a response within 48 hours. 好的！需要你最近两张工资单、身份证复印件，我们会做信用和背景调查。48小时内给你答复。 pay stubs = 工资单。credit and background check = 信用和背景调查。美国租房的标配流程。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国租房须知

  • credit score：信用分通常需要 650+ 才能顺利租房。信用分低可能需要额外的押金或担保人（co-signer）
  • income requirement：月收入通常需要达到月租金的 3 倍（3x rent）
  • renters insurance：很多公寓要求租客买租客保险（约 $15-30/月）
  • pet policy：养宠物通常需要交 pet deposit（$200-500）和/或 pet rent（$25-50/月）
  • Zillow / Apartments.com / Craigslist：主要找房平台。Craigslist 上要当心骗局（listing scam）
  • competitive market：热门区域的公寓可能同时有十几个人看，看到满意的要当天就申请

🇬🇧 英国租房差异

  • Rightmove / Zoopla / SpareRoom：主要找房平台。SpareRoom 专门用于找合租
  • holding deposit：英国看中房子后先交 holding deposit（通常一周租金）来锁定
  • ** referencing**：英国做背景调查叫 referencing，包括收入、信用、前房东调查
  • guarantor：如果收入不够，需要担保人（通常要求英国本土居民）
  • EPC rating：能源性能证书，英国法律规定房东必须提供
  • council tax band：市政税等级，影响你每月要交的 council tax 金额

看房时要问的关键问题

  • 租金包含什么（what's included）？
  • 押金多少（how much is the deposit）？
  • 最短租期多长（minimum lease term）？
  • 允许养宠物吗（pet policy）？
  • 有停车位吗（parking）？
  • 可以自己装饰/钉钉子吗（can I make changes）？
  • 维修谁负责（who handles maintenance）？
