第8课：家庭服务 Home Services（清洁、快递、上门维修）

场景背景

在美国，家庭服务（home services）种类繁多：房屋清洁（house cleaning）、管道维修（plumbing）、快递送件（package delivery）、除虫（pest control）等等。很多服务可以通过 Yelp、Angi（原 Angie's List）、TaskRabbit 等平台预约。上门服务人员（service provider）通常会给你一个时间窗口（time window），比如 "between 1 and 4 PM"，但经常迟到或提前到。给上门服务人员小费在美国很常见：清洁工、送快递的、搬家工人都可能收到小费。英国的上门服务类似，但小费文化不如美国浓厚。

子场景一：预约和接待家庭清洁服务

背景：你第一次请清洁工来家里打扫，通过网上的介绍预约了。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
1 Cleaner: Hi, I'm Maria from Sparkle Clean. I'm here for the deep clean? Hello, I am Maria from Sparkle Clean. I am here for the deep cleaning service. 你好，我是Sparkle Clean的Maria。我来做深度清洁的。 deep clean = 深度清洁（比 regular clean 更彻底，通常首次清洁选这个）。✅推荐
2 You: Hi Maria, come on in! Let me show you around. Hello Maria, please come in. Let me show you around. 你好Maria，请进！我带你看看。 come on in = 请进（比 come in 更热情随意）。✅推荐
3 Cleaner: So what are the main areas you'd like me to focus on? What are the main areas you would like me to focus on? 你主要想让我重点打扫哪些区域？ focus on = 重点关注/重点做。✅推荐
4 You: The kitchen and bathrooms are the priority. Other than that, just a general wipe-down of everything — dusting, vacuuming, mopping. The kitchen and bathrooms are the priority. Otherwise, just a general cleaning — dusting, vacuuming, and mopping. 厨房和卫生间是重点。其他的就大致擦一下——除尘、吸尘、拖地。 priority = 优先/重点。wipe-down = 擦拭。dusting = 除尘。vacuuming = 吸尘。mopping = 拖地。✅推荐
5 Cleaner: Got it. Do you have a preference for cleaning products? I brought my own, but some clients like me to use theirs. Understood. Do you have a preference for cleaning products? I brought my own, but some clients prefer I use theirs. 明白了。你对清洁用品有偏好吗？我自己带了，但有些客户喜欢用他们的。 preference = 偏好。有些客户对清洁产品过敏或有环保要求。✅推荐
6 You: Yours should be fine. Oh, and please don't use bleach — I've got a cat and it's not great for him. Your products should be fine. Please do not use bleach. I have a cat and it is not safe for him. 你的就行。哦对了，请不要用漂白剂——我有猫，对他不好。 not great for = 对…不太好。提出特殊要求时的委婉表达。✅推荐
7 Cleaner: No problem, I'll stick to pet-safe stuff. I should be done in about three hours. No problem, I will use pet-safe products. I should be finished in about three hours. 没问题，我用宠物安全的清洁剂。大概三个小时能做完。 stick to = 坚持/只用。pet-safe = 对宠物安全的。✅推荐

子场景二：接收快递和处理问题

背景：你在等一个重要快递，快递员送来了但包裹有损坏。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
8 Delivery driver: Hey, I've got a package for Li. Need a signature. Hello, I have a package for Li. I need a signature. 嘿，有个Li的包裹。需要签字。 Need a signature = 需要签名。有些快递需要签收（signature required），有些不需要。✅推荐
9 You: That's me. (signs) Hey, wait a sec — the box is pretty banged up. Can I open it real quick to check? That is me. Wait, the box is quite damaged. Can I open it quickly to check? 我就是。（签字）等等——这个箱子损坏挺严重的。我能快速打开检查一下吗？ banged up = 损坏的/变形的。real quick = 很快/就一下。✅推荐
10 Driver: Sure, go ahead. Sure, go ahead. 当然，请便。 简短回应。✅推荐
11 You: (opens box) Yeah, the item inside is cracked. I'm gonna have to refuse this one. The item inside is cracked. I will have to refuse this delivery. （打开箱子）里面的东西碎了。这个我得拒收。 cracked = 碎裂的/有裂缝的。refuse = 拒收。如果签收时发现损坏可以当场拒收。✅推荐
12 Driver: No worries. I'll mark it as "damaged in transit" and send it back. You'll need to contact the seller for a replacement. No problem. I will mark it as "damaged in transit" and return it. You will need to contact the seller for a replacement. 没问题。我标注"运输中损坏"然后退回去。你需要联系卖家重新发一个。 damaged in transit = 运输中损坏。mark it as = 标注为。✅推荐
13 You: Thanks, I appreciate it. Thank you, I appreciate it. 谢谢，感谢。 礼貌感谢。✅推荐

子场景三：管道维修上门服务

背景：家里厨房水管堵了（clogged drain），约了水管工（plumber）上门。

# 原汁原味口语 正式英语对应 中文翻译 详细解释
14 Plumber: Hey, I'm Mike from ABC Plumbing. Got a call about a clogged drain? Hello, I am Mike from ABC Plumbing. I received a call about a clogged drain. 嘿，我是ABC管道的Mike。接到电话说有下水道堵了？ Got a call about = 接到电话说……✅推荐
15 You: Yeah, come on in. It's the kitchen sink — it's draining really slow, like barely at all. Yes, please come in. It is the kitchen sink. It is draining very slowly, almost not at all. 对，请进。是厨房水槽——排水特别慢，几乎不排水了。 draining slow = 排水慢。barely at all = 几乎完全不。✅推荐
16 Plumber: (checks it out) Yeah, it's pretty backed up. Looks like there's grease buildup in the trap. I'm gonna snake it and see if that clears it up. It is quite backed up. There appears to be grease buildup in the trap. I will snake it to see if that clears it. （检查）嗯，堵得挺厉害。看起来是存水弯里有油污堆积。我用蛇管通一下看看能不能疏通。 backed up = 堵了/不通了。grease buildup = 油污堆积。snake it = 用管道蛇（plumbing snake）疏通。trap = 存水弯（U型管道）。✅推荐
17 You: Sounds good. How long do you think this'll take? Sounds good. How long do you think this will take? 好的。你觉得大概需要多久？ ✅推荐
18 Plumber: Shouldn't be more than 30, 45 minutes tops. If it's a deeper blockage, I might need to run a camera down there, but let's try the snake first. It should not take more than 30 to 45 minutes at most. If it is a deeper blockage, I might need to use a camera, but let us try the snake first. 最多30到45分钟。如果是更深的堵塞，可能需要用摄像头看，但先用蛇管试试。 tops = 最多（放在数字后面）。run a camera down there = 用管道摄像头检查。✅推荐
19 You: OK, go for it. And about the cost — is it a flat rate or by the hour? OK, proceed. Regarding the cost — is it a flat rate or hourly? 好的，开始吧。费用呢——是固定价还是按小时？ flat rate = 统一价/固定价。by the hour = 按小时计费。问清楚费用结构很重要。✅推荐
20 Plumber: It's a flat rate — $125 for a standard drain clearing. If we need to do more, I'll let you know before I do anything. It is a flat rate of $125 for a standard drain clearing. If additional work is needed, I will inform you before proceeding. 固定价——标准疏通125美元。如果需要额外的活，我会在做之前告诉你。 before I do anything = 在我做任何事情之前（= 先征求同意）。靠谱的维修工会提前报价。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国家庭服务文化

  • 预约时间窗口：上门服务通常给一个 2-4 小时的窗口，如 "between 1 and 4 PM"，不保证精确时间
  • 小费：清洁工每次 $20-50（或每次服务的 15-20%），水管工/电工通常不给小费，搬家工人每人 $20-50
  • Yelp 评价：找服务人员时先看 Yelp 评价，低于 4 星的要谨慎
  • "Free estimate"：很多维修工提供免费估价（estimate），先报价再开工
  • 许可要求：水电维修需要 licensed（持证），请确认对方有执照

🇬🇧 英国家庭服务差异

  • 英国人找维修工常用 Checkatrade 或 Rated People（类似 Yelp）
  • 英国清洁工时薪约 £12-20，通常按小时收费
  • 英国快递员几乎不需要签名，包裹通常直接放门口（"left in safe place"）
  • 英国水管工叫 plumber，但报价通常含 VAT（增值税）
  • 英国小费不是必须，但对好的服务可以给 5-10%
