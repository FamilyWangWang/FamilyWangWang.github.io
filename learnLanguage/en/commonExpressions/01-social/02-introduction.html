第二章：自我介绍与认识新朋友（Introductions & Meeting New People）

场景背景

自我介绍是社交的基础。教科书教你 "My name is... I am from... Nice to meet you."，但实际生活中，自我介绍的方式取决于场合——派对上的随意介绍和商务场合的正式介绍完全不同。关键原则是：大多数社交场合越随意越好，商务场合稍微正式但仍要自然。

关键文化差异：

  • 🇺🇸 美国人喜欢快速建立友好关系，初次见面就可能叫你的名字（first name），这不代表不尊重
  • 🇬🇧 英国人更含蓄，初次见面通常保持礼貌距离，熟悉之后才变得随意
  • 英美文化中，握手要坚定有力（firm handshake），软绵绵的握手给人不好的印象
  • 自我介绍时通常伴随简短的 small talk，不要干巴巴地说完名字就停下来

场景一：在朋友的烧烤派对上认识新朋友

背景： 周末下午，你的朋友 John 在家里办烧烤派对（BBQ）。你到了之后，John 把你介绍给他的另一位朋友 Sarah。

John: Hey! You made it! Come on in. Sarah, this is my friend — we work together. And this is Sarah, we go way back.
口语原文 Hey! You made it! Come on in. Sarah, this is my friend — we work together. And this is Sarah, we go way back.
正式说法 Hello! You arrived! Please come in. Sarah, this is my friend — we work at the same company. And this is Sarah, we have known each other for a long time.
中文翻译 嘿！你来了！快进来。Sarah，这是我朋友——我们一起工作的。这位是 Sarah，我们是老朋友了。
详解 ✅推荐使用。"You made it" 是迎接客人时最自然的说法，意思是"你到了/你来了"，不是"你成功了"。"Come on in" 比 "Come in" 更热情友好。"We go way back" 是经典口语表达，意思是"我们是老相识了"。注意介绍别人的顺序：先把男性介绍给女性，或把客人介绍给主人——不过现在这些规矩越来越随意了。

Sarah: Hey, nice to meet you! John's told me a lot about you.
口语原文 Hey, nice to meet you! John's told me a lot about you.
正式说法 Hello, it is nice to meet you. John has told me a lot about you.
中文翻译 嘿，很高兴认识你！John 跟我说了很多关于你的事。
详解 ✅推荐使用。"Nice to meet you" 是最标准的初次见面用语，英美通用。"John's told me a lot about you" 是寒暄的固定说法——即使 John 其实没怎么提过你，这也是一种礼貌的客套话。注意 "John's" = "John has"。

你: All good things, I hope! Nice to meet you too. I'm [你的名字], by the way.
口语原文 All good things, I hope! Nice to meet you too. I'm [你的名字], by the way.
正式说法 I hope they were all positive things! It is nice to meet you as well. My name is [你的名字], by the way.
中文翻译 我希望都是好话！我也很高兴认识你。对了，我叫[你的名字]。
详解 ✅推荐使用。"All good things, I hope" 是经典的幽默回应，意思是"希望你说的是好话"，这是一种轻松的自嘲式玩笑，非常自然。"By the way" 在这里用来补充自己的名字，因为 John 刚才只说了 "my friend" 没说名字。

Sarah: Ha, of course! So, how do you know John? From work?
口语原文 Ha, of course! So, how do you know John? From work?
正式说法 Ha, of course! So, how do you know John? From your workplace?
中文翻译 哈，那是当然！那你是怎么认识 John 的？工作上的？
详解 ✅推荐使用。"How do you know [某人]" 是认识新朋友时的标准提问，比 "Where did you meet" 更开放。"From work?" 是省略句，完整的是 "Are you from work?" 这种省略在口语中非常普遍。

你: Yeah, we've been working together for... gosh, must be five years now? Time flies.
口语原文 Yeah, we've been working together for... gosh, must be five years now? Time flies.
正式说法 Yes, we have been working together for... goodness, it must be five years now. Time passes quickly.
中文翻译 对，我们一起工作... 天哪，得有五年了吧？时间过得真快。
详解 ✅推荐使用。"Gosh" 是表示感叹的温和用语，中年人很常用。注意这里 "must be" 的用法——用"肯定有...了"来表示不确定的时间估算。"Time flies" 是最经典的时间感叹。整句话用省略号和问号体现了口语的思考停顿。

Sarah: Wow, that's a long time! And what do you do, if you don't mind me asking?
口语原文 Wow, that's a long time! And what do you do, if you don't mind me asking?
正式说法 Wow, that is a long time! And what is your profession, if you do not mind me asking?
中文翻译 哇，挺长时间了！你是做什么工作的，如果不介意我问的话？
详解 ✅推荐使用。"What do you do" 是问职业的标准口语表达——比 "What is your job" 或 "What is your profession" 自然得多。注意它省略了 "for a living"。"If you don't mind me asking" 是礼貌的缓冲语，让提问显得不那么冒犯。

你: Not at all. I'm in IT — I handle the tech side of things at our company. What about you?
口语原文 Not at all. I'm in IT — I handle the tech side of things at our company. What about you?
正式说法 Not at all. I work in Information Technology. I manage the technical aspects at our company. What about you?
中文翻译 不介意。我是做IT的——负责我们公司的技术方面。你呢？
详解 ✅推荐使用。"I'm in IT" 比说 "I am an IT engineer" 自然得多——口语中说 "I'm in [行业]" 是介绍工作的最常见方式。"Handle the tech side of things" 是用通俗语言描述工作的好例子。"What about you" 把话题自然地转给对方。

Sarah: Oh nice. I'm actually a teacher — primary school, year four.
口语原文 Oh nice. I'm actually a teacher — primary school, year four.
正式说法 Oh, that is nice. I am actually a teacher — at a primary school, teaching year four.
中文翻译 哦，不错。其实我是个老师——小学，教四年级。
详解 ✅推荐使用。"Oh nice" 是万能的简短回应。"Actually" 在这里用来补充信息，有"其实"的意思。注意英式英语说 "primary school" 和 "year four"，美式会说 "elementary school" 和 "fourth grade"。

你: Oh, that must keep you busy. Kids that age are... a handful, I imagine?
口语原文 Oh, that must keep you busy. Kids that age are... a handful, I imagine?
正式说法 Oh, that must keep you very busy. Children of that age are... difficult to manage, I imagine.
中文翻译 哦，那你肯定很忙。那个年纪的孩子... 挺难搞的吧？
详解 ✅推荐使用。"A handful" 是极其重要的口语表达，意思是"很难对付/很难管"，形容孩子或棘手的事。"I imagine" 是委婉的猜测，比 "I think" 更谦虚。"Must keep you busy" 是一种共情式的评论，而不是直接的提问。

Sarah: Ha, you have no idea! But I love it. Anyway, let me get you a drink. What are you having?
口语原文 Ha, you have no idea! But I love it. Anyway, let me get you a drink. What are you having?
正式说法 Ha, you have no idea! But I love my job. Anyway, let me get you a drink. What would you like to have?
中文翻译 哈，你根本想象不到！但我很喜欢。对了，我给你拿杯喝的。你想喝什么？
详解 ✅推荐使用。"You have no idea" 是强调"你完全不知道"的口语表达，很常用。"Anyway" 在这里用来转换话题。"What are you having" 比 "What do you want to drink" 自然得多——在餐厅、酒吧、派对中都用 "What are you having"。

你: Oh, a beer would be great, thanks. Whatever's cold!
口语原文 Oh, a beer would be great, thanks. Whatever's cold!
正式说法 Oh, a beer would be wonderful, thank you. Any cold beer is fine.
中文翻译 哦，来杯啤酒就好，谢谢。冰的就行！
详解 ✅推荐使用。"A beer would be great" 是礼貌但随意的接受方式。"Whatever's cold" 是不挑剔的随和表达，意思是"什么冰的就给我来什么"。

场景二：在商务会议上介绍自己

背景： 你参加一个行业会议，在茶歇时（coffee break）走到一位参会者旁边，需要自我介绍。

你: Hi there. Mind if I join you?
口语原文 Hi there. Mind if I join you?
正式说法 Hello there. Do you mind if I join you?
中文翻译 你好。介意我坐这儿吗？
详解 ✅推荐使用。"Mind if I join you" 省略了 "Do you"，是请求加入某人时的标准口语表达。在会议、派对等社交场合，这是非常自然的开场方式。

对方: Not at all, go ahead. I'm Greg, by the way. Greg Morrison.
口语原文 Not at all, go ahead. I'm Greg, by the way. Greg Morrison.
正式说法 Not at all, please go ahead. I am Greg, by the way. Greg Morrison.
中文翻译 完全不介意，请坐。对了，我叫 Greg。Greg Morrison。
详解 ✅推荐使用。"Go ahead" 意思是"请便/请做"。先说名字再重复带姓氏的完整名字，这是自我介绍的常见模式——先说亲切的名字，再说正式的全名。

你: Nice to meet you, Greg. I'm [你的名字]. First time at this conference — you been here before?
口语原文 Nice to meet you, Greg. I'm [你的名字]. First time at this conference — you been here before?
正式说法 It is nice to meet you, Greg. I am [你的名字]. This is my first time at this conference. Have you been here before?
中文翻译 很高兴认识你，Greg。我叫[你的名字]。第一次参加这个会议——你以前来过吗？
详解 ✅推荐使用。用对方的名字来称呼是一个好习惯，显得你很用心。"First time" 省略了 "This is my"。"You been here before" 省略了 "Have"。这些省略让对话更流畅自然。

Greg: Yeah, I come every year, actually. It's always worth it. What brings you here today?
口语原文 Yeah, I come every year, actually. It's always worth it. What brings you here today?
正式说法 Yes, I attend every year, actually. It is always worthwhile. What brings you here today?
中文翻译 对，其实我每年都来。一直都很值得。你今天来是为了什么？
详解 ✅推荐使用。"It's always worth it" 意思是"一直都很值得"。"What brings you here today" 是询问对方来意的优雅说法，比 "Why are you here" 礼貌得多，在商务场合非常常用。

你: Oh, you know, just wanted to see what's new in the industry. Keep up with things. And do a bit of networking, I suppose.
口语原文 Oh, you know, just wanted to see what's new in the industry. Keep up with things. And do a bit of networking, I suppose.
正式说法 Oh, you know, I just wanted to see what is new in the industry. To stay informed. And to do some networking, I suppose.
中文翻译 哦，你知道的，就想看看行业里有什么新东西。跟上潮流。还有拓展一下人脉吧。
详解 ✅推荐使用。"Keep up with things" 是"跟上最新动态"的口语说法。"Networking" 是商务社交的专有名词，意思是"建立人脉关系网"。"I suppose" 在句末是一种谦虚的说法，相当于"大概/也许吧"。多个短句组合是口语的典型特征。

Greg: Absolutely. Well, if you're looking to network, you should definitely check out the session this afternoon — the speaker's really good. I can introduce you to a few people afterwards, if you like.
口语原文 Absolutely. Well, if you're looking to network, you should definitely check out the session this afternoon — the speaker's really good. I can introduce you to a few people afterwards, if you like.
正式说法 Absolutely. Well, if you are looking to network, you should definitely attend the session this afternoon — the speaker is very good. I can introduce you to a few people afterwards, if you would like.
中文翻译 完全同意。嗯，如果你想拓展人脉，下午那场一定要去——演讲者非常棒。会后我可以给你介绍几个人，如果你愿意的话。
详解 ✅推荐使用。"Check out" 在这里不是"结账"，而是"去看看"——这是极其常用的短语动词。"If you like" 放在句末是一种非常礼貌的提议方式，比 "Do you want me to" 更不具压力。"Absolutely" 在这里是"完全同意"的意思。

你: That'd be great, I appreciate it. Looking forward to it.
口语原文 That'd be great, I appreciate it. Looking forward to it.
正式说法 That would be great. I appreciate it. I am looking forward to it.
中文翻译 那太好了，谢谢。很期待。
详解 ✅推荐使用。"That'd be great" 是 "That would be great" 的缩略。"I appreciate it" 比 "Thank you" 更真诚，表示你真心感激对方的好意。"Looking forward to it" 是表达期待的常用语。三句短话组合，简洁有力。

场景三：在英国被朋友介绍给他的朋友圈

背景： 你在英国出差，你的英国朋友 Pete 带你去当地一家酒吧，介绍你认识他的朋友们。

Pete: Everyone, this is my mate [你的名字]. He's over from China — here on business. Be nice to him!
口语原文 Everyone, this is my mate [你的名字]. He's over from China — here on business. Be nice to him!
正式说法 Everyone, this is my friend [你的名字]. He has come over from China — he is here on business. Please be nice to him.
中文翻译 大家好，这是我哥们[你的名字]。他从中国来的——来出差的。对人家好点！
详解 🇬🇧✅推荐使用（英式）。"Mate" 是英式最常用的朋友称呼。"He's over from China" 中 "over" 表示从远方来，这是英式英语的特色用法。"Be nice to him" 是开玩笑式的嘱托，让介绍更轻松。

朋友A: Alright, mate? Welcome! Grab a seat. What can I get you — pint?
口语原文 Alright, mate? Welcome! Grab a seat. What can I get you — pint?
正式说法 Hello, friend? Welcome! Please take a seat. What can I get you to drink — would you like a pint of beer?
中文翻译 你好，兄弟？欢迎欢迎！坐吧。给你拿点什么——来杯啤酒？
详解 🇬🇧✅推荐使用（英式）。"Alright, mate" 是英式标准的打招呼方式。"Grab a seat" 意思是"随便坐"，比 "Please sit down" 随意得多。"Pint" 直接省略了 "a" 和 "of beer"，在英国酒吧文化中，"pint" 就是啤酒的代名词。

你: Cheers! I'll have a pint of whatever you're having. Don't want to be the odd one out!
口语原文 Cheers! I'll have a pint of whatever you're having. Don't want to be the odd one out!
正式说法 Thank you! I will have a pint of whatever you are having. I do not want to be the odd one out.
中文翻译 谢谢！你喝什么我就喝什么。不想搞特殊嘛！
详解 🇬🇧✅推荐使用。这里的 "Cheers" 是英式英语中"谢谢"的意思（不是干杯）。"Whatever you're having" 是随和的点酒方式。"The odd one out" 意思是"格格不入/搞特殊的人"。注意 "I'll" 和 "Don't" 的缩略——口语中几乎总是用缩略形式。

朋友B: Ha, good man! So what brings you to our lovely little town then? Just work, or are you getting some sightseeing in as well?
口语原文 Ha, good man! So what brings you to our lovely little town then? Just work, or are you getting some sightseeing in as well?
正式说法 Ha, good man! So what brings you to our lovely little town? Just for work, or are you doing some sightseeing as well?
中文翻译 哈，好样的！那你来我们这可爱的小镇干嘛呢？只是出差，还是也顺便逛逛？
详解 🇬🇧✅推荐使用（英式）。"Good man" 是英式英语中对男性表示赞许的常用语，相当于"好样的/真不错"。"Our lovely little town" 是英国人典型的自嘲式幽默——即使是个大城市也会这么说。"Getting some sightseeing in" 意思是"挤出时间观光"，"get... in" 表示在忙碌中抽空做某事。"Then" 放在句末是英式英语的口语习惯。

你: Bit of both, actually. Meetings during the day, but Pete's been showing me around in the evenings. It's been brilliant.
口语原文 Bit of both, actually. Meetings during the day, but Pete's been showing me around in the evenings. It's been brilliant.
正式说法 A bit of both, actually. I have meetings during the day, but Pete has been showing me around in the evenings. It has been wonderful.
中文翻译 其实两样都有。白天开会，但 Pete 晚上带我到处逛。真的挺好的。
详解 🇬🇧✅推荐使用。"Bit of both" 省略了 "A"，意思是"两个都有点"。"Showing me around" 是"带某人参观/到处看看"的标准说法。"Brilliant" 在英式英语中经常用来表示"很棒/很好"，不只是"才华横溢"的意思。

朋友A: Oh, Pete knows all the best spots. He's a proper local, this one.
口语原文 Oh, Pete knows all the best spots. He's a proper local, this one.
正式说法 Oh, Pete knows all the best places. He is a true local person, this one.
中文翻译 哦，Pete 知道所有最好的地方。他可是真正的本地通。
详解 🇬🇧✅推荐使用（英式）。"Spots" 在这里指"地方/去处"。"Proper" 在英式英语中常用来强调"真正的/地道的"，"a proper local" 就是"真正的本地人"。"This one" 放在句末指代 Pete，是英式口语中常见的语序。

年龄与场合注意事项

你这个年龄段（50岁左右男性）

推荐使用：

  • ✅ "Nice to meet you" —— 最经典、不分年龄的初次见面用语
  • ✅ "What do you do?" —— 询问职业的标准方式，你问别人也很自然
  • ✅ "I'm in [行业]" —— 最简洁的职业介绍方式
  • ✅ "Mind if I join you?" —— 加入别人时礼貌又自然

了解即可：

  • 👂 "Pleased to meet you" —— 比 "Nice to meet you" 稍正式，适合商务场合
  • 👂 "How do you do?" —— 非常正式，几乎只在英国王室等极端正式场合使用

避免使用：

  • ❌ "Let me introduce myself" —— 听起来太正式、太生硬
  • ❌ "My name is [名字], I am [年龄] years old" —— 英语文化中自我介绍不说年龄
  • ❌ "What is your job?" —— 听起来像面试官提问，太生硬
  • ❌ 握手时用力过猛或完全不用力 —— 要坚定但适度

文化提示

  1. 名字的使用：英美人初次见面就直呼 first name 是正常的。不要等对方说"你可以叫我 John"——直接叫就行。只有极其正式的场合才用 Mr./Mrs. + 姓氏。

  2. 个人空间：🇺🇸 美国人交谈时保持约一臂距离；🇬🇧 英国人可能稍微远一点。不要站得太近，也不要在说话时有太多肢体接触。

  3. 名片交换：不像中国/日本，英美人交换名片没有那么正式。不需要双手递上，也不需要仔细端详。简单递过去，看一眼收起来就好。

  4. 闲聊（small talk）很重要：自我介绍之后，立刻进入 small talk（天气、周末计划、会议感受等）是很自然的。不要在介绍完之后就沉默了。
