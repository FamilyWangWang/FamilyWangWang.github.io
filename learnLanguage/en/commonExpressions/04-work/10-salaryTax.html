第十章：薪资、税务与福利 (Salary, Tax & Benefits)

场景背景

在美国，薪资和福利是职场生活的重要组成部分。美国职场有一些独特的制度：401(k)退休计划、健康保险（通常由雇主提供）、弹性支出账户（FSA）、股票期权（stock options）等。美国实行预扣税制度（withholding tax），工资单（paystub/paycheck）上会列出各种扣除项。了解这些术语和制度对于在美国工作的人非常重要。

文化要点：

  • 在美国，与同事讨论具体薪资是社交禁忌，但与HR或经理讨论是完全正常的
  • 401(k) 是最重要的退休储蓄工具，很多公司有匹配（match）政策
  • 美国没有全民医保，健康保险主要通过雇主获得
  • 工资单上的 gross pay（税前）和 net pay（税后）差距很大
  • 英国有NI（National Insurance）和 workplace pension

场景一：HR解释薪资与福利 (HR Explaining Compensation & Benefits)

人物： 李明、Mark（HR经理）

Mark： Alright, Li Ming, let's go over your compensation package. So your base salary is $95,000 a year, paid biweekly. That works out to about $3,653 per paycheck before taxes — that's your gross pay. After all the deductions, your net pay — what actually hits your bank account — will be around $2,700 give or take.
口语 Alright, Li Ming, let's go over your compensation package. So your base salary is $95,000 a year, paid biweekly. That works out to about $3,653 per paycheck before taxes — that's your gross pay. After all the deductions, your net pay — what actually hits your bank account — will be around $2,700 give or take.
正式 Let us review your compensation package. Your base salary is $95,000 annually, paid biweekly. That calculates to approximately $3,653 per paycheck before taxes — your gross pay. After all deductions, your net pay — the amount deposited into your bank account — will be approximately $2,700, more or less.
中文 好，李明，我们来过一下你的薪资套餐。你的基本年薪是95,000美元，每两周发一次。算下来每张工资单税前大约$3,653——这是你的毛收入。扣除所有项目后，你的净收入——实际到账的——大约$2,700左右。
解说 "Compensation package" = 薪资套餐（包含工资、福利、奖金等）。"Works out to" = 算下来是/结果是。"Gross pay" = 税前收入/毛收入。"Net pay" = 税后收入/净收入。"Deductions" = 扣除项（税、保险等）。"Give or take" = 大约/左右，是口语中表示估算的说法。"Hits your bank account" = 到账（形象化的说法）。✅推荐

Li Ming： OK, and what exactly gets deducted? I know there's federal tax and state tax...
口语 OK, and what exactly gets deducted? I know there's federal tax and state tax...
正式 OK, and what exactly is deducted? I know there is federal tax and state tax...
中文 好，具体扣什么项目？我知道有联邦税和州税……
解说 "Gets deducted" = 被扣除。美国工资单的扣除项（deductions）包括：联邦所得税、州所得税、Social Security（社会保障）、Medicare（医疗保险）、健康保险费、401(k) 缴纳等。✅推荐

Mark： Right. So the big ones are federal income tax, state tax — and since we're in a state with income tax, that's another chunk. Then there's FICA, which is your Social Security and Medicare contributions — that's about 7.65% of your gross. On top of that, your share of the health insurance premium, which for the plan you picked is about $280 a month, and your 401(k) contribution.
口语 Right. So the big ones are federal income tax, state tax — and since we're in a state with income tax, that's another chunk. Then there's FICA, which is your Social Security and Medicare contributions — that's about 7.65% of your gross. On top of that, your share of the health insurance premium, which for the plan you picked is about $280 a month, and your 401(k) contribution.
正式 Correct. The primary deductions are federal income tax and state tax. Since we are in a state with income tax, that is another significant amount. Then there is FICA, which covers your Social Security and Medicare contributions — approximately 7.65% of your gross pay. Additionally, your portion of the health insurance premium, which for your selected plan is approximately $280 per month, and your 401(k) retirement contribution.
中文 对。大项是联邦所得税和州税——因为我们在有州所得税的州，那又是另一笔。然后是FICA，就是你的社会保障和医疗保险缴纳——大约是税前的7.65%。除此之外，你的健康保险费份额，你选的计划大约每月$280，还有你的401(k)缴纳。
解说 "The big ones" = 大项/主要的部分。"Another chunk" = 另一大笔。"FICA" = Federal Insurance Contributions Act，联邦保险贡献法案，包括Social Security和Medicare。"On top of that" = 除此之外。"Premium" = 保险费。"Your share" = 你的份额（雇主通常支付一部分保险费）。✅推荐

Li Ming： Yeah, about the 401(k) — I'm a little confused about how that works. And I heard something about a company match?
口语 Yeah, about the 401(k) — I'm a little confused about how that works. And I heard something about a company match?
正式 Regarding the 401(k) plan — I am somewhat confused about how it operates. I also heard about a company matching contribution?
中文 对，关于401(k)——我有点不太明白怎么运作的。我听说了公司匹配的事？
解说 401(k) 是美国最重要的退休储蓄工具，名称来自税法第401(k)条款。"Company match" = 公司匹配，即公司按你的缴纳比例额外存入一定金额（相当于白送的钱）。不懂就问是非常正确的态度。✅推荐

Mark： Great question. So the 401(k) is a retirement savings account — the money goes in pre-tax, which means it lowers your taxable income. The company matches 100% of your contribution up to 5% of your salary. So if you put in 5%, the company kicks in another 5% — that's essentially free money. It'd be crazy not to take full advantage of it, honestly.
口语 Great question. So the 401(k) is a retirement savings account — the money goes in pre-tax, which means it lowers your taxable income. The company matches 100% of your contribution up to 5% of your salary. So if you put in 5%, the company kicks in another 5% — that's essentially free money. It'd be crazy not to take full advantage of it, honestly.
正式 Great question. The 401(k) is a retirement savings account. Contributions are made with pre-tax income, which reduces your taxable income. The company matches 100% of your contributions up to 5% of your salary. If you contribute 5%, the company adds another 5%. That is essentially free money. It would be unwise not to maximize this benefit.
中文 好问题。401(k)是一个退休储蓄账户——钱是税前存入的，这意味着降低了你的应税收入。公司100%匹配你的缴纳，最高到工资的5%。也就是说如果你存5%，公司再给你5%——这基本上就是白送的钱。不充分利用就太傻了，说实话。
解说 "Pre-tax" = 税前的。"Taxable income" = 应税收入。"Matches 100%" = 100%匹配。"Kicks in" = 投入/贡献（口语化）。"Free money" = 白送的钱（金融顾问常说这个词）。"Take full advantage of" = 充分利用。"It'd be crazy not to" = 不这样做就太傻了。✅推荐

Li Ming： OK, that makes sense. I'll definitely do the full 5%. And what about health insurance — what's the difference between the plans?
口语 OK, that makes sense. I'll definitely do the full 5%. And what about health insurance — what's the difference between the plans?
正式 That makes sense. I will definitely contribute the full 5%. Regarding health insurance, what is the difference between the available plans?
中文 好，这我明白了。我肯定存满5%。那健康保险呢——各计划之间有什么区别？
解说 "Makes sense" = 有道理/明白了。"Do the full 5%" 中的 "do" 是万能动词。美国的健康保险计划通常有PPO（Preferred Provider Organization，自由就医）和HDHP（High Deductible Health Plan，高免赔额计划）等选择。✅推荐

Mark： So we offer two main plans. The PPO is more expensive per paycheck — higher premium — but you've got more flexibility with doctors and a lower deductible. The HDHP has a lower premium, but you pay more out of pocket before insurance kicks in. The upside is you can pair it with an HSA — a Health Savings Account — which is also tax-advantaged. A lot of younger, healthier folks go with the HDHP.
口语 So we offer two main plans. The PPO is more expensive per paycheck — higher premium — but you've got more flexibility with doctors and a lower deductible. The HDHP has a lower premium, but you pay more out of pocket before insurance kicks in. The upside is you can pair it with an HSA — a Health Savings Account — which is also tax-advantaged. A lot of younger, healthier folks go with the HDHP.
正式 We offer two main plans. The PPO is more expensive per paycheck with a higher premium, but offers more flexibility with doctors and a lower deductible. The HDHP has a lower premium, but you pay more out of pocket before insurance coverage begins. The advantage is that you can pair it with an HSA — a Health Savings Account — which also offers tax benefits. Many younger, healthier employees choose the HDHP.
中文 我们提供两个主要计划。PPO每张工资单更贵——保费更高——但看医生更灵活，免赔额更低。HDHP保费更低，但在保险生效前你要自付更多。好处是可以搭配HSA——健康储蓄账户——也有税务优惠。很多年轻、身体好的人选HDHP。
解说 "Premium" = 保费。"Deductible" = 免赔额（自己先付的金额）。"Out of pocket" = 自付费用。"Insurance kicks in" = 保险开始生效/报销。"Upside" = 好处/优点。"Tax-advantaged" = 有税务优惠的。"Folks" = 人们（口语中对"people"的替代）。✅推荐

Li Ming： I think I'll go with the PPO — I'd rather pay a bit more upfront and have peace of mind.
口语 I think I'll go with the PPO — I'd rather pay a bit more upfront and have peace of mind.
正式 I think I will choose the PPO plan. I would prefer to pay somewhat more upfront and have peace of mind.
中文 我想选PPO——我宁愿 upfront 多付一点，买个安心。
解说 "Go with" = 选择。"I'd rather" = 我宁愿。"Upfront" = 预先/提前。"Peace of mind" = 安心/放心。这是做选择时的常见表达方式。✅推荐

Mark： Solid choice. Now, one more thing — open enrollment happens every November. That's when you can make changes to your benefits. Outside of that, you can only make changes if you have a qualifying life event — like getting married, having a baby, or changing jobs. Oh, and don't forget to designate a beneficiary for your life insurance and 401(k).
口语 Solid choice. Now, one more thing — open enrollment happens every November. That's when you can make changes to your benefits. Outside of that, you can only make changes if you have a qualifying life event — like getting married, having a baby, or changing jobs. Oh, and don't forget to designate a beneficiary for your life insurance and 401(k).
正式 Good choice. One more item — open enrollment occurs every November. That is when you can modify your benefit selections. Otherwise, changes are only permitted during a qualifying life event, such as marriage, the birth of a child, or a job change. Also, please remember to designate a beneficiary for your life insurance and 401(k) accounts.
中文 不错的选择。还有一件事——每年11月是开放注册期。那时候可以更改你的福利选择。除此之外，只有在符合条件的重大生活事件时才能更改——比如结婚、生孩子、或换工作。对了，别忘了为你的人寿保险和401(k)指定受益人。
解说 "Open enrollment" = 开放注册/开放投保期（美国保险制度的特色）。"Qualifying life event" = 符合条件的重大生活事件。"Designate a beneficiary" = 指定受益人。这些是美国福利制度的关键概念。✅推荐

场景二：同事间聊税务与工资 (Talking Taxes with a Colleague)

人物： 李明、Brian（同事）

Brian： Hey, did you get your W-2 yet? I'm trying to get my taxes done early this year for once.
口语 Hey, did you get your W-2 yet? I'm trying to get my taxes done early this year for once.
正式 Have you received your W-2 form yet? I am attempting to complete my tax filing early this year for a change.
中文 嘿，你收到W-2了吗？我今年想破例早点报税。
解说 "W-2" 是美国的年度工资收入报表，雇主必须在1月底前发放。"Get my taxes done" = 完成报税。"For once" = 破例一次/难得一次。美国的报税截止日期通常是4月15日。✅推荐

Li Ming： Yeah, got it last week. This is my first time filing taxes in the US though — it's a bit overwhelming. Do you use an accountant or do it yourself?
口语 Yeah, got it last week. This is my first time filing taxes in the US though — it's a bit overwhelming. Do you use an accountant or do it yourself?
正式 Yes, I received it last week. However, this is my first time filing taxes in the United States — it is somewhat overwhelming. Do you use an accountant or file independently?
中文 对，上周收到的。不过这是我第一次在美国报税——有点头大。你是找会计师还是自己报？
解说 "Filing taxes" = 报税/申报税务。"Overwhelming" = 令人不知所措的/头大的。美国税法确实复杂，很多美国人也会感到头疼。"Do it yourself" / "DIY" 是自己做的意思。✅推荐

Brian： I use TurboTax — it walks you through everything step by step. Pretty user-friendly. But honestly, since it's your first time and you might have some international stuff to deal with, you might wanna talk to a CPA. They'll make sure you're not leaving money on the table — or worse, missing something and getting hit with a penalty.
口语 I use TurboTax — it walks you through everything step by step. Pretty user-friendly. But honestly, since it's your first time and you might have some international stuff to deal with, you might wanna talk to a CPA. They'll make sure you're not leaving money on the table — or worse, missing something and getting hit with a penalty.
正式 I use TurboTax — it guides you through the process step by step. It is quite user-friendly. However, since this is your first time and you may have international tax considerations, you might want to consult a Certified Public Accountant. They will ensure you are not forfeiting eligible deductions or, worse, missing required filings and incurring penalties.
中文 我用TurboTax——它一步步带你操作。挺用户友好的。但说实话，既然你是第一次而且可能涉及国际税务问题，你可能想找个注册会计师聊聊。他们会确保你没有遗漏可以省的钱——或者更糟，漏报了什么东西然后被罚款。
解说 "Walks you through" = 一步步教你。"CPA" = Certified Public Accountant，注册会计师。"Leaving money on the table" = 白白浪费钱/没有利用应得的福利。"Get hit with a penalty" = 被罚款。TurboTax是美国最常用的DIY报税软件。✅推荐

Li Ming： Good advice. What about the standard deduction versus itemizing? I've been reading up on it but I'm still not sure which one applies to me.
口语 Good advice. What about the standard deduction versus itemizing? I've been reading up on it but I'm still not sure which one applies to me.
正式 Good advice. What about the standard deduction versus itemized deductions? I have been researching this topic but remain uncertain which applies to my situation.
中文 好建议。标准扣除和逐项扣除呢？我一直在研究但还是不确定哪个适合我。
解说 "Standard deduction" = 标准扣除额（固定金额，不需证明）。"Itemizing" = 逐项列报（把各项扣除一一列出）。"Reading up on" = 研究/阅读了解。2024年美国单身标准扣除额约为$14,600。大多数工薪阶层用标准扣除更划算。✅推荐

Brian： Honestly, unless you own a home with a big mortgage or have massive medical expenses, the standard deduction is usually the way to go. It's simpler and most people come out ahead with it. But again, a CPA can look at your specific situation and tell you for sure.
口语 Honestly, unless you own a home with a big mortgage or have massive medical expenses, the standard deduction is usually the way to go. It's simpler and most people come out ahead with it. But again, a CPA can look at your specific situation and tell you for sure.
正式 Honestly, unless you own a home with a substantial mortgage or have significant medical expenses, the standard deduction is typically the better option. It is simpler and most taxpayers benefit more from it. However, a CPA can review your specific situation and provide a definitive recommendation.
中文 说实话，除非你有大额房贷或者巨额医疗费用，标准扣除通常是更好的选择。更简单，而且大多数人用这个更划算。但还是那句话，CPA可以看你的具体情况给你确切答案。
解说 "The way to go" = 更好的选择/应该走的路。"Come out ahead" = 更划算/赚了。"Tell you for sure" = 给你确切答案。"Massive" = 巨大的。这些是美国人讨论税务时的典型对话。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国薪资税务要点

  1. 税前 vs 税后：在美国，$95,000年薪到手大约$65,000-$70,000，差距约30%
  2. 401(k) 一定要参加：公司match的部分相当于每年白送你几千美元，不参加就是浪费
  3. 报税截止日：通常为每年4月15日，可以申请延期到10月15日
  4. W-2 vs 1099：W-2是正式员工的收入报表，1099是合同工/自由职业者的收入报表
  5. SSN（Social Security Number）：在美国工作必须有社会安全号，用于报税和信用记录

🇬🇧 英国薪资税务对比

  1. 英国有PAYE（Pay As You Earn）系统，雇主直接代扣税款
  2. 英国的National Insurance（NI）相当于美国的FICA
  3. 英国的退休金叫做workplace pension，也有雇主匹配
  4. 英国的税单叫P60（年度收入总结），报税相对简单
  5. 英国的个人所得税有个人免税额（Personal Allowance），2024/25年为£12,570
  6. 英国人说 "salary" 而不是 "wages"（wages在英式英语中有时暗示低收入工作）
  7. 英国没有州税的概念，但有Council Tax（地方税）

📊 美国工资单常见项目

  • Gross Pay：税前收入
  • Federal Tax：联邦所得税
  • State Tax：州所得税（部分州没有，如德州、佛罗里达州）
  • Social Security：社会保障（6.2%）
  • Medicare：医疗保险（1.45%）
  • Health Insurance：健康保险费
  • 401(k)：退休储蓄
  • Net Pay：到手收入

❌ 避免的错误

  1. 不要和同事讨论具体薪资数字——这在大多数公司是禁忌
  2. 不要不参加401(k)——至少要拿到公司的match
  3. 不要逾期报税——罚款和利息会累积
  4. 不要忽视健康保险的开放注册期——错过要等一年
  5. 不要把SSN告诉不相关的人——身份盗窃在美国是严重问题
  6. 不要以为退税（tax refund）是"赚了"——那是你多交了钱被退回来
