第九章：远程办公 (Remote Work & Video Calls)

场景背景

2020年后，远程办公（remote work）和混合办公（hybrid work）成为了美国职场的常态。Zoom、Microsoft Teams、Google Meet等视频会议工具成为日常工作中不可或缺的一部分。远程办公带来了新的沟通挑战：网络问题、麦克风故障、屏幕共享出问题、在家里被打扰等。掌握远程办公的英语表达是现代职场人的必备技能。

文化要点：

  • "Can you see my screen?" 是远程办公中说得最多的话
  • 美国2024-2025年流行 RTO（Return to Office）回归办公室的趋势，但很多公司仍保留远程选项
  • 远程会议中 muting/unmuting 是基本礼仪
  • 英国远程办公接受度也很高，很多公司采用"每周三天在办公室"的混合模式

场景一：视频会议开场与技术问题 (Video Call Start & Tech Issues)

人物： 李明、Karen（经理）、Rachel（同事）、Brian（同事）——视频会议中

Karen： OK, I think everyone's here. Brian, you're on mute. Can you unmute yourself?
口语 OK, I think everyone's here. Brian, you're on mute. Can you unmute yourself?
正式 OK, I believe everyone has joined. Brian, your microphone is muted. Could you please unmute yourself?
中文 好，我想大家都到了。Brian，你静音了。能取消静音吗？
解说 "You're on mute" = 你静音了，这可能是2020年以来全球说得最多的一句话。"Unmute yourself" = 取消静音。这些已经成为远程办公的标准词汇。✅推荐

Brian： Oh, sorry about that. Can you hear me now? How about now? OK, there we go. Sorry, my mic was acting up.
口语 Oh, sorry about that. Can you hear me now? How about now? OK, there we go. Sorry, my mic was acting up.
正式 Oh, I apologize for that. Can you hear me now? How about now? OK, there we go. Sorry, my microphone was malfunctioning.
中文 哦，抱歉。现在能听到吗？现在呢？好，行了。不好意思，我的麦克风出了点问题。
解说 "Acting up" = 出毛病了/不太正常，是描述设备问题的极常用口语。"There we go" = 好了/行了（问题解决后的感叹）。远程会议中测试音量是标准流程。✅推荐

Rachel： We can hear you loud and clear. Although your video is a bit grainy — might want to check your lighting.
口语 We can hear you loud and clear. Although your video is a bit grainy — might want to check your lighting.
正式 We can hear you perfectly. However, your video quality is somewhat poor — you may want to check your lighting.
中文 听得很清楚。不过你的视频有点模糊——可能要检查一下灯光。
解说 "Loud and clear" = 非常清楚。"Grainy" = 画面颗粒感重/模糊的。"Might want to" = 你可能需要（委婉建议）。远程办公中互相提醒技术问题很正常。✅推荐

Li Ming： Hey guys, sorry I'm a minute late — I had a hard time getting in. It kept saying "waiting for host to start the meeting."
口语 Hey guys, sorry I'm a minute late — I had a hard time getting in. It kept saying "waiting for host to start the meeting."
正式 Hello everyone, I apologize for joining one minute late. I had difficulty connecting. The system kept displaying "waiting for host to start the meeting."
中文 嘿大家，抱歉晚了一分钟——我进不来。它一直显示"等待主持人开始会议"。
解说 "Had a hard time getting in" = 很难加入进来（指加入视频会议）。"It kept saying" = 它一直显示。远程会议中迟到一两分钟完全正常，简单道个歉就行。✅推荐

Karen： No worries. My bad — I forgot to enable the waiting room. Alright, now that everyone's here, let's get started. Oh, and Rachel, there's a dog barking in the background — you might wanna mute when you're not speaking.
口语 No worries. My bad — I forgot to enable the waiting room. Alright, now that everyone's here, let's get started. Oh, and Rachel, there's a dog barking in the background — you might wanna mute when you're not speaking.
正式 No worries. My mistake — I forgot to enable the waiting room. Alright, now that everyone is here, let us begin. Rachel, there is a dog barking in the background — you may want to mute yourself when you are not speaking.
中文 没事。我的锅——我忘了开等候室。好，大家都到了，开始吧。对了，Rachel，你那边有狗叫——不说话的时候你可能要静音。
解说 "My bad" = 我的错/我的锅，是非常口语化的认错方式。"Waiting room" 是视频会议中等候室功能。"Background" = 背景（指背景噪音）。提醒别人静音时用 "might wanna" 比 "you should" 委婉得多。✅推荐

Rachel： Oh, sorry! That's my neighbor's dog. Can't do much about it. I'll stay on mute.
口语 Oh, sorry! That's my neighbor's dog. Can't do much about it. I'll stay on mute.
正式 Oh, I apologize! That is my neighbor's dog. I cannot do much about it. I will remain muted.
中文 哦，抱歉！那是我邻居家的狗。我也没办法。我会保持静音。
解说 "Can't do much about it" = 没什么办法/无能为力。"Stay on mute" = 保持静音。远程办公中，家里有噪音是很常见的，大家都很理解。✅推荐

场景二：屏幕共享与远程协作 (Screen Sharing & Remote Collaboration)

Li Ming： Alright, let me share my screen. Can everyone see that? No? Give me a sec... OK, how about now? You should be seeing the Q4 dashboard.
口语 Alright, let me share my screen. Can everyone see that? No? Give me a sec... OK, how about now? You should be seeing the Q4 dashboard.
正式 Alright, let me share my screen. Can everyone see it? No? Please give me a moment. OK, how about now? You should be viewing the Q4 dashboard.
中文 好，让我分享一下屏幕。大家能看到吗？不能？等一下……好，现在呢？你们应该能看到Q4的仪表盘了。
解说 "Share my screen" = 分享我的屏幕。"Give me a sec" = 等一下（sec = second）。"You should be seeing..." = 你们应该能看到...。远程会议中屏幕共享出问题是家常便饭，不要太紧张。✅推荐

Brian： Yep, got it. Looks good. Can you zoom in on that chart in the top right? It's a bit small on my end.
口语 Yep, got it. Looks good. Can you zoom in on that chart in the top right? It's a bit small on my end.
正式 Yes, I can see it. It looks good. Could you zoom in on the chart in the top right corner? It is somewhat small on my screen.
中文 是的，看到了。看起来不错。能把右上角的图表放大吗？我这边看起来有点小。
解说 "Got it" = 看到了/明白了。"Zoom in" = 放大。"On my end" = 在我这边的屏幕上。远程会议中，不同设备看到的画面大小不同，这种请求很常见。✅推荐

Li Ming： Sure, one sec. There you go. Better?
口语 Sure, one sec. There you go. Better?
正式 Sure, one moment. There you go. Is that better?
中文 当然，等一下。好了。这样好些吗？
解说 "One sec" = 等一下（比 "one moment" 口语化）。"There you go" = 好了/给你。"Better?" = 好些了吗？简洁高效的沟通。✅推荐

Rachel： Much better, thanks. So what's the story with the dip in week three? That looks concerning.
口语 Much better, thanks. So what's the story with the dip in week three? That looks concerning.
正式 Much better, thank you. What is the explanation for the decline in week three? That appears concerning.
中文 好多了，谢谢。那第三周那个下降是怎么回事？看起来有点问题。
解说 "What's the story with..." = ...是怎么回事/...是什么情况，是非常口语化的询问方式。"Dip" = 下降/凹陷。"Concerning" = 令人担忧的。✅推荐

Li Ming： Good catch. So that was actually due to a server outage — it was down for about six hours on a Tuesday night. We've since added redundancy, so it shouldn't happen again. I can send you the incident report if you'd like.
口语 Good catch. So that was actually due to a server outage — it was down for about six hours on a Tuesday night. We've since added redundancy, so it shouldn't happen again. I can send you the incident report if you'd like.
正式 Good observation. That was actually caused by a server outage — the server was down for approximately six hours on a Tuesday evening. We have since implemented redundancy measures, so it should not happen again. I can send you the incident report if you wish.
中文 好问题。那其实是因为服务器宕机——某个周二晚上停了大概六个小时。我们已经加了冗余备份，所以不应该再发生了。需要的话我可以把事故报告发给你。
解说 "Due to" = 因为/由于。"Server outage" = 服务器宕机/中断。"Added redundancy" = 增加了冗余备份。"Incident report" = 事故报告。远程汇报时，准备好回答数据异常问题是基本功。✅推荐

场景三：远程工作日常对话 (Remote Work Daily Chat)

人物： 李明、Rachel（Slack/Teams消息）

Rachel（消息）： Hey, you got a sec? Quick call?
口语 Hey, you got a sec? Quick call?
正式 Hello, do you have a moment? Could we have a brief call?
中文 嘿，有空吗？快速打个电话？
解说 远程办公中，Slack/Teams上的简短沟通极常见。"Got a sec?" = 有空吗？"Quick call?" = 快速打个电话？有时打字说不清楚，直接打电话更高效。✅推荐

Li Ming（消息）： Sure, give me 5. Just wrapping up something. I'll ring you.
口语 Sure, give me 5. Just wrapping up something. I'll ring you.
正式 Sure, please give me five minutes. I am just finishing something. I will call you.
中文 当然，给我5分钟。我刚收尾一件事。我打给你。
解说 "Give me 5" = 给我5分钟。"Wrapping up" = 收尾/快做完了。"Ring you" = 打给你（🇬🇧 英国人常用 "ring"，美国人更常用 "call"）。✅推荐

（电话接通）

Rachel： Hey, thanks for hopping on. So I'm having trouble with the shared drive — I can't access the client folder. It keeps saying "access denied." Any idea what's going on?
口语 Hey, thanks for hopping on. So I'm having trouble with the shared drive — I can't access the client folder. It keeps saying "access denied." Any idea what's going on?
正式 Hey, thank you for joining the call. I am experiencing difficulty with the shared drive — I cannot access the client folder. It keeps displaying "access denied." Do you know what is happening?
中文 嘿，谢谢你接电话。我的共享盘有点问题——打不开客户文件夹。一直显示"访问被拒绝"。你知道怎么回事吗？
解说 "Hopping on" = 上线/接通电话。"Having trouble with" = ...有问题。"Access denied" = 访问被拒绝。"Any idea what's going on?" = 知道怎么回事吗？远程办公中IT问题是日常。✅推荐

Li Ming： Oh yeah, IT migrated some folders over the weekend. You probably just need to request access again. Just submit a ticket through the IT portal — it usually gets resolved within a couple of hours. Want me to send you the link?
口语 Oh yeah, IT migrated some folders over the weekend. You probably just need to request access again. Just submit a ticket through the IT portal — it usually gets resolved within a couple of hours. Want me to send you the link?
正式 Yes, the IT department migrated some folders over the weekend. You likely need to request access again. Simply submit a ticket through the IT portal — it is usually resolved within a few hours. Would you like me to send you the link?
中文 哦对，IT周末迁移了一些文件夹。你可能只需要重新申请权限。在IT门户提交个工单就行——通常几个小时内就解决了。要我发你链接吗？
解说 "Migrated" = 迁移了。"Submit a ticket" = 提交工单（IT问题报告的标准流程）。"IT portal" = IT门户/网站。"Gets resolved" = 被解决。在远程环境中帮助同事解决技术问题，是建立信任的好机会。✅推荐

Rachel： That'd be great. Thanks, you're a lifesaver. Ugh, remote work problems, right? Some days I feel like I spend half my time fighting with technology.
口语 That'd be great. Thanks, you're a lifesaver. Ugh, remote work problems, right? Some days I feel like I spend half my time fighting with technology.
正式 That would be great. Thank you, you are very helpful. Remote work challenges, right? Some days I feel like I spend half my time dealing with technology issues.
中文 太好了。谢谢，你是救星。呃，远程办公的烦恼对吧？有些天我觉得一半的时间都在跟技术问题搏斗。
解说 "You're a lifesaver" = 你是救星/你帮了大忙。"Fighting with technology" = 跟技术搏斗（幽默的夸张说法）。远程办公中互相吐槽技术问题是拉近距离的方式。✅推荐

Li Ming： Tell me about it! Well, at least we don't have to commute. Silver linings, right?
口语 Tell me about it! Well, at least we don't have to commute. Silver linings, right?
正式 I completely agree! Well, at least we do not have to commute. Every cloud has a silver lining, right?
中文 可不是嘛！不过至少不用通勤了。因祸得福嘛，对吧？
解说 "Tell me about it!" = 可不是嘛/我深有同感（表示强烈认同，不是让你讲）。"Silver linings" = 乌云背后的银边/因祸得福，源自 "every cloud has a silver lining"。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国远程办公文化

  1. 摄像头礼仪：很多公司鼓励开摄像头（camera on），但不是强制的。开会时开摄像头表示你在认真参与
  2. 背景：可以用虚拟背景（virtual background）或模糊背景（blur background），保持专业
  3. 着装：上半身正式即可，但不要在站起来时暴露睡裤（这有真实案例）
  4. 时间管理：远程办公容易模糊工作和生活的界限，设定清晰的工作时间
  5. Slack/Teams礼仪：不要期望秒回，异步沟通是远程工作的常态

🇬🇧 英国远程办公对比

  1. 英国远程办公中更注重 "camera on" 文化
  2. 英国人说 "Shall we jump on a call?" = 我们打个电话吧？
  3. 英国公司普遍采用 "three days in, two days remote" 的混合模式
  4. 英国的远程办公邮件通常更正式
  5. 英国人说 "I'll ping you on Teams" = 我在Teams上找你

💻 远程会议最佳实践

  1. 提前2-3分钟加入：不要踩点到
  2. 静音规则：不说话时静音，这是基本礼仪
  3. Look at the camera：说话时看摄像头而不是屏幕，这样对方感觉你在看他
  4. 使用耳机：避免回声和背景噪音
  5. 会议总结：远程会议后发送总结邮件或聊天消息，确认行动项

❌ 避免的错误

  1. 不要在共享屏幕时打开敏感信息——先关掉邮件、聊天窗口
  2. 不要在会议中多任务处理（比如看手机）——开摄像头的话大家都能看到你的眼神
  3. 不要说 "Can you hear me?" 十几次——说两次就够了，如果还不行就重启
  4. 不要忘记关掉摄像头和麦克风——特别是去洗手间的时候
  5. 不要把所有沟通都变成会议——有些事一封邮件或一条消息就能解决（"This could have been an email"）
