第八章：辞职与告别 (Resignation & Farewell)

场景背景

辞职是职业生涯中几乎不可避免的环节。在美国，辞职被称为"two weeks' notice"——标准做法是提前两周通知雇主。辞职过程需要专业和体面：提交正式辞职信、与直属经理面谈、完成交接、与同事好好告别。"Never burn bridges"（永远不要断桥/不要得罪人）是美国的职场金句——因为你永远不知道什么时候还会再遇到这些人。

文化要点：

  • 美国是"at-will employment"（自由雇佣制），理论上可以随时走人，但两周通知是礼仪
  • 辞职理由可以说得模糊一些，"a new opportunity" 就够了
  • 告别邮件和告别聚会（farewell party）是标配
  • 英国的辞职通知期通常更长，一个月到三个月不等
  • 保持积极正面的态度离开，不管你真实感受如何

场景一：向经理提出辞职 (Resigning to Your Manager)

人物： 李明、Karen（经理）

Li Ming： Karen, do you have a few minutes? There's something I'd like to discuss with you in private.
口语 Karen, do you have a few minutes? There's something I'd like to discuss with you in private.
正式 Karen, do you have a few minutes? There is something I would like to discuss with you privately.
中文 Karen，有几分钟吗？有些事想私下跟你聊聊。
解说 "In private" = 私下地/单独地。提出辞职时选择私下场合非常重要，不要在公共区域或会议上提。"Something I'd like to discuss" 比直接说 "I want to quit" 更得体。✅推荐

Karen： Sure, come on in. Close the door if you'd like. Everything OK?
口语 Sure, come on in. Close the door if you'd like. Everything OK?
正式 Certainly, please come in. You may close the door if you wish. Is everything alright?
中文 当然，进来吧。想关门就关。一切还好吗？
解说 "Close the door if you'd like" 是暗示如果话题敏感可以关门的礼貌说法。经理说 "Everything OK?" 说明她可能感觉到了什么。✅推荐

Li Ming： So, this is a bit hard to say, but I've accepted an offer from another company. I want you to know that this wasn't an easy decision — I've really valued my time here and everything I've learned from you and the team. But this new role aligns more with where I want to take my career long-term. My last day would be two weeks from Friday, per our policy.
口语 So, this is a bit hard to say, but I've accepted an offer from another company. I want you to know that this wasn't an easy decision — I've really valued my time here and everything I've learned from you and the team. But this new role aligns more with where I want to take my career long-term. My last day would be two weeks from Friday, per our policy.
正式 This is somewhat difficult to say, but I have accepted a job offer from another company. I want you to know that this was not an easy decision. I have truly valued my time here and everything I have learned from you and the team. However, this new role aligns better with my long-term career goals. My final day would be two weeks from Friday, in accordance with company policy.
中文 这有点难以启齿，但我已经接受了另一家公司的offer。我想让你知道这不是一个容易的决定——我真的很珍惜在这里的时间和从你和团队学到的一切。但这个新职位更符合我长期的职业发展方向。按照公司规定，我的最后一天将是两周后的周五。
解说 这是教科书级的辞职话术：1）感性开场（hard to say）；2）感恩（valued my time）；3）中性理由（aligns with career goals）；4）明确日期（two weeks）。"Per our policy" = 按照公司规定。"Aligns with" = 与...一致/符合。✅推荐

Karen： Wow, honestly I'm a little surprised, but I appreciate you telling me in person. Can I ask — is there anything that could have changed your mind? Was it the money, the role, or...?
口语 Wow, honestly I'm a little surprised, but I appreciate you telling me in person. Can I ask — is there anything that could have changed your mind? Was it the money, the role, or...?
正式 Wow, honestly I am somewhat surprised, but I appreciate you informing me in person. May I ask — was there anything that could have changed your decision? Was it related to compensation, the role, or something else?
中文 哇，说实话有点意外，但感谢你当面告诉我。我能问一下——有什么能改变你的决定吗？是因为薪资、职位、还是……？
解说 经理问这种问题是为了做留人分析（retention analysis）。"In person" = 当面（美国职场重视面对面辞职，不要发邮件或短信辞职）。"Changed your mind" = 改变你的主意。✅推荐

Li Ming： It wasn't about the money, honestly. It was really about the growth opportunity. This new role has a bigger scope and it's more aligned with where I see myself in five years. I want to stress — this has been a great place to work, and I'm not leaving because I'm unhappy.
口语 It wasn't about the money, honestly. It was really about the growth opportunity. This new role has a bigger scope and it's more aligned with where I see myself in five years. I want to stress — this has been a great place to work, and I'm not leaving because I'm unhappy.
正式 It was not about the compensation, honestly. It was genuinely about the growth opportunity. This new role has a broader scope and is more aligned with my five-year career vision. I want to emphasize — this has been an excellent workplace, and my departure is not due to dissatisfaction.
中文 不是因为钱，说实话。是真的因为成长机会。这个新职位范围更大，更符合我对五年后的定位。我想强调——这里一直是个很好的工作地方，我离开不是因为不开心。
解说 "Not about the money" = 不是因为钱。"Bigger scope" = 更大的范围/职责。"Where I see myself in five years" = 我五年后的定位。"I want to stress" = 我想强调。反复强调正面的东西，不给公司留坏印象。✅推荐

Karen： I appreciate that. And for what it's worth, I think you're making a smart career move. You've grown a lot here and you're ready for the next challenge. I'm going to miss having you on the team, but I'm also genuinely happy for you. Let's figure out the transition plan — I want to make sure your last two weeks are productive but also that you have time to say proper goodbyes.
口语 I appreciate that. And for what it's worth, I think you're making a smart career move. You've grown a lot here and you're ready for the next challenge. I'm going to miss having you on the team, but I'm also genuinely happy for you. Let's figure out the transition plan — I want to make sure your last two weeks are productive but also that you have time to say proper goodbyes.
正式 I appreciate that. And for what it is worth, I believe you are making a prudent career decision. You have developed considerably here and are prepared for the next challenge. I will miss having you on the team, but I am also genuinely pleased for you. Let us develop a transition plan — I want to ensure your final two weeks are productive while also allowing time for appropriate farewells.
中文 我很感激。不管怎样，我觉得你做了一个聪明的职业选择。你在这里成长了很多，已经准备好迎接下一个挑战了。我会想念你在团队的日子，但也真心为你高兴。我们来安排交接计划——确保你最后两周有产出，也有时间好好告别。
解说 "For what it's worth" = 不管有没有价值/说句实在话。"Smart career move" = 聪明的职业选择。"Transition plan" = 过渡/交接计划。"Proper goodbyes" = 正式的/恰当的告别。好的经理会祝福你而不是挽留你。✅推荐

Li Ming： That means a lot, Karen. Thank you for understanding. And thank you for everything — I really mean that. I've learned so much from you.
口语 That means a lot, Karen. Thank you for understanding. And thank you for everything — I really mean that. I've learned so much from you.
正式 That is very meaningful, Karen. Thank you for your understanding. And thank you for everything — I truly mean that. I have learned a great deal from you.
中文 这对我来说很有意义，Karen。谢谢你的理解。也谢谢你的一切——我说真的。我从你身上学到了很多。
解说 "I really mean that" = 我说真的/不是客套。在感性时刻加上这句话，让对方知道你不是随便说说。"Means a lot" = 很有意义。✅推荐

场景二：与同事告别 (Saying Goodbye to Colleagues)

人物： 李明、Rachel（同事）、Brian（同事）、多位同事

Rachel： Wait, what? You're leaving? I had no idea! Where are you going?
口语 Wait, what? You're leaving? I had no idea! Where are you going?
正式 Wait, really? You are leaving? I was not aware of this. Where are you going?
中文 等等，什么？你要走了？我完全不知道！你要去哪？
解说 "Wait, what?" 是听到意外消息时最自然的反应。"I had no idea" = 我完全不知道。同事们的反应通常是惊讶+好奇+不舍。✅推荐

Li Ming： Yeah, I've taken a new role at a startup downtown. It was a tough call, but it felt like the right move for me right now. I'm gonna miss you guys, though. For real.
口语 Yeah, I've taken a new role at a startup downtown. It was a tough call, but it felt like the right move for me right now. I'm gonna miss you guys, though. For real.
正式 Yes, I have accepted a new position at a startup in downtown. It was a difficult decision, but it felt like the right career move at this time. I will miss all of you, though. Genuinely.
中文 对，我在市中心一家初创公司接受了一个新职位。这个决定很难做，但感觉现在是对的时机。不过我会想你们的。真的。
解说 "A tough call" = 艰难的决定/选择。"For real" = 真的/认真的（比 "seriously" 更口语化）。用 "startup" 来描述新公司比说具体的公司名更安全（避免信息泄露）。✅推荐

Brian： Hey man, congrats! We're definitely gonna miss you around here. Who's gonna keep me in line now? Let's grab lunch before you go — my treat.
口语 Hey man, congrats! We're definitely gonna miss you around here. Who's gonna keep me in line now? Let's grab lunch before you go — my treat.
正式 Hey, congratulations! We will certainly miss you here. Who will keep me disciplined now? Let us have lunch together before you depart — it will be my treat.
中文 嘿哥们，恭喜！我们一定会想你的。以后谁来管住我啊？走之前一起吃个午饭——我请客。
解说 "Keep me in line" = 管住我/让我守规矩，是幽默的说法。"My treat" = 我请客。在美国职场，离职前同事请你吃饭是常见传统。"Congrats" 是 "congratulations" 的缩略。✅推荐

Li Ming： I'd love that. How about Thursday? And honestly, Brian, working with you has been a highlight. I've learned a ton from you.
口语 I'd love that. How about Thursday? And honestly, Brian, working with you has been a highlight. I've learned a ton from you.
正式 I would love that. How about Thursday? And honestly, Brian, working with you has been one of the highlights of my time here. I have learned a great deal from you.
中文 好啊。周四怎么样？说实话，Brian，跟你一起工作是这段时间的亮点之一。从你这里学到了很多。
解说 "A highlight" = 亮点/最精彩的部分。"A ton" = 很多（比 "a lot" 更口语化）。离开时真诚地表达感谢和赞美，是维持人脉的好方式。✅推荐

Rachel： OK, you can't leave without a proper send-off. I'm organizing a little something on your last day — nothing crazy, just drinks after work. You in?
口语 OK, you can't leave without a proper send-off. I'm organizing a little something on your last day — nothing crazy, just drinks after work. You in?
正式 You cannot leave without a proper farewell gathering. I am organizing a small event on your last day — nothing extravagant, just drinks after work. Are you interested?
中文 好吧，你不能没有个像样的欢送会就走。我在你最后一天安排了个小活动——不搞大的，就是下班后喝两杯。你来吗？
解说 "Proper send-off" = 像样的欢送。"A little something" = 一点小安排，故意说得轻松以减轻压力。"Nothing crazy" = 不搞大的。"You in?" = 你参加吗？（比 "Will you attend?" 随意得多）。✅推荐

Li Ming： Absolutely. Wouldn't miss it. And hey, let's definitely stay in touch. I'm not going far — just across town. And my LinkedIn's always open.
口语 Absolutely. Wouldn't miss it. And hey, let's definitely stay in touch. I'm not going far — just across town. And my LinkedIn's always open.
正式 Absolutely. I would not miss it. And please, let us definitely stay in touch. I am not moving far — just across town. My LinkedIn is always available.
中文 当然。不会缺席的。嘿，我们一定要保持联系。我也不走远——就在城市另一边。LinkedIn随时可以找我。
解说 "Wouldn't miss it" = 不会错过的。"Stay in touch" = 保持联系。"Across town" = 城市的另一边。"My LinkedIn's always open" = 随时可以LinkedIn联系我。在现代职场，LinkedIn是最重要的职业社交工具。✅推荐

场景三：告别邮件 (Farewell Email)

Li Ming（发送全组邮件）：

Hey everyone,

As some of you may already know, Friday will be my last day here at Apex. I've accepted a new role at another company, and while I'm excited about the next chapter, saying goodbye to this team is genuinely bittersweet.

I want to thank each and every one of you for making my time here so memorable. I've learned an incredible amount, not just about the work, but about what it means to be part of a great team. Special shout-out to Karen for being an amazing manager, and to Rachel and Brian for making every day more fun.

Please stay in touch! You can find me on LinkedIn [link] or reach me at my personal email [email]. I'd love to grab coffee sometime.

Wishing you all the best. Keep crushing it!

— Li Ming
解说 "Bittersweet" = 苦乐参半。"Each and every one of you" = 你们每一个人。"Special shout-out to" = 特别感谢（口语化的致谢方式）。"Keep crushing it" = 继续加油/继续出色地做下去（"crush it" 在口语中指做得非常好）。告别邮件要：真诚感恩、不抱怨、提供联系方式、保持积极。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国辞职文化

  1. 提前两周通知：这是不成文的标准，有些公司可能有不同的通知期要求
  2. 当面辞职：先跟直属经理面谈，然后再告诉同事。不要让经理从别人那里听到
  3. 书面辞职信：面谈后再提交正式的辞职信，内容要简短专业
  4. 不要炫耀新工作：可以说 "a new opportunity"，不需要详细描述新公司的待遇
  5. 最后两周要认真工作：完成交接，留个好印象——"Never burn bridges"
  6. 离职面谈（Exit Interview）：HR可能会约你做一次离职面谈，这时候可以稍微诚实一些，但仍然要保持建设性

🇬🇧 英国辞职文化

  1. 英国的通知期（notice period）通常更长——一个月到三个月
  2. 英国的辞职信更正式，通常需要书面提交
  3. 英国人辞职时更加含蓄，理由说得更模糊
  4. 英国的花园假（garden leave）更常见——辞职后在家等通知期结束，不需要来上班
  5. 英国人告别时可能会送一张贺卡让大家签名，以及一份集体礼物

📝 辞职信模板

Dear [Manager's Name],

Please accept this letter as formal notification that I am resigning from my position as [Job Title] at [Company Name]. My last day will be [Date], in accordance with the notice period in my contract.

I want to thank you for the opportunities I've had during my time here. I've learned a great deal and am grateful for the experience.

Please let me know how I can help during the transition.

Sincerely, [Your Name]

❌ 避免的错误

  1. 不要在找到新工作前就辞职——除非有充足的存款
  2. 不要发邮件辞职而不面谈——这是非常不专业的
  3. 不要在辞职时抱怨或发泄——不管你有多不满
  4. 不要带走公司数据或客户信息——这可能违法
  5. 不要在社交媒体上吐槽前公司——HR会看的
  6. 不要在告别邮件中提及新公司的具体信息
