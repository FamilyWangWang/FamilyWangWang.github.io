第四章：请假与休假 (Leave & Vacation)

场景背景

在美国职场，请假（taking time off）是正常的工作安排。美国没有法定带薪年假（这是发达国家中的例外），但大多数公司提供15-20天的PTO（Paid Time Off，带薪假期）。请假需要提前通知、合理规划交接，尤其是请长假。请病假（calling in sick）比较简单，通常发邮件或短信即可。英国法律规定了法定年假（statutory leave），流程上略有不同。

文化要点：

  • 美国人通常不会全部用完PTO，但近年来鼓励休假的文化在增长
  • "PTO" 是最常用的假期说法，有些公司把病假和年假合并计算
  • 请假前要确认工作交接（coverage）
  • 病假不需要详细解释病情，"I'm not feeling well" 就够了
  • 英国法律规定每年至少28天年假（含公共假日）

场景一：请年假 (Requesting Vacation Time)

人物： 李明、Karen（经理）

Li Ming： Hey Karen, got a minute? I wanted to talk to you about taking some time off.
口语 Hey Karen, got a minute? I wanted to talk to you about taking some time off.
正式 Hello Karen, do you have a moment? I would like to discuss taking some time off from work.
中文 嘿Karen，有空吗？我想跟你聊聊请假的事。
解说 "Got a minute?" 是请求私下谈话最常见的方式，意思是"能耽误你一分钟吗？"。"Take some time off" = 请假/休假，是最自然的说法。✅推荐

Karen： Sure, come on in. What's up?
口语 Sure, come on in. What's up?
正式 Certainly, please come in. What is it?
中文 当然，进来吧。什么事？
解说 "Come on in" 比 "Please enter" 热情很多，多了一个 "on" 表示欢迎。"What's up?" = 有什么事？/怎么了？是最常见的非正式问话。✅推荐

Li Ming： So my family is visiting from China next month, and I'd like to take a week off to spend some time with them. I'm thinking the week of June 16th. Would that work?
口语 So my family is visiting from China next month, and I'd like to take a week off to spend some time with them. I'm thinking the week of June 16th. Would that work?
正式 My family is visiting from China next month, and I would like to take one week off to spend time with them. I am considering the week of June 16th. Would that be acceptable?
中文 我家人下个月从中国来看我，我想请一周假陪陪他们。我想定在6月16号那周。可以吗？
解说 "Take a week off" = 请一周假。"I'm thinking..." = 我在想/我考虑...（比 "I want" 委婉）。"Would that work?" = 可以吗？/方便吗？是请求许可的标准委婉句式。✅推荐

Karen： Oh nice, that's exciting! Family visits are important. Let me check the team calendar... June 16th, huh? That should be fine — it's a pretty quiet week for us. How many days are you looking at?
口语 Oh nice, that's exciting! Family visits are important. Let me check the team calendar... June 16th, huh? That should be fine — it's a pretty quiet week for us. How many days are you looking at?
正式 That is exciting! Family visits are important. Let me check the team calendar. June 16th? That should be acceptable — it is a relatively quiet week for us. How many days are you requesting?
中文 哦太好了，真不错！家人来访很重要的。让我看看团队日历……6月16号是吧？应该没问题——我们那周比较安静。你打算请几天？
解说 "How many days are you looking at?" 比 "How many days do you need?" 更口语化。"Looking at" 在这里是"考虑/打算"的意思。"Pretty quiet week" = 比较轻松的一周（指工作量）。"Huh?" 放在句末表示确认。✅推荐

Li Ming： Five business days — Monday through Friday. I'll make sure everything's covered before I go. I was thinking Rachel could handle anything urgent that comes up.
口语 Five business days — Monday through Friday. I'll make sure everything's covered before I go. I was thinking Rachel could handle anything urgent that comes up.
正式 Five business days, Monday through Friday. I will ensure that all responsibilities are covered before my departure. I was thinking Rachel could manage any urgent matters that arise.
中文 五个工作日——周一到周五。走之前我会确保所有事情都有人接手。我想Rachel可以处理紧急事务。
解说 "Business days" = 工作日（区别于周末和假日）。"Make sure everything's covered" = 确保所有工作都有人代管。"Handle anything urgent that comes up" = 处理可能出现的紧急事务。"Comes up" = 出现/发生。主动提及交接安排会让经理放心。✅推荐

Karen： That works. Rachel's usually good with that. Just make sure you put in the PTO request through the system so it's official. And enjoy the time with your family — that's what PTO is for!
口语 That works. Rachel's usually good with that. Just make sure you put in the PTO request through the system so it's official. And enjoy the time with your family — that's what PTO is for!
正式 That is acceptable. Rachel is usually reliable with such matters. Please ensure you submit the PTO request through the system to make it official. And enjoy the time with your family — that is what PTO is for.
中文 没问题。Rachel通常能搞定这些。确保在系统里提交PTO申请让它正式生效。好好享受和家人在一起的时光——PTO就是干这个用的！
解说 "Put in a request" = 提交申请。"Through the system" = 通过系统（指公司的人事/请假系统）。"So it's official" = 让它正式化。"That's what PTO is for!" = 这就是PTO的用途呀！是表示支持的说法。✅推荐

Li Ming： Will do. Thanks, Karen. I really appreciate it.
口语 Will do. Thanks, Karen. I really appreciate it.
正式 I will do that. Thank you, Karen. I truly appreciate it.
中文 会的。谢谢，Karen。真的很感谢。
解说 "Will do" = 我会做的/好的，是 "I will do that" 的极简形式，非常常用。"Really appreciate it" 是比单纯 "Thank you" 更真诚的感谢方式。✅推荐

场景二：打电话请病假 (Calling in Sick)

人物： 李明、Karen（经理，电话中）

Li Ming： Hey Karen, it's Li Ming. Sorry to bother you early, but I'm not feeling great today. I think I'm gonna need to take a sick day.
口语 Hey Karen, it's Li Ming. Sorry to bother you early, but I'm not feeling great today. I think I'm gonna need to take a sick day.
正式 Hello Karen, this is Li Ming. I apologize for contacting you early, but I am not feeling well today. I believe I will need to take a sick day.
中文 嘿Karen，我是李明。抱歉一大早打扰你，但我今天不太舒服。我想我需要请一天病假。
解说 "Sorry to bother you early" = 抱歉一大早打扰你（因为通常要在上班前通知）。"Not feeling great" 是 "not feeling well" 的更口语化说法。"Take a sick day" = 请病假（一天）。注意：美国通常不需要说具体是什么病。✅推荐

Karen： Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that. Don't even worry about work — just take care of yourself. Do you need anything, or are you good?
口语 Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that. Don't even worry about work — just take care of yourself. Do you need anything, or are you good?
正式 I am sorry to hear that. Please do not worry about work — just focus on your recovery. Do you need anything, or are you managing?
中文 哦不，真遗憾听到这个。别操心工作了——好好休息。你需要什么吗，还是自己能行？
解说 "Oh no" 是听到不好消息时的自然反应。"Don't even worry about it" 中的 "even" 起强调作用，意思是"完全不用担心"。"Take care of yourself" = 照顾好自己。"Or are you good?" = 还是你自己能应付？✅推荐

Li Ming： I'll be fine, just need to rest up. I'll check my emails later if I'm up to it. Oh, and the Johnson report is due today — Brian should have the latest version. Can you ask him to send it to the client?
口语 I'll be fine, just need to rest up. I'll check my emails later if I'm up to it. Oh, and the Johnson report is due today — Brian should have the latest version. Can you ask him to send it to the client?
正式 I will be fine. I just need to rest. I will check my emails later if I feel well enough. Additionally, the Johnson report is due today — Brian should have the latest version. Could you ask him to send it to the client?
中文 我没事，就是需要休息。如果身体允许的话我晚点看看邮件。对了，Johnson报告今天截止——Brian那边应该有最新版本。你能让他发给客户吗？
解说 "Rest up" = 好好休息。"If I'm up to it" = 如果我有精力/身体允许的话。"Is due today" = 今天截止。即使请假，主动交代紧急工作交接会让你显得非常职业。✅推荐

Karen： Absolutely, I'll take care of it. No worries. Just keep me posted on how you're feeling, and come back when you're ready. Feel better!
口语 Absolutely, I'll take care of it. No worries. Just keep me posted on how you're feeling, and come back when you're ready. Feel better!
正式 Absolutely, I will handle it. Do not worry. Please keep me updated on how you are feeling, and return when you are ready. I hope you feel better.
中文 当然，我来处理。别担心。随时告诉我你的情况，准备好了再回来。早日康复！
解说 "I'll take care of it" = 我来处理。"No worries" = 别担心/没事。"Keep me posted" = 随时告诉我进展/情况。"Feel better!" 是请病假时最常用的祝福语，简洁温暖。✅推荐

场景三：临时请假 (Unexpected Time Off)

人物： 李明（给同事Rachel发消息）

Li Ming（文字消息）： Hey Rachel, I've got a personal matter to deal with this afternoon — nothing serious, just something I need to handle during business hours. Can you cover for me at the 3 PM call?
口语 Hey Rachel, I've got a personal matter to deal with this afternoon — nothing serious, just something I need to handle during business hours. Can you cover for me at the 3 PM call?
正式 Hello Rachel, I have a personal matter to attend to this afternoon — nothing serious, just something I need to handle during business hours. Could you cover for me during the 3 PM conference call?
中文 嘿Rachel，我今天下午有点私事要处理——不是什么大事，就是需要在上班时间处理一下。3点的电话会议你能替我一下吗？
解说 "Personal matter" = 私事，不需要具体解释。"Cover for me" = 替我顶一下/帮我代班。"During business hours" = 在上班时间内。这是请临时短假的典型措辞。✅推荐

Rachel： Of course! No problem at all. I've got you. Everything OK?
口语 Of course! No problem at all. I've got you. Everything OK?
正式 Of course! No problem at all. I will handle it for you. Is everything alright?
中文 当然！完全没问题。我替你搞定。一切都好吧？
解说 "I've got you" / "I got you" / "Gotcha" 在这里是"我帮你搞定了/包在我身上"的意思。"Everything OK?" 是表达关心的简短问法，但不会追问太多。✅推荐

Li Ming： Yeah, all good. Just some errands I can't put off. I owe you one! I'll be back online tomorrow morning.
口语 Yeah, all good. Just some errands I can't put off. I owe you one! I'll be back online tomorrow morning.
正式 Yes, everything is fine. I just have some errands that I cannot postpone. I owe you a favor. I will be back online tomorrow morning.
中文 嗯，没事。就是有些事情不能再拖了。我欠你一个人情！明天早上我就会上线。
解说 "All good" = 都挺好的。"Errands" = 杂事/跑腿的事。"Can't put off" = 不能再推迟了。"I owe you one" = 我欠你一个人情，是表示感谢帮忙的经典口语。"Back online" = 重新上线/回到工作状态。✅推荐

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国请假文化

  1. PTO是权利，不是施舍：使用PTO是正常的，不需要感到愧疚（不过很多美国人确实有愧疚感）
  2. 提前通知：请长假最好提前两周以上通知，短假几天就行
  3. 主动安排交接：提前想好谁来接替你的工作，会让经理更容易批准
  4. 病假不需要解释细节：说 "I'm not feeling well" 或 "I'm under the weather" 就够了，不需要描述症状
  5. 系统提交：口头同意后还需要在公司系统（如Workday、BambooHR）里正式提交

🇬🇧 英国请假文化

  1. 英国叫 "annual leave" 或 "holiday"，不说 "vacation"（vacation在英国指大学假期）
  2. 英国法律规定每年至少28天年假（含银行假日），比美国多很多
  3. 英国请病假可能需要 "fit note"（医生证明），如果超过7天
  4. 英国也有 "Statutory Sick Pay"（法定病假工资）
  5. 英国人请病假时常说 "I'm feeling a bit under the weather" 或 "I've come down with something"

📝 常用请假邮件模板

简短病假：

Hi [Name], I'm not feeling well today and need to take a sick day. I'll check emails later if I'm up to it. [Colleague's name] can cover anything urgent. Thanks.

年假申请：

Hi [Name], I'd like to request PTO for [dates]. [Colleague's name] has agreed to cover for me while I'm out. Please let me know if this works. Thanks!

❌ 避免的错误

  1. 不要在请假时编造具体病情——"I'm not feeling well" 足够了，说得越详细越不自然
  2. 不要因为愧疚而在休假时还一直工作——美国人越来越强调 "unplug"（断开工作）
  3. 不要在团队最忙的时候请长假——至少提前沟通
  4. 不要忘了在系统里提交申请——口头同意不算数
  5. 不要说 "I want to ask for leave"——这太中式英语了，说 "I'd like to take some time off"
