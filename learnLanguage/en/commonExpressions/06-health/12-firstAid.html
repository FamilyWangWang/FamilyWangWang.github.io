第12章：急救课程（First Aid Course）

场景背景

学习急救（first aid）是一项非常有价值的技能。在美国，红十字会（American Red Cross）和美国心脏协会（American Heart Association, AHA）提供各种急救课程，包括CPR（心肺复苏）、AED（自动体外除颤器）使用、基础急救（basic first aid）等。50岁左右学习急救特别有意义，因为心血管疾病风险增加，家人和朋友也可能需要帮助。英国有 St John Ambulance 和英国红十字会提供类似课程。

关键文化差异：

  • 🇺🇸 美国：很多工作场所要求员工持有CPR/First Aid证书
  • 🇬🇧 英国：St John Ambulance 是最知名的急救培训机构
  • 美国的Good Samaritan Law（好撒玛利亚人法）保护在紧急情况下施救的人
  • 英国也有类似的免责保护

完整对话

子场景1：报名急救课程（Signing Up for a Course）

You: Hi, I'm interested in taking a first aid course. What do you offer?

正式: Hello, I am interested in enrolling in a first aid course. What courses do you offer? 中文: 你好，我对参加急救课程感兴趣。你们有什么课程？ 详解: "Interested in taking" = 有兴趣参加。"What do you offer?" = 你们提供什么？简洁直接的询问方式。✅推荐

Instructor: Great! We've got a few options. The most popular one is our combined CPR, AED, and First Aid class — it covers everything from choking to heart attacks to bleeding control. It's a full-day course, about eight hours, and you walk away with a two-year certification.

正式: We have several options. The most popular is our combined CPR, AED, and First Aid course. It covers topics from choking to cardiac arrest to bleeding control. It is a full-day course, approximately eight hours, and includes a two-year certification. 中文: 好的！我们有几个选项。最受欢迎的是我们的CPR、AED和急救综合课——涵盖了从窒息到心脏病发作到出血控制的所有内容。一整天的课，大约八小时，课后获得两年有效期的证书。 详解: "Walk away with" = 离开时获得（口语表达）。"Certification" = 证书/认证。"CPR" = Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation（心肺复苏）。"AED" = Automated External Defibrillator（自动体外除颤器）。✅推荐

You: That sounds like exactly what I need. I've got a family — two kids, and my parents are getting up there in age. I figure it's about time I learned this stuff.

正式: That sounds exactly what I need. I have a family with two children, and my parents are advancing in age. I believe it is time I learned these skills. 中文: 听起来正好是我需要的。我有个家——两个孩子，父母也上了年纪。我觉得是时候学学这些东西了。 详解: "Getting up there in age" = 上了年纪（委婉说法）。"I figure" = 我觉得/我认为。"It's about time" = 是时候了。"This stuff" = 这些东西（口语化的指代）。✅推荐

Instructor: That's a really responsible decision. We actually hear that a lot — people signing up after becoming parents or when their parents start having health issues. The next class is this Saturday from 8 AM to 5 PM. There's a lunch break around noon. Does that work for your schedule?

正式: That is a responsible decision. Many people enroll after becoming parents or when their parents develop health issues. The next class is this Saturday from 8 AM to 5 PM, with a lunch break at noon. Does that fit your schedule? 中文: 这是一个很负责任的决定。我们经常听到这个——人们当了父母或者父母开始有健康问题后来报名。下一次课是这个周六早上8点到下午5点。中午有午休。你时间可以吗？ 详解: "Hear that a lot" = 经常听到。"Signing up" = 报名。"Works for your schedule" = 时间安排合适。✅推荐

You: Saturday works perfectly. How much is the course?

正式: Saturday is convenient. What is the course fee? 中文: 周六很合适。课程多少钱？ 详解: 直接问价格。✅推荐

Instructor: It's $85 for the full-day course, and that includes the certification card, the training manual, and all materials. You can pay online or bring a check on the day of the class. Wear comfortable clothes — you'll be doing a lot of hands-on practice.

正式: The course fee is $85, which includes the certification card, training manual, and all materials. Payment can be made online or by check on the day. Please wear comfortable clothing, as there will be extensive hands-on practice. 中文: 全天课程85美元，包括证书卡、培训手册和所有材料。可以在线付或者上课当天带支票。穿舒适的衣服——会有很多实操练习。 详解: "Hands-on practice" = 实操练习/亲自动手练习。"Materials" = 材料。"On the day of the class" = 上课当天。✅推荐

子场景2：课堂学习（During the Course）

Instructor: Alright everyone, welcome to the CPR, AED, and First Aid course. Let's start with the basics. If someone collapses in front of you, the very first thing you do is check if they're responsive. Tap them on the shoulder and shout, "Are you okay?" If no response, that's when you spring into action.

正式: Welcome to the CPR, AED, and First Aid course. Let us begin with the basics. If someone collapses, first check their responsiveness. Tap their shoulder and ask, "Are you okay?" If there is no response, you should act immediately. 中文: 好了各位，欢迎来到CPR、AED和急救课程。我们从基础开始。如果有人在你面前倒下了，你做的第一件事是检查他们有没有反应。拍拍他们的肩膀喊"你还好吗？"如果没反应，那就是你行动的时候了。 详解: "Collapses" = 倒下/昏倒。"Responsive" = 有反应的。"Spring into action" = 立即行动/迅速反应。急救的第一步是确认意识和安全。✅推荐

You: What about calling 911? Should I do that first?

正式: What about calling 911? Should I do that first? 中文: 打911呢？我应该先打吗？ 详解: 问急救步骤的优先级。"What about...?" = ...呢？✅推荐

Instructor: Great question. If you're alone, call 911 first, put it on speaker, and then start CPR. If there are other people around, point at someone specific and say, "You — call 911 and get an AED." Don't just yell "Somebody call 911!" — people tend to assume someone else will do it. Be specific.

正式: Excellent question. If you are alone, call 911 first, activate speakerphone, and then begin CPR. If others are present, point to a specific individual and say, "You — call 911 and get an AED." Avoid saying "Someone call 911!" as bystanders tend to assume others will respond. Be specific. 中文: 好问题。如果你一个人，先打911，开免提，然后开始CPR。如果周围有其他人，指着一个具体的人说"你——打911并去拿AED。"不要只是喊"有人打911吗！"——人们会假设别人会打。要具体。 详解: "Put it on speaker" = 开免提。"Point at someone specific" = 指向一个具体的人。"Tend to" = 倾向于。"Assume someone else will do it" = 假设别人会做（旁观者效应）。这是急救课中最关键的知识之一。✅推荐

Instructor: Now let's practice chest compressions. Kneel beside the manikin, put the heel of your hand on the center of the chest, lock your elbows, and push hard and fast. We're aiming for at least 2 inches deep, at a rate of 100 to 120 compressions per minute. A good trick is to push to the beat of "Stayin' Alive" by the Bee Gees.

正式: Let us practice chest compressions. Kneel beside the manikin, place the heel of your hand on the center of the chest, lock your elbows, and push firmly and rapidly. Target a depth of at least 2 inches at a rate of 100 to 120 compressions per minute. A helpful technique is to compress to the rhythm of "Stayin' Alive." 中文: 现在我们来练习胸外按压。跪在假人旁边，把手掌根部放在胸部中央，锁定手肘，用力快速按压。目标是至少2英寸深，每分钟100到120次。一个好技巧是跟着Bee Gees的"Stayin' Alive"的节拍按压。 详解: "Heel of your hand" = 手掌根部。"Lock your elbows" = 锁定手肘（伸直手臂）。"Push hard and fast" = 用力快速按压。"Stayin' Alive" 的节拍正好是约100-120 BPM，这是急救课的经典教学梗。✅推荐

You: (练习中) Man, this is more tiring than I expected. My arms are already burning.

正式: This is more fatiguing than I expected. My arms are already experiencing muscle burn. 中文: 天哪，这比我想象的累多了。我胳膊已经在酸了。 详解: "More tiring than I expected" = 比我预想的累。"Burning" = 酸痛/灼烧感（运动中的肌肉感觉）。CPR确实很费体力，这是正常反应。✅推荐

Instructor: That's real CPR for you! In an actual emergency, you'd be switching out every two minutes because it's so exhausting. If someone else knows CPR, tag them in. Alright, now let's move on to the AED. Does anyone know what AED stands for?

正式: That is characteristic of actual CPR. In a real emergency, rescuers rotate every two minutes due to fatigue. If another trained person is present, switch with them. Now let us discuss the AED. Does anyone know what AED stands for? 中文: 这就是真正的CPR！在实际急救中，你会每两分钟换人，因为太累了。如果其他人也会CPR，换他们上。好了，现在我们来说AED。有人知道AED代表什么吗？ 详解: "Switch out" = 换人。"Tag them in" = 换他们上（来自摔跤比赛的术语）。"Stands for" = 代表/全称是。✅推荐

You: Automated External Defibrillator?

正式: Automated External Defibrillator? 中文: 自动体外除颤器？ 详解: 正确！AED的全称。✅推荐

Instructor: Spot on. And here's the most important thing about using an AED — turn it on and it will literally talk you through everything. It's designed for people with zero medical training. You can't mess it up — the machine won't let you shock someone who doesn't need it.

正式: Correct. The most important aspect of AED use is that you simply turn it on, and it provides verbal instructions. It is designed for individuals without medical training. The machine prevents inappropriate shocks. 中文: 完全正确。关于使用AED最重要的一点——打开它，它就会一步一步用语音教你。它是为零医学培训的人设计的。你不会搞砸——机器不会让你电击不需要电击的人。 详解: "Spot on" = 完全正确。"Talk you through everything" = 一步一步教你。"Zero medical training" = 零医学培训。"Mess it up" = 搞砸。AED的设计确实非常智能和安全。✅推荐

子场景3：其他急救场景（Other First Aid Scenarios）

Instructor: Now let's cover choking. If someone is choking but can still cough and make noise, encourage them to keep coughing. But if they can't cough, speak, or breathe — that's when you step in with the Heimlich maneuver. Stand behind them, make a fist, place it just above the belly button, and give upward thrusts.

正式: Let us discuss choking. If a person can cough and make sounds, encourage continued coughing. If they cannot cough, speak, or breathe, perform the Heimlich maneuver. Stand behind them, form a fist, position it above the navel, and administer upward abdominal thrusts. 中文: 现在我们来讲窒息。如果有人噎住了但还能咳嗽和发出声音，鼓励他们继续咳。但如果他们不能咳、不能说话、不能呼吸——这时候你就用海姆立克法介入。站在他们身后，握拳，放在肚脐上方，向上用力推。 详解: "Step in" = 介入/采取行动。"Heimlich maneuver" = 海姆立克急救法。"Make a fist" = 握拳。"Belly button" = 肚脐（口语说法）。"Upward thrusts" = 向上推击。✅推荐

You: What about for a child? Is it different?

正式: What about for a child? Is the technique different? 中文: 儿童呢？方法不一样吗？ 详解: 儿童和婴儿的急救方法确实不同，主动提问是好习惯。✅推荐

Instructor: Good thinking. For a child, you'd use less force, and for an infant under one year, you'd use back blows instead. We'll practice that with the infant manikin after lunch. The key takeaway for today is — doing something is always better than doing nothing. Even imperfect CPR doubles someone's chance of survival compared to no CPR at all.

正式: Good question. For children, use less force, and for infants under one year, use back blows. We will practice with the infant manikin later. The key takeaway is that any intervention is better than none. Even imperfect CPR doubles survival chances compared to no intervention. 中文: 好问题。对儿童，力度要小，一岁以下的婴儿则用拍背法。午饭后我们会用婴儿假人练习。今天的关键收获是——做点什么总比什么都不做好。即使不完美的CPR也比没有CPR的存活率高一倍。 详解: "Back blows" = 拍背。"Key takeaway" = 关键收获/核心要点。"Doing something is better than doing nothing" = 做总比不做好。"Doubles" = 翻倍。这是急救课最核心的理念之一。✅推荐

核心口语表达清单

必学表达 ✅推荐

  1. I'm interested in taking a first aid course. — 我对参加急救课感兴趣。
  2. You walk away with a two-year certification. — 你离开时获得两年证书。
  3. It's about time I learned this stuff. — 是时候学学这个了。
  4. Tap them on the shoulder and shout. — 拍他们的肩膀并大喊。
  5. Push hard and fast. — 用力快速按压。
  6. Doing something is always better than doing nothing. — 做总比不做好。
  7. It will literally talk you through everything. — 它会一步步语音教你。
  8. You can't mess it up. — 你不会搞砸的。
  9. That's when you spring into action. — 那就是你行动的时候。
  10. Point at someone specific. — 指向一个具体的人。

了解即可 👂了解

  1. I'm certified in CPR. — 我有CPR证书。
  2. The Good Samaritan law protects you. — 好撒玛利亚人法会保护你。
  3. Apply direct pressure to the wound. — 对伤口直接施压。
  4. Do not remove an embedded object. — 不要拔出扎入的物体。

避免说法 ❌避免

  1. ~~Just leave them.~~ → Check if they're responsive first.（不能不管）
  2. ~~I'm not a doctor, I can't help.~~ → Even imperfect CPR helps.（不要袖手旁观）
  3. ~~Move the injured person.~~ → Don't move them unless there's danger.（不要随意移动）
  4. ~~Give them water.~~ → Don't give an unconscious person anything by mouth.（昏迷者不能喂水）

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国急救须知

  • Good Samaritan Law：各州都有好撒玛利亚人法，保护紧急情况下善意施救的人
  • CPR/AED认证：通常有效期2年，需要重新认证
  • 工作场所要求：很多工作要求持有First Aid/CPR证书
  • AED位置：大多数公共场所（商场、机场、办公楼）都配有AED
  • 911：拨打后接线员会指导你做急救

🇬🇧 英国急救须知

  • 999 / 112：紧急电话
  • St John Ambulance：最知名的急救培训机构
  • British Red Cross：也提供急救课程
  • NHS First Aid：nhs.uk上有免费急救指南
  • Defibrillator：英国更多叫defibrillator而非AED

💡 实用建议

  • 每个人都应该学习基础CPR和AED使用
  • 记住急救的关键步骤：检查安全→确认反应→呼救→开始CPR
  • AED非常智能，打开就有语音指导，不用怕用错
  • 课程中学到的"Stayin' Alive"节拍法非常实用
  • 定期复习和重新认证
  • 家里和车里准备一个急救包（first aid kit）
