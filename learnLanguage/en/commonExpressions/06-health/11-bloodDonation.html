第11章：献血（Blood Donation）

场景背景

在美国，献血（blood donation）通常由红十字会（American Red Cross）或当地血液中心组织。可以在流动献血车（blood drive）、社区中心、或固定献血中心进行。英国由NHS Blood and Transplant（NHSBT）组织。献血是免费的（你不会因此获得报酬），但在美国，有偿献血浆（plasma donation）是另一回事，通常由商业机构运营。

关键文化差异：

  • 🇺🇸 美国：由Red Cross主导，也可以在 Vitalant、New York Blood Center 等机构献血
  • 🇬🇧 英国：由 NHS Blood and Transplant 管理，通过 blood.co.uk 预约
  • 美国献血年龄17岁以上，没有上限；英国也是17岁以上，但65岁以上首次献血需要医生同意
  • 献血后通常会得到零食（cookies、juice）作为恢复补给

完整对话

子场景1：到达献血现场（Arriving at the Blood Drive）

Staff: Welcome! Are you here to donate today?

正式: Welcome. Are you here to donate blood today? 中文: 欢迎！你今天是来献血的吗？ 详解: "Donate" 在此语境中特指献血。"Donate today" 口语简洁。献血活动通常有醒目的标志。✅推荐

You: Yes, first time actually. A buddy of mine donates regularly and finally talked me into it.

正式: Yes, this is actually my first time. A friend donates regularly and persuaded me to participate. 中文: 是的，其实是第一次。我的一个朋友定期献血，终于说服我了。 详解: "First time" = 第一次。"Talked me into it" = 说服我（做某事）。"Buddy" = 哥们儿/朋友（男性间的口语称呼）。✅推荐

Staff: That's awesome! We love first-timers. I'm gonna walk you through the whole process. First, you'll need to read this information sheet and fill out a health history questionnaire. It's pretty straightforward — takes about 10 minutes. Got your ID on you?

正式: That is wonderful! We appreciate first-time donors. I will guide you through the entire process. First, please read this information sheet and complete a health history questionnaire. It is straightforward and takes approximately 10 minutes. Do you have your identification? 中文: 太棒了！我们很喜欢第一次来的人。我会带你走完整个流程。首先，你需要阅读这份信息表和填写健康史问卷。挺简单的——大约10分钟。带身份证了吗？ 详解: "Walk you through" = 带你过一遍/详细解释流程。"First-timers" = 第一次的人。"Health history questionnaire" = 健康史问卷。"Got your ID on you?" = 你身上带身份证了吗？✅推荐

You: Yeah, I've got my driver's license. Is there anything that would disqualify me from donating?

正式: Yes, I have my driver's license. Is there anything that would disqualify me from donating? 中文: 带了驾照。有什么情况会让我不能献血吗？ 详解: "Disqualify" = 取消资格/不合格。献血有严格的筛选标准，包括近期旅行、用药、健康状况等。✅推荐

Staff: We'll go through all of that in the screening. Generally, you need to be in good health, weigh at least 110 pounds, and haven't gotten a tattoo in the last three months — that's a big one. Also, if you've traveled to certain countries recently, that could be a temporary deferral. But don't worry, we'll check everything.

正式: We will review all eligibility criteria during screening. Generally, you must be in good health, weigh at least 110 pounds, and not have received a tattoo in the past three months. Recent travel to certain countries may result in a temporary deferral. We will verify everything. 中文: 我们会在筛查中全部检查的。一般来说，你需要身体健康，体重至少110磅（约50公斤），最近三个月没纹过身——这条很重要。还有，如果你最近去过某些国家，可能会被暂时推迟。但别担心，我们都会检查的。 详解: "Screening" = 筛查。"110 pounds" ≈ 50公斤。"Big one" = 重要的/关键的一条。"Deferral" = 推迟（不能献血的术语，不是说你有问题）。纹身后三个月不能献血是因为感染风险。✅推荐

子场景2：健康筛查（Health Screening）

Nurse: Hi, I'm Karen. I'll be doing your quick health check before you donate. Let me check your hemoglobin level first — I'm just gonna prick your finger. Small pinch.

正式: Hello, I am Karen. I will perform a brief health assessment before your donation. Let me first check your hemoglobin level — I will perform a finger prick. You will feel a small pinch. 中文: 你好，我是Karen。献血前我来给你做个简单的健康检查。先检查一下你的血红蛋白水平——我就扎一下手指。有一点刺痛。 详解: "Hemoglobin level" = 血红蛋白水平（检查是否贫血）。"Prick your finger" = 扎手指。献血前的标准检查。✅推荐

You: Ouch. Not my favorite part. Is the level okay?

正式: That was uncomfortable. Is the level satisfactory? 中文: 哎哟。不是我最喜欢的部分。指标没问题吧？ 详解: "Not my favorite part" = 不是我最喜欢的部分（幽默地说不喜欢）。"Ouch" = 疼痛的感叹词。✅推荐

Nurse: Hemoglobin is 14.2 — nice and healthy. You're good to go. I see you marked on the form that you take medication for blood pressure. What are you taking?

正式: Your hemoglobin is 14.2, which is healthy. You are eligible. I noted on your form that you take blood pressure medication. What medication are you taking? 中文: 血红蛋白14.2——很健康。没问题。我看到你表格上标注了你吃降压药。你吃的是什么？ 详解: "Nice and healthy" = 很好很健康（"nice and + adj." 是口语中强调正面评价的句型）。"Good to go" = 可以了/没问题。"Marked on the form" = 在表格上标注的。高血压药物不一定影响献血，但需要记录。✅推荐

You: Lisinopril, 10 milligrams. My doctor said it's fine to donate while taking it.

正式: Lisinopril, 10 milligrams. My physician confirmed it is acceptable to donate while taking this medication. 中文: Lisinopril，10毫克。我的医生说吃这个药可以献血。 详解: 直接说出药物名称和剂量。"Fine to donate while taking it" = 吃这个药可以献血。✅推荐

Nurse: That's correct, lisinopril is not a problem. Alright, everything checks out. Let me go over a few things — make sure you're well hydrated today, and after you donate, take it easy for the rest of the day. No heavy lifting or vigorous exercise. Sound good?

正式: That is correct, lisinopril does not present a problem. Everything is satisfactory. Please ensure you are well hydrated today, and after donation, rest for the remainder of the day. Avoid heavy lifting or vigorous exercise. Is that understood? 中文: 对的，lisinopril没问题。好的，一切正常。我再说几点——确保今天你水分充足，献完血后今天放松休息。不要提重物或剧烈运动。明白吗？ 详解: "Checks out" = 检查通过/没问题。"Well hydrated" = 充分补水。"Take it easy" = 放松/不要做太多。"Heavy lifting" = 提重物。"Sound good?" = 明白吗？/可以吗？✅推荐

子场景3：献血过程和恢复（Donation & Recovery）

Phlebotomist: Alright Wei, hop up on the chair. We're gonna draw from your left arm. I'll clean the area and then you'll feel a quick stick. Squeeze this little ball every few seconds to keep the blood flowing. The whole thing takes about 8 to 10 minutes.

正式: Please sit on the chair. We will draw blood from your left arm. I will clean the area, and you will feel a brief needle stick. Squeeze this ball every few seconds to maintain blood flow. The entire process takes approximately 8 to 10 minutes. 中文: 好了Wei，坐到椅子上。我们从你的左臂抽血。我给你消毒，然后你会感觉到一下扎。每隔几秒捏一下这个小球帮助血液流动。整个过程大约8到10分钟。 详解: "Hop up" = 上去/坐上去。"Draw from" = 从...抽血。"Quick stick" = 快速的扎（指针头刺入）。"Squeeze this ball" = 捏这个球。献血通常抽约1品脱（pint，约470毫升）。✅推荐

You: Okay. How will I know if something's not right?

正式: How will I know if something is wrong? 中文: 好的。我怎么知道有没有什么不对？ 详解: 担心献血过程中的问题，完全正常。✅推荐

Phlebotomist: If you feel dizzy, lightheaded, or nauseous at any point, just let me know right away — don't try to tough it out. We can pause or stop if needed. But most people do just fine. You're doing great — the bag's filling up nicely.

正式: If you experience dizziness, lightheadedness, or nausea at any point, please inform me immediately. Do not attempt to endure it. We can pause or stop if necessary. Most donors tolerate the procedure well. You are doing well — the collection bag is filling adequately. 中文: 如果任何时候你觉得头晕、头轻飘飘的、或者恶心，马上告诉我——不要硬撑。如果需要我们可以暂停或停止。但大多数人都没问题。你做得很好——袋子装得很好。 详解: "Lightheaded" = 头晕/轻飘飘的。"Nauseous" = 恶心。"Tough it out" = 硬撑/忍耐。"Bag's filling up nicely" = 袋子装得很好（轻松的鼓励）。✅推荐

You: Actually, I feel fine. It's really not that bad.

正式: I feel fine. It is not as uncomfortable as I anticipated. 中文: 其实我感觉还好。真的没那么糟。 详解: "Not that bad" = 没那么糟。大多数献血者确实感觉不糟糕。✅推荐

Phlebotomist: And... we're done! Hold this gauze firmly for a couple of minutes. I'm gonna put a bandage on it. Head over to the refreshment area — grab some juice and cookies. Stay there for at least 15 minutes to make sure you're feeling okay before you take off.

正式: We are finished. Hold this gauze firmly for several minutes. I will apply a bandage. Please proceed to the refreshment area for juice and cookies. Remain there for at least 15 minutes to ensure you feel well before leaving. 中文: 好了... 完成了！按压这个纱布几分钟。我给你贴上绷带。去休息区——喝点果汁吃点饼干。至少在那里待15分钟，确认感觉好了再走。 详解: "Gauze" = 纱布。"Take off" = 离开。献血后必须在现场休息15分钟，喝饮料吃零食补充能量。这是标准流程。✅推荐

You: Will do. Thanks for making it so easy. How often can I do this?

正式: I will. Thank you for making the process easy. How frequently may I donate? 中文: 好的。谢谢你们让这变得这么简单。多久可以献一次？ 详解: 询问下次献血时间。✅推荐

Phlebotomist: You can donate whole blood every 56 days — that's about every eight weeks. We'll send you a reminder when you're eligible again. And hey — thanks for coming in. You might've just saved up to three lives today.

正式: You may donate whole blood every 56 days, approximately every eight weeks. We will send a reminder when you are eligible again. Thank you for donating. You may have saved up to three lives today. 中文: 全血每56天可以献一次——大约每八周。你有资格再献的时候我们会发提醒。嘿——谢谢你来了。你今天可能救了多达三条命。 详解: "Whole blood" = 全血（区别于血浆或血小板捐赠）。"Eligible" = 有资格的。"Up to three lives" = 多达三条命（一袋全血可以分为红细胞、血浆、血小板，分别给不同病人）。✅推荐

核心口语表达清单

必学表达 ✅推荐

  1. A buddy of mine talked me into it. — 我一个哥们儿说服我的。
  2. Walk me through the process. — 带我走一遍流程。
  3. Is there anything that would disqualify me? — 有什么情况会让我不合格吗？
  4. Everything checks out. — 一切正常。
  5. Make sure you're well hydrated. — 确保你水分充足。
  6. Take it easy for the rest of the day. — 今天剩余时间放松休息。
  7. Don't try to tough it out. — 不要硬撑。
  8. You might've just saved three lives. — 你可能刚救了三条命。
  9. How often can I donate? — 多久可以献一次？
  10. It's really not that bad. — 真的没那么糟。

了解即可 👂了解

  1. I'm a universal donor. — 我是万能输血者（O型阴性）。
  2. I've been deferred. — 我被推迟了（暂时不能献）。
  3. Can I donate platelets instead? — 我可以献血小板吗？
  4. I've got a rare blood type. — 我的血型很稀有。

避免说法 ❌避免

  1. ~~Take my blood.~~ → I'd like to donate blood.（太直接）
  2. ~~How much do you pay?~~ → Is this a volunteer donation?（美国献血不付费）
  3. ~~I'm scared of the needle.~~ → I'm a little nervous, but I want to do this.（更积极）
  4. ~~I feel like dying.~~ → I'm feeling a bit lightheaded.（太夸张）

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国献血须知

  • Red Cross：最大的血液收集机构，网站 redcrossblood.org
  • 资格：17岁以上，110磅以上，身体健康
  • 频率：全血每56天，血小板每7天（每年最多24次）
  • 无报酬：美国法律禁止有偿献血（血浆除外）
  • 带什么：带照片的ID，穿短袖（方便抽血）

🇬🇧 英国献血须知

  • NHS Blood and Transplant：管理献血，网站 blood.co.uk
  • 资格：17-65岁（首次），65岁以上需医生同意
  • 频率：男性每12周，女性每16周
  • 预约：网上预约，也有walk-in时段
  • Welsh Blood Service：威尔士有独立的血液服务

💡 实用建议

  • 献血前多喝水，吃一顿好饭（不要空腹）
  • 穿宽松的短袖衣服
  • 献血后在休息区待够15分钟
  • 当天多喝水，避免剧烈运动
  • 如果感觉头晕，立即坐下或躺下
  • 知道自己的血型很有用（O型阴性是万能输血者）
