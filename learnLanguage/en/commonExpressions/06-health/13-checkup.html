第13章：年度体检（Annual Physical / Health Checkup）

场景背景

50岁是健康管理的关键年龄。美国推荐每年做一次全面体检（annual physical / wellness exam），通常由家庭医生（primary care physician / PCP）进行。体检项目包括：生命体征、血液检查（血脂、血糖、肝肾功能）、癌症筛查（结肠癌筛查从45岁开始）、前列腺检查等。英国NHS的NHS Health Check是40-74岁之间每5年一次免费健康检查。

50岁男性特别关注：

  • 结肠镜检查（colonoscopy）：45-50岁开始，每10年一次
  • 前列腺特异性抗原（PSA）检测：与医生讨论是否需要
  • 血脂检查（cholesterol/lipid panel）
  • 血糖（blood sugar / A1C）
  • 血压监测
  • 体重和BMI

关键文化差异：

  • 🇺🇸 美国：年度体检通常由保险100%覆盖（preventive care / wellness visit）
  • 🇬🇧 英国：NHS Health Check每5年一次，更频繁的检查需和GP讨论
  • 美国体检更全面，英国更注重有症状时才检查
  • 结肠镜在美国是50岁体检的重要项目，在英国也从50岁开始邀请

完整对话

子场景1：预约年度体检（Scheduling the Physical）

You: Hi, I'd like to schedule my annual physical. I'm turning 51 this year, so I figure I should get a thorough checkup.

正式: Hello, I would like to schedule my annual physical examination. I am turning 51 this year, so I believe a thorough checkup is warranted. 中文: 你好，我想预约年度体检。我今年51了，我觉得应该做一个全面的检查。 详解: "Annual physical" = 年度体检。"Turning 51" = 即将51岁/今年51岁。"Thorough checkup" = 全面检查。"I figure" = 我觉得/我认为。✅推荐

Receptionist: Absolutely, that's a great idea. Since you're over 50, Dr. Patel will probably want to run a comprehensive blood panel and discuss some age-appropriate screenings. We have openings next Tuesday at 9 AM or Thursday at 2 PM.

正式: That is an excellent idea. Since you are over 50, Dr. Patel will likely order a comprehensive blood panel and discuss age-appropriate screenings. We have availability next Tuesday at 9 AM or Thursday at 2 PM. 中文: 当然，这个想法很好。既然你超过50岁了，Patel医生可能要做全面的血液检查，还会讨论一些适合年龄的筛查。下周二上午9点或周四下午2点有空档。 详解: "Comprehensive blood panel" = 全面血液检查。"Age-appropriate screenings" = 适合年龄的筛查项目。"Run" 在医疗语境中指"做（检查）"。✅推荐

You: Tuesday at 9 works. Do I need to fast for the blood work?

正式: Tuesday at 9 AM is convenient. Do I need to fast for the blood test? 中文: 周二上午9点可以。验血需要空腹吗？ 详解: "Fast" = 空腹/禁食。血脂和血糖检查通常需要空腹8-12小时。这是非常实际的问题。✅推荐

Receptionist: Yes, please fast for at least 10 hours before your appointment — no food, just water. That way we can get accurate readings on your cholesterol and blood sugar. If you take regular medications, you can still take those with a sip of water unless Dr. Patel says otherwise.

正式: Yes, please fast for at least 10 hours prior to your appointment — no food, only water. This ensures accurate cholesterol and blood sugar readings. You may take regular medications with a small amount of water unless instructed otherwise. 中文: 是的，预约前至少空腹10小时——不吃东西，只喝水。这样胆固醇和血糖读数才准确。如果你平时吃药，可以用一小口水吃药，除非Patel医生另有指示。 详解: "Accurate readings" = 准确读数。"Sip of water" = 一小口水。"Unless... says otherwise" = 除非...另有说法。空腹体检的标准建议。✅推荐

子场景2：体检当天（During the Physical）

Dr. Patel: Good morning, Wei. How have you been since I last saw you? Any new concerns?

正式: Good morning, Wei. How have you been since your last visit? Do you have any new concerns? 中文: 早上好，Wei。上次见面以来怎么样？有什么新的担忧吗？ 详解: 医生的标准开场。"Any new concerns?" = 有什么新的担忧吗？体检时会先问一般情况。✅推荐

You: Overall pretty good. But I've noticed I've been waking up to use the bathroom more often at night — like two, sometimes three times. And I've put on a few pounds over the last year.

正式: Overall I am feeling well. However, I have been waking to urinate more frequently at night — approximately two to three times. I have also gained some weight over the past year. 中文: 总体挺好。但我注意到我晚上起来上厕所更频繁了——两到三次。过去一年还长了几磅。 详解: "Put on a few pounds" = 长了几磅/胖了一点。"Waking up to use the bathroom at night" = 夜间起夜上厕所（50岁男性常见问题，可能与前列腺有关）。用 "use the bathroom" 比 "urinate" 更口语化。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: Those are both really common at your age. The nighttime urination could be related to your prostate — as men get older, the prostate tends to enlarge, which can press on the bladder. We'll check your PSA levels with the blood work. As for the weight, let's see where your numbers are.

正式: Those are both common at your age. The nighttime urination may be related to your prostate — benign prostatic enlargement can press on the bladder. We will check your PSA levels via blood work. Regarding the weight, let us review your results. 中文: 这些在你这个年纪都很常见。夜间尿频可能和你的前列腺有关——随着年龄增长，前列腺倾向于增大，可能会压迫膀胱。验血时我们会查你的PSA水平。至于体重，让我看看你的数字。 详解: "Prostate" = 前列腺。"Enlarged prostate" = 前列腺增大/肥大（BPH）。"PSA" = Prostate-Specific Antigen（前列腺特异性抗原），筛查前列腺问题的指标。"Where your numbers are" = 你的各项指标在哪里（口语化的说法）。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: Let's do a quick physical exam. I'm gonna listen to your heart and lungs... Heart sounds good, lungs are clear. Let me check your abdomen... Your liver feels normal in size. Now I'd like to do a quick prostate exam — I know it's nobody's favorite part, but it's important at your age.

正式: Let me perform a physical examination. I will auscultate your heart and lungs — they sound normal. Your liver feels normal in size. I would like to perform a prostate examination — it is an important screening at your age. 中文: 我们做个快速体检。我来听听你的心和肺... 心音正常，肺部清晰。让我检查一下腹部... 肝脏大小正常。我想做一个快速前列腺检查——我知道这不是任何人的最爱，但对你这个年纪很重要。 详解: "Heart sounds good" = 心音正常。"Nobody's favorite part" = 没人的最爱（幽默地承认前列腺检查不舒服）。直肠指检（DRE）是50岁以上男性体检的标准项目。✅推荐

You: I figured it was coming. Let's just get it over with.

正式: I expected this. Let us proceed. 中文: 我就知道会有这一步。赶紧做完吧。 详解: "I figured" = 我猜到了/我就知道。"Coming" = 会到来的。"Get it over with" = 做完了事。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: It'll just take a moment... Alright, all done. Everything feels normal as far as I can tell. Now let's talk about the big one — colon cancer screening. Have you ever had a colonoscopy?

正式: That is complete. Everything feels normal. Now let us discuss colon cancer screening. Have you ever had a colonoscopy? 中文: 只要一下... 好了，完成了。我感觉一切正常。现在说说重要的事——结肠癌筛查。你做过结肠镜吗？ 详解: "The big one" = 重要的事/大事。"As far as I can tell" = 就我能判断的而言。"Colonoscopy" = 结肠镜检查。45-50岁开始推荐每10年做一次。✅推荐

You: No, never. I've been kind of putting it off, to be honest.

正式: No, I have never had one. I have been postponing it, to be honest. 中文: 没有，从没做过。说实话我一直在拖。 详解: "Putting it off" = 拖延/推迟。"To be honest" = 说实话。很多人拖延结肠镜检查，这很常见但不好。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: You're definitely not alone in that. But here's the thing — colon cancer is one of the most preventable cancers when caught early. A colonoscopy is the gold standard. You'll be sedated — you won't feel a thing, and you won't even remember it. The prep the day before is the worst part, and even that's gotten much better in recent years.

正式: Many people delay screening. However, colon cancer is highly preventable when detected early. A colonoscopy is the gold standard. You will be sedated and will not feel any discomfort. The bowel preparation the day before is the most unpleasant aspect, but it has improved significantly. 中文: 你绝对不是唯一一个。但事实是这样的——结肠癌如果早期发现是最可预防的癌症之一。结肠镜是金标准。你会被麻醉——你什么都感觉不到，甚至都不记得。前一天的准备是最糟糕的部分，但近年来已经好多了。 详解: "You're not alone in that" = 你不是唯一一个。"Here's the thing" = 事实是这样的/关键在于。"Gold standard" = 金标准（最好的检查方法）。"Sedated" = 被麻醉/镇静。"Prep" = 准备工作（这里指清肠准备）。✅推荐

You: What about that at-home test? I've seen ads for Cologuard.

正式: What about the at-home test? I have seen advertisements for Cologuard. 中文: 那个在家做的检测呢？我看到过Cologuard的广告。 详解: "At-home test" = 在家做的检测。Cologuard 是一种粪便DNA检测，可以在家采样邮寄。"Seen ads for" = 看到...的广告。主动了解替代方案很好。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: Cologuard is a good option if you're really opposed to a colonoscopy. It checks for DNA markers in your stool. But keep in mind — if it comes back positive, you'll still need a colonoscopy. And it needs to be repeated every three years, versus every ten for a colonoscopy. I'd recommend going with the colonoscopy, but the choice is yours.

正式: Cologuard is a reasonable alternative if you are opposed to colonoscopy. It detects DNA markers in stool samples. However, a positive result would still require a colonoscopy. It must be repeated every three years versus every ten for colonoscopy. I recommend colonoscopy, but the choice is yours. 中文: 如果你真的很反对做结肠镜，Cologuard是个好选择。它检查粪便中的DNA标记。但记住——如果结果是阳性的，你还是需要做结肠镜。而且它每三年要做一次，结肠镜每十年一次。我建议做结肠镜，但选择在你。 详解: "Opposed to" = 反对/排斥。"Comes back positive" = 结果是阳性的。"Versus" = 对比。"The choice is yours" = 选择在你。医生给了选择权但明确表达了建议。✅推荐

子场景3：讨论血液检查结果（Reviewing Blood Work Results）

Dr. Patel: (一周后电话) Wei, your blood work came back. Let me give you the highlights. Your cholesterol is a little elevated — total cholesterol is 223, and your LDL — that's the bad one — is at 148. We'd like to see that under 100. Your blood sugar, or A1C, is 5.7, which is in the prediabetes range. Nothing to panic about, but it's a wake-up call.

正式: Your blood work results are available. Your total cholesterol is slightly elevated at 223, and your LDL is 148, which should be below 100. Your A1C is 5.7, indicating prediabetes. This is not cause for panic, but it warrants attention. 中文: Wei，你的血液结果出来了。我给你说重点。你的胆固醇有点高——总胆固醇223，LDL——就是坏的那种——148。我们希望那个在100以下。你的血糖，也就是A1C，是5.7，处于糖尿病前期范围。不需要恐慌，但这是一个警钟。 详解: "Highlights" = 重点/关键信息。"LDL" = 低密度脂蛋白（坏胆固醇）。"A1C" = 糖化血红蛋白（反映3个月的平均血糖水平）。"Prediabetes" = 糖尿病前期。"Wake-up call" = 警钟/警醒。"Nothing to panic about" = 不必恐慌。✅推荐

You: Prediabetes? That sounds serious. What do I need to do?

正式: Prediabetes? That sounds serious. What action is required? 中文: 糖尿病前期？听起来挺严重的。我需要做什么？ 详解: "What do I need to do?" = 我需要做什么？积极面对问题的好态度。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: The good news is, prediabetes is reversible. You can bring those numbers down with diet and exercise. Cut back on processed foods and added sugars, try to get 150 minutes of moderate exercise a week — even brisk walking counts. Losing just 5 to 10 percent of your body weight can make a huge difference. I'd like to recheck in three months to see how you're doing.

正式: The encouraging news is that prediabetes is reversible through diet and exercise. Reduce processed foods and added sugars, aim for 150 minutes of moderate exercise weekly — even brisk walking is beneficial. Losing 5 to 10 percent of body weight can significantly improve results. I recommend retesting in three months. 中文: 好消息是，糖尿病前期是可以逆转的。你可以通过饮食和运动降低这些指标。减少加工食品和添加糖，每周尽量做150分钟中等强度运动——快走都算。只要减掉体重的5%到10%就有很大区别。我想三个月后复查看看你的情况。 详解: "Reversible" = 可逆转的/可恢复的。"Cut back on" = 减少。"Processed foods" = 加工食品。"Brisk walking counts" = 快走也算。"Make a huge difference" = 产生巨大区别。这些都是非常实用的健康建议。✅推荐

You: Okay, I can do that. What about the cholesterol? Do I need medication?

正式: What about the cholesterol? Do I require medication? 中文: 好的，这个我能做到。胆固醇呢？需要吃药吗？ 详解: 问是否需要用药是常见的合理问题。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: Let's try lifestyle changes first. If your numbers don't budge in three months, we can talk about a statin. But honestly, with some dietary changes and regular exercise, a lot of my patients are able to bring their cholesterol down without medication. You've got this.

正式: Let us attempt lifestyle modifications first. If there is no improvement in three months, we can discuss statin medication. Many patients successfully reduce cholesterol through diet and exercise alone. 中文: 我们先试试改变生活方式。如果三个月后数据没动静，我们可以讨论用他汀类药物。但说实话，通过一些饮食改变和规律运动，我的很多病人都能不吃药把胆固醇降下来。你可以的。 详解: "Don't budge" = 没有变化/不动。"Statin" = 他汀类药物（降胆固醇药）。"You've got this" = 你可以的（鼓励用语）。✅推荐

核心口语表达清单

必学表达 ✅推荐

  1. I'd like to schedule my annual physical. — 我想预约年度体检。
  2. Do I need to fast for the blood work? — 验血需要空腹吗？
  3. I've put on a few pounds. — 我长了几磅。
  4. It's a wake-up call. — 这是一个警钟。
  5. Prediabetes is reversible. — 糖尿病前期可以逆转。
  6. Cut back on processed foods. — 减少加工食品。
  7. Let's try lifestyle changes first. — 先试试改变生活方式。
  8. If numbers don't budge, we can talk about medication. — 如果数据没变化，可以讨论用药。
  9. You've got this. — 你可以的。
  10. A colonoscopy is the gold standard. — 结肠镜是金标准。

了解即可 👂了解

  1. I'm due for a colonoscopy. — 我该做结肠镜了。
  2. What's my risk factor? — 我的危险因素是什么？
  3. I have a family history of... — 我家有...的家族史。
  4. Is this covered under preventive care? — 这个算预防性检查，保险覆盖吗？

避免说法 ❌避免

  1. ~~I don't need checkup, I feel fine.~~ → I'd like to stay on top of my health.（预防很重要）
  2. ~~Am I going to die?~~ → Is this something to be concerned about?（太极端）
  3. ~~Just give me pills.~~ → What can I do to improve my numbers?（更积极）
  4. ~~I'm too old to change.~~ → It's never too late to make changes.（不要放弃）

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国年度体检须知

  • Preventive care：保险通常100%覆盖年度体检和推荐筛查
  • Colonoscopy：45-50岁开始，每10年一次（如果有家族史可能更早更频繁）
  • PSA争议：是否做PSA检测需要和医生讨论利弊
  • 结果解读：医生会通过患者门户（patient portal）发结果
  • Follow-up：如果指标异常，医生会安排复查

🇬🇧 英国健康检查须知

  • NHS Health Check：40-74岁每5年一次免费
  • Bowel cancer screening：从50-54岁开始（英格兰），使用粪便免疫化学检测（FIT）
  • 前列腺检查：NHS不常规筛查PSA，有症状时才检查
  • GP讨论：可以主动和GP讨论你的健康担忧
  • 私人体检：如Bupa、Nuffield Health提供全面体检（约£300-600）

💡 实用建议

  • 50岁以后不要跳过年度体检
  • 提前写下你想和医生讨论的问题
  • 了解你的家族病史（父母、兄弟姐妹的疾病史）
  • 关注腰围——腹部肥胖（belly fat）比体重更能反映健康风险
  • 三大可预防疾病：心脏病、糖尿病、结肠癌——都可以通过体检早期发现
  • 如果有糖尿病前期，认真对待生活方式改变——它真的可以逆转
