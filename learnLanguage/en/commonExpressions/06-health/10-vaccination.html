第10章：疫苗接种（Vaccination）

场景背景

在美国，疫苗接种（vaccination/immunization）可以在多个地方完成：医生诊所、药房（CVS/Walgreens）、社区卫生中心等。50岁左右建议接种的疫苗包括：年度流感疫苗（flu shot）、COVID加强针（COVID booster）、带状疱疹疫苗（shingles vaccine，50岁以上推荐）、破伤风加强针（Tdap，每10年一次）等。英国的NHS提供免费疫苗接种，药房和GP诊所都可以打。

关键文化差异：

  • 🇺🇸 美国：疫苗可以在药房直接打，不需要预约医生，很多保险100%覆盖
  • 🇬🇧 英国：NHS免费提供推荐疫苗，GP或药房都可以打
  • 美国人口语中叫 "shot" 而不是 "injection"
  • "Flu shot"（流感疫苗）是每年秋天最常见的疫苗接种

完整对话

子场景1：在药房打流感疫苗（Flu Shot at Pharmacy）

Pharmacist: Hi there! What can I help you with today?

正式: Hello! How may I assist you today? 中文: 你好！今天有什么可以帮你的？ 详解: 药房的标准问候。流感季节（秋冬季），药房门口通常会有 "Flu shots available here" 的标志。✅推荐

You: I'd like to get a flu shot. Do I need an appointment?

正式: I would like to receive a flu vaccination. Do I need an appointment? 中文: 我想打流感疫苗。需要预约吗？ 详解: "Get a flu shot" = 打流感疫苗。"Shot" 在医疗语境中指注射/打针，比 "injection" 口语化得多。在美国药房打流感疫苗通常不需要预约（walk-in即可）。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Nope, walk-ins are welcome! Just fill out this quick form — it asks about allergies and whether you've had a reaction to vaccines before. Do you have your insurance card?

正式: No appointment is necessary — walk-ins are welcome. Please complete this brief form regarding allergies and prior vaccine reactions. Do you have your insurance card? 中文: 不需要，直接来就行！填一下这个简单的表格——问的是过敏情况和以前是否对疫苗有反应。你有保险卡吗？ 详解: "Walk-ins are welcome" = 直接来就可以/无需预约。"Fill out this quick form" = 填一下这个简单的表格。"Reaction to vaccines" = 对疫苗的反应。✅推荐

You: Sure, here's my card. I've never had a bad reaction to a flu shot before. The only thing is, I always get a sore arm afterwards — is that normal?

正式: Here is my card. I have never experienced an adverse reaction to a flu vaccine. However, I consistently experience arm soreness afterwards — is that normal? 中文: 好的，这是我的卡。我以前打流感疫苗没出现过不良反应。唯一的是，之后胳膊总是酸痛——这正常吗？ 详解: "Bad reaction" = 不良反应。"Sore arm" = 胳膊酸痛，打完疫苗最常见的副作用。"Afterwards" = 之后。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Totally normal. That's just your immune system doing its thing. Some people also get a mild fever or feel a little run down for a day or two, but that's about it. You're doing the right thing — the CDC recommends everyone six months and older get a flu shot every year.

正式: That is completely normal. It indicates your immune system is responding. Some individuals also experience a mild fever or fatigue for one to two days. The CDC recommends annual flu vaccination for everyone aged six months and older. 中文: 完全正常。那只是你的免疫系统在起作用。有些人还会轻度发烧或感觉一两天有点累，但也就这样了。你做得对——CDC建议6个月以上的人每年打流感疫苗。 详解: "Doing its thing" = 在做它该做的事（口语化）。"Run down" = 疲惫/虚弱。"That's about it" = 大概就这样/没什么别的了。CDC = Centers for Disease Control and Prevention（美国疾病控制与预防中心）。✅推荐

You: Good to know. What strain does this year's shot cover?

正式: What strains does this year's vaccine cover? 中文: 好的。今年的疫苗覆盖什么毒株？ 详解: "Strain" = 毒株。"Shot" 这里指疫苗。流感疫苗每年更新，根据WHO预测的流行毒株制备。✅推荐

Pharmacist: This season's formula targets three influenza strains — two A types and one B type. They update it every year based on what's circulating. Alright, are you ready? It'll be a quick pinch in your left arm. Try to relax your shoulder for me.

正式: This season's vaccine targets three influenza strains — two type A and one type B. It is updated annually based on circulating strains. Are you ready? You will feel a brief pinch in your left arm. Please relax your shoulder. 中文: 本季的配方针对三种流感毒株——两种A型和一种B型。每年根据流行情况更新。好了，准备好了吗？左臂很快会有一阵刺痛。肩膀放松。 详解: "Quick pinch" = 快速刺痛（打针时的感觉）。"Relax your shoulder" = 放松肩膀（肌肉紧绷会更疼）。✅推荐

You: Yeah, go ahead. I'm not great with needles but let's just get it over with.

正式: Yes, proceed. I am not comfortable with needles, but let us complete this. 中文: 好，来吧。我不太擅长应付针头，但干脆打完了事。 详解: "Go ahead" = 来吧/开始吧。"Not great with needles" = 不太喜欢打针/怕针。"Get it over with" = 做完了事/赶紧结束。很多人不喜欢打针，这样承认很正常。✅推荐

Pharmacist: And... we're done! That wasn't so bad, right? Keep the bandage on for a little while. Try to move your arm around today — it actually helps with the soreness. You're all set!

正式: We are finished. That was not too uncomfortable, correct? Keep the bandage on briefly. Moving your arm today will help reduce soreness. You are all set. 中文: 好了... 打完了！没那么糟对吧？绷带保留一会儿。今天多活动活动胳膊——其实有助于缓解酸痛。搞定了！ 详解: "We're done" = 完成了/搞定了。"Wasn't so bad" = 没那么糟。"All set" = 搞定了/一切妥当。✅推荐

子场景2：询问其他疫苗（Asking About Other Vaccines）

You: Hey, while I'm here — I turned 50 last year. Are there any other vaccines I should be thinking about?

正式: While I am here, I turned 50 last year. Are there other vaccines I should consider? 中文: 嘿，既然我来了——我去年50了。还有什么其他疫苗我应该考虑的吗？ 详解: "While I'm here" = 既然我来了（利用现有机会）。"Thinking about" = 考虑。主动询问推荐疫苗是好的健康管理习惯。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Great question. At 50, the CDC recommends the shingles vaccine — it's a two-dose series called Shingrix. It's really effective. You should also stay up to date on your COVID boosters, and if it's been more than 10 years since your last tetanus shot, you're due for a Tdap booster.

正式: Excellent question. At 50, the CDC recommends the shingles vaccine — a two-dose series called Shingrix. It is highly effective. You should also maintain current COVID booster vaccinations, and if more than 10 years have passed since your last tetanus vaccination, you are due for a Tdap booster. 中文: 好问题。50岁时，CDC推荐带状疱疹疫苗——两针系列，叫Shingrix。效果很好。你还应该保持COVID加强针的更新，如果离你上次破伤风针超过10年了，该打Tdap加强针了。 详解: "Shingles vaccine" = 带状疱疹疫苗（50岁以上推荐）。"Two-dose series" = 两针系列。"Stay up to date" = 保持最新/及时更新。"Due for" = 该做...了/到了...的时候。"Tdap" = 破伤风、白喉、百日咳联合疫苗。✅推荐

You: I've heard the shingles vaccine can really knock you out for a day or two. Is that true?

正式: I have heard the shingles vaccine can cause significant side effects for one to two days. Is that accurate? 中文: 我听说带状疱疹疫苗能让你一两天动不了。是真的吗？ 详解: "Knock you out" = 让你倒下/让你很不舒服（口语）。Shingrix的副作用确实比流感疫苗重，可能出现发烧、肌肉酸痛、疲劳等。✅推荐

Pharmacist: It's not uncommon to feel pretty rough for a day or so — sore arm, fatigue, maybe a low-grade fever. But it beats getting shingles, trust me. Most people schedule it for a Friday so they can rest over the weekend. I'd recommend the same.

正式: It is not uncommon to experience discomfort for approximately one day — arm soreness, fatigue, and possibly a low-grade fever. However, it is preferable to contracting shingles. Most people schedule it for a Friday to rest over the weekend. 中文: 觉得一整天不舒服并不罕见——胳膊酸痛、疲劳、可能低烧。但这比得带状疱疹好多了，相信我。大多数人安排在周五打，这样周末可以休息。我建议你也这样。 详解: "Feel pretty rough" = 感觉很不好/很难受。"It beats getting shingles" = 比得带状疱疹好（"beats" = 比...好）。"Low-grade fever" = 低烧。这是非常实用的建议。✅推荐

You: That's smart. Can I get the shingles vaccine today too, or do I need to wait?

正式: That is a sensible approach. Can I receive the shingles vaccine today, or must I wait? 中文: 这主意不错。我今天也能打带状疱疹疫苗吗，还是要等？ 详解: 问多种疫苗是否可以同时打。✅推荐

Pharmacist: You can actually get both at the same time, but honestly, I'd space them out — get the flu shot today and come back next week for Shingrix. That way your body only has to deal with one set of side effects at a time. The second Shingrix dose comes two to six months after the first.

正式: You can receive both simultaneously, but I recommend spacing them apart. Receive the flu shot today and return next week for Shingrix. This allows your body to process one set of side effects at a time. The second Shingrix dose is administered two to six months after the first. 中文: 其实可以同时打两种，但说实话，我建议间隔开——今天打流感疫苗，下周回来打Shingrix。这样你的身体一次只需要处理一组副作用。第二针Shingrix在第一针后2到6个月打。 详解: "Space them out" = 间隔开/分开打。"Deal with" = 处理/应对。"One set of side effects at a time" = 一次处理一组副作用。✅推荐

核心口语表达清单

必学表达 ✅推荐

  1. I'd like to get a flu shot. — 我想打流感疫苗。
  2. Walk-ins are welcome. — 直接来就行，无需预约。
  3. I always get a sore arm afterwards. — 之后胳膊总是酸痛。
  4. Let's just get it over with. — 干脆打完了事。
  5. Are there any other vaccines I should be thinking about? — 还有什么疫苗我该考虑的？
  6. You're due for a booster. — 你该打加强针了。
  7. It can knock you out for a day. — 能让你一整天动不了。
  8. It beats getting shingles. — 比得带状疱疹好多了。
  9. I'd space them out. — 我建议间隔开。
  10. Stay up to date on your vaccines. — 保持疫苗及时更新。

了解即可 👂了解

  1. I'm not great with needles. — 我不太擅长应付针头。
  2. I've got a weak stomach for shots. — 我一打针就受不了。
  3. Do you offer the nasal spray version? — 你们有鼻腔喷雾版的吗？
  4. I'm allergic to eggs. — 我对鸡蛋过敏。（某些流感疫苗不能用）

避免说法 ❌避免

  1. ~~Give me injection.~~ → I'd like to get a shot.（太生硬）
  2. ~~I don't believe in vaccines.~~ → I have some concerns about vaccines.（如果真的有顾虑，礼貌表达）
  3. ~~Vaccines make you sick.~~ → I had a reaction last time.（区分副作用和生病）
  4. ~~I'm too healthy for flu shot.~~ → I'd like to stay healthy with a flu shot.（逻辑不对）

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国疫苗须知

  • 药房接种：CVS、Walgreens、Walmart、Costco等都可以打疫苗，不需要去诊所
  • 保险覆盖：大多数保险100%覆盖推荐疫苗（无copay）
  • 没有保险：社区卫生中心（community health center）提供低价或免费疫苗
  • Vaccine card：保存好你的疫苗记录卡
  • Flu season：每年9月到次年3月，最佳接种时间是10月底之前

🇬🇧 英国疫苗须知

  • NHS免费：所有推荐疫苗免费
  • GP或药房：都可以接种
  • NHS邀请信：到了推荐年龄会收到NHS的邀请信
  • "Jab"：英国人口语中说打针用 "jab"（如 "flu jab"）
  • Autumn booster：秋季加强针计划

💡 实用建议

  • 50岁一定要打带状疱疹疫苗（Shingrix），效果非常好
  • 每年秋天打流感疫苗
  • 保持破伤风疫苗更新（每10年）
  • 打完疫苗在药房等15分钟，观察有无严重过敏反应
  • 打完Shingrix安排在周五，周末可以休息
  • 保存好所有疫苗记录
