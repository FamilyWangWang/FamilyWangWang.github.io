第5章：看牙医（Dentist Visit）

场景背景

美国的牙科保险（dental insurance）通常和医疗保险分开，需要单独购买。看牙是美国人非常重视的事情，一般建议每半年洗一次牙（cleaning）。英国NHS提供牙科服务但需付费（Band 1约£26.80），私人牙医更贵。对于50岁左右的人来说，牙齿问题（牙龈萎缩、牙齿敏感、牙周病）开始增多，了解牙科口语很重要。

关键文化差异：

  • 🇺🇸 美国：牙科保险通常有年度上限（annual maximum，通常$1000-$2000），洗牙通常100%覆盖
  • 🇬🇧 英国：NHS牙科分三个费用等级（Band 1/2/3），私人牙医更贵
  • 美国人对牙齿美观非常重视，正畸（braces）和美白（whitening）很常见
  • 牙医的称呼：dentist，助手叫 dental hygienist（牙科洁牙师/助手）

完整对话

子场景1：预约和前台登记

Receptionist: Bright Smile Dental, this is Jessica. How can I help you?

正式: Bright Smile Dental, this is Jessica. How may I assist you? 中文: Bright Smile牙科诊所，我是Jessica。有什么可以帮您的？ 详解: 牙科诊所接电话的标准格式。"Dental" 这里是诊所名称的一部分。✅推荐

You: Hi, I'd like to set up an appointment for a cleaning and a checkup. It's been a while since my last visit.

正式: Hello, I would like to schedule an appointment for a dental cleaning and examination. It has been some time since my last visit. 中文: 你好，我想预约洗牙和检查。我上次去已经有一段时间了。 详解: "Set up an appointment" = 预约，和 "make an appointment" 类似。"Cleaning" = 洗牙。"Checkup" = 检查。"It's been a while" = 有段时间了，含蓄地承认拖了很久。✅推荐

Receptionist: No worries. Are you an existing patient or are you new to our practice?

正式: That is not a problem. Are you an existing patient or new to our practice? 中文: 没关系。您是我们的老病人还是新病人？ 详解: "No worries" = 没关系/不用担心（非常口语化）。"Practice" 在医疗语境中指诊所/执业机构。"Existing patient" = 老病人。✅推荐

You: I'm new. A friend of mine recommended Dr. Kim.

正式: I am a new patient. A friend recommended Dr. Kim. 中文: 我是新病人。一个朋友推荐了Kim医生。 详解: "Recommended" = 推荐，病人推荐（word of mouth）是美国找医生的重要方式。✅推荐

Receptionist: Great choice! Dr. Kim's got an opening next Wednesday at 10 AM or Thursday at 3:30 PM. Any preference?

正式: Excellent choice! Dr. Kim has availability next Wednesday at 10 AM or Thursday at 3:30 PM. Do you have a preference? 中文: 好选择！Kim医生下周三上午10点或周四下午3:30有空。有偏好吗？ 详解: "Great choice" 是对选择的正面反馈。"Got an opening" = 有空位。"Any preference?" = 有偏好吗？✅推荐

You: Wednesday works. Do you take Delta Dental?

正式: Wednesday is convenient. Do you accept Delta Dental insurance? 中文: 周三可以。你们接受Delta Dental保险吗？ 详解: "Take" 在保险语境中 = 接受（某保险）。Delta Dental 是美国最大的牙科保险公司之一。✅推荐

Receptionist: We sure do. Just bring your insurance card and a photo ID. And plan to arrive about 20 minutes early for the new-patient paperwork.

正式: We certainly do. Please bring your insurance card and a photo ID. Plan to arrive approximately 20 minutes early to complete the new-patient paperwork. 中文: 当然接受。带上你的保险卡和有照片的身份证件。提前大约20分钟到，需要填新病人表格。 详解: "We sure do" = 我们当然接受（口语化肯定）。"Paperwork" = 表格/文件。✅推荐

子场景2：洗牙和检查（Cleaning & Exam）

Dental Hygienist: Hi, I'm Sarah, I'll be your hygienist today. Hop up in the chair and we'll get started. When was your last cleaning?

正式: Hello, I am Sarah, your dental hygienist today. Please sit in the dental chair and we will begin. When was your last professional cleaning? 中文: 你好，我是Sarah，今天你的洁牙师。坐到椅子上我们就开始。你上次洗牙是什么时候？ 详解: "Hop up" = 上去/坐上去（轻松口语）。"Hygienist" = 牙科洁牙师，通常先由她洗牙，然后牙医来检查。在美国，洗牙和检查通常是分开的两个人做。✅推荐

You: Honestly, it's probably been over a year. I know, I know — I should've come in sooner.

正式: Honestly, it has probably been over a year. I am aware that I should have come in sooner. 中文: 说实话，大概一年多了。我知道我知道——我该早点来的。 详解: "I know, I know" = 我知道（承认错误时的重复强调）。"Should've" = should have 的缩读。"Come in" 在医疗语境中指来就诊。✅推荐

Dental Hygienist: Hey, no judgment here! Better late than never. Let me take some X-rays first. Bite down on this — there you go.

正式: No judgment here. Better late than never. Let me take some X-rays first. Please bite down on this device. 中文: 嘿，这里不做评判！迟来总比不来好。我先拍几张X光片。咬住这个——好的。 详解: "No judgment" = 不评判/不批评。"Better late than never" = 迟做总比不做好。"Bite down" = 咬住/咬下来。拍牙片时需要咬住一个传感器。✅推荐

Dental Hygienist: Alright, your teeth look pretty good overall. You've got some tartar buildup back here, especially behind your lower front teeth. I'm gonna scale that off — it might be a little sensitive. Let me know if you need a break.

正式: Your teeth look generally good. You have some tartar buildup in the back, particularly behind your lower front teeth. I am going to remove it with a scaler — it may cause some sensitivity. Please let me know if you need a pause. 中文: 好的，你的牙齿整体看起来不错。后面有一些牙结石，特别是下门牙后面。我把它刮掉——可能会有点敏感。需要休息的话告诉我。 详解: "Tartar buildup" = 牙结石堆积。"Scale off" = 刮除/除垢。"Sensitive" = 敏感的（指牙齿）。"Need a break" = 需要休息一下。洗牙过程中可以随时要求暂停。✅推荐

You: (呻吟) Yeah, that spot's pretty sensitive. Can we take a quick breather?

正式: That area is quite sensitive. Can we take a brief pause? 中文: 那个位置挺敏感的。能喘口气吗？ 详解: "Breather" = 短暂休息。"Quick breather" = 快速休息一下。在牙科治疗中要求暂停完全正常。✅推荐

Dental Hygienist: Of course. Take your time. Rinse if you need to. When you're ready, I'll do the polishing and flossing, and then Dr. Kim will come in for the exam.

正式: Of course. Take your time. You may rinse if needed. When you are ready, I will proceed with polishing and flossing, and then Dr. Kim will conduct the examination. 中文: 当然。不着急。需要的话漱个口。你准备好了的话，我给你抛光和用牙线，然后Kim医生来做检查。 详解: "Rinse" = 漱口。"Polishing" = 抛光。"Flossing" = 用牙线。美国洗牙通常包括：X光→除垢（scaling）→抛光→牙线。✅推荐

子场景3：牙医检查（Dentist's Exam）

Dr. Kim: Hi Wei, I'm Dr. Kim. Sarah did a great job with the cleaning. Let me take a quick look. Open wide for me... Looking good. You've got a small cavity on your upper left molar — number 14. Nothing urgent, but we should get it filled before it gets worse.

正式: Hello Wei, I am Dr. Kim. Sarah did an excellent job with the cleaning. Let me examine your teeth briefly. Please open your mouth... Your teeth look good overall. You have a small cavity on your upper left molar — tooth number 14. It is not urgent, but we should place a filling before it worsens. 中文: 你好Wei，我是Kim医生。Sarah洗牙做得很好。让我快速看一下。张大嘴... 看起来不错。你左上磨牙有一个小蛀牙——14号牙。不紧急，但我们应该在恶化之前补上。 详解: "Open wide" = 张大嘴（牙医的标准用语）。"Cavity" = 蛀牙/龋洞。"Molar" = 磨牙。"Get it filled" = 补牙。美国牙医给牙齿编号（1-32），"number 14" 是左上第一磨牙。✅推荐

You: Oh, okay. How long would that take? And how much would it cost?

正式: How long would the procedure take? And what would be the cost? 中文: 哦，好的。这要多久？多少钱？ 详解: 问时间和费用是完全正常的。"How long would that take?" 是询问时间的标准句型。✅推荐

Dr. Kim: It's a pretty straightforward filling — about 30 to 40 minutes. With your Delta Dental plan, you'd be looking at a copay of around $40 to $60. The insurance covers most of it.

正式: It is a routine filling procedure — approximately 30 to 40 minutes. With your Delta Dental plan, your copayment would be approximately $40 to $60. The insurance covers the majority of the cost. 中文: 这是一个很简单的补牙——大约30到40分钟。有Delta Dental保险的话，你的共付额大约40到60美元。保险报销大部分。 详解: "Straightforward" = 简单的/直接的。"You'd be looking at" = 你大概要（面对/支付的）。"Covers most of it" = 报销大部分。✅推荐

You: Alright, let's do it. Can we schedule that before I leave today?

正式: Alright, let us proceed. Can we schedule the appointment before I leave today? 中文: 好的，就做吧。走之前能约好吗？ 详解: "Let's do it" = 就做吧/同意。"Before I leave today" 是请求同时安排下次预约。✅推荐

Dr. Kim: Absolutely. I'll have the front desk set that up for you on your way out. One more thing — I noticed some gum recession on your lower teeth. It's common at your age, but I'd recommend switching to a soft-bristle toothbrush and being gentler when you brush. And don't forget to floss daily — only floss the teeth you want to keep!

正式: Absolutely. I will have the front desk schedule that for you before you leave. Additionally, I noticed some gum recession on your lower teeth. It is common at your age, but I recommend switching to a soft-bristle toothbrush and brushing more gently. Please floss daily. 中文: 当然。走的时候前台会帮你约好。还有一件事——我注意到你下牙有一些牙龈萎缩。你这个年纪很常见，但我建议换成软毛牙刷，刷牙时轻一点。别忘了每天用牙线——只想保留的牙才用牙线！ 详解: "Gum recession" = 牙龈萎缩（50岁左右常见问题）。"Soft-bristle" = 软毛的。"Only floss the teeth you want to keep" 是牙医的经典笑话，意思是所有牙都要用牙线。✅推荐

You: Ha, I'll keep that in mind. Thanks, Dr. Kim.

正式: I will remember that. Thank you, Dr. Kim. 中文: 哈，我记住了。谢谢Kim医生。 详解: "Keep that in mind" = 记住了/会注意的。对医生幽默的得体回应。✅推荐

核心口语表达清单

必学表达 ✅推荐

  1. I'd like to set up an appointment for a cleaning. — 我想预约洗牙。
  2. It's been a while since my last visit. — 我上次来已经有一阵了。
  3. Do you take Delta Dental? — 你们接受Delta Dental保险吗？
  4. That spot's pretty sensitive. — 那个位置挺敏感的。
  5. You've got a small cavity. — 你有个小蛀牙。
  6. We should get it filled. — 应该补上。
  7. How much would it cost out of pocket? — 自费多少钱？
  8. Let's do it. — 就做吧。
  9. I'll keep that in mind. — 我记住了。
  10. Better late than never. — 迟做总比不做好。

了解即可 👂了解

  1. My tooth is killing me. — 我牙疼死了。
  2. I think I need a root canal. — 我觉得我需要根管治疗。
  3. My wisdom teeth are coming in. — 我的智齿在长。
  4. I've got a tooth that's loose. — 我有颗牙松了。

避免说法 ❌避免

  1. ~~My tooth is broken.~~ → I chipped a tooth / I broke a tooth.（更具体）
  2. ~~I need to fix my tooth.~~ → I need to get a cavity filled.（太笼统）
  3. ~~Clean my teeth.~~ → I need a cleaning.（命令式）
  4. ~~Pull my tooth out.~~ → I think I need a tooth pulled.（太直接）

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国牙科须知

  • Dental insurance separate：牙科保险通常和医疗保险分开买
  • Annual maximum：牙科保险通常有年度上限（$1000-$2000），用完就没了
  • 100-80-50 coverage：通常洗牙100%报销，补牙80%，大项目50%
  • 每半年洗牙：大多数保险每6个月覆盖一次免费洗牙
  • 牙科焦虑：如果你怕看牙，可以提前说 "I get anxious at the dentist"

🇬🇧 英国牙科须知

  • NHS Band charges：Band 1 (£26.80) 检查，Band 2 (£73.50) 补牙，Band 3 (£319.10) 牙冠
  • NHS dentist难找：很多NHS牙医不再接收新病人
  • Private dentist：更贵但更容易约到
  • Hygienist：英国的洁牙师服务通常额外收费

💡 实用建议

  • 预约时说明你需要洗牙+检查（cleaning and checkup）
  • 如果对某个步骤不确定，问 "What are you doing now?"
  • 洗牙时有任何不适，举手示意（有些牙科椅有按钮）
  • 了解你的牙科保险覆盖什么，避免意外账单
  • 50岁以上注意牙龈健康（gum health），使用软毛牙刷
