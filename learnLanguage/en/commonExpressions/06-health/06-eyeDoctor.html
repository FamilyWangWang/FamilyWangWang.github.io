第6章：看眼科医生（Eye Doctor / Ophthalmologist）

场景背景

50岁左右是眼睛问题开始增多的年纪——老花眼（presbyopia/reading glasses）、白内障风险增加、青光眼筛查等。在美国，验光师（optometrist）和眼科医生（ophthalmologist）是不同的：前者主要验光配镜，后者是医学博士，处理眼疾。眼镜可以在眼科诊所、LensCrafters、Costco等地方配。英国的眼科检查可以在optician（眼镜店里的验光师）处进行。

关键文化差异：

  • 🇺🇸 美国：vision insurance（视力保险）通常和医疗保险分开，每年覆盖一次眼科检查和部分眼镜费用
  • 🇬🇧 英国：NHS眼科检查免费（60岁以上）或约£20-30，配镜需额外付费
  • 美国配眼镜很贵（一副$200-$500+），Costco和网上配镜更便宜
  • "Eye doctor" 日常口语中可以指 optometrist 或 ophthalmologist

完整对话

子场景1：预约和视力检查（Scheduling & Eye Exam）

You: Hi, I'd like to make an appointment for an eye exam. I think I might need reading glasses — I've been having a hard time seeing things up close.

正式: Hello, I would like to schedule an appointment for an eye examination. I believe I may need reading glasses — I have been experiencing difficulty seeing objects at close range. 中文: 你好，我想预约眼科检查。我觉得我可能需要老花镜了——我看近处的东西一直看不清。 详解: "Eye exam" = 眼科检查。"Reading glasses" = 老花镜/阅读眼镜。"Having a hard time" = 有困难/费劲。"Up close" = 近距离。这些都是描述视力问题的地道口语。✅推荐

Receptionist: Sure, we can get you in. When did you last have your eyes checked?

正式: Certainly, we can schedule you. When did you last have an eye examination? 中文: 当然，可以安排。你上次检查眼睛是什么时候？ 详解: "Get you in" = 安排你看上医生。"Have your eyes checked" = 检查眼睛，口语中用 "have sth done" 结构。✅推荐

You: It's been probably two years. I've also been getting more headaches lately, especially when I'm reading or on the computer for a long time.

正式: It has been approximately two years. I have also been experiencing more frequent headaches recently, especially after prolonged reading or computer use. 中文: 大概两年了。我最近头痛也多了，特别是长时间看书或用电脑的时候。 详解: "On the computer" = 用电脑。"Getting more headaches" = 头痛更频繁了。眼睛疲劳引起头痛是常见症状。✅推荐

Receptionist: That sounds like it could be related to your vision. We have openings next Tuesday at 2 PM or Thursday at 10 AM with Dr. Chen, our optometrist.

正式: That may be vision-related. We have availability next Tuesday at 2 PM or Thursday at 10 AM with Dr. Chen, our optometrist. 中文: 听起来可能跟视力有关。下周二下午2点或周四上午10点我们的验光师陈医生有空。 详解: "Related to your vision" = 跟视力有关。"Openings" = 空位/可预约时间。✅推荐

子场景2：眼科检查（During the Eye Exam）

Dr. Chen: Hi Wei, I'm Dr. Chen. I'll be doing your eye exam today. Let's start with the vision test. Can you read the letters on the screen? Cover your left eye for me.

正式: Hello Wei, I am Dr. Chen. I will be conducting your eye examination today. Let us begin with the visual acuity test. Can you read the letters on the screen? Please cover your left eye. 中文: 你好Wei，我是陈医生。今天我来给你做眼科检查。我们先做视力测试。你能读出屏幕上的字母吗？捂住左眼。 详解: "Cover your left eye" = 捂住/盖住左眼。"Vision test" = 视力测试。美国视力表用字母（Snellen chart），不用中国的"E字表"。✅推荐

You: Sure. That first line is easy — Z, V, N, R, K. But down here... I'm struggling. It's kind of blurry.

正式: Certainly. The first line is easy — Z, V, N, R, K. However, the lower lines are difficult. The text appears blurry. 中文: 好的。第一行很简单——Z, V, N, R, K。但下面的... 看不清。有点模糊。 详解: "I'm struggling" = 我看不清/费劲。"Blurry" = 模糊的，描述视力最常用的词。"Down here" 指视力表的下面几行。✅推荐

Dr. Chen: That's perfectly normal. Now switch to the other eye... Okay. I'm gonna use the phoropter — that's the big machine you look through. It'll flip between different lenses. Just tell me which one looks clearer — one or two.

正式: That is perfectly normal. Please switch to the other eye. I am going to use the phoropter — that is the large instrument you look through. It will alternate between different lenses. Please indicate which lens provides clearer vision — option one or option two. 中文: 这很正常。现在换另一只眼睛... 好的。我要用综合验光仪——就是你透过看的大机器。它会在不同的镜片之间切换。告诉我哪个更清楚——一还是二。 详解: "Phoropter" = 综合验光仪（专业术语但眼科医生会解释）。"Flip between" = 在...之间切换。"Which one looks clearer" = 哪个更清楚。"One or two" 是眼科检查最经典的问题。✅推荐

You: One... no wait, two. Actually, they look about the same.

正式: One... wait, two. They appear approximately the same. 中文: 一... 等等，二。其实看起来差不多。 详解: "About the same" = 差不多一样。如果两个差不多说明这个度数区间已经确定了。✅推荐

Dr. Chen: That's fine, that tells me what I need to know. Now let me check the pressure in your eyes — this will just take a second. You'll feel a tiny puff of air.

正式: That is fine, that provides the necessary information. Now let me check your intraocular pressure — this will take only a moment. You will feel a small puff of air. 中文: 没关系，这已经告诉我需要知道的了。现在让我测一下眼压——很快就好。你会感到一小股气流。 详解: "Pressure in your eyes" = 眼压（intraocular pressure），检查青光眼的重要指标。"Puff of air" = 一股气流。非接触式眼压计会向眼睛吹气，很多人会不自觉地眨眼。✅推荐

You: Oh! That startled me a bit.

正式: That startled me somewhat. 中文: 哦！吓了我一跳。 详解: "Startled me" = 让我吓了一跳。对气流检查的正常反应。✅推荐

Dr. Chen: Happens to everybody! Your eye pressure looks good — no signs of glaucoma. Now I'm gonna put in some drops to dilate your pupils. It'll take about 15 to 20 minutes to kick in, and then I'll take a look at the back of your eyes.

正式: That happens to everyone. Your intraocular pressure is normal — no signs of glaucoma. I am going to administer eye drops to dilate your pupils. It will take approximately 15 to 20 minutes to take effect, and then I will examine your retina. 中文: 每个人都这样！你的眼压正常——没有青光眼迹象。现在我给你滴扩瞳的眼药水。大概15到20分钟起效，然后我检查一下你的眼底。 详解: "Dilate your pupils" = 扩瞳。"Kick in" = 起效。"Back of your eyes" = 眼底（retina/视网膜的口语说法）。扩瞳后几小时内看近处会模糊，需要戴墨镜。✅推荐

子场景3：检查结果和配镜（Results & Glasses）

Dr. Chen: Alright, Wei, here's the rundown. Your distance vision is still pretty good, but you definitely have presbyopia — age-related farsightedness. It's completely normal, especially around your age. You need reading glasses, or if you want, we can do progressive lenses that handle both distance and close-up.

正式: Here is a summary. Your distance vision remains good, but you have presbyopia — age-related farsightedness. This is completely normal at your age. You require reading glasses, or alternatively, progressive lenses that correct both distance and near vision. 中文: 好的，Wei，情况是这样的。你的远视视力还挺好的，但你确实有老花眼——年龄相关的远视。这完全正常，特别是你这个年纪。你需要老花镜，或者如果你想要，可以做渐进镜片，兼顾远距离和近距离。 详解: "Here's the rundown" = 情况是这样的/总结一下。"Presbyopia" = 老花眼。"Progressive lenses" = 渐进镜片（多焦点镜片，外观上看不出分界线）。✅推荐

You: What's the difference between reading glasses and progressive lenses?

正式: What is the difference between reading glasses and progressive lenses? 中文: 老花镜和渐进镜片有什么区别？ 详解: 问清选项是好习惯。✅推荐

Dr. Chen: Reading glasses are just for close-up — you'd take them off to see far away. Progressive lenses let you see at all distances in one pair of glasses — far away, mid-range like your computer, and up close for reading. They're more expensive but more convenient.

正式: Reading glasses are solely for near vision — you would remove them for distance viewing. Progressive lenses enable vision at all distances in one pair — far, mid-range such as computer screens, and near for reading. They are more expensive but more convenient. 中文: 老花镜只看近处用——看远处你要摘掉。渐进镜片让你一副眼镜看清所有距离——远处、中间距离比如电脑、近处阅读。更贵但更方便。 详解: "Take them off" = 摘掉。"All distances" = 所有距离。"Mid-range" = 中间距离。"More expensive but more convenient" = 更贵但更方便。✅推荐

You: I think I'll go with the progressive lenses. How much are we talking about?

正式: I will choose the progressive lenses. What is the estimated cost? 中文: 我选渐进镜片吧。大概多少钱？ 详解: "Go with" = 选择。"How much are we talking about?" = 大概多少钱？非常口语化的询价方式。✅推荐

Dr. Chen: The frames range from about $80 to $300, and progressive lenses run about $200 to $400. With your VSP insurance, you'd get about $150 toward frames and the lenses are usually covered at a significant discount. I'll give you your prescription and you can take it to any optical shop.

正式: Frames range from approximately $80 to $300, and progressive lenses cost approximately $200 to $400. With your VSP insurance, you receive approximately $150 toward frames and lenses are discounted significantly. I will provide your prescription, which you can take to any optical retailer. 中文: 镜框大约80到300美元，渐进镜片大约200到400美元。有VSP保险的话，镜框报销约150美元，镜片通常有很大折扣。我给你处方，你可以拿到任何眼镜店去配。 详解: "Range from... to..." = 从...到...不等。"Run about" = 大约（花费）。"Toward" = 用于/抵。"Optical shop" = 眼镜店。"Take it to" = 拿到...去。VSP是美国最大的视力保险公司。✅推荐

You: Can I just get it done here?

正式: Can I have the glasses made here? 中文: 可以在这里配吗？ 详解: "Get it done here" = 在这里做。很多眼科诊所都有配镜服务。✅推荐

Dr. Chen: Absolutely. We've got a good selection of frames right out here. My assistant can help you pick something out and get everything ordered. Your glasses should be ready in about a week to ten days.

正式: Certainly. We have a good selection of frames available. My assistant can help you select frames and place the order. Your glasses should be ready in approximately one to two weeks. 中文: 当然。我们这里有很好的镜框选择。我的助手可以帮你挑选并下订单。你的眼镜大约一周到十天就能做好。 详解: "Good selection" = 选择很多。"Pick something out" = 挑选。"Get everything ordered" = 下好订单。"A week to ten days" = 一周到十天。✅推荐

3. 眼科检查常见指令

口语表达 中文
Read the smallest line you can. 读你能看到的最小一行。

核心口语表达清单

必学表达 ✅推荐

  1. I've been having a hard time seeing things up close. — 我看近处的东西一直看不清。
  2. It's kind of blurry. — 有点模糊。
  3. Which one looks clearer — one or two? — 哪个更清楚——一还是二？
  4. I think I need reading glasses. — 我觉得我需要老花镜了。
  5. How much are we talking about? — 大概多少钱？
  6. I'll go with the progressive lenses. — 我选渐进镜片。
  7. Can I get it done here? — 可以在这里做吗？
  8. Your glasses should be ready in about a week. — 你的眼镜大约一周就好。
  9. I've been getting more headaches. — 我最近头痛更多了。
  10. No signs of glaucoma. — 没有青光眼迹象。

了解即可 👂了解

  1. I'm due for an eye exam. — 我该做眼科检查了。
  2. My prescription hasn't changed much. — 我的度数没怎么变。
  3. I need to get my glasses adjusted. — 我需要调整一下眼镜。
  4. These frames are too tight on my nose. — 这个镜框夹鼻子太紧。

避免说法 ❌避免

  1. ~~My eyes are bad.~~ → I've been having trouble seeing.（太笼统）
  2. ~~I can't see.~~ → Things look blurry.（太绝对）
  3. ~~Give me glasses.~~ → I need a prescription for glasses.（命令式）
  4. ~~My glasses number is...~~ → My prescription is...（"number" 不对）

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国眼科须知

  • Vision insurance：VSP, EyeMed, Davis Vision 是主要保险公司
  • FSA/HSA：可以用灵活支出账户或健康储蓄账户支付眼镜费用
  • 配镜渠道：眼科诊所、LensCrafters、Costco、Warby Parker（在线）、Zenni Optical（便宜在线）
  • Costco配镜：不要求会员，且价格通常是零售店的一半
  • 扩瞳后：开车会不安全，最好有人陪同或等几小时

🇬🇧 英国眼科须知

  • NHS眼科检查：60岁以上免费，否则约£20-30
  • NHS眼镜券：部分人群可获得NHS代金券用于配镜
  • Optician：英国更常用这个词指验光师/眼镜店
  • Specsavers：英国最大的连锁眼镜店，价格亲民

💡 实用建议

  • 50岁以上建议每年检查一次眼睛
  • 如果有糖尿病或高血压，更要注意眼底检查
  • 扩瞳检查后记得带墨镜，光线会非常刺眼
  • 保存好处方，可以在其他地方配镜比价
  • 如果每天用电脑时间长，问医生蓝光过滤镜片（blue light filtering lenses）
