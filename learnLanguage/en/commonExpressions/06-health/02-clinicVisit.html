第2章：看诊就医（Doctor's Visit）

场景背景

在美国看医生（seeing a doctor），流程通常是：先在前台登记 → 护士带你量血压、称体重等（叫"rooming"）→ 医生进来问诊 → 医生检查 → 给出诊断和处方。整个过程大约15-30分钟，医生会非常注重沟通和解释。英国的GP问诊时间通常更短（约10分钟），风格也更简洁直接。

关键文化差异：

  • 🇺🇸 美国医生更注重"bedside manner"（医患沟通技巧），会详细解释病情
  • 🇬🇧 英国GP问诊时间短（标准10分钟），更直接，不太"寒暄"
  • 美国医生会问你很多问题（take history），这是正常的，不是不信任你
  • 两国医生都可能让你填疼痛评分（pain scale 1-10）

完整对话

子场景1：护士预检（Triage/Vitals）

Nurse: Hi there, Wei? Follow me, please. Let me get your weight and height first.

正式: Hello, Wei? Please follow me. Let me measure your weight and height first. 中文: 你好，Wei是吗？请跟我来。先量一下体重和身高。 详解: "Hi there" 是友好的打招呼方式。"Get your weight" 口语中替代 "measure your weight"。在美国，体重用磅（pounds），身高用英尺/英寸。✅推荐

You: Sure. Do I need to take my shoes off?

正式: Certainly. Should I remove my shoes? 中文: 好的。需要脱鞋吗？ 详解: "Take off" 是穿脱衣物最常用的短语动词。在美国诊所，通常需要脱鞋称体重。✅推荐

Nurse: Shoes off, please. Step right on the scale... Alright, 178 pounds. Let's head to the exam room. It's the second door on the left.

正式: Please remove your shoes. Step onto the scale. 178 pounds. Let us proceed to the examination room. It is the second door on the left. 中文: 请脱鞋。站到秤上... 好了，178磅。我们去检查室吧。左边第二扇门。 详解: "Step right on" = 直接站上去。"Head to" = 前往。"Exam room" = 检查室，美国诊所比中国更常用独立小房间。178磅≈80.7公斤。✅推荐

Nurse: Have a seat on the exam table. I'm gonna take your vitals — blood pressure, temperature, and heart rate. Roll up your sleeve for me?

正式: Please sit on the examination table. I am going to take your vital signs — blood pressure, temperature, and heart rate. Could you roll up your sleeve? 中文: 请坐在检查床上。我来量一下生命体征——血压、体温和心率。能卷一下袖子吗？ 详解: "Vitals" = 生命体征（vital signs的简称），这是极其常用的医疗口语。"Roll up your sleeve" = 卷起袖子。末尾用升调的 "for me?" 是口语化的礼貌请求。✅推荐

You: No problem. Is my blood pressure okay?

正式: No problem. Is my blood pressure within the normal range? 中文: 没问题。我血压正常吗？ 详解: 直接问结果是完全正常的行为。✅推荐

Nurse: 132 over 85. A little on the high side, but nothing to worry about right now. Dr. Patel will go over everything with you. She should be right in.

正式: 132 over 85. Slightly elevated, but nothing to be concerned about at this time. Dr. Patel will review everything with you. She will be in shortly. 中文: 132比85。有点偏高，但现在不用担心。Patel医生会跟你详细说的。她马上就来。 详解: 血压读数口语说 "132 over 85"（收缩压 over 舒张压）。"On the high side" = 偏高。"Go over" = 详细讨论/过一遍。"Right in" = 马上就来。"Should be" 表示预计会发生。✅推荐

子场景2：医生问诊（Doctor's Consultation）

Dr. Patel: Hi, Wei. I'm Dr. Patel. How are you doing today? I see you've been feeling under the weather.

正式: Hello, Wei. I am Dr. Patel. How are you feeling today? I understand you have been feeling unwell. 中文: 你好，Wei。我是Patel医生。今天感觉怎么样？我看你最近不太舒服。 详解: 美国医生通常会先自我介绍并寒暄。"I see..." 表示她已经看过你之前的描述。这是典型的美国bedside manner。✅推荐

You: Yeah, it started about a week ago. I've had this cough that just won't quit, and I've been feeling really wiped out — like, no energy at all.

正式: Yes, it began approximately one week ago. I have had a persistent cough, and I have been feeling extremely fatigued — with no energy whatsoever. 中文: 是的，大概一周前开始的。一直咳嗽停不下来，而且特别累——就是完全没精力。 详解: "Won't quit" = 不会停止/一直持续。"Wiped out" = 精疲力竭，非常口语化。"Like" 在这里是填充词，用来强调后面的描述。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: I'm sorry to hear that. Can you describe the cough for me? Is it dry or are you bringing anything up?

正式: I am sorry to hear that. Can you describe the nature of your cough? Is it a dry cough, or are you producing any phlegm? 中文: 抱歉听到这个。你能描述一下咳嗽吗？是干咳还是有痰？ 详解: "Bring up" 在医疗语境中指咳出痰液（phlegm/sputum），比说 "produce phlegm" 口语化得多。这是医生问诊的典型问题。✅推荐

You: Mostly dry, but sometimes in the morning I cough up some yellowish stuff.

正式: It is predominantly dry, but occasionally in the morning I expectorate some yellowish sputum. 中文: 多数是干咳，但有时候早上会咳出一些黄色的东西。 详解: "Yellowish stuff" = 黄色的东西（指痰）。口语中说 "stuff" 替代具体名词很常见。"Cough up" = 咳出。黄痰可能提示感染。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: Okay. Any other symptoms? Fever, chills, shortness of breath, body aches?

正式: Understood. Any additional symptoms? Fever, chills, shortness of breath, or body aches? 中文: 好的。还有其他症状吗？发烧、发冷、呼吸困难、全身酸痛？ 详解: 医生快速列举常见症状是标准问诊流程。"Shortness of breath" = 气短/呼吸困难。"Body aches" = 全身酸痛。✅推荐

You: No fever that I know of. But yeah, I've been a little short of breath when I walk up stairs. And my throat's been kind of scratchy.

正式: No fever that I am aware of. However, I have been experiencing mild shortness of breath when climbing stairs. And my throat has been somewhat irritated. 中文: 据我所知没发烧。但是，上楼梯的时候有点喘。嗓子也有些发痒。 详解: "That I know of" = 据我所知，留有余地。"Scratchy throat" = 嗓子发痒/刺痒，非常口语化的说法。"Kind of" 弱化了描述的程度。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: Got it. Let me listen to your lungs. Take a deep breath in... and out. Again... Okay, one more time. Alright, you can breathe normally now.

正式: Understood. Let me auscultate your lungs. Inhale deeply... and exhale. Again... Okay, one more time. You may breathe normally now. 中文: 好的。让我听一下你的肺部。深吸气... 呼气。再来一次... 好的，再来一次。好了，可以正常呼吸了。 详解: "Take a deep breath in... and out" 是听诊时医生的标准指令。"One more time" = 再来一次。✅推荐

You: Is everything okay in there?

正式: Is everything normal? 中文: 里面一切正常吗？ 详解: "In there" 口语中指身体内部。这是病人焦虑时常问的问题。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: Your lungs sound clear, which is good. No signs of pneumonia. I wanna run some blood work just to rule a few things out. I'm also gonna order a chest X-ray to be on the safe side.

正式: Your lungs sound clear upon auscultation, which is encouraging. There are no signs of pneumonia. I would like to order some blood tests to rule out certain conditions. I am also ordering a chest X-ray as a precaution. 中文: 你的肺部听诊是清晰的，这很好。没有肺炎的迹象。我想做一下血液检查排除一些情况。我还打算拍个胸片保险起见。 详解: "Run blood work" = 做血液检查。"Rule out" = 排除。"To be on the safe side" = 保险起见/以防万一。这三个都是医生口语中的高频短语。✅推荐

You: Sounds good. What do you think it might be, just a heads-up?

正式: That sounds reasonable. What do you suspect it might be, if you could give me a preliminary assessment? 中文: 好的。你觉得可能是什么，先给个大概？ 详解: "Just a heads-up" 这里有点误用，更准确应该说 "Just curious" 或 "Any idea what it could be?" "Heads-up" 通常指提前告知某事。👂了解

Dr. Patel: Hard to say for sure without the test results, but it could be a lingering viral infection or maybe mild bronchitis. Nothing too serious, I don't think. In the meantime, I'll prescribe something to help with the cough so you can get some sleep.

正式: It is difficult to determine without the test results, but it could be a persistent viral infection or mild bronchitis. I do not believe it is anything serious. In the meantime, I will prescribe medication to help with the cough so you can sleep. 中文: 没出检查结果很难确定，但可能是迁延的病毒感染或者轻度支气管炎。我觉得不会太严重。同时，我给你开点止咳的药，让你能睡好。 详解: "Lingering" = 迟迟不退的/缠绵的。"I don't think" 放在句末是口语特色。"Help with" = 帮助缓解。"Get some sleep" = 睡个觉。✅推荐

You: That would be great. The cough's been keeping me up at night.

正式: That would be greatly appreciated. The cough has been preventing me from sleeping at night. 中文: 那太好了。这咳嗽一直让我晚上睡不着。 详解: "Keeping me up" = 让我睡不着，比 "preventing me from sleeping" 口语化得多。"That would be great" 是接受建议的常见回应。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: I'll send the prescription to your pharmacy. You should be able to pick it up in about an hour. I want you to come back in two weeks for a follow-up. If the symptoms get worse before then — fever spikes, trouble breathing — head to the ER right away, okay?

正式: I will send the prescription to your pharmacy. You should be able to collect it in approximately one hour. I would like you to return in two weeks for a follow-up appointment. If your symptoms worsen before then — such as a spike in fever or difficulty breathing — please go to the emergency room immediately. 中文: 我会把处方发到你的药房。大约一小时后就能取。我希望你两周后来复查。如果在那之前症状加重——比如突然发烧、呼吸困难——马上去急诊，好吗？ 详解: "Send the prescription to your pharmacy" = 电子处方直接传到药房。"Follow-up" = 复查/随访。"Fever spikes" = 突然高烧。"Head to" = 前往。"ER" = Emergency Room 急诊室。✅推荐

You: Will do. Thanks, Dr. Patel. I really appreciate it.

正式: I will do that. Thank you, Dr. Patel. I truly appreciate it. 中文: 好的。谢谢Patel医生。非常感谢。 详解: "Will do" = 会照做的，简洁有力的口语回应。"Appreciate it" 是感谢的标准口语表达。✅推荐

Dr. Patel: Of course. Take care of yourself, Wei. Get some rest, and we'll see you in two weeks.

正式: Of course. Take care of yourself, Wei. Get some rest, and we will see you in two weeks. 中文: 不客气。照顾好自己，Wei。好好休息，两周后见。 详解: "Take care of yourself" 对病人有"保重"的含义。"Get some rest" = 好好休息。✅推荐

子场景3：前台结账

Receptionist: Alright, Wei, you're all set. That'll be a $30 copay for today's visit. Would you like to pay with card or cash?

正式: Alright, Wei, everything is complete. The copayment for today's visit is $30. Would you prefer to pay with card or cash? 中文: 好了，Wei，都办完了。今天的就诊共付额是30美元。您想刷卡还是付现金？ 详解: 看完病后在前台付copay是美国标准流程。有些诊所在就诊前收，有些在之后收。✅推荐

You: Card, please. And the blood work and X-ray — are those covered by my insurance?

正式: Card, please. Regarding the blood tests and X-ray — are those covered by my insurance plan? 中文: 刷卡。还有血液检查和X光——这些保险报销吗？ 详解: "Covered" 在保险语境中指"被保险覆盖/报销的"。这是非常重要的实际问题。✅推荐

Receptionist: They should be, but I'd recommend calling your insurance company just to double-check. Sometimes there's a separate deductible for lab work.

正式: They should be covered, but I would recommend contacting your insurance company to verify. Sometimes there is a separate deductible for laboratory work. 中文: 应该是报销的，但我建议你打电话给保险公司确认一下。有时候化验有单独的免赔额。 详解: "Double-check" = 再次确认。"Lab work" = 化验/实验室检查。"Deductible" = 免赔额/自付额。美国的保险系统很复杂，即使是前台也不一定完全清楚你的保险覆盖范围。✅推荐

逐句详解：核心语言点

1. 医疗短语动词

口语表达 正式表达 中文
come down with (something) develop an illness 得了（某种病）
fight off (an infection) resist/recover from 抵抗/击退（感染）
bring up / cough up expectorate 咳出
rule out eliminate as a possibility 排除
come back for a follow-up return for a subsequent visit 回来复查
pass out lose consciousness 晕倒
throw up vomit 呕吐 ✅推荐
come to regain consciousness 苏醒

2. 描述症状的口语表达

口语表达 中文 使用场景
I've been feeling wiped out. 我累死了。 极度疲惫 ✅推荐
My throat's scratchy. 我嗓子痒。 喉咙不适 ✅推荐
I'm a little short of breath. 我有点喘不上气。 呼吸问题 ✅推荐
I've got a splitting headache. 我头疼欲裂。 剧烈头痛
My stomach's been acting up. 我胃不太舒服。 胃部问题 ✅推荐
I can't keep anything down. 我吃什么吐什么。 恶心呕吐
It hurts when I breathe in. 吸气时疼。 胸部疼痛

核心口语表达清单

必学表达 ✅推荐

  1. I've been feeling wiped out. — 我累死了。
  2. It started about a week ago. — 大概一周前开始的。
  3. The cough won't quit. — 咳嗽一直不停。
  4. I wanna run some blood work. — 我想验个血。
  5. Let's rule a few things out. — 咱们排除几种可能。
  6. Just to be on the safe side. — 保险起见。
  7. I'll send the prescription to your pharmacy. — 我把处方发到你药房。
  8. Come back in two weeks for a follow-up. — 两周后回来复查。
  9. Is that covered by my insurance? — 这个保险报销吗？
  10. If symptoms get worse, head to the ER. — 如果症状加重去急诊。

了解即可 👂了解

  1. It's been going around. — 最近在流行（指疾病）。
  2. I think I'm coming down with something. — 我觉得我要生病了。
  3. My immune system's been run down. — 我免疫力下降了。
  4. It's nothing to sneeze at. — 这可不是小事。（双关语）

避免说法 ❌避免

  1. ~~I have pain in my body.~~ → I've been having body aches.（太笼统）
  2. ~~I am suffering from cough.~~ → I've got a cough that won't go away.（太戏剧化）
  3. ~~Doctor, you must help me.~~ → I've been dealing with this for a week.（太急切）
  4. ~~What is wrong with my body?~~ → What do you think it could be?（太模糊）

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国看诊文化

  • 不要害羞：美国医生鼓励你提问，越详细越好
  • 疼痛评分：医生会问 "On a scale of 1 to 10?"，1是最轻微，10是最疼
  • 第二诊疗意见（second opinion）：你有权利要求另一位医生再看一次
  • 病历隐私（HIPAA）：你的信息受法律保护
  • 医生迟到是常态：等30-60分钟很常见，可以带本书

🇬🇧 英国看诊文化

  • GP问诊10分钟：时间很短，要提前准备好要说的话
  • 更加直接：英国医生不太寒暄，直接进入正题
  • NHS转诊：看专科需要GP转诊（referral），不能直接预约
  • 处方：GP直接开处方，你去药房取药

💡 实用建议

  • 把症状用英文写下来带去，紧张时容易忘
  • 记住：onset（什么时候开始的）、duration（持续多久）、severity（严重程度）
  • 如果听不懂医生的话，大胆说："Could you explain that in simpler terms?"
  • 不要怕描述尴尬的症状，医生什么场面都见过
  • 记下医生说的药名和注意事项，或请求写下来
