第4章：急诊室（Emergency Room / A&E）

场景背景

急诊室是处理紧急医疗情况的地方。🇺🇸 美国叫 ER（Emergency Room）或 ED（Emergency Department），🇬🇧 英国叫 A&E（Accident & Emergency）。注意：美国急诊非常昂贵（可能几千到上万美元），只应在真正的紧急情况下使用。英国NHS的A&E免费，但也只应用于真正的紧急情况。

什么时候去急诊：

  • 胸痛（chest pain）、呼吸困难（difficulty breathing）
  • 严重出血（severe bleeding）
  • 骨折（broken bone）、严重烧伤（severe burn）
  • 中风症状（stroke symptoms）
  • 严重过敏反应（severe allergic reaction / anaphylaxis）

什么时候不去急诊（去 Urgent Care 或 GP）：

  • 感冒发烧、小伤口、轻度疼痛
  • 美国可以用 Urgent Care（无需预约，比ER便宜很多）
  • 英国打 111（非紧急医疗咨询热线）

完整对话

子场景1：到达急诊室（Arriving at the ER）

You: (妻子突然腹痛剧烈) Hi, my wife is in a lot of pain — her stomach's killing her. We need to see someone right away.

正式: Hello, my wife is experiencing severe abdominal pain. We need to see a physician immediately. 中文: 你好，我妻子非常痛——她肚子疼得厉害。我们需要马上看医生。 详解: "Killing her" 口语中表示"疼死了/难受死了"，不是字面的"杀死"。"See someone" 在医院语境中指看医生。"Right away" = 立刻/马上。✅推荐

Triage Nurse: Okay, let's get her into a wheelchair. Ma'am, on a scale of 1 to 10, how bad is the pain? Where exactly does it hurt?

正式: Okay, let us assist her into a wheelchair. Ma'am, on a scale of 1 to 10, how would you rate the pain? Where precisely is the pain located? 中文: 好的，让她坐轮椅。女士，按1到10分，疼痛有多严重？具体哪里疼？ 详解: "Triage nurse" = 分诊护士，负责评估病人的紧急程度。"Scale of 1 to 10" = 疼痛评分标准。"Get her into a wheelchair" = 让她坐上轮椅。✅推荐

Wife: It's like an 8 or 9. It's right here, on the lower right side. It just came out of nowhere about an hour ago.

正式: I would rate it an 8 or 9. The pain is located here, on the lower right side. It began suddenly approximately one hour ago. 中文: 8到9分吧。就在这里，右下侧。大概一小时前突然开始的。 详解: "Like an 8 or 9" 中的 "like" 是口语填充词。"Lower right side" = 右下腹（可能提示阑尾炎appendicitis）。"Came out of nowhere" = 突然出现/毫无预兆。✅推荐

Triage Nurse: Lower right side — that's something we need to check out quickly. Is she allergic to any medications? And when was the last time she ate or drank anything?

正式: Lower right side — that requires prompt evaluation. Is she allergic to any medications? When did she last consume food or liquids? 中文: 右下侧——这个需要赶快检查一下。她对什么药物过敏吗？最后一次吃喝是什么时候？ 详解: "Check out" = 检查/查看。"Something we need to check out quickly" 暗示护士也认为可能比较紧急。问过敏史和禁食时间是为可能的手术做准备。✅推荐

You: She's allergic to penicillin. And she had breakfast around 8 this morning, nothing since then.

正式: She is allergic to penicillin. She consumed breakfast at approximately 8 AM and has had nothing since. 中文: 她对青霉素过敏。她早上8点左右吃了早饭，之后什么都没吃。 详解: "Nothing since then" = 之后什么都没有。"Penicillin" = 青霉素，是最常见的药物过敏之一。✅推荐

子场景2：急诊检查与等待（Examination & Waiting）

ER Doctor: I'm Dr. Rodriguez. I understand your wife's having severe abdominal pain. I'm gonna examine her now. Ma'am, I'm going to press on your belly — tell me if it hurts.

正式: I am Dr. Rodriguez. I understand your wife is experiencing severe abdominal pain. I will examine her now. I am going to press on your abdomen — please inform me if it causes pain. 中文: 我是Rodriguez医生。我知道你妻子严重腹痛。我现在给她检查。女士，我要按压你的腹部——疼就告诉我。 详解: "Belly" 是 "abdomen" 的口语说法，在急诊中很常用。"Press on" = 按压。腹部检查的标准流程。✅推荐

ER Doctor: How about here? Does that hurt when I let go?

正式: How about here? Does it cause pain when I release the pressure? 中文: 这里呢？我松手的时候疼吗？ 详解: "Let go" = 松手。这里检查的是 rebound tenderness（反跳痛），是阑尾炎的典型体征。✅推荐

Wife: Oh — yeah, that really hurts when you let go.

正式: Yes, that causes significant pain when you release the pressure. 中文: 哦——是的，你松手的时候很疼。 详解: "Really hurts" = 很疼。✅推荐

ER Doctor: Okay, I'm fairly confident this could be appendicitis. We need to get a CT scan to confirm. I'm also going to order blood work and a urine test. We'll get you something for the pain in the meantime.

正式: I am fairly confident this may be appendicitis. We need to perform a CT scan to confirm. I am also ordering blood tests and a urinalysis. We will administer pain medication in the meantime. 中文: 好的，我很确定这可能是阑尾炎。需要做CT确认。我还安排验血和尿检。同时给你用点止痛药。 详解: "Fairly confident" = 相当确定。"CT scan" = CT扫描。"Order" 在医疗中指"安排/开（检查或药物）"。"Get you something for the pain" = 给你用点止痛的东西。✅推荐

You: Is it serious? Does she need surgery?

正式: Is it serious? Will she require surgery? 中文: 严重吗？需要手术吗？ 详解: 家属问这种问题是完全正常的。✅推荐

ER Doctor: Let's not jump the gun. We need the scan results first. If it is appendicitis, yes, she may need her appendix taken out. But let's take this one step at a time. The pain meds should kick in shortly.

正式: Let us not make premature conclusions. We need the scan results first. If it is appendicitis, she may require an appendectomy. Let us proceed one step at a time. The pain medication should take effect shortly. 中文: 别急着下结论。我们先看扫描结果。如果是阑尾炎的话，是的，她可能需要切除阑尾。但一步一步来。止痛药很快就会起效。 详解: "Jump the gun" = 过早行动/下结论（来自赛跑中的抢跑）。"Taken out" = 切除（口语化的手术说法）。"Kick in" = 开始起效。"One step at a time" = 一步一步来。✅推荐

子场景3：出结果与安排（Results & Next Steps）

ER Doctor: Good news — the CT scan ruled out appendicitis. It looks like a pretty bad case of gastroenteritis, basically a stomach bug. We're going to give her IV fluids to help with the dehydration, and she should be good to go in a few hours.

正式: The CT scan has ruled out appendicitis. It appears to be a severe case of gastroenteritis, commonly known as a stomach virus. We will administer intravenous fluids to address the dehydration, and she should be ready for discharge in a few hours. 中文: 好消息——CT排除了阑尾炎。看起来是严重的胃肠炎，就是肠胃病毒。我们给她输液缓解脱水，几个小时后应该就可以走了。 详解: "Ruled out" = 排除。"Stomach bug" = 肠胃病毒/肠胃炎的口语说法。"IV fluids" = 静脉输液/打点滴。"Good to go" = 可以了/没问题了。✅推荐

You: Oh, thank goodness. So she doesn't need surgery?

正式: Thank goodness. She does not require surgery? 中文: 哦，谢天谢地。那她不需要手术了？ 详解: "Thank goodness" = 谢天谢地，表示如释重负。✅推荐

ER Doctor: No surgery needed. I'll write her a prescription for anti-nausea medication. Make sure she drinks plenty of fluids — water, broth, Gatorade, stuff like that. If the pain comes back or she develops a fever over 102, bring her back in immediately.

正式: No surgery is required. I will prescribe anti-nausea medication. Ensure she drinks plenty of fluids — water, broth, sports drinks, and similar beverages. If the pain returns or she develops a fever exceeding 102°F, bring her back immediately. 中文: 不需要手术。我给她开止吐药。确保她多喝液体——水、汤、运动饮料之类的。如果疼痛回来或发烧超过102华氏度（约38.9°C），立刻把她带回来。 详解: "Anti-nausea" = 止吐的。"Broth" = 清汤/高汤。"Gatorade" = 佳得乐（美国最知名的运动饮料品牌，补充电解质）。"Stuff like that" = 诸如此类的。"102" 指华氏度（≈38.9°C），美国使用华氏度。✅推荐

You: Got it. What about the bill? How does that work?

正式: Understood. Regarding the bill, how does the payment process work? 中文: 明白了。账单怎么弄？怎么付费？ 详解: 美国ER费用高昂，问账单是很实际的问题。✅推荐

ER Doctor: That I'm not sure about — they'll sort that out at the front desk. Your insurance should cover most of it, but you may get separate bills from the hospital, the lab, and from me. That's just how it works in this country, unfortunately.

正式: I am not certain about billing — the front desk can assist with that. Your insurance should cover most of the cost, but you may receive separate bills from the hospital, the laboratory, and from the physician. That is the standard billing process. 中文: 这个我不太清楚——前台会处理。你的保险应该能报销大部分，但你可能会分别收到医院、化验室和我的账单。这个国家的医疗账单就是这样的。 详解: 美国医疗账单的一大特色：你会收到多张不同的账单（医院、医生、化验室各自开账单）。"Sort out" = 处理/解决。"Unfortunately" 表达了医生对这种复杂制度的不满。✅推荐

核心口语表达清单

必学表达 ✅推荐

  1. Her stomach's killing her. — 她肚子疼死了。
  2. We need to see someone right away. — 我们需要马上看医生。
  3. It came out of nowhere. — 突然就开始了。
  4. Let's not jump the gun. — 别急着下结论。
  5. The pain meds should kick in shortly. — 止痛药很快就起效。
  6. CT scan ruled out appendicitis. — CT排除了阑尾炎。
  7. She should be good to go in a few hours. — 她几小时后应该就好了。
  8. Make sure she drinks plenty of fluids. — 确保她多喝水。
  9. Bring her back in immediately. — 立刻把她带回来。
  10. If the pain comes back... — 如果疼痛回来了...

了解即可 👂了解

  1. We had to rush her in. — 我们不得不赶紧送她进来。
  2. She's been in and out of consciousness. — 她意识时有时无。
  3. It's a waiting game now. — 现在只能等。
  4. We're doing everything we can. — 我们在尽一切努力。

避免说法 ❌避免

  1. ~~She is very sick.~~ → She's in a lot of pain.（太笼统）
  2. ~~Help her quickly!~~ → We need to see someone right away.（太急切但没信息）
  3. ~~What is wrong with her?~~ → What do you think it could be?（焦虑但不pushy）
  4. ~~I cannot pay for this.~~ → How does the billing work with my insurance?（更实际）

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国急诊须知

  • ER非常昂贵：即使有保险，一次ER访问可能要$500-$5000+
  • Urgent Care更便宜：非紧急情况用Urgent Care，$50-$200
  • 多张账单：你可能会收到医院、医生、化验室分别寄来的账单
  • 等待时间长：ER按紧急程度排序（triage），非紧急可能等4-8小时
  • 911急救车：叫救护车可能要$500-$3000，除非真正紧急否则自己开车

🇬🇧 英国急诊须知

  • A&E免费：NHS的A&E不收费
  • 111热线：不确定是否紧急，打111咨询（24小时）
  • 999急救车：紧急情况打999，免费的
  • 等待时间：NHS A&E也按紧急程度排序，非紧急可能等很久
  • Walk-in Centre：轻伤和轻微疾病可以去Walk-in Centre，不用预约

💡 实用建议

  • 学会说关键急救词汇：chest pain, difficulty breathing, allergic reaction
  • 把保险卡随身携带
  • 手机里存好紧急联系人（ICE: In Case of Emergency）
  • 如果有药物过敏，考虑戴医疗警示手环（medical alert bracelet）
  • 知道自己血型（blood type）
