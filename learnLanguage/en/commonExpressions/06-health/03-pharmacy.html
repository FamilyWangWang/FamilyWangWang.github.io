第3章：药房取药（Pharmacy）

场景背景

在美国，药房（pharmacy）通常在CVS、Walgreens、Rite Aid等连锁药店内，或者超市如Walmart、Costco内。医生电子处方直接传到药房，你只需去取药。英国药房（chemist / pharmacy）如Boots、Lloyds Pharmacy也提供类似服务。OTC（over-the-counter）非处方药可以直接购买，不需要处方。

关键文化差异：

  • 🇺🇸 美国：处方药（prescription / Rx）和非处方药（OTC）分开放，处方药需要药剂师（pharmacist）调配
  • 🇬🇧 英国：类似，但"chemist"这个词比"pharmacy"更口语化（虽然两者都通用）
  • 美国药房 drive-through（免下车取药）很方便
  • 美国药物品牌和英国可能不同（如 Tylenol 美国vs Paracetamol 英国）

完整对话

子场景1：处方药取药（Picking Up a Prescription）

You: Hi, I'm here to pick up a prescription. The name's Alex Chen.

正式: Hello, I am here to collect a prescription. The name is Alex Chen. 中文: 你好，我来取处方药。我叫Alex Chen。 详解: "Pick up a prescription" = 取处方药，是最常用的说法。英国也可以说 "collect a prescription"。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Let me look that up for you. Do you have your date of birth for verification?

正式: Let me search for that in our system. Could you provide your date of birth for verification purposes? 中文: 我帮你查一下。能告诉我你的出生日期做验证吗？ 详解: 出于安全考虑，取处方药时药房通常会核对你的身份信息。✅推荐

You: April 23rd, 1988.

正式: April 23rd, 1988. 中文: 1988年4月23日。 详解: 美国日期格式：月+日+年。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Alright, I see it here. Dr. Patel called in a prescription for cough syrup and an inhaler. Give me just a minute, it's almost ready.

正式: Alright, I can see it in our system. Dr. Patel submitted a prescription for cough syrup and an inhaler. Please give me a moment, it is almost ready. 中文: 好的，我看到了。Patel医生电话开了止咳糖浆和吸入剂的处方。稍等一下，快好了。 详解: "Called in" = 医生通过电话或电子方式发送处方。"Almost ready" 说明药剂师正在调配。"Cough syrup" = 止咳糖浆，"inhaler" = 吸入器。✅推荐

You: Take your time.

正式: Take your time. 中文: 不着急。 详解: "Take your time" 是礼貌说法，表示"不用急"。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Here you go. So the cough syrup — take 10 milliliters every six hours as needed. Don't exceed four doses in 24 hours. And the inhaler — two puffs every four to six hours. Shake it well before each use.

正式: Here is your medication. The cough syrup — take 10 milliliters every six hours as needed. Do not exceed four doses in 24 hours. The inhaler — two puffs every four to six hours. Shake it thoroughly before each use. 中文: 给你。止咳糖浆——需要时每6小时服10毫升。24小时内不超过4次。吸入剂——每4到6小时吸两次。每次使用前充分摇晃。 详解: "Here you go" = 给你。"As needed" = 按需服用（PRN）。"Puffs" = 吸入剂的"喷/吸"。"Don't exceed" = 不要超过。药剂师必须提供用药指导（counseling）。✅推荐

You: Got it. Should I take it with food?

正式: Understood. Should I take this medication with food? 中文: 明白了。要饭后服用吗？ 详解: "Take it with food" = 饭后/随餐服用，是关于药物用法的常见问题。"Got it" = 明白了。✅推荐

Pharmacist: The cough syrup is fine on an empty stomach, but the inhaler doesn't really matter. Any allergies to medications that you know of?

正式: The cough syrup is safe to take on an empty stomach, but the inhaler is not affected by food. Are you aware of any medication allergies? 中文: 止咳糖浆空腹也可以，吸入剂无所谓。你知道自己对什么药物过敏吗？ 详解: "On an empty stomach" = 空腹。"Doesn't really matter" = 无所谓/不太重要。"That you know of" = 据你所知的。✅推荐

You: Not that I know of. How much do I owe you?

正式: Not that I am aware of. How much is the cost? 中文: 据我所知没有。要付多少钱？ 详解: "How much do I owe you?" = 我要付多少钱？非常口语化的询问价格方式。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Let me check... With your insurance, it comes to $12.50. Would you like to sign up for our rewards program? You'd save a few bucks on refills.

正式: Let me check. With your insurance, the total comes to $12.50. Would you like to enroll in our rewards program? You would save some money on prescription refills. 中文: 我看看... 有保险的话，总共12.50美元。你要加入我们的会员计划吗？续药的时候能省点钱。 详解: "Comes to" = 总共是/合计。"A few bucks" = 几块钱（bucks是dollar的口语说法）。"Refills" = 续药/重新配药。药房经常推销会员计划。✅推荐

You: Sure, why not.

正式: Sure, why not. 中文: 好吧，为什么不呢。 详解: "Sure, why not" 是随意但友好的同意方式。✅推荐

子场景2：购买非处方药（Buying OTC Medicine）

You: Excuse me, do you guys carry NyQuil?

正式: Excuse me, do you carry NyQuil? 中文: 打扰一下，你们有NyQuil卖吗？ 详解: "Do you guys carry..." = 你们有卖...吗？"Carry" 在零售语境中表示"销售/有货"。"NyQuil" 是美国最知名的感冒药品牌之一。✅推荐

Store Clerk: Aisle 3, right next to the DayQuil. Is there anything else you're looking for?

正式: Aisle 3, right next to the DayQuil. Is there anything else you are looking for? 中文: 第三通道，就在DayQuil旁边。还需要找别的吗？ 详解: "Aisle" = 商店通道。"Right next to" = 就在...旁边。DayQuil是白天吃的感冒药，NyQuil是晚上吃的（含安眠成分）。✅推荐

You: Yeah, actually I've been having trouble sleeping. What do you recommend for that?

正式: Yes, actually I have been having difficulty sleeping. What do you recommend for that? 中文: 对，其实我最近睡眠不好。你推荐什么？ 详解: "Having trouble sleeping" = 睡不好觉。"What do you recommend?" = 你推荐什么？直接问店员推荐是很常见的做法。✅推荐

Store Clerk: Melatonin works pretty well for mild sleep issues. It's over-the-counter, so you don't need a prescription. It's in the same aisle, bottom shelf.

正式: Melatonin is effective for mild sleep issues. It is available over-the-counter, so no prescription is required. It is in the same aisle, on the bottom shelf. 中文: 褪黑素对轻度睡眠问题效果不错。它是非处方药，不需要处方。在同一个通道，底层货架上。 详解: "Works pretty well" = 效果不错。"Over-the-counter" (OTC) = 非处方药。"Bottom shelf" = 底层货架。Melatonin = 褪黑素，美国很常见的助眠补充剂。✅推荐

You: Awesome, I'll grab a bottle. Oh, and do you have any ibuprofen? Generic is fine.

正式: Great, I will get a bottle. Also, do you carry ibuprofen? The generic version is acceptable. 中文: 太好了，我拿一瓶。哦，你们有布洛芬吗？仿制药就行。 详解: "Grab" = 拿取（口语化的"拿"）。"Generic" = 仿制药/通用名药，比品牌药便宜很多。在美国说 "generic is fine" 表示你不介意买便宜的通用版本。✅推荐

Store Clerk: Sure do. Aisle 2, right behind the Advil. The generic stuff is way cheaper and does the exact same thing.

正式: We certainly do. Aisle 2, right behind the Advil. The generic version is significantly cheaper and works identically. 中文: 当然有。第二通道，Advil后面。仿制药便宜很多，效果完全一样。 详解: "Sure do" = 当然有（口语化确认）。"Way cheaper" = 便宜很多，"way" 在口语中表示"很/非常"。"Does the exact same thing" = 效果完全一样。"Stuff" 口语中泛指东西/产品。✅推荐

子场景3：电话续药（Refilling a Prescription）

You: Hi, I need to refill a prescription. It's for Alex Chen, date of birth April 23rd, 1988.

正式: Hello, I need to refill a prescription. It is for Alex Chen, date of birth April 23rd, 1988. 中文: 你好，我需要续药。Alex Chen，出生日期1988年4月23日。 详解: "Refill a prescription" = 续处方药。这是当你的药吃完了需要重新配药时用的表达。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Let me pull up your profile... Okay, which medication would you like refilled?

正式: Let me access your profile. Which medication would you like refilled? 中文: 我调出你的档案... 好的，你要续哪种药？ 详解: "Pull up your profile" = 打开你的个人资料/档案。药房系统里会有你所有的用药记录。✅推荐

You: The blood pressure medication — I think it's called lisinopril. I'm running low.

正式: The blood pressure medication — I believe it is called lisinopril. My supply is running low. 中文: 降压药——好像叫lisinopril。快吃完了。 详解: "Running low" = 快用完了/快没了，非常实用的口语表达。"I think it's called..." 表示不太确定药名，这很正常。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Got it right here. I see you have two refills left on this one. When would you like to pick it up?

正式: I have found it. You have two refills remaining on this prescription. When would you like to pick it up? 中文: 找到了。这个处方你还有两次续药机会。你想什么时候来取？ 详解: "Refills left" = 剩余续药次数。处方药通常有一定的续药次数限制，用完后需要医生重新开处方。✅推荐

You: Can I get it tomorrow afternoon?

正式: Can I pick it up tomorrow afternoon? 中文: 明天下午能取吗？ 详解: 简洁明了的预约取药时间。✅推荐

Pharmacist: Absolutely. It'll be ready by 2 PM. We'll send you a text when it's good to go.

正式: Certainly. It will be ready by 2 PM. We will send you a text message when it is ready. 中文: 当然可以。下午2点前就准备好了。好了我们会发短信给你。 详解: "Absolutely" = 当然/没问题。"When it's good to go" = 准备好了的时候，非常口语化的说法。药房通常会发短信或App通知。✅推荐

核心口语表达清单

必学表达 ✅推荐

  1. I'm here to pick up a prescription. — 我来取处方药。
  2. How much do I owe you? — 我要付多少钱？
  3. Should I take it with food? — 要饭后吃吗？
  4. I'm running low on my meds. — 我的药快吃完了。
  5. I need to refill a prescription. — 我需要续药。
  6. Do you carry...? — 你们有卖...吗？
  7. Generic is fine. — 仿制药就行。
  8. What do you recommend for...? — 你推荐什么治...？
  9. It'll be ready by 2 PM. — 下午2点前就好。
  10. It's good to go. — 准备好了/没问题了。

了解即可 👂了解

  1. Can I get the drive-through? — 可以免下车取药吗？（美国特色）
  2. Is there a generic equivalent? — 有通用名替代药吗？
  3. Does this have any side effects? — 这有什么副作用吗？
  4. Can I drink alcohol while taking this? — 吃这个药能喝酒吗？

避免说法 ❌避免

  1. ~~I want to buy medicine.~~ → I need to pick up a prescription.（太笼统）
  2. ~~Give me the drug.~~ → I'm here to pick up my medication.（命令语气）
  3. ~~I need more pills.~~ → I need to refill my prescription.（"pills" 太随意）
  4. ~~What is this medicine?~~ → Could you tell me what this medication is for?（太突然）

注意事项

🇺🇸 美国药房文化

  • CVS / Walgreens / Rite Aid 是三大连锁药房
  • Drive-through：很多药房有免下车窗口，非常方便
  • 90-day supply：很多保险计划鼓励你一次开90天的药量，比30天便宜
  • GoodRx：如果没有保险或保险不报销，可以用GoodRx App查折扣价
  • Mail-order pharmacy：慢性病药物可以通过邮寄药房订购，通常更便宜

🇬🇧 英国药房文化

  • Boots / Lloyds Pharmacy / Superdrug 是常见药房
  • NHS处方费：每项药£9.90（2025年），苏格兰/威尔士免费
  • Prescription Prepayment Certificate (PPC)：如果经常吃药，买PPC更划算（约£31.40/3个月）
  • "Chemist"：英国人仍然常用这个词指药房
  • Paracetamol：英国最常用的止痛/退烧药，相当于美国的Tylenol

💡 实用建议

  • 取药时一定要核对你的名字和药名
  • 问清楚用药方法：剂量（dose）、频率（frequency）、注意事项
  • 如果药有副作用，打电话告诉药房或医生
  • 保管好你的处方号（Rx number），方便续药
  • 有些药需要冰箱保存，记得问一下
