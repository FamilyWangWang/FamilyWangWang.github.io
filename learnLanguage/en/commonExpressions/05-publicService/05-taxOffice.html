第5章：税务局（Tax Office）

场景背景

在美国，联邦税务局叫 IRS（Internal Revenue Service，国内税务局），各州也有自己的税务局。美国人每年4月15日前要提交联邦税表（tax return）。在英国，税务局叫 HMRC（His Majesty's Revenue and Customs，国王税务海关总署），英国的报税日是每年的1月31日（针对自雇人士）。大部分有工作的雇员通过 PAYE（Pay As You Earn）系统自动扣税，不需要自己报税。但自雇人士（self-employed）和有额外收入的人需要填写 Self Assessment tax return。

文化提示： 美国的报税系统比较复杂，很多人用 TurboTax、H&R Block 等软件或请会计师帮忙。英国的自雇报税可以通过 HMRC 网站在线完成。两国的税务局都非常严肃，欠税不还会面临严厉处罚。✅推荐 按时报税，如有困难及时联系税务局。

子场景一：在美国咨询报税问题

完整对话（含逐句详解）

背景： 张先生第一次在美国报税，有一些问题，来到当地的 IRS 纳税人援助中心（Taxpayer Assistance Center）。

1. 张先生： Hi there, this is my first time filing taxes in the US and I'm a bit lost. Could someone walk me through the basics?

正式表达： Hello, this is my first time filing taxes in the US and I am somewhat confused. Could someone guide me through the basics?

中文翻译： 你好，这是我第一次在美国报税，有点懵。能有人给我讲讲基本流程吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "filing taxes" 是"报税"的标准说法，不说 "reporting taxes"。"a bit lost" 在口语中不一定是迷路，而是"困惑/摸不着头脑"。"walk me through" 再次出现，意思是"一步步教我/带我过一遍"。

2. IRS 工作人员： Of course. First off, do you have your W-2 form from your employer? That's the form that shows how much you earned and how much tax was taken out.

正式表达： Certainly. First, do you have your W-2 form from your employer? That form shows your earnings and taxes withheld.

中文翻译： 当然。首先，你有雇主给你的 W-2 表格吗？那个表格显示你赚了多少和扣了多少税。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "First off" 是口语中"首先"的说法，比 "Firstly" 自然得多。"W-2" 是美国的工资收入表，每年1月底前雇主必须提供。"taken out" 是口语中"扣除（税款）"的说法。

3. 张先生： Yeah, I've got that right here. I also have a 1099 form from some freelance work I did on the side. Do I need to report that too?

正式表达： Yes, I have it here. I also have a 1099 form from freelance work I did additionally. Do I need to report that?

中文翻译： 有，在这。我还有一些兼职的自由职业收入，有一张1099表格。那个也需要报吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "on the side" 是口语中"副业/兼职/额外"的说法，非常地道。"1099" 表格是美国用于报告非工资收入的表格（自由职业、投资等）。"report" 在税务语境中是"申报"的意思。

4. IRS 工作人员： Absolutely, all income needs to be reported. The 1099 goes on a different part of your return. You might owe a little extra on that since no taxes were withheld from it.

正式表达： Yes, all income must be reported. The 1099 income goes in a different section. You may owe additional tax since none was withheld.

中文翻译： 当然，所有收入都需要申报。1099的收入填在税表的不同部分。你可能需要额外补交一些税，因为那部分收入没有预扣税。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "owe" 在税务语境中是"欠（税）"的意思。"withheld" 是"预扣/代扣"。美国的自由职业收入通常没有预扣税，所以报税时可能需要补交。

5. 张先生： Oh, I didn't realize that. Roughly how much would I owe on, say, $5,000 of freelance income?

正式表达： I was not aware of that. Approximately how much would I owe on $5,000 of freelance income?

中文翻译： 哦，我不知道这个。大概来说，5000美元的自由职业收入我需要交多少税？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "roughly" 是口语中"大概/大约"的说法。"say" 在这里表示"假设/比如说"，用于举例。

6. IRS 工作人员： It's hard to say off the top of my head since it depends on your tax bracket and deductions. But as a ballpark, you're looking at maybe $800 to $1,200 in self-employment tax on top of regular income tax.

正式表达： It is difficult to estimate without checking, as it depends on your tax bracket and deductions. As an estimate, you may owe approximately $800 to $1,200 in self-employment tax in addition to regular income tax.

中文翻译： 一时说不准，因为要看你的税率和扣除额。但大致来说，你可能要额外交800到1200美元的自雇税，加上正常的所得税。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "off the top of my head" 是口语中"不查资料/凭记忆"的说法，意思是"一时想不起来/随口说"。"tax bracket" 是税率档次。"deductions" 是扣除项。"on top of" 意思是"在...之外还..."。"self-employment tax" 是自雇税，包含社保和医疗税。

7. 张先生： That's quite a bit. Is there anything I can do to lower my tax bill? Any deductions I might be missing out on?

正式表达： That is quite a lot. Is there anything I can do to reduce my tax liability? Any deductions I might be missing?

中文翻译： 挺多的。有什么办法能减税吗？有没有什么我可能漏掉的扣除项？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "quite a bit" 是口语中"相当多"的说法。"tax bill" 在口语中指"应交税额"，不是账单。"missing out on" 意思是"错过/漏掉"。

8. IRS 工作人员： Well, a lot of people take the standard deduction, which is $14,600 for a single filer this year. But if you have a lot of itemized deductions — like mortgage interest, charitable donations, medical expenses — you might be better off itemizing. It really depends on your situation.

正式表达： Many people take the standard deduction, which is $14,600 for single filers this year. But if you have significant itemized deductions such as mortgage interest, charitable donations, or medical expenses, itemizing may be more beneficial.

中文翻译： 嗯，很多人选择标准扣除，今年单身申报者是14600美元。但如果你有很多逐项扣除——比如房贷利息、慈善捐款、医疗费用——逐项列报可能更划算。主要看你的具体情况。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "standard deduction" 是标准扣除额，"itemized deductions" 是逐项扣除。"filer" 是申报者。"might be better off" 意思是"可能更划算/更好"。👂了解 大多数美国人选择标准扣除，比较简单。

9. 张先生： I think the standard deduction makes more sense for me. I don't own a house or anything like that.

正式表达： I think the standard deduction is more appropriate for me. I do not own a home or have similar deductions.

中文翻译： 我觉得标准扣除对我来说更合适。我没有房子之类的。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "makes more sense" 意思是"更合理/更合适"。"or anything like that" 是口语中"诸如此类的"补充说法。租房者通常选择标准扣除。

10. IRS 工作人员： That's probably the way to go then. Now, have you thought about using tax software? TurboTax, H&R Block — they make it pretty painless. They walk you through everything step by step.

正式表达： That is probably the best approach. Have you considered using tax software? They make the process straightforward and guide you step by step.

中文翻译： 那大概就是最佳选择了。你考虑过用报税软件吗？TurboTax、H&R Block——它们让报税变得挺轻松的。一步步带你走完全部流程。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "the way to go" 是口语中"最佳选择/正确方向"的说法。"painless" 字面是"无痛的"，口语中意思是"不痛苦/轻松的"。👂了解 大多数美国人用报税软件而不是找会计师。

11. 张先生： Yeah, a friend of mine recommended TurboTax. I'll probably go that route. One last question — what happens if I can't pay everything I owe by April 15th?

正式表达： A friend recommended TurboTax. I will likely use that option. One last question — what happens if I cannot pay the full amount by April 15th?

中文翻译： 对，一个朋友推荐了 TurboTax。我可能会走那条路。最后一个问题——如果我4月15日前交不全所欠的税怎么办？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "go that route" 意思是"走那条路/选择那个方案"，口语中表达选择非常自然。"what happens if" 是询问后果的标准问法。

12. IRS 工作人员： Don't panic. You can set up a payment plan with us. There's a small setup fee and interest on the balance, but it's way better than ignoring it. Whatever you do, make sure you file on time even if you can't pay — the penalty for not filing is actually worse than the penalty for not paying.

正式表达： Do not worry. You can set up a payment plan with us. There is a small setup fee and interest on the balance, but it is better than ignoring the obligation. File on time even if you cannot pay, as the penalty for not filing is worse than for not paying.

中文翻译： 别慌。你可以跟我们设置一个分期付款计划。有少量的设置费和余额利息，但比不管不问好得多。无论怎样，确保按时申报——不申报的罚款其实比不交税的罚款更重。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Don't panic" 是"别慌"。"payment plan" 是分期付款计划。"way better" 中 "way" 是口语副词"远比...好"。"whatever you do" 是口语中"无论如何"的强调说法。👂了解 IRS 的重要原则：报税比交税更重要。不报税的处罚更严重。

13. 张先生： Good to know. Thanks for all the help, you've been really helpful.

正式表达： That is good to know. Thank you for all the help, you have been very helpful.

中文翻译： 知道了。感谢所有帮助，你真的很帮忙。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Good to know" 是口语中"这个信息很有用/记住了"的说法。简洁自然。

14. IRS 工作人员： Happy to help. And hey, if you get stuck on anything, you can always call our helpline or check out IRS.gov. There's tons of info on there.

正式表达： I am happy to help. If you encounter difficulties, you can call our helpline or visit IRS.gov for extensive information.

中文翻译： 很高兴帮忙。如果在任何地方卡住了，随时可以打我们的热线或上 IRS.gov。上面有大量信息。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "get stuck" 是口语中"卡住/遇到困难"的说法。"tons of" 是口语中"大量的/很多"的说法，比 "a lot of" 更生动。

子场景二：在英国咨询自雇报税

完整对话（含逐句详解）

背景： 张先生在英国做自雇工作，需要向 HMRC 注册并了解自雇报税流程。

1. 张先生： Hello, I've recently started working for myself and I was told I need to register for Self Assessment. Could you help me get that sorted?

正式表达： Hello, I have recently become self-employed and I was told I need to register for Self Assessment. Could you help me with that?

中文翻译： 你好，我最近开始自雇了，有人告诉我需要注册 Self Assessment。能帮我处理一下吗？

详细解释： 🇬🇧 "working for myself" 是英式口语中"自雇"的说法，不说 "self-employed" 也很自然。"Self Assessment" 是英国自雇报税的正式名称。"get that sorted" 是英式口语"处理/解决"的万能说法。

2. HMRC 客服： Of course, I can help with that. When did you start trading? We need to register you by the 5th of October in your business's second tax year.

正式表达： Certainly. When did you start your business? Registration is required by October 5th of your business's second tax year.

中文翻译： 当然可以。你什么时候开始经营的？需要在业务第二个税务年度的10月5日前注册。

详细解释： 🇬🇧 "trading" 在英国税务语境中是"经营/做生意"的意思，不限于贸易。"tax year" 在英国是4月6日到次年4月5日，和美国不同。

3. 张先生： I started in September last year, so I should be within the deadline. What information do you need from me?

正式表达： I started in September last year, so I should be within the deadline. What information do you need?

中文翻译： 我去年9月开始的，所以应该在截止日期内。你需要我提供什么信息？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "within the deadline" 即"在截止日期内"。"What information do you need from me?" 是提供信息的标准问法。

4. HMRC 客服： Brilliant. I'll need your National Insurance number, your UTR if you have one already, and your personal details. Do you have your NI number handy?

正式表达： Excellent. I will need your National Insurance number, your UTR if available, and your personal details. Do you have your NI number available?

中文翻译： 好的。我需要你的国民保险号，如果有的话还有 UTR，以及你的个人信息。你手边有国民保险号吗？

详细解释： 🇬🇧 "NI number"（National Insurance number）相当于英国的社会安全号，非常重要。"UTR"（Unique Taxpayer Reference）是纳税人唯一参考号。"handy" 在口语中是"手边的/方便拿到的"。

5. 张先生： Yeah, let me just dig it out... Here we are. It's QQ 12 34 56 A.

正式表达： Yes, let me find it. Here it is. It is QQ 12 34 56 A.

中文翻译： 有，我找一下……找到了。是 QQ 12 34 56 A。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "dig it out" 是口语中"找出来"的说法，暗示需要翻找。"Here we are" 是找到东西时的口语说法，意思是"找到了/在这里了"。

6. HMRC 客服： Perfect. I've pulled up your records. Now, for Self Assessment, you'll need to keep track of all your income and expenses throughout the year. You can file your return online through the Government Gateway.

正式表达： Perfect. I have accessed your records. For Self Assessment, you must track all income and expenses throughout the year. You can file online through the Government Gateway.

中文翻译： 好的。我调出了你的记录。自雇报税的话，你需要在全年追踪所有收入和支出。你可以通过 Government Gateway 网上申报。

详细解释： 🇬🇧 "keep track of" 意思是"追踪/记录"。"Government Gateway" 是英国政府的在线服务入口。👂了解 英国的自雇报税比美国简单得多，很多人自己就能完成。

7. 张先生： Is there a simple way to work out my expenses? I'm not great with numbers, to be honest.

正式表达： Is there a simple method for calculating expenses? I am not skilled with numbers, to be honest.

中文翻译： 有没有简单的方法计算支出？说实话我不太擅长算数。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "work out" 是英式口语中"计算/算出"的说法。"not great with numbers" 是口语中谦虚地表示"不太擅长算数"的说法。

8. HMRC 客服： Absolutely. You can use the £1,000 trading allowance instead of claiming actual expenses, which keeps things nice and simple. Or if your actual expenses are higher, you can claim those instead. It's whichever works out better for you.

正式表达： Yes. You can use the £1,000 trading allowance instead of claiming actual expenses, which is simpler. Or you can claim actual expenses if they are higher. Choose whichever is more beneficial.

中文翻译： 当然有。你可以用1000英镑的交易津贴代替申报实际支出，简单省事。如果你的实际支出更高，也可以申报实际支出。选对你更有利的那个。

详细解释： 🇬🇧 "trading allowance" 是英国自雇者的1000英镑免税津贴。"nice and simple" 是口语中"简单明了"的说法。"whichever works out better" 意思是"哪个更划算就用哪个"。

9. 张先生： That's straightforward enough. And when's the deadline for filing?

正式表达： That is straightforward. When is the filing deadline?

中文翻译： 够简单的。那申报截止日期是什么时候？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "straightforward enough" 意思是"足够简单/不难"。

10. HMRC 客服： Online filing is due by January 31st after the end of the tax year. So for the tax year ending April 2025, you'd need to file by January 31st, 2026. And pay any tax you owe by the same date.

正式表达： Online filing is due by January 31st after the tax year ends. For the tax year ending April 2025, the deadline is January 31st, 2026. Payment is due by the same date.

中文翻译： 网上申报截止日期是税务年度结束后的1月31日。所以截至2025年4月的税务年度，你需要在2026年1月31日前申报。并在同一日期前缴纳所欠税款。

详细解释： 🇬🇧 英国的税务年度是4月6日到次年4月5日，和美国（1月到12月）不同。自雇申报和缴税的截止日期都是1月31日。

注意事项

  1. 按时报税： 无论美国还是英国，按时报税非常重要。美国不报税的罚款比不交税更重。✅推荐 宁可报了交不上，也不要不报。
  2. 保留记录： ✅推荐 保留所有收入和支出记录至少3-5年。美国 IRS 可以追溯审计（audit）过去3年的税表，特殊情况可达6年。
  3. 寻求专业帮助： 如果收入来源复杂（投资、自雇、海外收入等），建议请会计师（CPA 在美国、accountant 在英国）。✅推荐 报税软件对简单的 W-2 收入足够了。
  4. 海外资产申报： 中国公民在美国有海外账户超过一定金额需要申报 FBAR（Foreign Bank Account Report）。✅推荐 了解相关要求。
  5. ❌避免 逃税或虚报扣除。两国对逃税的处罚都非常严厉，包括罚款甚至刑事起诉。
  6. 英国 vs 美国税制差异： 英国的税制对雇员来说简单很多（PAYE 自动扣税），自雇报税也相对简单。美国则复杂得多，即使普通雇员也需要每年报税。
