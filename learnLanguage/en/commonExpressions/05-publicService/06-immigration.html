面试前先做什么：用自己的材料准备

张先生收到下周面试通知，有些紧张，律师先把准备落到通知、申请与相关证明上。

张先生：

Hi, my green card interview is coming up next week and I'm getting a little nervous. What should I expect?

你好，我的绿卡面试下周就要到了，我有点紧张。我应该预期什么？

律师：

It's understandable to feel nervous. Let's use your interview notice to prepare. The officer will review your application and relevant evidence; in this marriage-based case, that includes whether the marriage is bona fide — genuine. We can't predict the exact questions or outcome.

紧张可以理解，先按面试通知准备。官员会核申请和相关证据，这个婚姻申请也涉及婚姻是否真实。具体问题和结果不能预先确定。

张先生：

What kind of questions do they usually ask? Like, really personal stuff?

他们通常问什么类型的问题？比如，很私人的那种？

律师：

Some questions may be personal. They could concern how you met, your shared home or daily arrangements. Those are examples, not a script to memorize. Review your own records, and say if you don't understand a question or don't remember something.

有些可能涉及私事，比如怎么认识、共同住所或日常安排。这些只是例子，不是背诵脚本。看自己的记录，问题没听懂或事情不记得，要说明。

张先生：

That makes sense. Should we bring any evidence with us?

有道理。我们需要带证据吗？

律师：

Let's check the notice and the documents you've already submitted. We'll organize the requested originals and copies, plus relevant updates, such as current joint-account or housing records if they apply. Clear, accurate evidence is more useful than a pile of unrelated papers.

先看通知和已交文件，整理要求的原件、复印件及相关更新，例如适用的近期联名账户或住房记录。清楚准确的证据比一堆无关文件更有用。

张先生：

Got it. What about the tone of the interview? Is it like an interrogation?

明白了。面试的氛围怎么样？像审讯吗？

律师：

The tone can be matter-of-fact, and follow-up questions are possible. Listen carefully and answer truthfully and to the point. If a short answer would leave a misleading impression, explain the relevant detail; if something is unclear, ask for clarification.

语气可能公事公办，也可能追问。认真听，如实并切题回答。短答若会让人误解，就补相关细节；不清楚就请澄清。

独立H-1B咨询：停留、工作与旅行贴签分开

另一名申请人仍为同一雇主工作，询问I-94所示停留期将满时怎样办理。

张先生：

Hi, I'm in H-1B status, and the admitted-until date on my I-94 is approaching. I need to ask about an extension of stay. What does the process look like?

你好，目前是H-1B身份，I-94上准许停留截止日快到了，想问延长停留。流程怎样？

USCIS 客服：

I can explain the general process. Just to confirm, are you asking about continuing your H-1B employment in the US with the same employer, rather than obtaining a new visa stamp for travel?

可以解释一般流程。先确认，是要在美国继续为同一雇主做H-1B工作，而不是为旅行取得新的签证贴签，对吗？

张先生：

Yes, the same employer. I've been with them for about three years, and I'm asking about extending my stay and employment here.

对，同一雇主，约三年了，问的是延长在这里的停留和工作。

USCIS 客服：

For that H-1B extension, the employer files the petition. Contact your employer's HR team or immigration lawyer to review the I-94, current approval and supporting documents. They'll handle the filing for the employer and tell you what information they need from you.

这种H-1B延期由雇主提交申请。联系雇主人事或移民律师，核I-94、现批准文件和证明材料。他们代表雇主办理，也会说明需要你提供什么。

张先生：

Oh right, I remember now. How far in advance should we start the process? I don't wanna cut it too close.

哦对，我想起来了。我们应该提前多久开始？我不想拖到最后。

USCIS 客服：

Get in touch with them now to set a timetable. They'll check the permitted filing window and your exact expiration date. Processing times vary, so don't cut it too close or use the visa stamp's expiration date as the sole guide to your stay.

现在就联系并定时间表。他们会核可提交窗口和准确到期日。处理时间有变化，别拖得太紧，也别只凭贴签到期日判断停留期限。

张先生：

Understood. And can I keep working while the extension is being processed?

明白了。延期处理期间我能继续工作吗？

USCIS 客服：

A rule may allow continued work for the same employer for up to 240 days after the authorized stay expires when an eligible extension is timely filed and remains pending. It has conditions; a denial can end that authorization earlier. Have your employer and lawyer confirm whether it applies and the exact dates before relying on it.

符合条件的延期及时提交且仍待审时，有规则可能允许准许停留到期后继续为同一雇主工作最多240天。它有条件，拒绝决定也可能使许可更早结束。依赖这项规则前，请雇主和律师核是否适用及准确日期。

张先生：

That gives me a starting point. I'll contact HR today and get the ball rolling, including written confirmation of the filing and work-authorization dates. Thanks.

知道从哪开始了。今天联系人事，着手办，也要书面确认提交与工作许可日期。谢谢。

What does the process look like?是完整问法。问境内延期时，先把I-94、当前批准与雇主材料对上；签证贴签的有效期与准许停留期用途不同，可查美国国务院的日期说明。on your behalf在这里是由相应人员代表雇主办理，不把电话解释当申请已经提交。

cut it too close说时间留得太紧，get the ball rolling说着手推进。符合条件的同雇主待审延期可能涉及最多240天继续工作规则，且可能因更早拒绝而终止，参见USCIS对非移民雇佣延期的说明。pending只是待审，还要核适用条件及准确日期；最后联系人事取得确认，才把“知道一点规则”接成下一步行动。
