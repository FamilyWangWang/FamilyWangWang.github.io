第6章：移民局（Immigration Office）

场景背景

在美国，移民相关事务由 USCIS（United States Citizenship and Immigration Services，美国公民及移民服务局）处理，涉及签证（visa）、绿卡（green card/permanent resident card）、入籍（naturalization/citizenship）等。在英国，移民事务由 UKVI（UK Visas and Immigration）处理。两国的移民系统都非常复杂，申请过程漫长，需要准备大量文件。

文化提示： 美国的移民系统以缓慢和复杂著称。绿卡申请（通过婚姻、工作或亲属）可能需要数月到数年。uscis.gov 是美国移民局的官方网站，几乎所有表格都可以在线提交。面试时要准时、着装得体、带齐文件。英国有积分制移民系统（points-based system），与美国体系不同。

子场景一：绿卡面试准备咨询

完整对话（含逐句详解）

背景： 张先生通过婚姻申请绿卡，在面试前向移民律师咨询注意事项。

1. 张先生： Hi, my green card interview is coming up next week and I'm getting a little nervous. What should I expect?

正式表达： Hello, my green card interview is next week and I am somewhat nervous. What should I expect?

中文翻译： 你好，我的绿卡面试下周就要到了，我有点紧张。我应该预期什么？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "coming up" 是口语中"即将到来/快到了"的说法。"getting a little nervous" 中的 "getting" 表示状态的渐进变化。"What should I expect?" 是询问面试流程的标准问法。

2. 律师： Totally normal to feel that way. The interview is pretty straightforward, actually. A USCIS officer will go through your application with you and ask questions to verify that your marriage is bona fide — that it's a real marriage, not just for immigration purposes.

正式表达： It is normal to feel nervous. The interview is straightforward. A USCIS officer will review your application and ask questions to verify that your marriage is genuine and not solely for immigration purposes.

中文翻译： 有这种感觉完全正常。面试其实挺简单的。USCIS 官员会和你过一遍申请，问一些问题来验证你们的婚姻是真实的——是真正的婚姻，不是只为了移民。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Totally normal to feel that way" 是安慰别人的自然说法。"bona fide" 是拉丁语法律术语，意思是"真实的/善意的"，在移民法律中常用。"go through" 是"过一遍/审查"的意思。

3. 张先生： What kind of questions do they usually ask? Like, really personal stuff?

正式表达： What kind of questions do they usually ask? Are they very personal?

中文翻译： 他们通常问什么类型的问题？比如，很私人的那种？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Like" 作为填充词引出举例。"really personal stuff" 是"很私人的事情"的口语说法。"stuff" 是口语中"事情/东西"的万能词。

4. 律师： Some can be personal, yeah. They might ask how you met, when you moved in together, who pays the bills, what you had for dinner last night — things like that. They're trying to get a sense of your day-to-day life together.

正式表达： Some questions can be personal. They may ask how you met, when you moved in together, who manages finances, what you ate recently. They are trying to understand your daily life together.

中文翻译： 有些确实比较私人。他们可能会问你们怎么认识的、什么时候搬一起住的、谁付账单、昨晚吃了什么——诸如此类的。他们想了解你们日常生活在一起的状况。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "get a sense of" 意思是"了解/感受"。"day-to-day life" 即"日常生活"。"who pays the bills" 和 "what you had for dinner" 都是面试中的典型问题。👂了解 婚姻绿卡面试的目的是确认关系真实性。

5. 张先生： That makes sense. Should we bring any evidence with us?

正式表达： That is understandable. Should we bring any evidence?

中文翻译： 有道理。我们需要带证据吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "That makes sense" 再次出现，是"有道理/说得通"的万能回应。"evidence" 在移民语境中是"证明材料/证据"。

6. 律师： Absolutely. Bring anything that shows you're a genuine couple — joint bank account statements, lease or mortgage with both names, photos from trips and events, wedding photos, letters from friends and family. The more, the better.

正式表达： Absolutely. Bring anything demonstrating a genuine relationship — joint bank statements, shared lease or mortgage, photos, wedding photos, and letters from friends and family. More is better.

中文翻译： 当然。带上任何能证明你们是真正夫妻的东西——联名银行账单、两人名字的租约或房贷、旅行和活动的照片、婚礼照片、朋友和家人的信。越多越好。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "joint" 在法律和财务语境中是"共同的/联名"的意思。"The more, the better" 是口语中"越多越好"的说法。"genuine couple" 意思是"真正的夫妻/伴侣"。

7. 张先生： Got it. What about the tone of the interview? Is it like an interrogation?

正式表达： I understand. What about the tone? Is it like an interrogation?

中文翻译： 明白了。面试的氛围怎么样？像审讯吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "tone" 在这里指"氛围/基调"。"interrogation" 是"审讯"。这是很多申请人的担忧。

8. 律师： Not at all, don't worry about that. Most officers are professional and matter-of-fact. Just be yourself, answer honestly, and don't volunteer more information than what's asked. Keep your answers short and to the point.

正式表达： Not at all. Most officers are professional and straightforward. Be yourself, answer honestly, and do not provide more information than asked. Keep answers concise.

中文翻译： 完全不是，别担心。大多数官员都很专业、实事求是。做你自己，诚实回答，不要主动提供超出问题的信息。保持回答简洁到位。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "matter-of-fact" 意思是"实事求是的/不带感情的"。"don't volunteer more information" 中 "volunteer" 意思是"主动提供"，这是一个非常重要的建议。"to the point" 是"切中要害/简洁"。

9. 张先生： That's good advice. What should I wear? Is a suit necessary?

正式表达： That is good advice. What should I wear? Is a suit necessary?

中文翻译： 好建议。我穿什么？需要穿西装吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "good advice" 是回应建议的自然说法。面试着装在移民面试中确实重要。

10. 律师： You don't need a full suit, but dress nicely — like business casual. You want to show respect for the process. Slacks and a button-down shirt would be perfect. No jeans or sneakers.

正式表达： A full suit is not necessary, but dress nicely in business casual attire. Show respect for the process. Slacks and a button-down shirt are ideal. Avoid jeans and sneakers.

中文翻译： 不需要全套西装，但要穿得体面——商务休闲那种。你要表现出对这个过程的尊重。休闲裤和衬衫就很完美。别穿牛仔裤和运动鞋。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "business casual" 是商务休闲装，介于正式和休闲之间。"button-down shirt" 是带领扣的衬衫。"slacks" 是休闲西裤。👂了解 美国的 "business casual" 已经比很多国家随意，但移民面试还是建议穿得比平时正式一点。

11. 张先生： Sounds doable. One more thing — how long does the whole thing usually take?

正式表达： That sounds manageable. One more thing — how long does the interview typically last?

中文翻译： 听起来可以。还有一件事——整个流程大概要多久？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "doable" 是口语中"可行的/能做到的"。"the whole thing" 指整个面试过程，不说 "the entire process"。

12. 律师： The interview itself is usually about 30 minutes to an hour. If everything goes smoothly, you might get a decision on the spot, or they'll mail it to you within a few weeks. Either way, you're in the home stretch.

正式表达： The interview itself typically lasts 30 minutes to one hour. If everything goes well, you may receive a decision immediately or by mail within a few weeks. Either way, you are near the end of the process.

中文翻译： 面试本身通常30分钟到一小时。如果一切顺利，你可能会当场获得结果，或者几周内邮寄给你。不管怎样，你已经快到终点了。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "on the spot" 是口语中"当场/立即"的说法。"in the home stretch" 来自赛马术语，意思是"快到终点了/最后阶段"。这两个都是非常地道的口语表达。

13. 张先生： That's reassuring. Thanks so much for walking me through all this. I feel a lot better now.

正式表达： That is reassuring. Thank you for guiding me through everything. I feel much better.

中文翻译： 这让人安心多了。非常感谢你带我过了一遍这些。我感觉好多了。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "reassuring" 是"令人安心的"。"walking me through" 再次出现。"a lot better" 中的 "a lot" 起强调作用。

子场景二：签证到期前咨询续签

完整对话（含逐句详解）

背景： 张先生的工作签证即将到期，打电话给 USCIS 咨询续签流程。

1. 张先生： Hi, my work visa is about to expire and I need to get it renewed. What's the process look like these days?

正式表达： Hello, my work visa is about to expire and I need to renew it. What does the process entail?

中文翻译： 你好，我的工作签证快要到期了，需要续签。现在的流程是什么样的？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "about to" 意思是"即将/快要"。"get it renewed" 是"让别人帮我续签"的口语结构（get + object + past participle）。"What's the process look like?" 口语中在 "what" 后面加 "does" 常被省略。

2. USCIS 客服： Sure, I can help with that. What type of visa do you currently have?

正式表达： Certainly. What type of visa do you currently hold?

中文翻译： 好的，我能帮你。你目前持有什么类型的签证？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "currently have" 是"目前持有"的自然说法。

3. 张先生： It's an H-1B. I've been with the same employer for about three years now.

正式表达： I have an H-1B visa. I have been with the same employer for approximately three years.

中文翻译： H-1B签证。我在同一个雇主那干了大约三年了。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "been with" 意思是"在...工作"，不说 "worked at"。"about three years now" 中 "now" 强调到现在为止。

4. USCIS 客服： Okay, so your employer would need to file a petition to extend your status. You don't actually file it yourself — your company's HR or immigration attorney handles that on your behalf.

正式表达： Your employer needs to file a petition to extend your status. You do not file it yourself — your company's HR or immigration attorney handles it for you.

中文翻译： 好的，你的雇主需要提交延期申请。你不需要自己申请——你们公司的人事或移民律师会代你处理。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "file a petition" 是"提交申请"的法律术语。"on your behalf" 意思是"代表你/替你"。H-1B 是雇主担保的工作签证，续签由雇主发起。

5. 张先生： Oh right, I remember now. How far in advance should we start the process? I don't wanna cut it too close.

正式表达： I remember now. How far in advance should we begin the process? I do not want to wait too long.

中文翻译： 哦对，我想起来了。我们应该提前多久开始？我不想拖到最后。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "How far in advance" 是询问提前量的标准说法。"cut it too close" 是口语中"时间太紧/拖到最后一刻"的说法，非常地道。

6. USCIS 客服： I'd say start at least 4 to 6 months before your current visa expires. Processing times can vary quite a bit, so the earlier, the better. You don't want to end up out of status.

正式表达： I recommend starting 4 to 6 months before expiration. Processing times vary, so earlier is better. You do not want to fall out of status.

中文翻译： 我建议至少在当前签证到期前4到6个月开始。处理时间差异很大，所以越早越好。你不会想变成非法滞留的。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "the earlier, the better" 是"越早越好"的口语表达。"vary quite a bit" 意思是"变化相当大"。"out of status" 是移民法律术语，意思是"失去合法身份/非法滞留"。👂了解 在美国 "out of status" 是非常严重的问题，会严重影响未来签证申请。

7. 张先生： Understood. And can I keep working while the extension is being processed?

正式表达： I understand. Can I continue working while the extension is being processed?

中文翻译： 明白了。延期处理期间我能继续工作吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "keep working" 比 "continue to work" 更口语化。"while...is being processed" 表示"正在处理期间"。

8. USCIS 客服： Yes, as long as your employer filed the petition before your current status expires, you're covered under what we call a "240-day rule." That means you can keep working for up to 240 days while the extension is pending.

正式表达： Yes, as long as the petition was filed before your current status expires, you are covered under the 240-day rule. You may continue working for up to 240 days while the extension is pending.

中文翻译： 可以，只要雇主在你当前身份到期前提交了申请，你就受所谓的"240天规则"保护。这意味着延期审批期间你可以继续工作最多240天。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "as long as" 是口语中"只要"的说法。"you're covered" 意思是"你受到保护/覆盖"。"pending" 是"待处理/审批中"的意思。👂了解 "240-day rule" 是美国移民法中关于工作签证延期的重要规定。

9. 张先生： That's a relief. Alright, I'll get in touch with my HR department and get the ball rolling. Thanks for the info.

正式表达： That is reassuring. I will contact my HR department and begin the process. Thank you for the information.

中文翻译： 那就放心了。好的，我联系人事部门，开始着手办。谢谢你的信息。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "That's a relief" 是口语中"那就放心了/松了口气"的说法。"get in touch with" 意思是"联系"。"get the ball rolling" 是口语中"开始行动/着手做"的习语，非常生动。

注意事项

  1. 文件准备： 移民面试务必带齐所有文件原件和复印件。✅推荐 准备一个文件夹，按类别整理好。
  2. 诚实为上： 对移民官员必须100%诚实。❌避免 任何虚假陈述，被发现可能导致拒签甚至被禁入境。
  3. 保持合法身份： 在美国，失去合法身份（out of status）后果严重。✅推荐 签证到期前尽早申请延期。
  4. 律师咨询： 复杂的移民案件建议咨询专业移民律师。✅推荐 找有经验的律师，避免便宜的"移民顾问"。
  5. 面试礼仪： 着装得体（business casual 以上），提前到达，对官员保持礼貌和尊重。✅推荐 提前15分钟到达。
  6. ❌避免 在社交媒体上讨论移民状态或面试细节。
