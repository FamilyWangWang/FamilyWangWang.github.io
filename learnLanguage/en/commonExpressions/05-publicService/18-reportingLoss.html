第18章：报失与被盗（Reporting Lost Items / Stolen Wallet）

场景背景

在国外丢失钱包、手机或重要证件是海外生活中可能遇到的问题。处理流程通常是：先冷静回想可能丢失的地方 → 挂失银行卡和信用卡 → 报警（如果是被盗）→ 补办证件。美国的报警电话是 911（紧急）/ 311（非紧急），英国是 999（紧急）/ 101（非紧急）。两国都有在线报案系统。

文化提示： 在美国，如果钱包被盗，最大的风险不是现金损失（信用卡有盗刷保护），而是身份盗窃（identity theft）。✅推荐 第一时间冻结信用卡、通知银行、查看信用报告。在英国，类似的风险也存在，但英国的银行保护机制也比较完善。两国都建议在 Credit Karma（美国）或 Experian（两国都有）等信用机构设置欺诈警报。

子场景一：钱包被盗后的紧急处理

完整对话（含逐句详解）

背景： 张先生在商场发现自己的钱包不见了，怀疑被盗，立即开始处理。

1. 张先生： Oh no, my wallet's gone! I just had it like five minutes ago. I think someone took it.

正式表达： My wallet is missing. I had it five minutes ago. I believe someone stole it.

中文翻译： 糟了，我的钱包不见了！我五分钟前还拿着的。我觉得被人偷了。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "gone" 在这里是"不见了/没了"的意思。"just had it like five minutes ago" 是口语中表达"刚才还在"的自然说法。"like" 作为填充词缓冲语气。"someone took it" 比 "someone stole it" 更口语化。

2. 朋友： Oh man, that sucks. OK, don't panic. What was in it?

正式表达： That is unfortunate. Do not panic. What was in the wallet?

中文翻译： 天啊，真糟糕。好吧，别慌。里面有什么？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "That sucks" 是口语中"太糟糕了/真倒霉"的说法，非常常用，但偏非正式。"don't panic" 再次出现。朋友的第一反应是了解情况以便帮助。

3. 张先生： My driver's license, two credit cards, my debit card, some cash... oh no, and my green card too! This is a disaster.

正式表达： My driver's license, two credit cards, debit card, cash, and my green card. This is a disaster.

中文翻译： 驾照、两张信用卡、借记卡、一些现金……糟了，还有我的绿卡！这是灾难。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "This is a disaster" 是口语中夸张表达"完了/糟透了"的说法。列出丢失的内容很重要，帮助确定优先级。绿卡丢失是最严重的问题之一，补办需要时间和费用。

4. 朋友： OK, first things first — you need to freeze your cards right now, before whoever took it goes on a shopping spree. Pull out your phone and call your bank.

正式表达： First, freeze your cards immediately before the thief uses them. Call your bank.

中文翻译： 好的，最重要的事先来——你需要马上冻结你的卡，在偷的人去大采购之前。拿出手机打给你银行。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "first things first" 再次出现。"freeze your cards" 是"冻结你的卡"，这是最紧急的措施。"go on a shopping spree" 是口语中"疯狂购物/大采购"的说法。"Pull out your phone" 是"拿出手机"。

5. 张先生： Right, right. Let me call Chase first... Hi, my wallet was stolen and I need to freeze all my cards immediately. Can you help me?

正式表达： Let me call Chase first. My wallet was stolen and I need to freeze my cards immediately. Can you help?

中文翻译： 对对。我先打给大通银行……你好，我的钱包被偷了，我需要立即冻结所有的卡。能帮我吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 银行电话中的表达要简洁明了：说明情况 + 要求采取行动。"freeze" 是冻结，"cancel" 是注销。冻结可以后续解冻，注销则需要重新发卡。

6. 银行客服： I'm sorry to hear that. I can definitely help. Let me pull up your account. Can you verify your identity — what's your date of birth and the last four digits of your SSN?

正式表达： I am sorry. I can help. Let me access your account. Please verify your identity with your date of birth and last four digits of your SSN.

中文翻译： 很抱歉。我肯定能帮。我调出你的账户。你能验证一下身份吗——你的出生日期和社安号后四位？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "pull up your account" 是"调出你的账户"。"verify your identity" 是验证身份。"last four digits" 是"后四位数字"。银行会通过个人信息验证身份。

7. 张先生： April 23rd, 1988, and the last four are 1234.

正式表达： April 23, 1988, and the last four digits are 1234.

中文翻译： 1988年4月23日，后四位是1234。

详细解释： ✅推荐 提供信息时清晰准确。

8. 银行客服： Thank you. I've frozen both your credit card and debit card effective immediately. No charges will go through. I'm also flagging your account for any suspicious activity. Your new cards will be mailed within 5 to 7 business days.

正式表达： I have frozen both cards immediately. No charges will be processed. I am flagging your account for suspicious activity. New cards will arrive in 5 to 7 business days.

中文翻译： 谢谢。我已经冻结了你的信用卡和借记卡，立即生效。不会通过任何扣款。我还在你的账户上标记了可疑活动监控。新卡会在5到7个工作日内邮寄给你。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "effective immediately" 是"立即生效"。"No charges will go through" 意思是"所有扣款都不会通过"。"flagging your account" 是"在你的账户上做标记"。"suspicious activity" 是可疑活动。

9. 张先生： Thank you so much. That's a huge relief. What about recent charges — can you check if there's been anything fishy?

正式表达： Thank you. Can you check for any suspicious recent charges?

中文翻译： 非常感谢。那是个巨大的安慰。最近的消费呢——能看看有没有什么可疑的吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "a huge relief" 是口语中"大大的安慰/松了口气"。"fishy" 是口语中"可疑的/不对劲的"说法，非常形象。

10. 银行客服： Let me take a look... The last legitimate charge was at a coffee shop at 2:15 PM. Nothing after that. So it looks like they haven't used your cards yet. You got lucky there.

正式表达： The last charge was at a coffee shop at 2:15 PM with nothing after. It appears your cards have not been used. You were fortunate.

中文翻译： 我看看……最后一笔正常消费是下午2:15在一家咖啡店。之后没有。看起来他们还没用你的卡。你运气不错。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "legitimate charge" 是正常/合法的消费。"You got lucky there" 是口语中"你运气不错/算走运了"的说法。

11. 张先生： Small victories. Now I need to report the theft to the police and figure out how to replace my green card.

正式表达： I need to report the theft to the police and replace my green card.

中文翻译： 小小胜利。现在我需要报警然后想办法补办绿卡。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Small victories" 是口语中"小小的胜利/至少这关过了"的说法，表达在糟糕情况中找到一丝安慰。"figure out" 再次出现。

子场景二：报警和补办证件

完整对话（含逐句详解）

背景： 张先生到警察局报案，然后开始补办绿卡的流程。

1. 张先生： Hi, I'd like to report a stolen wallet. It was taken at the Westfield Mall this afternoon, probably between 2 and 2:30.

正式表达： I would like to report a stolen wallet. It was taken at the Westfield Mall between 2 and 2:30 PM.

中文翻译： 你好，我要报案，钱包被偷了。今天下午在 Westfield 商场，大概2点到2点半之间。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "report a stolen wallet" 是报案的标准说法。提供时间、地点和具体经过。这类非紧急报案可以去警察局或者打非紧急电话。

2. 警察： I'm sorry to hear that. Let me take down the details. Can you describe the wallet and list what was in it?

正式表达： I am sorry. Let me record the details. Can you describe the wallet and list its contents?

中文翻译： 很抱歉。让我记录详情。你能描述一下钱包和里面的东西吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "take down the details" 是"记录详情"。

3. 张先生： It's a brown leather wallet — nothing fancy. Inside was my California driver's license, two credit cards, a Chase debit card, about $60 in cash, and my permanent resident card — my green card.

正式表达： It is a brown leather wallet. It contained my California driver's license, two credit cards, a Chase debit card, approximately $60 in cash, and my permanent resident card.

中文翻译： 棕色皮质钱包——不是什么名牌。里面有我的加州驾照、两张信用卡、一张大通借记卡、大约60美元现金，还有我的永久居民卡——绿卡。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "nothing fancy" 再次出现表示"普通的/不贵的"。"permanent resident card" 是绿卡的正式名称。尽量详细地列出所有丢失物品。

4. 警察： Got it. Do you have any idea who might have taken it? Did you notice anyone suspicious?

正式表达： Do you know who might have taken it? Did you notice anyone suspicious?

中文翻译： 好的。你有没有线索谁可能偷的？有没有注意到什么可疑的人？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "any idea who" 是口语中"有没有线索/知不知道"的说法。警察的标准询问。

5. 张先生： Not really. I was in a crowded store and my wallet was in my back pocket. I know that's stupid — I should've kept it in my front pocket. Lesson learned.

正式表达： Not really. I was in a crowded store and the wallet was in my back pocket. I should have kept it in my front pocket.

中文翻译： 没有。我在一家很挤的店里，钱包放在后面口袋。我知道那很蠢——应该放在前面口袋的。吸取教训了。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Not really" 是口语中委婉的否定。"should've" = "should have"，表示"本应该"。"Lesson learned" 是口语中"吸取了教训/学到了"的说法。后面口袋放钱包确实容易被偷。

6. 警察： Happens to the best of us. I'll file a report and give you a copy with the report number. You'll need that for your insurance claim and for replacing your green card. The report should be ready in about 15 minutes.

正式表达： It happens to anyone. I will file a report and provide a copy with the report number. You will need it for insurance and green card replacement. It will be ready in approximately 15 minutes.

中文翻译： 谁都会遇到。我会立案然后给你一份报告副本带报案号。你保险理赔和补办绿卡都需要用。报告大约15分钟后就好。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Happens to the best of us" 是口语中安慰别人的说法，意思是"再厉害的人也会遇到这种事"。"file a report" 再次出现。"insurance claim" 是保险理赔。

7. 张先生： Thank you. About the green card — how do I go about replacing it? That's the one I'm most worried about.

正式表达： How do I replace my green card? That is my biggest concern.

中文翻译： 谢谢。关于绿卡——我怎么补办？那是我最担心的。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "how do I go about" 是口语中"我该怎么做/怎么处理"的说法。"the one I'm most worried about" 是"我最担心的那个"。

8. 警察： You'll need to file Form I-90 with USCIS to replace your green card. You can do it online. The police report will help speed things up. In the meantime, if you need proof of your status, you can make an appointment with USCIS to get an I-551 stamp in your passport — that serves as temporary proof.

正式表达： File Form I-90 with USCIS online. The police report helps. For temporary proof of status, visit USCIS for an I-551 stamp in your passport.

中文翻译： 你需要向 USCIS 提交 I-90 表格来补办绿卡。可以网上办。报警记录有助于加速。同时，如果你需要身份证明，可以约 USCIS 在你的护照上盖 I-551 章——那可以作为临时证明。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Form I-90" 是绿卡补办的申请表格。"speed things up" 是"加速"。"in the meantime" 再次出现。"I-551 stamp" 是临时绿卡身份证明章。👂了解 绿卡补办可能需要数月，费用约$455加上指纹费$85。

9. 张先生： That's really helpful. What about my driver's license? Can I still drive without it?

正式表达： What about my driver's license? Can I drive without it?

中文翻译： 真的很有帮助。驾照呢？没有驾照还能开车吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 直接问具体问题。

10. 警察： Technically, you need to carry your license when driving. But if you get pulled over, you can show the police report as proof that it was stolen. I'd head to the DMV ASAP to get a replacement — they can usually issue a temporary one on the spot.

正式表达： You are required to carry your license. If stopped, show the police report. Visit the DMV for a replacement — they can issue a temporary license.

中文翻译： 从法律上说，你需要带驾照开车。但如果你被拦下来了，可以出示报案记录作为被盗证明。我建议尽快去 DMV 补办——他们通常能当场给你一个临时的。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Technically" 是口语中"从技术上/法律上说"的说法。"pulled over" 再次出现表示"被警察拦下"。"ASAP" 是 "as soon as possible" 的缩写，口语中读作 "ay-sap"。"on the spot" 再次出现。

11. 张先生： OK, so the game plan is: one, get the police report; two, freeze credit cards — already done; three, file I-90 for the green card; four, go to the DMV for a new license. Anything I'm missing?

正式表达： The plan is: get the report, freeze cards, file I-90, visit the DMV. Am I missing anything?

中文翻译： 好的，行动计划是：第一，拿报案记录；第二，冻结信用卡——已经做了；第三，提交 I-90 补绿卡；第四，去 DMV 补驾照。有什么遗漏的吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "game plan" 是口语中"行动计划/策略"的说法，来自体育术语。"Anything I'm missing?" 是口语中确认是否遗漏的自然问法。把要做的事列出来是很实用的方法。

12. 警察： That covers the big stuff. I'd also suggest putting a fraud alert on your credit report, just to be safe. You can do that through Equifax, Experian, or TransUnion. And keep an eye on your bank statements for the next few weeks.

正式表达： That covers the major items. Place a fraud alert on your credit report through Equifax, Experian, or TransUnion. Monitor bank statements for the next few weeks.

中文翻译： 大事都覆盖了。我还建议在你的信用报告上设置欺诈警报，安全起见。可以通过 Equifax、Experian 或 TransUnion 设置。接下来几周注意看你的银行账单。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "covers the big stuff" 是口语中"大事都包括进去了"的说法。"fraud alert" 是欺诈警报。"keep an eye on" 再次出现。👂了解 美国三大信用机构是 Equifax、Experian 和 TransUnion。设置欺诈警报是防止身份盗窃的重要措施。

13. 张先生： Will do. Thanks for walking me through all this. I was freaking out a little but I feel like I have a handle on it now.

正式表达： I will. Thank you for guiding me. I was worried but now feel in control.

中文翻译： 好的。谢谢你带我过了一遍。我之前有点慌，但感觉现在有方向了。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "freaking out" 是口语中"非常焦虑/慌了"的说法，比 "worrying" 更强烈。"have a handle on it" 是口语中"能掌控/有方向了"的说法。"walking me through" 再次出现。

14. 警察： That's what we're here for. Here's your police report — case number is on the top right. Hang on to that. Good luck getting everything sorted out.

正式表达： That is our job. Here is your police report with the case number on the top right. Keep it safe. Good luck.

中文翻译： 这就是我们在这的职责。这是你的报案记录——案件号在右上角。保存好。祝你把所有事情都处理好。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "That's what we're here for" 是口语中"这就是我们的职责/我们就是做这个的"的说法。"Hang on to that" 是口语中"保存好/别弄丢了"的说法。"case number" 是案件编号。"sorted out" 再次出现。

注意事项

  1. 第一时间冻结银行卡： ✅推荐 钱包丢失后最紧急的事情是冻结所有银行卡和信用卡。大部分银行有24小时紧急热线。
  2. 身份盗窃防护： ✅推荐 如果丢失了 SSN 卡、驾照或绿卡，立即在三大信用机构设置欺诈警报（fraud alert）或信用冻结（credit freeze）。
  3. 保留报案记录： ✅推荐 报案记录（police report）是补办证件和保险理赔的重要文件。多复印几份备用。
  4. 拍照备份： ✅推荐 把所有重要证件（护照、绿卡、驾照、SSN 卡、银行卡）拍照存到加密的云端。万一丢失，这些照片能大大加速补办过程。
  5. ❌避免 把所有证件放在同一个钱包里。✅推荐 日常只带必需的卡，SSN 卡不要随身携带，放在家里的安全地方。
  6. 英国类似流程： 🇬🇧 英国的流程类似。丢失银行卡后立即联系银行冻结。护照丢失联系 HM Passport Office。报警可以通过 101（非紧急）或在线报案。英国的信用机构包括 Experian、Equifax 和 TransUnion。
