医疗计划从哪里买：先问自己适用的选择

工作不提供医疗福利，张先生想了解Marketplace及直接购买渠道。

张先生：

Hi, I'm looking to get health insurance on my own. My new job doesn't offer benefits, so I need to figure out my options.

你好，我想自己买健康保险。新工作不提供福利，所以我需要了解一下我的选择。

保险顾问：

I can help you compare the routes. You can shop for private health plans through the Marketplace or directly from an insurer. Let's first check which enrollment options apply to your situation.

可以比较购买渠道：通过Marketplace或直接向保险公司买私人医疗计划。先核你适用的登记选择。

张先生：

To be honest, I'm not really sure what the difference is. What would you recommend?

说实话我不太清楚区别在哪。你有什么建议？

保险顾问：

Let me break it down. The Marketplace lets you compare participating insurers' plans. Financial help has eligibility rules; we'll check those through the official application rather than assume that having no employer plan is enough.

来分开说。Marketplace可以比参与保险公司的计划。费用帮助有资格要求，要通过官方申请核，不因没有雇主计划就认定符合。

张先生：

Oh, that sounds pretty good. How do I know if I qualify for a subsidy?

听起来不错。我怎么知道自己符不符合补贴条件？

保险顾问：

The application asks about expected household income, household members and other eligibility information. The official eligibility notice explains the result and any documents still needed. We'll also check when you can enroll; I can't promise an immediate subsidy decision.

申请会问预计家庭收入、家庭成员及其他资格信息。正式资格通知说明结果与待补材料，也要核何时可登记，不能保证马上作出补助决定。

figure out my options说明还在了解，并没有选好产品。Marketplace是比较和购买计划的渠道，参与的也可以是私人保险公司，不该把它与private insurer说成公营、私营两种保险。break it down承诺把复杂问题分开讲，后面就从渠道走到资格。

申请会问收入、家庭成员及其他情况，结果看正式资格通知和待补材料。没有雇主计划不自动等于有补助，知道有补助也不等于已经登记。这里先确认可参加的时间与选择，避免读者听到We can help便误以为已经获保。

免赔额、共付额和药物：用自己的需求比较

张先生听术语困惑，并说明正在服用降压药；顾问先解释，再查实际计划。

张先生：

Got it. Now, I keep hearing about deductibles and copays. Can you explain those? It's all Greek to me.

明白了。我一直听到"免赔额"和"共付额"这些词。能解释一下吗？我完全听不懂。

保险顾问：

Sure. A deductible is the amount you pay for covered services subject to it before the plan starts paying its share. A $2,000 deductible doesn't mean the policy is inactive until then: some services are covered earlier. A copay is a fixed amount for a covered service — say $30 for a listed visit — and we must check when it applies.

可以。免赔额针对适用它的受保服务，达到后计划开始承担其份额。示例2,000美元不表示此前保单未生效，有些服务可更早获保障。共付额是某受保服务的固定费用，比如某项列明门诊30美元，但要核何时适用。

张先生：

Okay, that makes more sense now. So a plan with a lower deductible would have a higher monthly premium, right?

好的，现在清楚了。所以免赔额越低的方案月费越高，对吧？

保险顾问：

Often there's a trade-off, but don't treat it as a rule for every pair of plans. Compare the actual premium, deductible, copays, coinsurance, network and out-of-pocket limit. A higher premium alone doesn't tell you what every visit will cost.

常有这种取舍，但不是任意两份计划都按同一规律。要比实际保费、免赔额、共付额、比例自付、网络和自付上限。只看保费高，算不出每次看病费用。

我想选个中间的。那些叫什么来着——银级方案？

保险顾问：

We'll check the formulary for your exact medicine, including the dose and form. Then we'll check the tier, any separate drug deductible, network pharmacy and approval requirements. A metal category alone doesn't tell us those details.

要查确切药物，包括剂量与剂型，再核等级、独立药物免赔额、网络药房与批准要求。金属类别本身不能说明这些细节。

张先生：

Alright, let's compare the options and make sure my medicine and doctor are covered before I choose.
