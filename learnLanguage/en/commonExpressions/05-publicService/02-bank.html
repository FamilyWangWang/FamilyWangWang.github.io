第2章：银行（Bank）

场景背景

在美国和英国，银行服务是日常生活的重要组成部分。开银行账户（open a bank account）通常需要预约或直接走进分行（branch）。美国常用银行有 Chase、Bank of America、Wells Fargo 等，英国常用 Barclays、HSBC、Lloyds、NatWest 等。两国都有支票账户（🇺🇸 checking account / 🇬🇧 current account）和储蓄账户（savings account）。近年来手机银行（mobile banking）和网上银行（online banking）非常普及，很多基本操作不需要去银行。

文化提示： 美国的银行账户通常有最低余额要求（minimum balance），低于这个数会收月费（monthly fee）。英国很多银行提供免费的基础账户。美国的信用记录（credit history）非常重要，影响贷款、租房甚至找工作。

子场景一：开银行账户

完整对话（含逐句详解）

背景： 张先生刚到美国，去当地 Chase 银行开一个支票账户。

1. 张先生： Hi, I'd like to open up a checking account. Do I need an appointment or can I just walk in?

正式表达： Hello, I would like to open a checking account. Do I need an appointment or may I walk in without one?

中文翻译： 你好，我想开一个支票账户。需要预约还是可以直接来？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "open up" 是口语中"开设"的说法，比 "open" 更自然。"checking account" 是🇺🇸美式的"支票/活期账户"，🇬🇧英式叫 "current account"。"walk in" 表示不需要预约直接来，是口语中非常实用的表达。

2. 银行接待： You can absolutely walk in. Let me see if one of our bankers is available. Just have a seat and someone'll be right with you.

正式表达： Walk-ins are welcome. Let me check if a banker is available. Please take a seat and someone will assist you shortly.

中文翻译： 当然可以直接来。我看看有没有理财顾问有空。请坐一下，马上有人接待你。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "You can absolutely" 是口语中强调肯定的说法。"right with you" 意思是"马上来接待你"，比 "with you soon" 更口语化。"someone'll" 是 "someone will" 的缩略。

3. 张先生： Great, thanks. Before I sit down, quick question — what documents do I need to bring? I'm actually new to the States.

正式表达： Thank you. Before I sit down, I have a quick question — what documents do I need to bring? I am new to the United States.

中文翻译： 好的谢谢。坐下之前问一下——我需要带什么文件？我刚来美国。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "quick question" 是口语中插话提问的礼貌方式。"new to the States" 意思是"刚来美国"，"the States" 是美国的口语称呼。注意 "the States" 首字母大写。

4. 银行接待： Oh, no worries! You'll need a valid ID — passport works great — proof of address, and if you have one, your Social Security number. If you don't have an SSN yet, that's okay, we can still work with you.

正式表达： Do not worry. You will need a valid ID — a passport is acceptable — proof of address, and your Social Security number if available. If you do not have an SSN, we can still assist you.

中文翻译： 别担心！你需要有效身份证件——护照就行——地址证明，还有社会安全号，如果有的话。还没有也没关系，我们也能帮你办。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "works great" 表示"就可以/很好用"，非常口语。"we can still work with you" 意思是"我们还是可以帮你办"，比 "we can still help you" 更专业又不失友好。"No worries" 是放松语气的万能回复。

（被带到理财顾问面前）

5. 理财顾问： Hi there, I'm Sarah. So you're looking to open a checking account?

正式表达： Hello, I am Sarah. You are looking to open a checking account?

中文翻译： 你好，我是 Sarah。你想开一个支票账户？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "looking to" 是口语中表达意图/计划的说法，相当于 "planning to" 或 "wanting to"。"So you're looking to..." 是确认对方需求的自然开场白。

6. 张先生： Yeah, that's right. And maybe a savings account too, if it's not too complicated.

正式表达： Yes, that is correct. And perhaps a savings account as well, if it is not too complicated.

中文翻译： 对的。可能再开一个储蓄账户，如果不麻烦的话。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "that's right" 是确认信息的自然回应。"if it's not too complicated" 是口语中委婉添加要求的说法，比 "and I also want" 更礼貌。"maybe" 起到弱化语气的作用。

7. 理财顾问： Not at all, we can set you up with both at the same time. It's actually pretty easy. First things first, can I see your ID?

正式表达： Not at all, we can set up both accounts simultaneously. It is quite simple. First, may I see your ID?

中文翻译： 一点也不麻烦，我们可以同时开两个。其实挺简单的。先看看你的身份证件？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "set you up with" 是口语中"帮你开通/设置"的常用短语。"First things first" 是口语中"先做最重要的事"的说法，非常地道。

8. 张先生： Sure, here's my passport. And I printed out a bank statement from my old bank back home — would that work as proof of address?

正式表达： Certainly, here is my passport. I also printed a bank statement from my previous bank in my home country. Would that serve as proof of address?

中文翻译： 好的，这是我的护照。我还打印了国内旧银行的账单——这个能当地址证明吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "would that work" 是口语中确认某物是否合适的标准说法。"back home" 指"在国内/老家"，是口语中非常常见的说法。注意 "printed out" 比 "printed" 更口语化。

9. 理财顾问： Hmm, actually for proof of address we usually need something local — like a utility bill, a lease, or official mail with your name and US address on it. Do you have anything like that?

正式表达： For proof of address, we typically require something local, such as a utility bill, lease, or official mail with your name and US address. Do you have any such documents?

中文翻译： 嗯，地址证明我们通常需要美国本地的——比如水电账单、租房合同或写有你名字和美国地址的官方邮件。你有这类文件吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Hmm, actually" 是口语中委婉否定的经典开场。"something like that" 是口语中指代"类似的东西"的说法。"official mail" 指政府或机构寄来的信件。

10. 张先生： Oh, okay. I've got my lease agreement in my car. Let me run out and grab it real quick.

正式表达： I have my lease agreement in my car. Let me go and retrieve it quickly.

中文翻译： 哦好的。我的租房合同在车里。我出去拿一下。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "run out and grab it" 意思是"出去拿"，"run" 不一定是跑，只是表示"快速去"。"real quick" 是美式口语中"很快/马上"的说法，比 "quickly" 自然得多。👂了解 "real quick" 在年轻人中尤其常用。

11. 理财顾问： Take your time! I'll start entering your info while you're gone.

正式表达： Take your time. I will begin entering your information while you are away.

中文翻译： 不着急！你去的功夫我先录你的信息。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Take your time" 是口语中"不着急/慢慢来"的经典说法。"while you're gone" 表示"你不在的时候"。

（张先生回来后）

12. 张先生： Alright, here we go. Is this what you need?

正式表达： Here is the document. Is this what you need?

中文翻译： 好了，来了。这是你需要的吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Here we go" 是口语中递交东西或开始某事时的常用语。"Is this what you need?" 比 "Is this correct?" 更自然。

13. 理财顾问： Perfect, this'll do just fine. Alright, I'm gonna walk you through a few things. Do you want online banking? It's free and you can manage everything from your phone.

正式表达： This is perfect. I will guide you through a few things. Would you like online banking? It is free and you can manage everything from your phone.

中文翻译： 很好，这个没问题。我来给你介绍几项。你要不要网上银行？免费的，手机上就能管理所有业务。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "this'll do just fine" 意思是"这就完全可以了"。"walk you through" 是口语中"带你过一遍/一步步教你"的说法，超级常用。✅推荐 "from your phone" 不说 "on your phone" 在这个语境下更自然。

14. 张先生： Definitely. I do everything on my phone these days. Can I also use Apple Pay or Google Pay with this account?

正式表达： Absolutely. I use my phone for everything nowadays. Can I also use Apple Pay or Google Pay with this account?

中文翻译： 当然要。我什么都用手机。这个账户能用 Apple Pay 或 Google Pay 吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Definitely" 是口语中强烈肯定的方式，比 "Yes" 更热情。"these days" 意思是"现在/如今"，放在句末很自然。

15. 理财顾问： For sure. Once your debit card arrives — should be about 5 to 7 business days — you just link it up and you're good to go.

正式表达： Certainly. Once your debit card arrives in approximately 5 to 7 business days, you can link it and it will be ready to use.

中文翻译： 当然。等你的借记卡到了——大概5到7个工作日——你只要关联一下就可以用了。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "For sure" 是口语中表示"当然/肯定"的说法。"business days" 指"工作日"，不包括周末和假日。"link it up" 是"关联/绑定"的口语说法。"you're good to go" 是美式口语"你就可以用了/一切就绪"。

16. 张先生： Sounds great. Now, are there any fees I should know about? I've heard some banks charge monthly fees.

正式表达： That sounds good. Are there any fees I should be aware of? I have heard some banks charge monthly fees.

中文翻译： 听起来不错。有什么费用我需要知道的吗？我听说有些银行收月费。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "fees I should know about" 是口语中询问隐藏费用的自然说法。"I've heard" 表示"我听说"，引出已了解的信息。

17. 理财顾问： Good question. So this account does have a $12 monthly fee, but we waive it if you keep a minimum balance of $1,500 or if you have direct deposit set up.

正式表达： Good question. This account does have a $12 monthly fee, but we waive it if you maintain a minimum balance of $1,500 or if you have direct deposit set up.

中文翻译： 好问题。这个账户确实有12美元月费，但如果你保持最低1500美元余额或设置了工资直接存入，我们就免了。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "waive" 在银行业务中是"免除（费用）"的专业术语。"direct deposit" 是美式"工资直接存入银行"的说法，🇬🇧英式叫 "salary credit" 或 "BACS payment"。👂了解 保持最低余额是美国银行免月费的常见条件。

18. 张先生： Okay, that shouldn't be a problem. I'll make sure to keep enough in there.

正式表达： That should not be a problem. I will ensure I maintain a sufficient balance.

中文翻译： 好的，这应该不是问题。我会确保保持足够的余额。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "shouldn't be a problem" 是口语中表示"没问题"的自然说法。"make sure to" 是"确保"的口语表达。

19. 理财顾问： Awesome. Last thing — you'll need to make an initial deposit. There's no minimum, but I'd say throw in at least 25 bucks so the account's active. You can do cash or check.

正式表达： Excellent. Finally, you will need to make an initial deposit. There is no minimum, but I recommend depositing at least $25 to activate the account. You may use cash or a check.

中文翻译： 好的。最后一件事——你需要做首次存款。没有最低要求，但我建议至少存25美元让账户激活。现金或支票都行。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "throw in" 这里是口语中"存入/放入"的随意说法。"bucks" 是美元的口语说法，相当于中文的"块"。"Last thing" 是口语中引出最后一项的说辞。👂了解 "bucks" 是非正式用语，在银行里办事员也会说。

20. 张先生： I'll do a hundred in cash. Here you go.

正式表达： I will deposit one hundred dollars in cash. Here it is.

中文翻译： 我存一百现金。给你。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "I'll do a hundred" 是口语中"我来一百"的简练说法。"in cash" 指用现金。

21. 理财顾问： And... we're all done! Your account is officially open. You'll get a welcome packet in the mail. Any questions?

正式表达： We are finished. Your account is officially open. You will receive a welcome packet in the mail. Do you have any questions?

中文翻译： 好了！你的账户正式开通了。你会收到一封欢迎邮件包裹。还有什么问题吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "And... we're all done!" 是口语中表示流程结束的轻松说法。"in the mail" 指通过邮寄。"welcome packet" 是银行寄给新客户的一套资料。

子场景二：银行柜台日常业务

完整对话（含逐句详解）

背景： 张先生需要到银行柜台存款并询问国际转账。

1. 张先生： Hi, I'd like to make a deposit into my checking account. I've got a check and some cash.

正式表达： Hello, I would like to make a deposit into my checking account. I have a check and some cash.

中文翻译： 你好，我想往支票账户里存钱。我有一张支票和一些现金。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "make a deposit" 是银行业务中"存款"的标准说法。"I've got" 比 "I have" 更口语化。

2. 柜员： Sure thing. Can I see your debit card or ID?

正式表达： Certainly. May I see your debit card or ID?

中文翻译： 好的。能看看你的借记卡或身份证件吗？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "Sure thing" 是口语中"没问题/当然"的说法，比 "Certainly" 随意亲切。

3. 张先生： Here's my card. The check is for $850 and I've got $200 in cash. So $1,050 altogether.

正式表达： Here is my card. The check is for $850 and I have $200 in cash. The total is $1,050.

中文翻译： 这是我的卡。支票850美元，现金200美元。一共1050美元。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "altogether" 在口语中表示"总共/一共"，比 "in total" 更常用。

4. 柜员： Got it. The check might take a couple business days to clear, but the cash'll show up right away.

正式表达： Understood. The check may take a few business days to clear, but the cash will be available immediately.

中文翻译： 好的。支票可能需要几个工作日才能到账，但现金马上就能用。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "clear" 在银行业务中指支票"清算/到账"，是专业但常用的术语。"show up" 是口语中"出现/到账"的说法。"right away" 意思是"马上/立即"。

5. 张先生： That's fine. Oh, one more thing — can I do an international wire transfer from here? I need to send some money back to China.

正式表达： That is fine. One more thing — can I make an international wire transfer from here? I need to send money to China.

中文翻译： 没问题。还有一件事——能从这里做国际电汇吗？我需要往中国汇钱。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "wire transfer" 是"电汇"的英语说法。"back to China" 中的 "back" 表示"回到/回到（来源地）"。"One more thing" 是口语中追加问题的标准过渡。

6. 柜员： Yeah, we can definitely do that. There's a $45 fee for international wires, and it usually takes about 3 to 5 business days to go through.

正式表达： Yes, we can do that. There is a $45 fee for international wire transfers, and it usually takes 3 to 5 business days to process.

中文翻译： 可以，当然能办。国际电汇手续费45美元，通常3到5个工作日到账。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "go through" 是口语中"处理完成/到账"的说法。"fee" 是"费用/手续费"。

7. 张先生： That's a bit steep, but I guess it is what it is. What info do you need from me?

正式表达： That is somewhat expensive, but it cannot be helped. What information do you need from me?

中文翻译： 有点贵，但也只能这样了。你需要我提供什么信息？

详细解释： ✅推荐 "a bit steep" 是口语中"有点贵/有点过分"的说法。"it is what it is" 是非常常用的口语，意思是"没办法/就是这样"，表达无奈接受。"guess" 在口语中表示"想/觉得"。

8. 柜员： I'll need the recipient's bank name, their account number, and something called a SWIFT code. If you have their bank's routing number, that helps too.

正式表达： I will need the recipient's bank name, account number, and SWIFT code. Their bank's routing number would also be helpful.

中文翻译： 我需要收款银行名称、账号和一个叫 SWIFT 代码的东西。有银行的汇款路线号码也有帮助。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "recipient" 是"收款人"。"SWIFT code" 是国际汇款的银行识别码。"routing number" 是美国银行的路由号码，用于识别银行。"something called" 是口语中提到不熟悉事物时的说法。

9. 张先生： I've got all that written down. Here's a piece of paper with everything on it.

正式表达： I have all that information written down. Here is a paper with all the details.

中文翻译： 我都写下来了。这张纸上有所有信息。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "written down" 是"写下来了"的口语说法。"a piece of paper with everything on it" 非常口语化，不说 "a document containing all the information"。

10. 柜员： Perfect. I'll get this processed for you. Should be all set by the end of the week.

正式表达： Excellent. I will process this for you. It should be completed by the end of the week.

中文翻译： 完美。我来给你处理。这周内应该就能办好。

详细解释： ✅推荐 "get this processed" 是口语中"帮你处理"的说法。"by the end of the week" 是口语中表达时间节点的常用说法。

注意事项

  1. 最低余额： 美国银行普遍有最低余额要求，低于会被收月费（$10-15不等）。✅推荐 开户时问清楚 "Is there a minimum balance requirement?"
  2. SSN 问题： 新移民如果没有 SSN，有些银行可以用 ITIN（Individual Taxpayer Identification Number）或护照开户。✅推荐 提前打电话确认。
  3. 支票在美国： 虽然越来越少人用，但在美国付房租、某些账单仍常用个人支票（personal check）。
  4. 小费文化延伸： 银行不需要给小费。但银行理财顾问可能会推销产品，如果不感兴趣，礼貌说 "I'll think about it" 即可。
  5. ❌避免 把所有现金带在身上去银行，注意安全。
  6. ❌避免 在银行里大声说出密码或 PIN 码。✅推荐 在柜台小声说或在密码器上输入。
  7. 线上替代： 很多转账可以通过 Wise、Remitly 等第三方平台完成，手续费比银行电汇便宜很多。✅推荐 比较不同渠道的费率。
