42 读一段群聊：热情回复以后，谁真的会到场

Sam 说主意很好，却请先别把他算进去；Alex 明确参加，Nora 另有到达时间。三轮群聊依次处理意愿、食物与名单，最后三人的安排由实际确认形成。

Lea: Would anyone like a picnic on Saturday? I'm thinking of the park near the library, around one. We can decide on food once we know who's coming.
Sam: That sounds good. I may have a family visit, though. I'll know by Thursday evening, so don't count me in yet.
Nora: I'd like to come. I can get there at half past one, after my class. Is that too late?
Lea: Not at all. We'll meet near the main gate at one and stay nearby until you arrive.

Alex: I'm in. I can bring bread, but I can't carry the drinks as well. Does anyone want to take those?
Lea: I'll bring water. Let's keep the food simple rather than all making the same thing. Nora, would fruit work for you?
Nora: Yes, I'll bring some. Please don't wait to eat everything until I get there. Just save me a place.
Lea: Of course. If it rains, I'll message everyone by ten on Saturday. We haven't chosen an indoor alternative.

Sam, Thursday: The visit is going ahead, so I can't join the picnic. Have a lovely afternoon.
Lea: Thanks for confirming. Then it's three of us: Alex, Nora and me. Bread, water and fruit are covered. We'll keep the park plan unless I send a weather update on Saturday morning.
Alex: Thanks. I'll check the chat before leaving.
Nora: Me too. See you after class if the plan stays the same. I’ll keep to the time we agreed.

第 1 段：主意受到欢迎，不等于人数已经确定

Alex 接面包、Lea 接水、Nora 接水果，责任从提问走到各人答应。Nora 请先吃、只留位置，限定等待方式；下雨时十点前更新则另处理计划变化。
