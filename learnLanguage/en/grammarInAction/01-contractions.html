缩略与弱化：把连续声音对应到完整词组

字幕写着going to，耳朵里却像只有gonna。先把声音对应回词组，句子往往就通了；本篇沿常见缩略逐组说明，不要求把邮件也写成声音记法。

阅读起点：适合已经见过基本句子、想看实际口语怎样变化的读者。能把完整词组与中文大意对应即可，听辨和省略不要求一次掌握。 基本形式还陌生时，先从日常语法目录挑当前需要的一项（按需补读）。 连同讲解通读约12—21分钟，仅为篇幅估算；选读局部可更短。

从一段对话读起

A: I'm gonna grab some coffee. You want anything?

B: No, thanks. I've gotta finish this first.

A: Okay. Lemme know if you change your mind.

中文：A说自己准备去买咖啡，问B要不要带点什么。B说不用了，谢谢，自己得先做完这件事。A答应，并请B改主意时告诉自己。

gonna对应going to，gotta对应got to，lemme对应let me。三处仍分别表达打算、必须和允许我做某事；辨认声音后，还要看动作与回应。B说I’ve gotta保留了have，说明缩略不是任意删掉助动词或人称变化。

核心理解

日常语流里有弱读、合并和缩略。完整形式同样自然，尤其用于强调或澄清。本篇优先帮助听辨；gonna、wanna 等非正式拼写不必主动用于邮件。

第一类：Going to → Gonna

这是最常见的缩略之一。

普通表达 口语说法 音标提示
I am going to call you later. I'm gonna call you later. /ˈɡənə/
What are you going to do? What are you gonna do?
It is going to rain. It's gonna rain.
We are going to be late. We're gonna be late.
She is going to love this. She's gonna love this.

例句（真实口语）：

  1. I'm gonna grab some coffee. You want anything?
  2. It's gonna be a long day, I can tell.
  3. What's gonna happen to the old building?
  4. We're gonna have to leave early tomorrow.
  5. He's gonna freak out when he sees this.
  6. Are you gonna be okay on your own?
  7. They're gonna announce the results next week.

gonna 可在美英随意口语中听到。它不是把所有 going to 都缩短的规则：后接动词如 going to call 可有这种弱化，going to the station 不能机械写成 gonna the station。

第二类：Want to → Wanna

普通表达 口语说法 音标提示
I want to go home. I wanna go home. /wɑnə/
Do you want to come? Wanna come?
What do you want to do? Whaddaya wanna do?
She wants to be a doctor. She wants to be a doctor. 普通主动表达保留wants，不能由wanna推出所有第三人称都省-s
I do not want to bother you. I don't wanna bother you.

例句（真实口语）：

  1. I just wanna sleep all day.
  2. You wanna grab lunch together?
  3. She doesn't wanna talk about it right now.
  4. Do you wanna watch a movie or something?
  5. Nobody wants to do this, but we have to.
  6. I wanna say thanks for everything.
  7. He wants to know if you're coming or not.

第三类：Got to → Gotta

"have got to" 表示"必须"，在口语中变成 gotta。

普通表达 口语说法 音标提示
I have got to go now. I gotta go now. /gɑdə/
You have got to be kidding. You gotta be kidding.
We have got to finish this. We gotta finish this.
I have got to see this. I gotta see this.
He has got to be more careful. He’s gotta be more careful.

例句（真实口语）：

  1. I gotta go, my wife's waiting.
  2. You gotta try this pizza, it's amazing.
  3. We gotta figure this out before tomorrow.
  4. I gotta admit, I was wrong about that.
  5. You gotta be kidding me right now.
  6. He’s gotta stop doing that, seriously.
  7. I've gotta get up early tomorrow. （也可以保留 have 的缩略 I've）

gotta 主要按 have got to（必须）的非正式形式学习。got a 在快语流里可能听起来相近，但拥有一辆车的主动书写仍用 I have a car / I've got a car，不把 I gotta car 当作通用写法。见 Cambridge 的 gotta 条目。

例句（真实口语）：

  1. Dunno, maybe we should ask someone.
  2. I dunno why he said that.
  3. Dunno what time they're coming.
  4. I honestly dunno the answer to that.
  5. She doesn't know where she put her keys.

第六类：情态动词加have的弱化：Coulda, Shoulda, Woulda

这些是 could have / should have / would have 的弱化声音记法。普通缩写 could've、should've、would've 很常见；完整形式也可用于强调。不要把听到的声音写成 could of。

普通表达 口语说法 音标提示
I should have done that. I shoulda done that. /ʃʊdə/
I could have helped you. I coulda helped you. /kʊdə/
It would have been nice. It woulda been nice. /wʊdə/
You should have told me! You shoulda told me!
I would have gone if I had known. I woulda gone if I had known.

shoulda是非正式记音，should’ve是常规缩写；二者都对应should have。should of是把声音误写成介词of，不是更高级或更极端的缩略。

例句（真实口语）：

  1. I shoulda studied harder in school.
  2. We coulda won that game.
  3. That woulda been a disaster.
  4. You shoulda seen his face!
  5. I coulda told you that was gonna happen.
  6. It woulda been better if we'd left earlier.
  7. She shoulda known better.
  8. We shouldn'ta done that. （shouldn't + have 的缩略）
  9. I woulda said yes in a heartbeat.
  10. Who woulda thought? （常见感叹句：谁能想到呢？）

could’ve、should’ve和would’ve可用于不同地区。have的/h/是否弱化取决于重音和语流；不凭国籍推断一个人的具体读法。

kinda和sorta分别提示kind of和sort of的声音；两地均可遇到，不把二者按国籍硬分。它们常把评价说得不那么绝对，如kinda tired是“有点累”。

实战对比：完整段落

较完整的词组版本

"I am going to call you later because I have to finish my work. I do not know what time I will be done. I would have called you earlier, but I was busy. Let me know what you are going to do tonight."

集中展示声音记法的版本

"I'm gonna call you later 'cause I hafta finish up at work. I dunno what time I'll be done. I woulda called you earlier, but I was busy. Lemme know whatcha gonna do tonight."

完整中文：我之后会给你打电话，因为得先完成工作。我不知道几点能忙完。我本来会早点打给你，但当时在忙。告诉我你今晚准备做什么。

两个版本都交代稍后联系和当前限制。would have说明未能早点打电话；换成should have会变成“本来应该早点打”，含义也随之变化，不能只当声音不同。

怎样在真实语流里使用这些线索

先用清楚的完整句理解意思，再把录音里的声音对应回来。看熟悉的片段时，可留意gonna后面是否有动词、gotta是否在表达“必须”，以及说话人何时仍保留完整形式。需要时重听或跟读即可，不必把阅读变成逐句作答。

正式讲话也可能有I’m、don’t这类常规缩写；需要区分的是普通缩写、非正式记音和方言语法。和朋友聊天也没有把每个词写成gonna、hafta的义务，清楚自然比缩短数量重要。

使用入口与范围

  • 日常沟通语法：学习句型、语气与书信沟通。
  • 按沟通任务速查：从想完成的事情选表达。
