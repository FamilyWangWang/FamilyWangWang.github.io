短语动词实战：常用动词与小品词搭配

look into、put off和work out连在工作消息里，分别推动调查、调整安排和解决问题。先看它们怎样接住同一件事，再按主题查词；普通单词版本也可以自然使用。

阅读起点：适合已经见过基本句子、想看实际口语怎样变化的读者。能把完整词组与中文大意对应即可，听辨和省略不要求一次掌握。 基本形式还陌生时，先从日常语法目录挑当前需要的一项（按需补读）。 连同讲解通读约26—46分钟，仅为篇幅估算；选读局部可更短。

从一段对话读起

A: The figures don't match. Could you look into it?

B: Sure. Shall we put off the meeting until Friday?

A: Yes. Let's work out what went wrong first.

中文：A说数字对不上，请B查一下。B答应，并问是否把会议推迟到周五。A同意，提议先弄清哪里出了问题。

look into it的it跟在整个搭配后面；put off the meeting的名词则也能放在中间，成为put the meeting off。三句不是在描述“看进去、放离开、工作出去”，而是在安排解决问题的步骤。下面保留各组词条和例句，比较的重点是搭配、对象与含义。

为什么短语动词如此重要？

短语动词如 look into、put off 在日常英语中很有用。investigate、postpone、cancel 等普通动词也能自然用于对话和邮件，不是只能用于课本。按具体搭配和意思选择。

这里把动词与小品词、介词组成的常用搭配放在一起。组合义有时难以从字面推出，如look into表示调查；有时则比较透明。严格语法分类还会区分短语动词、介词动词等，不必认为本页所有搭配结构完全一样。

下面按理解、行动、社交与日常动作分组，保留展开词条和后面的补充速查。遇到一个词组时，先确认它作用于谁或什么，再比较同组搭配；不用一次记完。

第一组：思维与理解

1. Figure out — 想明白，搞清楚

普通表达 口语说法
I need to solve this problem. I need to figure out this problem.
I cannot understand why he did that. I can't figure out why he did that.

例句：

  1. I finally figured out how to use this app.
  2. Can you figure out what's wrong with my computer?
  3. We need to figure out a way to save money.
  4. I haven't figured it out yet, but I'm working on it.
  5. It took me a while, but I figured it out.
  6. Let's figure out a time that works for everyone.
  7. She figured out the answer before anyone else.

figure out常接问题、原因或how从句。I finally figured out how to use this app是“我终于弄明白这个应用怎么用了”；finally表现理解是逐步得到的，不只是“看见”某个信息。

2. Point out — 指出

普通表达 口语说法
I want to mention an important fact. I want to point out something important.
She indicated that there was an error. She pointed out the error.

例句：

  1. I just wanna point out that we're running out of time.
  2. He pointed out a few mistakes in my report.
  3. As she pointed out, this isn't the first time.
  4. Can I point out something here?
  5. Thanks for pointing that out, I didn't notice.

point out把注意力引向一处事实或问题。He pointed out a few mistakes in my report是“他指出了报告中的几处错误”，指的是指出，而非已经替你修改。

3. Work out — 解决/想清楚/锻炼

这个词有多个意思，根据上下文判断。

普通表达 口语说法
We need to resolve this issue. We need to work out this issue.
Things developed well. Things worked out well.
I exercise at the gym. I work out at the gym.

例句：

  1. Don't worry, everything's gonna work out fine.
  2. We need to work out the details before Friday.
  3. I work out three times a week.
  4. It didn't work out between them. （他们俩没成）
  5. We'll work something out. （我们会找到办法的）
  6. How's that working out for you? （那对你效果如何？可真诚追问，也可在已知失败的背景下讽刺。）
  7. I'm sure we can work this out.

We need to work out the details是“我们需要敲定细节”，I work out three times a week则是“我每周锻炼三次”。先看details还是身体活动；事情worked out fine又是在说结果顺利。

4. Make up — 编造/和好/组成/化妆

普通表达 口语说法
He invented a story. He made up a story.
They reconciled after the fight. They made up after the fight.
Women applied cosmetics. She made up her face. （更常见：put on makeup）

例句：

  1. Did you make that up, or is it true?
  2. They had a fight but they made up the next day.
  3. Don't make up excuses.
  4. I made up my mind. （我做了决定）
  5. You can't just make stuff up!
  6. We need to make up for lost time. （弥补失去的时间）

He made up a story是“他编了一个故事”，They made up则是“他们和好了”。make up for lost time中的for把任务变成弥补；make up one’s mind是另一个固定搭配，表示决定。

5. Come up with — 想出（主意/方案）

普通表达 口语说法
We need to think of a solution. We need to come up with a solution.
She proposed an idea. She came up with an idea.

例句：

  1. Can anyone come up with a better idea?
  2. He came up with a brilliant plan.
  3. I need to come up with something by Monday.
  4. Where did you come up with that?
  5. She's always coming up with creative solutions.

He came up with a brilliant plan是“他想出了一个很棒的计划”。with后的plan是产出的主意，和come up表示事情出现并不完全相同。

第二组：处理与行动

6. Look into — 调查，了解

普通表达 口语说法
We will investigate the matter. We'll look into it.
I need to research this topic. I need to look into this.

例句：

  1. I'll look into it and get back to you.
  2. Someone should look into this problem.
  3. We're looking into a few options right now.
  4. Have you looked into what it would cost?
  5. The police are looking into the incident.

I’ll look into it and get back to you是“我会调查一下，再回复你”。前半句承诺去查，不表示已经知道答案；it在整个look into后面。

7. Put off — 推迟，拖延

普通表达 口语说法
We postponed the meeting. We put off the meeting.
Stop procrastinating! Stop putting it off!

例句：

  1. Don't put it off, just do it now.
  2. The meeting's been put off until next week.
  3. I keep putting off going to the dentist.
  4. Why do you always put things off?
  5. We can't put this off any longer.

The meeting’s been put off until next week是“会议推迟到下周”。until给新时间；put off going to the dentist则是拖延去看牙，不是把牙医这个人推开。

8. Call off — 取消

普通表达 口语说法
They canceled the event. They called off the event.
The game was canceled due to rain. The game was called off because of rain.

例句：

  1. They called off the wedding.
  2. We might have to call off the trip.
  3. Don't call it off yet, let's wait and see.
  4. The search was called off after three days.
  5. Why was the meeting called off?

They called off the wedding是“他们取消了婚礼”。与put off推迟比较，call off本句没有承诺另一个日期。

9. Take over — 接管

普通表达 口语说法
He assumed control of the project. He took over the project.

例句：

  1. She took over as manager last month.
  2. Can you take over for me? I need a break.
  3. The company was taken over by a bigger one.
  4. I'll take over from here.
  5. Robots are gonna take over the world. （常用玩笑）

Can you take over for me?是“能接替我一下吗”。for me交代替谁做；She took over as manager则说明接任的角色。

10. Carry out — 执行，实施

普通表达 口语说法
We will execute the plan. We'll carry out the plan.
They conducted a survey. They carried out a survey.

例句：

  1. We need to carry out more tests.
  2. The plan was carried out successfully.
  3. Who's gonna carry out the investigation?
  4. It's easier said than done. Carrying out the plan will take time.

We need to carry out more tests是“我们需要做更多测试”。carry out指把计划或任务付诸实施，本身也可出现在正式报告中，不是“随意口语专属”。

11. Take up — 开始做（某事）/占据（时间/空间）

普通表达 口语说法
She started playing tennis. She took up tennis.
This occupies too much space. This takes up too much space.

例句：

  1. I took up gardening during the pandemic.
  2. This couch takes up way too much room.
  3. Don't take up his time with this nonsense.
  4. She recently took up yoga.
  5. The meeting took up the whole morning.

I took up gardening是“我开始学做园艺”；The meeting took up the whole morning是“会议占了一整个上午”。同一个搭配，活动宾语与时间宾语引出不同意思。

12. Go through — 经历/审查/通过

普通表达 口语说法
He experienced a difficult time. He went through a rough patch.
Let's review the documents. Let's go through the documents.

例句：

  1. She's going through a lot right now.
  2. Let's go through the plan one more time.
  3. I don't wanna go through that again.
  4. We need to go through all the applications.
  5. He went through a tough divorce.

Let’s go through the plan one more time是“我们再过一遍计划”，She’s going through a lot则是“她正经历很多困难”。through的字面画面能提示过程，但具体意思仍看对象。

13. Get over — 从...中恢复/克服

普通表达 口语说法
She recovered from the illness. She got over the illness.
I cannot believe he said that. （惊讶） I can't get over what he said.

例句：

  1. It took her months to get over the flu.
  2. I just can't get over how much you've grown!
  3. He never got over his fear of heights.
  4. You'll get over it, don't worry.
  5. Get over yourself! （别自以为是了！）

She got over the illness是“她病后恢复了”，I can’t get over how much you’ve grown表达惊讶难消。Get over yourself!带责备，不是普通康复祝愿。

14. Turn out — 结果是/证明是

普通表达 口语说法
It proved to be a good idea. It turned out to be a good idea.
The result was different from expected. It turned out differently than we thought.

例句：

  1. It turned out she was right all along.
  2. The movie turned out to be really good.
  3. As it turns out, we didn't need to worry.
  4. How did the interview turn out?
  5. Things usually turn out fine in the end.

It turned out she was right all along是“结果发现她一直是对的”。turn out突出后来显现的结果，不等于提前保证结果一定好。

15. Bring up — 提起（话题）/抚养

普通表达 口语说法
He mentioned an important issue. He brought up an important issue.
She raised her children well. She brought up her kids well.

例句：

  1. Don't bring up that topic again.
  2. She brought up a really good point.
  3. I was brought up in a small town.
  4. Who brought this up in the first place?
  5. He always brings up the same old complaints.

Don’t bring up that topic again是“别再提那个话题”；She brought up her kids well是“她把孩子养育得很好”。话题与孩子两个对象决定了不同任务。

第三组：沟通与社交

16. Come across — 偶然遇到/给人...印象

普通表达 口语说法
I encountered an old friend. I came across an old friend.
She appears to be confident. She comes across as confident.

例句：

  1. I came across this old photo while cleaning.
  2. He came across as a bit arrogant at first.
  3. I came across a really interesting article today.
  4. How do you want to come across in the interview?
  5. We came across a great restaurant by accident.

I came across this old photo是“我偶然翻到这张旧照片”；He came across as arrogant是“他给人的印象有些傲慢”。as后的特征描述印象，未必是对方真正性格。

17. Get along — 相处

普通表达 口语说法
They have a good relationship. They get along well.

例句：

  1. Do you get along with your neighbors?
  2. We don't get along at all.
  3. She gets along with everyone.
  4. I get along better with my sister than my brother.
  5. They seem to get along pretty well.

Do you get along with your neighbors?是“你和邻居相处得好吗”。with接交往对象；不加with时，语境中也可已知是谁之间的关系。

18. Catch up — 赶上/叙旧

普通表达 口语说法
We should reconnect. We should catch up.
I need to update myself on the news. I need to catch up on the news.

例句：

  1. It's been ages! Let's grab coffee and catch up.
  2. I need to catch up on my emails.
  3. She's trying to catch up with the rest of the class.
  4. Let me catch up on what I missed.
  5. I'm behind on the show, gotta catch up.

Let’s grab coffee and catch up是“我们喝杯咖啡叙叙旧”；catch up on emails则是补处理邮件，catch up with the class是赶上班级进度。宾语和介词一起读。

19. Hang out — 待在一起/闲逛

普通表达 口语说法
We spent time together. We hung out together.
Where do you socialize? Where do you hang out?

例句：

  1. Wanna hang out this weekend?
  2. We just hung out at his place and watched movies.
  3. I don't hang out with them anymore.
  4. There's nowhere to hang out around here.
  5. She's fun to hang out with.

We hung out at his place and watched movies是“我们在他家一起待着看电影”。hang out不要求一定在户外，也不自动表示没有正经事。

20. Open up — 敞开心扉

普通表达 口语说法
He finally shared his feelings. He finally opened up.

例句：

  1. It took a while, but she finally opened up to me.
  2. You need to open up more.
  3. He opened up about his childhood.
  4. It's hard for me to open up to strangers.

She finally opened up to me是“她终于对我敞开心扉”。to引出倾诉对象，about引出谈的内容；它描述愿意分享，不等于别人有义务交代隐私。

21. Run into — 偶然碰见

普通表达 口语说法
I met an old colleague by chance. I ran into an old colleague.

例句：

  1. Guess who I ran into today?
  2. I ran into Sarah at the grocery store.
  3. You never know who you'll run into.
  4. We ran into each other at the airport.

I ran into Sarah at the grocery store是“我在食品店偶遇Sarah”。此处run into不是跑着撞上；把地点和对象补齐就能看出偶遇义。

22. Set up — 安排/安装

普通表达 口语说法
We arranged a meeting. We set up a meeting.
I need to install the software. I need to set up the software.

例句：

  1. Can you set up a time for us to meet?
  2. They set up a new system at work.
  3. I'll set up the projector before the meeting.
  4. She set me up with her friend. （给我介绍了对象）
  5. It was a setup! （这是一个骗局/圈套！）

I’ll set up the projector before the meeting是“我会在会前把投影设备设置好”。set up a meeting是安排会议，set someone up with a friend又是介绍对象；名词setup还可指圈套。

第四组：日常动作

23. Pick up — 捡起/接人/学会

普通表达 口语说法
I will collect you at the airport. I'll pick you up at the airport.
She learned Spanish quickly. She picked up Spanish quickly.

例句：

  1. Can you pick up some milk on your way home?
  2. I'll pick you up at seven.
  3. He picked up the guitar pretty fast.
  4. Where did you pick up that habit?
  5. Pick up your clothes from the floor!

Can you pick up some milk on your way home?是“回家路上能买点牛奶吗”。pick someone up接人，pick up a language又可指逐渐学会；代词在可分用法中放中间。

24. Give up — 放弃

普通表达 口语说法
Never surrender. Never give up.
I stopped trying to fix it. I gave up on it.

例句：

  1. Don't give up, you're almost there!
  2. I gave up smoking last year.
  3. She'll never give up on her dream.
  4. I've given up trying to understand him.
  5. Never give up, never give in.

I gave up smoking last year是“我去年戒烟了”。gave up后接-ing，give up on a dream则是放弃对梦想的坚持，不把on随意移到宾语后面。

25. Look forward to — 期待

普通表达 口语说法
I am anticipating the trip. I'm looking forward to the trip.

例句：

  1. I'm really looking forward to the weekend.
  2. We look forward to hearing from you. （常用于邮件结尾）
  3. She's looking forward to meeting you.
  4. I'm not looking forward to Monday.
  5. What are you looking forward to this year?

注意：look forward to 后面接 -ing 形式。不是 "look forward to meet you"，是 "look forward to meeting you"。

She’s looking forward to meeting you是“她期待与你见面”。这里to是介词，后面用meeting；We look forward to hearing from you同样适用于邮件。

26. Hold on — 等一下

普通表达 口语说法
Please wait a moment. Hold on a sec.

例句：

  1. Hold on, let me check.
  2. Can you hold on a minute?
  3. Hold on, I'm not finished yet.
  4. Hold on tight! （抓紧了！）
  5. Hold on, that doesn't make sense.

Hold on, let me check是“等一下，我查查”。Hold on tight!则提醒抓紧；同一词组可暂停谈话、要求等候或要求握牢，先看后续动作。

27. Make sure — 确认/确保

普通表达 口语说法
Please confirm that everything is ready. Make sure everything's ready.

例句：

  1. Make sure you lock the door.
  2. I just wanna make sure we're on the same page.
  3. Did you make sure the stove is off?
  4. Make sure to bring your ID.
  5. I'll make sure it gets done.

Make sure you lock the door是“确保锁门”。make sure是常用固定结构，并非严格意义上的动词加小品词；放在本页是为按任务查找。

28. End up — 最终处于某种情况

普通表达 口语说法
He ultimately became a teacher. He ended up becoming a teacher.

end up常接-ing或地点、状态；turn out常说明后来发现的结果，见第14项。He ended up becoming a teacher是“他最终当了老师”，不是“证明他是老师”。

例句：

  1. We ended up staying until midnight.
  2. I ended up not going after all.
  3. She ended up marrying her high school sweetheart.
  4. How did you end up here?
  5. I never thought I'd end up doing this for a living.

We ended up staying until midnight是“我们最后待到了午夜”。end up后面的staying说明最终处于什么活动中，不表示这一定是原先打算。

29. Show up — 出现/到场

普通表达 口语说法
He arrived late. He showed up late.
She didn't attend the meeting. She didn't show up for the meeting.

例句：

  1. He didn't show up again.
  2. Only five people showed up.
  3. She showed up uninvited.
  4. What time are they supposed to show up?
  5. He always shows up at the worst time.

Only five people showed up是“只有五个人到场”。show up说明到没到，She showed up uninvited还多出未被邀请的背景。

30. Go on — 继续/发生

普通表达 口语说法
Please continue. Please go on.
What is happening here? What's going on here?

例句：

  1. What's going on? What happened?
  2. Please, go on, I'm listening.
  3. The show must go on. （节目必须继续）
  4. I can't believe this is going on.
  5. Go on, tell me more.

What’s going on here?是在问“这里发生什么了”；Please go on则是“请继续”。一个是当前情况，一个是鼓励继续，不能只记一个中文词“继续”。

第五组：短语动词的"可分"与"不可分"

先看这一处位置差别，就能主动组织很多句子；不必等到“凭感觉”才使用。下面的规则针对本组带宾语的用法，不据此推断同一词组所有义项。

规则简述：

可分短语动词（名词宾语可以放在中间或后面）：

  • Turn on the light. / Turn the light on. ✓ 都对
  • Pick up the kids. / Pick the kids up. ✓ 都对

但代词宾语只能放中间：

  • Turn it on. ✓ / Turn on it. ✗
  • Pick them up. ✓ / Pick up them. ✗

不可分短语动词（宾语只能放后面）：

  • Look into the matter. ✓ / Look the matter into. ✗
  • Run into an old friend. ✓ / Run an old friend into. ✗

本组可分用法中，代词放中间：turn it on、pick them up。look into与run into在这里整体后接宾语，分别保留into的对象。读例句时连着对象记，比只记中文释义更容易复用。
