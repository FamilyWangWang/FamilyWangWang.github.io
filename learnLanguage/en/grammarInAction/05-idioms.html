常用习语实战：母语者天天在用的"比喻句"

核心理解

习语（idiom）就是英语里的"成语"——字面意思和实际意思完全不同。比如中文说"拍马屁"不是真的去拍马的屁股，英语说 "piece of cake" 也不是真的在说蛋糕。

习语在口语中的密度极高。 你每天都会听到十几个习语，如果你不懂它们，你会在对话中频繁"卡壳"——每个词都听清了，但整句话的意思完全猜不到。

本章只收录真正高频使用的习语，跳过那些老掉牙的、母语者自己都不怎么用的。

第一组：描述人和行为

1. Hit the nail on the head — 说得完全正确/一针见血

字面意思：把钉子正好敲进头部。实际意思：说得非常准确。

课本说法 口语说法
You are completely correct. You hit the nail on the head.
That is exactly right. You hit the nail right on the head.

例句：

  1. — The real problem is lack of communication. — You hit the nail on the head.
  2. Her analysis hit the nail on the head.
  3. I think you hit the nail on the head when you said people are just tired.
  4. That review hit the nail on the head — the food was terrible.
  5. You nailed it. （更简短的版本，意思一样）

2. Under the weather — 身体不舒服/有点小病

字面意思：在天气下面。实际意思：不太舒服（通常是小毛病，不是重病）。

课本说法 口语说法
I am not feeling well. I'm feeling a bit under the weather.
She is slightly ill. She's a little under the weather.

例句：

  1. I'm gonna skip the party tonight, I'm feeling under the weather.
  2. He's been under the weather all week.
  3. You look a bit under the weather — you okay?
  4. She called in sick because she's under the weather.
  5. I think I'm coming down with something — feeling under the weather.

3. Break a leg — 祝你好运

字面意思：摔断一条腿。实际意思：祝你好运（特别用于演出前）。

课本说法 口语说法
Good luck on your performance! Break a leg tonight!

例句：

  1. You're gonna do great — break a leg!
  2. Break a leg in your interview tomorrow!
  3. The play opens tonight. Break a leg, everyone!
  4. — I have a big presentation today. — Break a leg!
  5. She told me to break a leg before my audition.

来源：这是戏剧界的传统——据说直接说 "good luck" 会带来坏运气，所以说反话 "break a leg"。现在已广泛用于各种场合。

4. Cost an arm and a leg — 超级贵/代价惨重

字面意思：花了一条胳膊一条腿。实际意思：非常昂贵。

课本说法 口语说法
That is very expensive. That costs an arm and a leg.
The repair fee was extremely high. The repair cost me an arm and a leg.

例句：

  1. I'd love to go to Japan, but the flights cost an arm and a leg.
  2. These designer bags cost an arm and a leg.
  3. Medical bills can cost you an arm and a leg in the US.
  4. We paid an arm and a leg for that vacation, but it was worth it.
  5. College tuition costs an arm and a leg these days.

5. Piece of cake — 小菜一碟/非常容易

字面意思：一块蛋糕。实际意思：非常简单。

课本说法 口语说法
The exam was very easy. The exam was a piece of cake.
This task is not difficult. This is a piece of cake.

例句：

  1. The test was a piece of cake.
  2. Once you know the trick, it's a piece of cake.
  3. — Was the interview hard? — Nah, piece of cake.
  4. Fixing that was a piece of cake — took me five minutes.
  5. Don't worry about it, it'll be a piece of cake.

6. Let the cat out of the bag — 不小心说漏了嘴

字面意思：把猫从袋子里放出来。实际意思：泄露秘密。

课本说法 口语说法
He accidentally revealed the secret. He let the cat out of the bag.
I spoiled the surprise. I let the cat out of the bag about the party.

例句：

  1. We were planning a surprise party, but Sarah let the cat out of the bag.
  2. Don't let the cat out of the bag — it's a secret!
  3. He let the cat out of the bag about the new product before the launch.
  4. Who let the cat out of the bag?
  5. I almost let the cat out of the bag when I started talking about the trip.

7. Spill the beans — 全说出来/交代吧

和 "let the cat out of the bag" 类似，但 "spill the beans" 更像"快说/别藏着了"，带有催促的意味。

课本说法 口语说法
Tell me the details. Spill the beans!
She told us everything. She spilled the beans about the whole thing.

例句：

  1. Come on, spill the beans — what happened?
  2. She finally spilled the beans about her new boyfriend.
  3. Alright, I'll spill the beans. We're moving to Chicago.
  4. Who's gonna spill the beans first?
  5. He spilled the beans after we promised not to tell anyone.

8. Give someone the cold shoulder — 冷落某人/不理某人

字面意思：给某人一个冷肩膀。实际意思：故意冷淡对待。

例句：

  1. I said hi, but she gave me the cold shoulder.
  2. Why are you giving me the cold shoulder? What did I do?
  3. He felt hurt when his friends gave him the cold shoulder.
  4. Don't give her the cold shoulder — just talk to her.
  5. I tried to start a conversation, but I got the cold shoulder.

9. Keep an eye on — 留意/看着点

课本说法 口语说法
Please monitor the situation. Can you keep an eye on things?
Watch my bag for a moment. Can you keep an eye on my bag?

例句：

  1. Can you keep an eye on the kids for a minute?
  2. I'll keep an eye on the stove while you're gone.
  3. We need to keep an eye on the weather forecast.
  4. Keep an eye out for any suspicious activity. （变体：keep an eye out）
  5. I'll keep an eye on that project for you.

10. Bite the bullet — 咬紧牙关/硬着头皮上

字面意思：咬住子弹。实际意思：面对不愉快的事情但不得不做。

课本说法 口语说法
I have to face the difficult situation. I just need to bite the bullet and do it.
We must proceed despite the difficulty. Let's just bite the bullet and get it over with.

例句：

  1. I hate going to the dentist, but I just gotta bite the bullet.
  2. We don't have a choice — let's bite the bullet and cut the budget.
  3. She bit the bullet and told him the truth.
  4. I guess I'll bite the bullet and pay the fine.
  5. Sometimes you just have to bite the bullet and move on.

第二组：描述情况和结果

11. On the same page — 达成共识/理解一致

课本说法 口语说法
We have reached an agreement. We're on the same page.
Do you understand what I mean? Are we on the same page?

例句：

  1. Let me make sure we're on the same page here.
  2. I just wanna make sure everyone's on the same page.
  3. Good, I think we're on the same page now.
  4. Before we start, let's get on the same page about the timeline.
  5. We're not on the same page — I thought you meant next week.

12. In the same boat — 同病相怜/处境相同

例句：

  1. Don't worry, we're all in the same boat.
  2. We're in the same boat — none of us knows what to do.
  3. I know how you feel. I'm in the same boat.
  4. Hey, we're all in the same boat here, so let's work together.
  5. She thinks she's the only one struggling, but we're all in the same boat.

13. The last straw — 压死骆驼的最后一根稻草

字面意思：最后一根稻草。实际意思：让人再也无法忍受的最后一件事。

例句：

  1. The broken printer was the last straw — I quit.
  2. He was already stressed, and that email was the last straw.
  3. She's been late every day this week, and today was the last straw.
  4. That was the straw that broke the camel's back. （完整版本，更长）
  5. I've put up with a lot, but this is the last straw.

14. Out of the blue — 突然/毫无预兆

字面意思：从蓝色中出来。实际意思：出乎意料地。

课本说法 口语说法
He called me unexpectedly. He called me out of the blue.
The news came as a surprise. The news came out of the blue.

例句：

  1. She showed up out of the blue after ten years.
  2. The offer came completely out of the blue.
  3. I got an email out of the blue from an old college friend.
  4. The storm came out of the blue — no warning at all.
  5. He just quit out of the blue, no explanation.

15. By the way — 顺便说一下/对了

这个不算"习语"，但它是极常用的话语标记，经常用来引入一个新话题。

课本说法 口语说法
Additionally, I wanted to mention... By the way, I wanted to ask you...
I also need to inform you that... By the way, the meeting's been moved.

例句：

  1. By the way, did you ever fix that problem?
  2. Oh, by the way, I'm not coming in tomorrow.
  3. Nice haircut, by the way.
  4. By the way, have you met my wife?
  5. I almost forgot — by the way, thanks for your help last week.
  6. By the way, the new season starts next Monday.
  7. That reminds me — by the way, can you send me that file?

16. Make a long story short — 长话短说

课本说法 口语说法
To summarize the situation briefly... To make a long story short...
In short... Long story short...

例句：

  1. Long story short, we didn't get the contract.
  2. To make a long story short, I ended up paying for everything.
  3. Long story short, he quit and started his own company.
  4. So, long story short, we're back to square one.
  5. Long story short, it took us three hours to get home.

17. Go the extra mile — 多做一步/加倍努力

字面意思：多走一英里。实际意思：比期望的做得更多。

例句：

  1. She always goes the extra mile for her students.
  2. If you wanna stand out, you need to go the extra mile.
  3. He went the extra mile to make sure we were happy.
  4. Companies love employees who go the extra mile.
  5. I'm willing to go the extra mile if it means getting this done right.

18. Call it a day — 今天就到这里吧/收工

课本说法 口语说法
We should stop working for today. Let's call it a day.
I am done for today. I'm gonna call it a day.

例句：

  1. It's almost six — let's call it a day.
  2. I've been working since seven this morning. I think I'll call it a day.
  3. After three hours of painting, we decided to call it a day.
  4. We're not gonna finish this today. Let's just call it a day.
  5. I'm beat. Let's call it a day and pick up tomorrow.

第三组：描述感受和态度

19. Over the moon — 高兴极了/欣喜若狂

例句：

  1. She was over the moon when she got the job.
  2. He's over the moon about the new baby.
  3. I was over the moon when I heard the news.
  4. They're over the moon about their engagement.

🇬🇧 这个习语在英式英语中更常用。🇺🇸 美式英语中更常说 "thrilled" 或 "on cloud nine"。

20. On cloud nine — 高兴极了/在九霄云上

和 "over the moon" 意思相同，美式英语更常用。

例句：

  1. Ever since he got engaged, he's been on cloud nine.
  2. She's been on cloud nine since she passed the exam.
  3. I was on cloud nine the whole weekend.

21. Sick and tired of — 厌烦透了

课本说法 口语说法
I am very annoyed by this. I'm sick and tired of this.
She is exhausted by the complaints. She's sick and tired of the complaints.

例句：

  1. I'm sick and tired of coming home to a messy house.
  2. People are sick and tired of the same old promises.
  3. I'm sick and tired of being treated like this.
  4. She's sick and tired of his excuses.
  5. We're all sick and tired of waiting.

22. Fed up with — 受够了

和 "sick and tired of" 类似，但更短、更口语化。

例句：

  1. I'm fed up with this weather.
  2. He's fed up with his boss.
  3. She's fed up with people not showing up on time.
  4. I'm just fed up, honestly.
  5. Everyone's fed up with the delays.

23. Take it easy — 别紧张/慢慢来/保重

这个词有三个常见用法：表示"放松"、表示告别、表示"别着急"。

课本说法 口语说法
Relax. Take it easy.
Goodbye. （告别时） Take it easy, man.
Do not worry. Just take it easy, everything's gonna be fine.

例句：

  1. Take it easy, it's not that serious.
  2. — See you later. — Take it easy!
  3. You need to take it easy for a few days and rest.
  4. Take it easy on him, he's new.
  5. Hey, take it easy, there's no rush.

24. Not my cup of tea — 不是我的菜/不感兴趣

字面意思：不是我的茶。实际意思：不是我喜欢的东西。

例句：

  1. Horror movies aren't really my cup of tea.
  2. Golf's not my cup of tea, to be honest.
  3. — Do you like jazz? — Not really, not my cup of tea.
  4. That kind of humor is not my cup of tea.
  5. I tried it, but it's just not my cup of tea.

🇬🇧 这个习语源自英国，但在美式英语中也广泛使用。

25. Give it a shot — 试一试

课本说法 口语说法
You should try it. You should give it a shot.
I want to attempt it. I'm gonna give it a shot.

例句：

  1. I've never tried sushi, but I'll give it a shot.
  2. Why not give it a shot? You've got nothing to lose.
  3. She decided to give it a shot and applied for the job.
  4. I don't know if I can do it, but I'll give it a shot.
  5. He gave it his best shot, but it wasn't enough.

26. Hang in there — 坚持住/别放弃

例句：

  1. I know it's tough, but just hang in there.
  2. Hang in there, the semester's almost over.
  3. The doctor said to hang in there — the treatment's working.
  4. Hang in there, buddy. Things will get better.
  5. She told herself to hang in there just a little longer.

27. Beat around the bush — 拐弯抹角/不直说

字面意思：绕着灌木丛打。实际意思：说话不直接。

课本说法 口语说法
Stop avoiding the topic. Stop beating around the bush.
Please speak directly. Don't beat around the bush — just tell me.

例句：

  1. Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.
  2. He kept beating around the bush instead of giving me a straight answer.
  3. I hate it when people beat around the bush. Just be honest.
  4. She was beating around the bush so much that I lost patience.
  5. Quit beating around the bush — how much is it gonna cost?

28. Cut to the chase — 直奔主题/说重点

和 "beat around the bush" 是反义词。这是美式英语特有的习语，源自早期好莱坞电影。

课本说法 口语说法
Please get to the point. Let's cut to the chase.

例句：

  1. Alright, let's cut to the chase — what do you want?
  2. I don't have much time, so can we cut to the chase?
  3. Cut to the chase — are you in or out?
  4. Enough small talk, let's cut to the chase.
  5. To cut to the chase, we can't afford it.

29. Ring a bell — 听起来耳熟

字面意思：敲响一个铃。实际意思：让我想起什么/听起来很熟悉。

例句：

  1. Does that name ring a bell?
  2. — Have we met before? — Your face rings a bell.
  3. That story rings a bell, but I can't remember where I heard it.
  4. Nothing rings a bell. Sorry.
  5. The address doesn't ring a bell — is it new?

30. On the fence — 犹豫不决/还在观望

字面意思：坐在栅栏上。实际意思：还没做决定。

例句：

  1. I'm still on the fence about buying a new car.
  2. She's on the fence — she might go, she might not.
  3. Are you in or are you still on the fence?
  4. He was on the fence until the very last minute.
  5. I'm kinda on the fence about this whole thing.

31. Once in a blue moon — 千载难逢/极少发生

字面意思：在蓝色月亮的时候。实际意思：非常罕见。

例句：

  1. I only eat fast food once in a blue moon.
  2. She visits her hometown once in a blue moon.
  3. We get snow here once in a blue moon.
  4. He cleans his room once in a blue moon — if that.
  5. That happens once in a blue moon, if ever.

32. Through thick and thin — 同甘共苦/风雨同路

例句：

  1. We've been friends through thick and thin.
  2. She stuck by him through thick and thin.
  3. My parents have been together through thick and thin for forty years.
  4. True friends stay with you through thick and thin.
  5. We've been through thick and thin together, and we're still going strong.

33. Put yourself in my shoes — 换位思考/站在我的角度想

例句：

  1. Put yourself in my shoes — what would you have done?
  2. If you put yourself in her shoes, you'd understand why she's upset.
  3. Just put yourself in my shoes for a second.
  4. Try to put yourself in their shoes before judging.
  5. If I were in your shoes, I'd take the job.

34. Barking up the wrong tree — 找错人了/搞错方向

字面意思：对着错误的树叫。实际意思：你在错误的方向上努力。

例句：

  1. If you think I took your pen, you're barking up the wrong tree.
  2. You're barking up the wrong tree — talk to HR about that.
  3. The police were barking up the wrong tree the whole time.
  4. If you're looking for sympathy, you're barking up the wrong tree.
  5. He realized he'd been barking up the wrong tree all along.

35. See eye to eye — 意见一致/看法相同

例句：

  1. We don't always see eye to eye, but we respect each other.
  2. Do you guys see eye to eye on this?
  3. They never see eye to eye on money matters.
  4. I'm glad we see eye to eye on this issue.
  5. My boss and I don't see eye to eye on much.

实战对比：完整段落

课本版本：

"I was very surprised when he called me unexpectedly. I think we need to be completely honest with each other and speak directly about the problem. The situation is difficult, but we must make an effort. In my opinion, the cost is too high. I am very tired of dealing with this issue."

母语者版本：

"I was totally caught off guard when he called me out of the blue. Honestly, I think we need to stop beating around the bush and just cut to the chase. The whole thing's a mess, but we gotta bite the bullet and deal with it. To be fair, it costs an arm and a leg, and I'm sick and tired of dealing with it."

习语学习策略

  1. 不要逐字翻译——习语的意思无法从字面猜出
  2. 按场景分组学——比如"描述情绪"、"描述工作"、"描述人际关系"
  3. 学一个用一个——今天学了 "piece of cake"，就在下次说 "easy" 的时候换成它
  4. 多看电影和美剧——习语在影视作品中密度最高
  5. 注意语气和场合——有些习语很随意，不适合正式场合
  6. 积累搭配——比如 "sick and tired" 总是连在一起说
